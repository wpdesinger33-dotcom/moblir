The Kingstar E700 is a budget feature phone in the market for use in calling and sending messages. Bangladeshi people got this phone into their hand on 24 September 2014. It comes equipped with a 2.4 inches TFT display resolution 240 x 320 pixels. The phone comes with 1.3MP of a primary camera and has 16GB internal storage. The E700 operates through a 1600 mAh Li-Ion battery giving talk time of up to 7 hours with the standby time capability of up to 380 hours. It has dual SIM connectivity, that means you can use two different numbers in a single device at the same time. Communication technology supported are: GSM and Bluetooth 2.0. This particular model also has an expandable memory slot on board in the form of a micro SD card with support to up to 32GB. The Kingstar E700 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,925 (Official).
