Kingstar A17 Plus is an entry level Android smartphone which is developed with an aim of fulfilling essential operations at a low cost. It has 5.0 inches, IPS LCD touchscreen with a resolution of 240 x 320 pixels. The phone is running on Cortex A7 processor. Powered by Android 7.0 Nougat. As for the photography, the features of the Kingstar A17 Plus include the rear camera with 5 MP and front camera with 2 MP. One major aspect of the phone is the battery power of 1500 mAh. The Kingstar A17 Plus price in Bangladesh is  BDT 2,599 (Official) for 256MB of RAM and 512MB of ROM.
