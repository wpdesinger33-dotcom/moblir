The Kingstar B21 is one of the most affordable feature phones on the market aimed mostly at those who need simple communication tools. With a 1.8-inch TFT display. Its 1.3MP primary camera. An 800 mAh Li-ion battery powers the phone which gives about 7 hours’ talk time and 380 hours of standby time. This means that dual SIM support is good for changing between two numbers. Bluetooth v2.0 therefore supports connection of other devices for audio and data transfer. It supports microSD cards, up to 8GB. Additional facilities like FM Radio and Audio & video players etc. The Kingstar B21 price in Bangladesh is BDT 999 (Official).
