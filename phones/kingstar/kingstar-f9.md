The Kingstar F9 is a good and cheap feature phone released on 20 October 2014 in Bangladesh. Boasting of 3.5 inches of TFT display with a resolution of 240 x 320 pixels. The phone is equipped with a 1.3MP primary camera. The F9 embodies a 1700mAh Li-Ion battery to afford users a battery life of 7 hours of talk time besides 420 hours of standby time. The ability to use two different numbers on the device and switching between the two is very easy. This is due to the availability of a microSD card which has a capacity of up to 16GB of expanded memory. It also has some utility options like, FM radio, audio player, and video player. The Kingstar F9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,099 (Official).
