The Kingstar TITANS 5 (i18) is endowed with truly useful features for Androids in the market of budget segment smartphones. It also has a screen size of 3.5 inches coupled with a pixel density of 720 x 1280. Equipped with a high performing Quad-Core 1.2GHz ARM Cortex A7. It is operable on Android 5.1 Lollipop. On the back it has a 2MP camera while it does not have a front-facing camera. The lithium-ion battery installed in the TITANS 5 (i18) is 2500 mAh and the phone provides up to 7 hours of talk time and up to 380 hours standby time. The Kingstar TITANS 5 (i18) price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,800 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of ROM.
