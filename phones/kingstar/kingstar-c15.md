Kingstar C15 is an affordable feature phone in Bangladesh, launched in April 2014. The phone comes with a 128 x 160 pixels, and  1.77-inch TFT display. It is expandable with the aid of a microSD card slot up to 8GB. The rear camera on the Kingstar C15 is 1.3 MP. It comes with dual SIM support, Bluetooth 2.0, USB and FM Radio. Battery of the device is 1750 mAh Li-Ion. The Kingstar C15 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,330 (Official).
