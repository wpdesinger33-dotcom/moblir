The Kingstar TITANS 1 (i19) is an uncomplicated and stable smartphone. The mobile has a 5 inches IPS, LCD touchscreen with the screen resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The phone has a Quad-Core 1.3GHz ARM Cortex A7 processor for its powering system. It runs on the Android operating system version 5.0 also known as Lollipop. It has a primary camera of 8 MP and the secondary camera is of 5 MP. Powered by a 2200mAh Li-Ion battery the Kingstar TITANS 1 (i19) provides up to 7 hours of continuous talk time and up to 300 hours of standby time. The Kingstar TITANS 1 (i19) price in Bangladesh is BDT 7,300 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of ROM.
