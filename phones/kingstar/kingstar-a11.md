The Kingstar A11 is one of the cheap Android smartphones in the Bangladesh market. It has a touch screen, with 3.5 inches display size and a resolution of 480 x 800 pixels. The phone operates with Quad-Core 1.2GHz ARM Cortex A7 processor. The A11 is built with features of Android 4.4.2 (KitKat). The device features a 2MP rear camera. The Kingstar A11 has a power of 1200mAh battery, which can last talk time up to 7 hours and 380 hours of standby time. It offers Dual SIM functionality which enables the User to manage two numbers at a time. The Kingstar A11 price in Bangladesh is BDT 3,450 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of ROM.
