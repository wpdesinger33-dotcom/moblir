The Kingstar A22 Plus is available at a low cost, it was first introduced in Bangladesh in January, 2016. It has an IPS LCD display with a screen size of 5 inches and supported by a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels. The operating mechanism of the device comprises a quad core ARM Cortex A7 clocked at 1.0 GHz. The phone has the main rear camera of 2-megapixel, and front-facing 0.3-megapixel camera. The battery of the A22 Plus is a Kingstar 1500mAh removable battery. It operates on the Android version 4.4.2, KitKat. The Kingstar A22 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,650 (Official) for 256MB of RAM and 512MB of ROM. This phone is available in Green colors.
