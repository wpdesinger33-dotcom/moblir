The Kingstar C10i is a feature phone which belongs to the entry level budget phone. It supports a 1.8-inch TFT display and a dot pitch of 160 x 128 pixels. The phone also comes with a 0.3MP primary camera on the rear of the phone. The product also has an expansion slot that supports MicroSD cards up to 32GB. It encompasses such basic offerings such as the FM radio. It uses a Li-Ion 1000mAh battery for which offers up to 310 hours stand-by time and 5 hours talk-time. One of the features is the availability of the dual SIM, which means a user can use two numbers on one device. Available interfaces are GSM and Bluetooth V 2.0. The Kingstar C10i price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,100 (Official).
