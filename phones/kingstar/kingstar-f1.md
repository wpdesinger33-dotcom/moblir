The Kingstar F1 was released in September 2016 in Bangladesh. It includes a 4.5-inch TFT capacitive touch screen with a density of 176 x 220 pixels. The power under the hood comprises a quad core cortex A7 at a frequency of 1.0 GHz. The phone consists of a 2-megapixel rear camera. It comes with a 1200mAh removable battery pack. It has a capability of dual SIM where a user can use two numbers concurrently. The device operates as a basic OS. The Kingstar F1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,460 (Official). This phone comes in Gray color.
