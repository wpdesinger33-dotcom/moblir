The Kingstar A25 is an Android entry level smartphone which was released on 24 September 2014 in Bangladesh. It has a TFT screen of 6.0 inches with screen resolution of 480 x 854 pixels. The phone comes with a Quad-Core 1.3GHz ARM Cortex A7 as the processor of the phone. KitKat version of Android 4.4.2. It boasts of a 5MP main camera on the rear side and another 2MP camera on the front side. The battery of Kingstar A25 is 2200mAh Li-Ion one which provides the chance of speaking up to 7 hours and stand by roughly 300 hours. The Kingstar A25 price in Bangladesh is BDT 5,649 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of ROM.
