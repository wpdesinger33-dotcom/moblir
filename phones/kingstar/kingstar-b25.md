The Kingstar B25 is a budget-friendly feature phone released in July 2014. It features a 1.77-inch TFT display with a resolution of 128 x 160 pixels. It comes with expandable via a microSD card slot up to 8GB. The Kingstar B25 boasts a 1.3 MP rear camera and supports dual SIM functionality, Bluetooth 2.0, USB, and FM radio. The device is equipped with a 3500 mAh Li-Po battery. The Kingstar B25 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,450 (Official).
