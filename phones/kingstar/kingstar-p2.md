Kingstar P2 is a feature phone launched in October 2013 in Bangladesh. It comes with a 1.8-inch TFT Display of resolution 128 x 160 Pixels. Includes a microSD card slot for expansion up to 8GB The Kingstar P2 offers a 1.3 MP rear camera and supports dual SIM, Bluetooth 2.1, USB and FM radio. It packs a 1050 mAh Li-Ion battery. The Kingstar P2 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,099 (Official).
