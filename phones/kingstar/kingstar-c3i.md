Kingstar C3i is an affordable feature phone launched in October 2013 in Bangladesh. There is a 1.8-inch TFT display with 128 x 160 pixels resolution. There is the expandable via a microSD card slot up to 16GB included. It has a 1.3 MP rear camera and supports dual SIM functionality, Bluetooth 2.1, USB as well as FM radio. Powering the device is a 2000 mAh Li-Ion battery. The Kingstar C3i price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,770 (Official).
