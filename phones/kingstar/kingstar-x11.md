The Chinese manufacturer’s Kingstar X11 came out in September 2014. It is an elegantly styled cheap feature phone for essential calling. It has a resolution of 128 x 160 pixels and it possesses a 2 inch TFT display. The device carries a 1.5-megapixel camera and a 1800 mAh battery to enhance battery efficiency. It has the mantle of dual SIM capabilities enabling the user to handle two numbers at any given time. It has features like the FM radio, the 3.5-mm audio jack, and a micro USB port. It is, however, running under a very simple operating system. The Kingstar X11 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,775 (Official).
