The Kingstar A20 is a budget-friendly Android smartphone that offers a blend of essential features and decent performance. It features a 4.5-inch TFT display with a resolution of 320 x 480 pixels. The phone is powered by a Quad-Core 1.0GHz ARM Cortex A9 processor. Running on Android 2.3.7 (Gingerbread). The device is equipped with a 3.2MP primary camera and a VGA secondary camera. With a 1500mAh Li-Ion battery, the Kingstar A20 offers up to 6 hours of talk time and 300 hours of standby time. The Kingstar A20 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,600 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of ROM.
