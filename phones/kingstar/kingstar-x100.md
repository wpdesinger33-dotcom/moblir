An enlarged phone of the Kingstar X100, the specifications of which were released September 2014, is a basic feature phone meant for calls and texting. It has a 2.0-inch TFT display screen with a resolution of 128x160 pixels. The phone is packaged with a 1.3-megapixel camera and a rechargeable 2000mAh battery that is likewise detachable. This Kingstar X100 comes with a Dual SIM feature where the owner can handle two numbers on one device. Some of the basic interface heads are an FM radio, a 3.5 mm audio jack and a micro USB port. The Kingstar X100 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,299 (Official).
