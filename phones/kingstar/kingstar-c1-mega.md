The Kingstar C1 Mega is an affordable feature phone, launched in February 2014 in Bangladesh. It comes with a 1.77-inch TFT display that has a resolution of 128 x 160 pixels. It has support for microSD card slots up to 4GB expandable memory. Kingstar C1 Mega features a 1.3 MP rear camera, is dual-SIM compatible and offers Bluetooth 2.0 connectivity, USB and FM radio. Under the hood, is a 2000 mAh Li-Ion battery. The Kingstar C1 Mega price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,099 (Official).
