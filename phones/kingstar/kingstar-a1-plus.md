The Kingstar A1 Plus is an Android smartphone for the low end market launched in January 2016 in Bangladesh. The device is equipped with a 5.0-inch TFT display with the resolution of 320 x 480 pixels. It is driven by a Cortex A7 processor. As it is built on Android 4.4.2 (KitKat). As for photography the cell phone features video with a 5MP rear and 0.3MP front camera. The battery of the Kingstar A1 Plus is a 1700 mAh battery. The Kingstar A1 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 3,999 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 8GB of ROM.
