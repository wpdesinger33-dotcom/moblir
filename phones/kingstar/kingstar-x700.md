The Kingstar X700 is an entry level feature phone for those who simply want to make calls and send messages. It was released in Bangladesh on September 24, 2015. This phone has incorporated a TFT type of screen with a size of 1.77 inches dimensioned 128 by 160 pixels. The phone has a primary camera of 1.3MP. It has a Li-Ion 1500 mAh battery, with maximum standby time of 380 hrs and maximum talk time of 7 hrs. Another feature is the availability of two SIM cards at once as well as the ability to work with two numbers. The available options for communication terminal are GSM, GPRS, and Bluetooth v2.0 Also, it comes with a microSD card slot for further storage with support for up to 8GB. It comprises informative functions such as FM radio, loudspeaker, and document reader. The Kingstar X700 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,150 (Official).
