The Kingstar C1 Metal is an economical device that helps in fulfilling the communication necessity of people who do not require the smart functionalities. Its screen size is 1.8 inch TFT and the screen resolution is 128 x 160.This phone has a 1.3MP main camera. It also has a microSD card slot for a memory expansion which accepts up to 4GB of memory card. It comes with some basic functionalities such as FM radio, and loudspeaker. Supported by Li-Ion 2000 mAh battery, standby time up to 410 hours and talk time up to 8 hours. The device also has dual SIM capability where the user can be able to use two different numbers at the same time. The Kingstar C1 Metal price in Bangladesh is BDT 950 (Official).
