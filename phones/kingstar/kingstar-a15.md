Kingstar A15 is an Android low-end smartphone which was unveiled for Bangladeshi consumers on 20 January, 2016. This phone has a display screen size of 4.5" and the display resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The phone is fitted with a Quad-Core 1.2GHz ARM Cortex A9 processor for an upgrade on the phone's operation. The A15 has the Google Android 4.4.2 KitKat version in it. The built-in camera is composed of a 5MP primary camera and 1.3MP secondary camera. It also has a 1200 mAh Li-Ion battery inside it. For the connectivity it is Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth and microUSB v2.0. The Kingstar A15 price in Bangladesh is BDT 4,640 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
