The Kingstar A22 set was launched in May 2014 to the Bangladesh market. It comes with a 4.5 Inches of IPS LCD screen and resolution 320 x 480 pixels. The device operates through a quad core Cortex A9 processor and its processing speed is 1.0 GHz. The phone has a 2-megapixel rear camera and the VGA front camera. Kingstar A22 comes with a 1750mAh non-removable battery. It is used on Android 4.2.2 (Jelly Bean). The Kingstar A22 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,590 (Official) for 256MB of RAM and 512MB of ROM.
