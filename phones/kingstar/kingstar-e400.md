The Kingstar E400 is a feature phone that has been manufactured to meet simple, everyday call requirements. It comes with a 1.8 inches TFT color screen with a depth of 128 x 160 pixels. The primary camera of the phone is VGA. It has a Li-Ion 1400 mAh battery,  which promises up to 380 hours of standby time and up to 7 hours of talk time. The phone also has the dual SIM feature, which enables a user to operate two lines at the same time. Other features for communication are the GSM, GPRS and Bluetooth 2.0. It also has a microSD card slot for an additional memory storage which can take up to 8GB. It also contains the options such as FM radio, loudspeaker, and document reader. The Kingstar E400 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,520 (Official).
