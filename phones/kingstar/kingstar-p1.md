Kingstar P1 is an affordable feature phone which was launched in Bangladesh in April 2014. This model includes a 1.77- inch TFT display, and a density of 128 x 160 pixels. It comes with expandable via a microSD card slot up to 4GB. The Kingstar P1 has a VGA rear camera and it has the options of dual SIM, Bluetooth 2.1, USB and FM Radio. The device is powered by a 1000 mAh Li-Ion battery. The Kingstar P1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,350 (Official).
