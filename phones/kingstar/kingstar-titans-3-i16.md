The Kingstar TITANS 3 (i16) is an Android budget smartphone released for the market in January, 2016. It comes with a 5.0 inches IPS LCD display, 480 x 854 pixels resolution. The phone uses a Quad core processor of 1.3 GHz cortex A7 as an enhanced processing power. The Kingstar TITANS 3 (i16) which is based on the Android 4.4.2 (KitKat) Operating System. It has 8 MP for the rear camera and 5 MP for the front camera. Its battery has a capacity of 2000 mAh. The Kingstar TITANS 3 (i16)  price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,500 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of ROM.
