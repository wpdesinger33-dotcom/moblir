The Leitz Phone 1 combines high-end performance capabilities with stylish design features. The smartphone includes a 6.6-inch Pro IGZO OLEDdisplaywhich supports 1260x2730 pixels resolution and operates at a 240Hz refresh rate. The device operates using the Qualcomm Snapdragon 888 5G chipset. Running on Android 11. It features one primary camera with 20.2MP and f/1.9 aperture that enables 8K video recording and high-quality image capture. The camera located on the front side of the device features a 12.6MP shooting capability. The Leitz Phone 1 combines durability with user-friendly features including IPX5/IPX8/IP6X ratings which protect against water and dust. The device features a 5000mAh non-removable Li-Polybatterythat provides extended usage and it has a USB Type-C 3.0 port for quick charging. Available in black colors. The Leitz Phone 1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 130,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
