The LG G6 is a 2017 Android smartphone that boasts a 5.7-inch IPS LCD display. Its sleek design features a glass front (Gorilla Glass 3), a glass back (Gorilla Glass 5), and an aluminum frame. The phone is water-resistant (IP68) and MIL-STD-810G compliant, although ruggedness isn’t guaranteed for extreme conditions. Keep reading this article to know about the LG G6 price in Bangladesh and its details. Its display offers a resolution of 1440 x 2880 pixels with an 18:9 aspect ratio, providing a pixel density of approximately 564 ppi. Under the hood, it runs on the Qualcomm Snapdragon 821 chipset, featuring a quad-core CPU (2x2.35 GHz Kryo and 2x1.6 GHz Kryo) and Adreno 530 GPU. Users can choose from three internal storage options: 32GB, 64GB, or 128GB, all paired with 4GB of RAM (UFS 2.0). The LG G6’s standout feature is its dual-camera setup on the back. It combines a 13 MP standard lens (f/1.8, 30mm) with a 13 MP ultrawide lens (f/2.4, 12mm). The camera system supports dual-LED flash, HDR, and panorama, and it can capture 4K videos at 30fps. On the front, there’s a 5 MP selfie camera (f/2.2, 18mm).
