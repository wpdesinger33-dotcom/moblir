The LG G Pro 2 is an Android smartphone that was announced in February 2014 during the Mobile World Congress in Barcelona, Spain. Serving as a successor to the LG Optimus G Pro, it aimed to compete with Samsung’s Note series. The phone features a large 5.9-inch True IPS+ LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. Its Snapdragon 800 chipset powers a quad-core 2.26 GHz Krait 400 CPU and Adreno 330 GPU. Keep reading this article to know about the LG G Pro 2 price in Bangladesh and its details. Its camera setup includes a 13 MP primary camera with autofocus, optical image stabilization (OIS), LED flash, panorama, and HDR capabilities. It can record 4K video at 30fps, 1080p at 60fps, and 720p at 120fps. The front-facing camera is a 2.1 MP shooter capable of 1080p video recording. Other notable features include SlimPort video out support, dual-band Wi-Fi, NFC, an infrared port, and a removable 3200 mAh Li-Ion battery. This phone runs Android 4.4.2 (KitKat) with LG’s Optimus UI, upgradable to 5.0.1 (Lollipop). The LG G Pro 2 price in Bangladesh is BDT 49,900 (Official) for its 3GB of RAM and 16/32GB of internal storage.
