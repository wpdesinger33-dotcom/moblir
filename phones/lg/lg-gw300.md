The LG GW300 was launched in 2009 in Bangladesh and is a classic feature phone which comes with good looks. It houses a 2.4 inch TFT screen with display resolution of 240 x 320 pixels. This model also comes with a QWERTY keyboard. The GW300 boasts of a 2MP camera. Wireless connectivity is through Bluetooth 2.1, A2DP for streaming of audio and FM radio for entertainment. It is backed by a 900mAh battery for standby up to 400 h and talk time up to 5 h. The LG GW300 price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,900 (Official) with 15MB of RAM.
