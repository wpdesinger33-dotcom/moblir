The LG K10 is a budget-friendly Android smartphone that was announced in January 2016. It features a 5.3” display and resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. Powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 410 chipset, and Cortex-A53 CPU. Keep reading this article to know about the LG K10 price in Bangladesh and its details. Depending on the variant, it comes with either 1.5 GB or 2 GB of RAM and 16 GB of internal storage. For photography, the K10 boasts a 13 MP primary camera with an f/2.2 aperture (in the LTE model) and an 8 MP front camera. Additionally, the phone features LED flash and panorama mode for capturing memorable moments. It runs on Android 5.1.1 (Lollipop) or Android 6.0 (Marshmallow). It’s equipped with a 3000 mAh removable battery. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 4.1, GPS, GLONASS, and NFC. You’ll find a microUSB 2.0 port for charging and data transfer.
