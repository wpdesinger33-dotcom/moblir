The LG G Flex was ahead of its time, offering a curved display and a self-healing back cover, but it didn’t achieve widespread popularity due to its unconventional form factor and limited availability. It features a 6.0-inch curved P-OLED display, which was a groundbreaking design choice at the time. Under the hood, the G Flex was powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 800 chipset with a quad-core 2.26 GHz Krait 400 CPU. It ran Android 4.2.2 Jelly Bean, which was later upgradable to Android 4.4.2 KitKat. The phone’s 13 MP rear camera, and the 2.1 MP front camera handled selfies. Other notable features included NFC, infrared port, and a 3500 mAh non-removable battery. The G Flex supported 4G LTE connectivity and had a microUSB 2.0 port for charging and data transfer. The LG G Flex price in Bangladesh is BDT 59900 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage.
