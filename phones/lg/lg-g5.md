The LG G5 is another product of the LG electronics company which was launched in April 2016 in Bangladesh. It comes with a 5.3 inches IPS LCD capacitive touch screen with a pixel density of 1440 x 2560. The device is fueled by the Qualcomm’s Snapdragon 820 processor with an Adreno 530. The LG G5 has 4 GB of RAM and 32 GB ROM expandable using a micro SD card which supports up to 2 TB. It has dual rear camera system-including 16MP, and 8MP selfie shooter. The phone comes with a 2800mAh removable battery and 18W fast charging. It comes in colours like silver, titan, gold and pink. The LG G5 price in Bangladesh is BDT 60,900 (Official).
