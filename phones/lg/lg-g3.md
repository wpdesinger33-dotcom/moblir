The LG G3, announced in May 2014, is an Android smartphone that stands out for its impressive features. This device adopts several design aspects from its predecessor, the G2, including narrow screen bezels and rear-positioned power and volume buttons. The device is powered by a Snapdragon 801 chipset and features a 5.5-inch display, 13 MP primary camera, and 2.1 MP front camera. Its 3000 mAh battery ensures decent battery life. The phone’s Corning Gorilla Glass 3 protects against scratches and minor impacts. The LG G3 price in Bangladesh is BDT 33,100 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage.
