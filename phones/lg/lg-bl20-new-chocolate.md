The LG BL20 New Chocolate is a very sleek stylish feature phone that was released in October of 2009. It has 2.4 inches TFT color display with a touch screen and a screen resolution of 240 x 320 pixels. The phone is small and easily portable; it has dimensions of 106.9 x 50.8 x 12.3 mm and it weighs 115 grams. Using an internal memory of 60MB, the BL20 New Chocolate has a support for the microSDHC card with a memory capacity of up to 2GB. It includes a 5MP camera. This particular phone has a capacity of 900 mAh battery. The LG BL20 New Chocolate price in Bangladesh is BDT 13,990 (Official).
