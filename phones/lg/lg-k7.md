The LG K7 is an entry-level Android smartphone that was announced in February 2016. It features a 5.0” IPS LCD display and resolution of 480 x 854 pixels. This smartphone is powered by Mediatek MT6580M processor. It comes with 8GB of internal storage and 1GB or 1.5GB of RAM, depending on the variant. Keep reading this article to know about the LG K7 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of cameras, the LG K7 sports either a 5 MP (f/2.6) or an 8 MP primary camera, along with a 5 MP front-facing camera. The main camera features an LED flash and can record videos at 720p@30fps. The phone runs on Android 5.1 (Lollipop) and includes features like Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 4.1, GPS, FM radio, and a microUSB 2.0 port. The LG K7 is powered by a removable 2125 mAh Li-Ion battery. It was available in colors such as White, Black, and Gold (with a Titan color option for the LTE model). Overall, the LG K7 offered decent performance and network connectivity, making it a suitable choice for budget-conscious users. The LG K7 price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,700 (Official) for its 1GB of RAM and 8GB of internal storage.
