The LG Nexus 4 E960 is an Android smartphone that was announced in October 2012. It features a 4.7-inch True HD IPS Plus display with a resolution of 768 x 1280 pixels. The phone is powered by a Qualcomm APQ8064 Snapdragon S4 Pro chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.5 GHz Krait CPU and Adreno 320 GPU. It comes with 2 GB of RAM and either 8 GB or 16 GB of internal storage (no microSD card slot). Keep reading this article to know about the LG Nexus 4 E960 price in Bangladesh and its details. The camera setup includes an 8 MP primary camera with autofocus and LED flash, capable of recording 1080p videos at 30fps. For selfies, there’s a 1.3 MP front camera. The device runs Android 4.2 (Jelly Bean) out of the box, but it’s upgradable to Android 5.1 (Lollipop). Other notable features include a non-removable 2100 mAh Li-Po battery with wireless (Qi) charging support, Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.0, GPS, GLONASS, NFC, and a microUSB 2.0 port. It also has sensors like accelerometer, gyro, proximity, compass, and barometer. It weighs 139 grams and measures 133.9 x 68.7 x 9.1 mm. Its design includes Corning Gorilla Glass 2 for added durability. The LG Nexus 4 E960 price in Bangladesh is BDT 31,000 (Official) for its 2GB of RAM and 8/16GB of internal storage.
