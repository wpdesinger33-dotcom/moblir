The LG G Flex2 is an Android phablet developed by LG Electronics. It was announced in January 2015 as a successor to the original LG G Flex released in 2013. The G Flex2 features a curved body and a flexible display, distinguishing it from conventional smartphones. Its design resembles the LG G3, and it boasts improved dimensions and a better display compared to its predecessor. Keep reading this article to know about the LG G Flex2 price in Bangladesh and its details. It comes with a 5.5-inch Curved P-OLED screen with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. This phone is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 810 chipset, and an Adreno 430 GPU. Users can choose between two variants: one with 16GB storage and 2GB RAM, and another with 32GB storage and 3GB RAM. In terms of photography, the G Flex2 features a 13 MP primary camera with Laser AF and OIS, capable of recording 4K videos at 30fps. The front camera is a 2.1 MP shooter for selfies and video calls. Other notable features include Wi-Fi 802.11 ac, Bluetooth 4.1, GPS, NFC, and microUSB 2.0 connectivity. Its 3000 mAh non-removable battery with 18W wired charging. The LG G Flex2 price in Bangladesh is BDT 28,620 (Unofficial) for its 3GB of RAM and 32GB of internal storage.
