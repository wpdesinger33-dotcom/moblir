The LG KP135 is a model of an LG company that is basically a feature phone for simple communication requirements. It includes a 1.5 inch TFT display screen with a pixel density of 128 x 128. The phone is small and portable; it has dimensions of 99.8mm in width, 45.3mm in thickness and 13.4mm in depth and a weight of 70 grams. Additional storage is also provided by the microSD card slot in the KP135 with storage up to 2GB is available. It has a 0.3 Megapixels VGA camera with LED flashlight. It features GSM network technology and basic facilities including SMS, WAP 2.0/xHTML, Radio and Speakerphone. This phone is empowered by a 900 mAh battery. The LG KP135 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,365 (Official).
