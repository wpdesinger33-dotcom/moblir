The LG KP105 is a slim and light weight model of a feature phone which is suitable for simple usage. This was launched in May 2008 in Bangladesh. It has a 1.5 inches CSTN display that has a pixel resolution of 128 x 128. The built-in memory is 1MB expandable with an external microSD card slot. It also incorporates a 900mAh Li-Ion battery which offers standby time up to 475 hours, talking time up to 4 hours 30 minutes. It also has SMS, a phonebook of 300 entries, and downloadable polyphonic ringtones. The LG KP105 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,600 (Official).
