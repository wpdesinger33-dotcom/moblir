The LG GB110 is a simple or indeed a special feature mobile that is aimed at simple forms of communications. It was released in August 2009. It has a tiny 1.5 inches CSTN display with a density of 128 x 128 pixels. The phone is slim and light, with a dimension of 103 x 45 x 14.6mm and a weight of 65 grams. This version is driven by a small internal memory of 1MB. It includes a VGA camera. The phone uses GSM network connection and such complementary features as text messaging, WAP 2.0/xHTML browser, and FM radio. The LG GB110 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,690 (Official).
