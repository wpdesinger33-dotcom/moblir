The LG K4 (2017) offers basic functionality and a budget-friendly option for users. It is an Android smartphone that was released in March 2017. It features a 5.0-inch display with a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels, protected by Corning Gorilla Glass 3. Under the hood, it’s powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 210 chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.1 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU and Adreno 304 GPU. The main camera is a 5 MP shooter with LED flash and HDR capabilities, while the front-facing camera is also 5 MP. The device runs on Android 6.0.1 Marshmallow and is equipped with a removable 2500 mAh Li-Ion battery. The LG K4 (2017) price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,900 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
