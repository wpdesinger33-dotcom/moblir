The LG KM380 which was released to the market in July 2008 is a simple cellular phone that is meant for simple calling. It has an opportunity to boast of a 2.0-inch TFT display with the resolution of 240 x 320 pixels. The phone is small and portable in size and weight; it measures 108mm in the width, 48mm in the height, and 13mm in thickness and weighs 79 grams. With internal memory of only 40MB, the KM380 is endowed with expandable memory by a microSD card that supports up to 32 GB. It includes a 1.3MP camera. Battery of the phone is 800 mAh. The LG KM380 price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,065 (Official).
