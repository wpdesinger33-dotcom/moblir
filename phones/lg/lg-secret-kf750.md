The LG Secret KF750 is actually a feature phone with slender build and the most important functions. It was launched in June 2008. It has a 2.4 inch TFT screen with a pixelation of 240 x 320 pixels. The phone is slim and compact with a dimension of 102.8mm in width, 50.8mm in height and 11.8mm in thickness and it weighs 116 grams. Built with an internal memory of 100MB, the KF750 has a microSDHC slot for external memory up to 2GB. It has a 5.0-megapixel camera which comes with autofocus and LED flash for taking pictures and makes use of 480p for recording of videos. Phone supports an 800 mAh battery. The LG Secret KF750 price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,800 (Official).
