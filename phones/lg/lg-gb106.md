The LG GB106, which was released in March 2009, is a simple model which targets individuals who need a phone simply for making calls. The LCD is a CSTN type with a screen size of 1,5 inches and a non-interpolated resolution of 128х128 pixels. The phone is small and thin, its dimensions are 102 x 45 x 14.6 mm and has a weight of 70 grams. Built-in ROM is of 1 MB while RAM is expandable up to 2 GB via a microSD card for further storage. It includes a VGA camera. The phone was operated by a 950 mAh battery. The LG GB106 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,550 (Official). This feature phone has Black, and White colors.
