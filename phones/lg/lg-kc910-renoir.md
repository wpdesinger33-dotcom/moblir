The LG KC910 Renoir is a feature phone that can be easily recommended to anyone due to its powerful camera and a clean design. The phone was released in November of 2008 in Bangladesh. It has a touch screen of TFT 3.0 inches in size, with a resolution of 240 x 400 pixels. It has a 1000mAh battery. The phone is also fitted with an 8MP main camera. In the case of videos, the standard resolution of the captured clips is 480p and a frame rate of 30fps. It also has 100MB of internal memory and can be expanded by installing a microSDHC card slot. It is compact and weighs only 114 grams; its dimensions are 107.8 x 55.9 x 14 mm. The LG KC910 price in Bangladesh is BDT 24,690 (Official).
