The LG KM710 which was released to the market in August 2008 is an LG feature phone. It has a TFT display that is of an advanced type with an extent of 2.0 inch that has 240 x 320 pixels. It is lightweight and portable in size, 103 x 48 x 15.4 mm, and a weight of 118 grams. Included with a meager built-in memory of 15MB, the KM710 has a microSD card slot for an extra memory of up to 2GB. It includes a 3.2MP camera. It has a 800 mAh battery. The LG KM710 LG KM710 price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,020 (Official).
