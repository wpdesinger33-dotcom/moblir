The LG KT520 is a simple, easy to use feature phone that is ideal for simple and easy communication. It has a display of 2.2 inches TFT with the display colors of 240 x 320 pixels. The phone is mini and lightweight, its dimensions are 101.4 x 49.6 x 15.3 mm and its weight is 97 g. With the internal memory of only 30MB, the KT520 has a Micro SDHC card slot for an expansion of up to 2GB. It has a rear and front facing camera with the rear facing camera being 3.15MP and the front facing camera having LED flash. The phone supports a 800 mAh battery. The LG KT520 price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,400 (Official).
