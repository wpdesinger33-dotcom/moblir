The LG KM900 was announced in April 2009, is a flagship multimedia phone by LG Electronics. It features a 3.0-inch TFT display with a resolution of 480x800 pixels and Corning Gorilla Glass protection. Its 3D S-Class user interface was a highlight at the time. With a weight of 105 grams, it’s compact and pocket-friendly. Keep reading this article to know about the LG KM900 price in Bangladesh and its details. Inside, the KM900 packs an 8GB internal storage, expandable via microSDHC. The 5-megapixel main camera boasts Schneider-Kreuznach optics and LED flash, capable of recording 480p videos at 30fps. For selfies and video calls, there’s a front-facing camera. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 802.11b/g, Bluetooth 4.0 with A2DP, GPS with A-GPS, and FM radio. The battery is a removable 1000mAh Li-Ion unit, offering up to 300 hours of standby time and around 3 hours and 50 minutes of talk time. The LG KM900 price in Bangladesh is BDT 18,990 (Official) for its 1GB of RAM and 8GB of internal storage.
