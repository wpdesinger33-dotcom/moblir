The LG KP220 is a simple cellular phone which has been manufactured with the intention of being easy to operate. It was launched in May 2008 in Bangladesh. It has a screen type as 1.7 inches TFT that has a display resolution of 120 x 160 pixels. It has a 1.3MP camera as part of the phone’s features. It also has 60MB of internal memory which is also expandable via the micro SD card slot. The device also features tri-band GSM that means good and reliable network connection and good quality calls. It also comes with a 750mAh battery, which supports up to 300 hours standby, and up to 3 hours talk time. The LG KP220 price in Bangladesh is BDT 5,890 (Official).
