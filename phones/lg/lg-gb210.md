The LG GB210 is a simple, small-sized LG model for people who need only the essentials from their mobile phone. It was officially launched in 2009 in Bangladesh. It comes with a 1.77-inch TFT color screen with a pixel density of 120 x 160 pixels. Other features that accompany the GB210, it also has a 1.3MP camera. It can be expanded through a microSDHC card. Bluetooth technology provided in the device is Bluetooth 2.0 and there is an added feature of FM radio for entertainment. But it does not have 3G or Wi-Fi, which restricts its Internet connection. The battery power of this mobile is 950 mAh, and the standby time is up to 500 h and talk time up to 5 h. The LG GB210 price in Bangladesh is BDT 3,690 (Official) with 4GB of phone storage.
