LG KP175 was launched in December 2008 and is a feature phone that is built with basic features used for easy calling. It boasts of a 1.7 inch TFT LED backlit display with a maximum resolution of 128 x 160. The phone is portable and easy to carry around being 105.2mm in width, 45.6mm in depth, and 13.9mm in thickness and weighing 72 grams. Integrated with a small internal memory of 4MB the KP175 has a space for a microSD card up to 2GB. It has a VGA camera for taking ordinary pictures. It has a 950 mAh battery. The LG KP175 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,990 (Official).
