The LG KP260 is a simple mobile phone meant to provide only call and messaging service. It was released in February 2009, has a 1.77 inch TFT display with a resolution of 128×160. The phone is easy to operate and carry, it has dimensions of 96.6mm in width, 46.5mm in width, and 16.9mm in thickness and it weighs 90 grams. With internal memory of only 5MB, the KP260 has a support for microSD card with a maximum of 2GB. It has a rear and front camera with 1.3MP and LED flash respectively. It provides a 900 mAh battery. The LG KP260 price in Bangladesh is BDT 5,690 (Official).
