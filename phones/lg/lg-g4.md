The LG G4 was released in April 2015, boasts a 5.5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1440 x 2560 pixels. Its design features a glass front (protected by Corning Gorilla Glass 3), a plastic back, and a plastic frame. Under the hood, it runs on Android 5.1.1 (Lollipop), upgradable to 7.0 (Nougat), with LG’s UX 4 UI. Keep reading this article to know about the LG G4 price in Bangladesh and its details. This smartphone is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 808 chipset, and Adreno 418 GPU. The camera setup includes a 16 MP primary camera with f/1.8 aperture, laser autofocus, and 3-axis optical image stabilization (OIS). It captures 4K videos at 30fps and offers features like panorama and HDR. For selfies, 8 MP front camera. Other notable features include Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac, Bluetooth 4.1, GPS, NFC, stereo FM radio, and a 3000 mAh removable battery. The LG G4 comes in various colors, including Grey, White, Gold, and Leather options. Despite its age, it remains a reliable choice for those seeking a well-rounded smartphone experience. The LG G4 price in Bangladesh is BDT 46,500 (Unofficial) for its 3GB of RAM and 32GB of ROM.
