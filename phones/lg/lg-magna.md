The LG Magna is one of the mid-end smartphones which gives a good performance and other features. It was launched in May 2015. With a screen size of 5.0 inches it uses an IPS LCD screen and has a pixel density of 720 x 1280. The phone comes loaded with the MediaTek MT6582 processor. Its excellent camera features are fitted with an 8 MP main camera with autofocus and a 5 MP front camera. It is operated by Android 5.0.1 (Lollipop) and Android 6.0.1 (Marshmallow) can be installed on this device. It has a 2540mAh battery. The LG Magna price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,300 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
