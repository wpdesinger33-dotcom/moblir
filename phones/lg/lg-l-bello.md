The LG L Bello is an Android smartphone that was announced in August 2014. It features a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels, resulting in a pixel density of approximately 196 ppi. The phone is powered by a 1.3GHz quad-core processor and comes with 1GB of RAM. It runs on Android 4.4.2 (KitKat) with LG’s UI 3. Keep reading this article to know about the LG L Bello price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of photography, the LG L Bello sports an 8 MP primary camera with autofocus and LED flash, capable of recording videos at 1080p@30fps. For selfies, there’s a 1 MP front camera. The phone offers 8GB of internal storage, expandable via a dedicated microSDHC slot. It also features GPS and GLONASS for positioning. The 2540mAh removable battery provides decent standby time and talk time. Available colors include White, Black, and Gold. The LG L Bello price in Bangladesh is BDT 18,250 (Official) for its 1GB of RAM and 8GB of internal storage.
