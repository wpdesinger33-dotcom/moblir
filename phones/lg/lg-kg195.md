The LG KG195 is a compact feature phone designed for basic communication needs. It was released on 10 August 2007 in bangladesh. It has a 1.8-inch TFT display with a resolution of 128 x 160 pixels. The KG195 also includes a 0.3 MP camera for capturing photos. The device supports Bluetooth 1.2, A2DP for wireless audio streaming, and FM radio for entertainment. Battery life is supported by a 830 mAh Li-Ion battery, which provides standby up to 300 h, and talk time up to 2 h 30 min. The LG KG195 price in Bangladesh is BDT 4,740 (Official) with 64MB phone storage.
