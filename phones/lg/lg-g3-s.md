The LG G3 S, also known as the LG G3 Beat or LG G3 Vigor in some regions, was released in August 2014. Sporting a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels, it offered a pixel density of approximately 294 ppi. The phone ran on Android 4.4.2 (KitKat), which could be upgraded to 5.0.2 (Lollipop). Under the hood, it featured a Qualcomm Snapdragon 400 chipset with a quad-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU and Adreno 305 GPU. In terms of photography, the LG G3 S boasted an 8 MP rear camera. For selfies, 1.3 MP front-facing camera. The phone’s 2540 mAh removable battery provided decent standby time (up to 700 hours on 2G) and talk time (up to 16 hours on 2G). The LG G3 S price in Bangladesh is BDT 27900 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
