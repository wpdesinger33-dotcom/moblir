The LG K10 (2017) is an Android smartphone that was announced in December 2016. It offers a 5.3-inch HD display. Powered by a 1.5 GHz MediaTek octa-core processor, and comes with 2 GB of RAM and 16 GB of storage. Keep reading this article to know about the LG K10 (2017) price in Bangladesh and its details. Its primary camera is 13 MP, while the front-facing camera is 5 MP with a wide-angle lens. The phone is equipped with a removable 2,800 mAh battery. Overall, the LG K10 (2017) offers a decent mid-range experience for users seeking a balance of features and affordability. The LG K10 (2017) price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,490 (Official) for its 2GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage.
