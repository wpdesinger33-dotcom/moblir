The LG KS660 is a simple style phone, released on 15 May 2009. It comes with a 3.0 inches of TFT touch screen with a pixel density of 240 x 400. The phone is slim and lightweight, it measures 105 x 55 x 15 mm and its weight is 110 grams. With internal memory of 85MB, KS660 is furnished with MicroSDHC card slot for expansion up to 2GB. It is equipped with a 5MP main camera with LED flash, for taking photos and recording HD video at 480p. The device is compatible with a 950 mAh battery. The LG KS660 price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,665 (Official) with 85 MB of phone storage.
