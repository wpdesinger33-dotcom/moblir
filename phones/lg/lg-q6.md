The LG Q6 is an Android smartphone that was released in August 2017. It features a 5.5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 2160 pixels and an 18:9 aspect ratio. The phone is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 435 chipset, which includes an octa-core 1.4 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and Adreno 505 GPU. Keep reading this article to know about the LG Q6 price in Bangladesh and its details. It has a 13 MP rear camera with an f/2.2 aperture and LED flash, capable of recording 1080p videos at 30fps. For selfies, there’s a 5 MP front camera with an f/2.2 aperture. The phone supports NFC (market-dependent) and is MIL-STD-810G compliant. It runs on Android 7.1.1 Nougat, upgradable to Android 8.0 Oreo, with LG’s UI 5.0. Design-wise, the LG Q6 features a glass front protected by Corning Gorilla Glass 3, a plastic back, and an aluminum frame. It weighs 149 grams and has a non-removable 3000 mAh Li-Po battery. Available colors include Astro Black, Ice Platinum, Mystic White, and Terra Gold. The LG Q6 price in Bangladesh is BDT 24,900 (Official) for its 3GB of RAM and 32GB of internal storage.
