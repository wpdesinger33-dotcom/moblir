The LG KP110 is an entry-level phone, only expects a phone for calling and texting. It is equipped with a 1.5 inch CSTN display having screen resolution of 128 x 128 dots. The phone is mini and very easy to carry, with dimensions of 103.1mm x 46mm x 13.4mm and weighing 65 grams. With internal memory of 1MB the KP110 has a microSD card slot for external memory up to 2GB. It includes a VGA camera. The device is driven by a 900 mAh battery. The LG KP110 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,590 (Official) with Black, Cherry, Silver, and White colors.
