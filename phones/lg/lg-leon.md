The LG Leon offers a budget-friendly package with a focus on essential features. It was announced in February 2015. It features a compact 4.5-inch display with a resolution of 480x854 pixels. Powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 410 chipset, it offers a quad-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and Adreno 306 GPU. In terms of photography, the LG Leon sports either a 8 MP primary camera, capable of recording 1080p videos. For selfies, there’s a VGA front-facing camera. The device runs on Android 5.0.1 Lollipop and is equipped with a 1900 mAh removable battery. The LG Leon price in Bangladesh is BDT 9800 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
