The LG G Pro Lite Dual is an Android smartphone that was announced in October 2013. This phone measures 150.2 x 76.9 x 9.4 mm and weighs 161 grams. It features a 5.5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 540 x 960 pixels, resulting in a pixel density of approximately 200 ppi. Keep reading this article to know about the LG G Pro Lite Dual price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of performance, the LG G Pro Lite Dual is powered by the MediaTek MT6577 chipset, which includes a dual-core 1.0 GHz Cortex-A9 CPU and the PowerVR SGX531 GPU. It comes with 1 GB of RAM and 8 GB of internal storage, expandable via a microSDHC card. It comes in an 8 MP main camera. For selfies, there’s a 1.3 MP front camera. A removable 3140 mAh Li-Ion battery keeps the device running. It ships with Android 4.1.2 Jelly Bean. The LG G Pro Lite Dual price in Bangladesh is BDT 24,990 (Official) for its 1GB of RAM and 8GB of internal storage.
