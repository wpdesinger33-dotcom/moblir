LG GM200 is a simple phone that only aims to provide communication to its users. This one has a 2.0 inch TFT color display with screen resolution of 176 x 220 pixel. The phone is small and easy to carry, its dimensions are 106mm by 48mm by 14.4mm and the phone weighs 85 grams. With only 14MB of internal memory, the GM200 has a slot for a microSD card with capacity of up to 2GB. It is integrated with a 2MP camera with LED flasher. Phone supports an 1100 mAh battery. The LG GM200 price in Bangladesh is BDT 4,990 (Official). The phone comes in Black, Silver, Gold, and Red colors.
