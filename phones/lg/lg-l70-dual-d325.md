The LG L70 Dual D325 was published in the month of May 2014 in Bangladesh. It is an Android smartphone in the low cost segment which however provides enough functionality. There is a 4.5 inches with an IPS LCD supporting 480 x 800 shield resolution of its screen. The Qualcomm Snapdragon 200 chipset is included with a dual core 1.2 GHz Cortex A7. They are accompanied with a 5MP rear camera and VGA front camera features with the LG L70 Dual D325. It also features options like the Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n), Bluetooth 4.0, GPS as far as networks are concerned it provides good connection. It has a 2100 mAh battery. The LG L70 Dual D325 price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,990 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
