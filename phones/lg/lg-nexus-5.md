The LG Nexus 5, announced in October 2013, was a notable Android smartphone. It featured a 4.95-inch True HD IPS+ display and resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. Its design included a glass front (protected by Gorilla Glass 3) and a plastic back and frame. At 130 grams, it was lightweight and easy to handle. Keep reading this article to know about the LG Nexus 5 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of performance, the Nexus 5 packed a Qualcomm Snapdragon 800 chipset, powered by a quad-core 2.3 GHz Krait 400 CPU and Adreno 330 GPU. It came with 2 GB of RAM and two storage options: 16 GB or 32 GB (non-expandable). The device ran Android 4.4 (Ice Cream Sandwich) out of the box but was upgradable to Android 6.0 (Marshmallow). The 8 MP primary camera featured an f/2.4 lens. For selfies, there was a 1.3 MP front camera. Its 2300 mAh non-removable battery offered up to 17 hours of talk time on 3G. Connectivity options included Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 4.0, GPS, and NFC. The LG Nexus 5 price in Bangladesh is BDT 34,490 (Official) for its 2GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage.
