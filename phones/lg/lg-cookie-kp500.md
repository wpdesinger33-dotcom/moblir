The LG Cookie KP500 is a keypad phone packed with many features. It has a 3.0 inch TFT Resistive touch screen display with QVGA display of 240 x 400 pixels.  It is a compact phone with size of 106.5 x 55.4 x 11.9 mm and it weighs 89 grams. With an internal memory of 48MB, the KP500 has a support slot for a microSDHC card for expansion to 2GB. It consists of a 3.15MP main camera. The phone battery power is 900 mAh. The LG Cookie KP500 price in Bangladesh is BDT 7,490 (Official). The device comes in Black, Vandyke brown, Anodizing silver, Elegant gold, Delicious white, and Pink colors.
