The LG G2 mini is an Android smartphone developed by LG Electronics. Unveiled at the Mobile World Congress in February 2014, it’s designed as a smaller version of its full-sized namesake. It features a 4.7” IPS LCD display with a resolution of 540 x 960 pixels. It’s powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 400 chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU and Adreno 305 GPU. In terms of photography, the G2 mini sports an 8 MP primary camera, capable of recording 1080p videos at 30fps. For selfies, 1.3 MP front camera that can record 720p videos. The phone runs Android 4.4.2 KitKat with LG’s Optimus UI. It has a removable 2440 mAh Li-Ion battery. The LG G2 mini price in Bangladesh is BDT 26900 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8G of phone storage. Available colors include Titan Black, Lunar White, Red, and Gold.
