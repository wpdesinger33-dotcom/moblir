This LG model, KP199, was out in the market in August of 2008 as an easy to use feature phone for everyone. It has a display size of 1.77 inches TFT type with display resolution of 128 x 160 pixels. The phone is really portable with the dimensions of 105 x 46 x 15 mm and a weight of 84 grams. With built-in memory of only 60MB the KP199 comes with an interface for a microSDHC card for external storage of up to 4GB. It includes a VGA camera. It has a 1350 mAh battery that can stand up to 720 hours and talk time up to 11 hours. The LG KP199 price in Bangladesh is BDT 5,025 (Official) for 60MB of phone storage.
