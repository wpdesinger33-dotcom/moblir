The LG GW620 was released in November of 2009 and is an innovative smartphone that brought Android to LG. It has an operational 3.0 inch TFT resistive touchscreen that supports a resolution of 320 x 480 pixels. The phone is portable, measuring 109 x 54.5 x 15.9mm in dimension and weighing 139 grams. A 528 MHz Qualcomm MSM7200A ARM11 processor handles the affairs of the GW620 and helps in achieving non-lag performance. It has a 1500 mAh battery life. Also, it has 150MB of onboard storage, which can be later augmented with a microSDHC card of up to 32GB in capacity. It has a 5MP camera with autofocus on the phone. The LG GW620 price in Bangladesh is BDT 23,890 (Official). It comes in Black, Gray, and Blue colors.
