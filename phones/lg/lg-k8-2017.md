The LG K8 (2017) is an Android smartphone that was announced in December 2016. It features a 5.0” IPS display and resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. This phone is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 425 chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.4 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and Adreno 308 GPU. Keep reading this article to know about the LG K8 (2017) price in Bangladesh and its details. It comes with 1.5 GB of RAM and 16 GB of internal storage (expandable via microSDHC). The main camera is a 13 MP shooter with an f/2.2 aperture, LED flash, and panorama mode. For selfies, a 5 MP front camera. The device runs on Android 7.0 Nougat and is equipped with a 2500 mAh removable battery. Other features include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 4.2, GPS, GLONASS, NFC, FM radio, and a microUSB 2.0 port. The LG K8 (2017) weighs 144 grams and comes in colors like Silver, Titan, Gold, and Dark Blue. The LG K8 (2017) price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,900 (Official) for its 1.5GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage.
