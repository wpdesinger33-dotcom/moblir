The LG GB230 Julia is a simple feature phone that was released in September 2009 for people who only need the phone for calling and or texting. The specification includes an internal display of 2.0 inch TFT, which has a pixel size of 176 x 220. The phone has a small and slim form factor that its dimensions are 101.2 x 49.2 x 15.7 mm and its weight is 89 grams. Equipped with an internal memory of 6MB, the GB230 Julia has the possibility to incorporate a microSD card with the capacity of up to 2GB. It includes a 2MP camera. The phone supports 800 mAh battery. The LG GB230 Julia price in Bangladesh is BDT 4,990 (Official). It comes in Black, and Dark Blue colors.
