The Google Pixel 7, released in October 2022, is a flagship Android smartphone that seamlessly combines cutting-edge technology with a minimalist design. As the latest addition to the Pixel lineup, it aims to deliver an exceptional user experience across various aspects. Keep reading this article to know more details on Google Pixel 7. The Google Pixel 7, released in October 2022, boasts a 6.3-inch AMOLED display with a 90Hz refresh rate and HDR10+ support. Powered by the Google Tensor G2 chipset, it offers 8GB of RAM and storage options of 128GB or 256GB. The dual-camera setup includes a 50MP main lens and a 12MP ultrawide lens, delivering impressive photo and video capabilities. The phone features an IP68 rating for dust and water resistance, and it’s 4355mAh battery supports 20W wired charging. With its sleek design and robust performance, the Google Pixel 7 is a compelling flagship device. The Google Pixel 7 smartphone is available in two variants: 128GB/8GB and 256GB/8GB.
