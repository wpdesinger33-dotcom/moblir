Google Pixel 3 XL which was a flagship since its release at the end of year 2018 is a high-end smartphone which focuses on performance and improved camera. Also the Google Pixel 3 XL has a 4GB + 64GB variant, which has the same display, processor and camera features. Only the ROM and the price differ. Both variants offer a 12.2MP rear camera and an 8MP front camera. It comes packed with a 6.3- inch OLED screen with a pixel density of 1440 x 2960. Supported by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 845 Developing Processor. The Google Pixel 3 XL price in Bangladesh is BDT 28,000 (Unofficial) for 4GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. The device comes in Clearly White, Just Black, and Not Pink colors.
