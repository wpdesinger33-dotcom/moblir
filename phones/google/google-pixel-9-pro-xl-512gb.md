The Google Pixel 9 Pro XL was released on 22 August 2024. This phone has 2 different variants, 16GB+256GB, and 16GB+512GB. The only differences between these 2 variants are ROM and price. Here we will know details about 16GB+512GB. It boasts a large 6.8-inch LTPO OLED display with a resolution of 1344 x 2992 pixels. The screen supports a smooth 120Hz refresh rate and HDR10+ content, reaching up to 3000 nits at peak brightness. The front and back panels are protected by Corning Gorilla Glass Victus 2, ensuring durability. Under the hood, the Pixel 9 Pro XL is powered by Google’s custom Tensor G4 chipset, built on a 4nm process. It comes with three rear cameras: a 50 MP wide-angle lens, a 48 MP periscope telephoto lens with 5x optical zoom, and a 48 MP ultrawide lens.
