Google Pixel Fold is Google’s first foldable smartphone that rivals the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 5, featuring a book-style folding design and a wide aspect ratio. The Pixel Fold is powered by the Google Tensor G2 chipset, which is optimized for AI and machine learning tasks. It also has a dual camera, a 4821 mAh battery, and supports 5G, NFC, and IP67 dust/water resistance.
