Google Pixel 7 Pro comes in 12GB+512GB variant. It also has 2 more variants, 12GB+128GB, and 12GB+256GB. All the features of the three variants are the same. The main difference is only the ROM. It features a huge 6.7-inch LTPO AMOLED  display with 120Hz refresh rate. It operates the Google Tensor G2 chipset. It has three back cameras: A 50-megapixel wide sensor camera, A 48-megapixel telephoto with 5X optical hybrid zoom, and a 12-megapixel ultra-wide camera. It runs Android 13 from the box and is upgradable to Android 15. The Google Pixel 7 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 56,500 (Unofficial) for 12GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage.
