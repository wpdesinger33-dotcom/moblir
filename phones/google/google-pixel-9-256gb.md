The Google Pixel 9 (12GB+256GB) variant is a powerhouse, featuring the advanced Google Tensor G4 chip and 12GB of RAM, ensuring smooth performance and efficient multitasking. Another variant of the Pixel 9 is the 12GB+128GB model. While it shares the same powerful Tensor G4 chip and 12GB of RAM, the key difference lies in the storage capacity. With 128GB of storage, this variant is more suited for users who do not require as much space for their files and applications. Despite the lower storage, it still offers the same high-quality display, camera capabilities, and overall performance as the 256GB variant. This model offers Dual rear cameras, 50 MP main sensor, and 48 MP ultra-wide angle camera. For selfie, 10.5 MP front camera. The Pixel 9 also boasts a stunning 6.3' display and resolution of 1080x2424 pixels.   It has a 4700mAh battery with 27W fast charging. The Google Pixel 9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 80,000 (Unofficial) for 12 GB of RAM and 256 GB of phone storage. It comes in Obsidian, Porcelain, Wintergreen, and Peony colors.
