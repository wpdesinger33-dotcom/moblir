The Google Pixel 5 is a flagship-range smartphone that emphasizes a streamlined user experience and robust camera capabilities, released in October 2020. It features a 6.0” OLED display and 90Hz refresh rate. The phone is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 765G chipset. The Pixel 5 also supports 5G connectivity, making it future-proof for faster network speeds. One of the standout features of the Pixel 5 is its camera system. It includes a dual-camera setup on the rear, with a 12.2 MP primary sensor and a 16 MP ultra-wide sensor. The front-facing camera is an 8 MP sensor, capable of capturing sharp and detailed selfies. The 4080 mAh battery supports 18W fast charging, 12W wireless charging, and reverse wireless charging, ensuring that the device can keep up with a busy lifestyle. Running on a clean version of Android, the Pixel 5. The Google Pixel 5 price in Bangladesh is BDT 70,000 (Unofficial) for 8GB of RAM and 128GB of ROM. It comes in Black, and Sorta Sage colors.
