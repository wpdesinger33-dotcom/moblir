The Google Pixel 4 XL has 6GB RAM and 128GB ROM. It has another variant 6GB+64GB. The 2 variants have all the same features, only the ROM and price changes. It was released in October 2019. With its 6.3-inch P-OLED display with 90Hz refresh rate. The Snapdragon 855 chipset. The dual-camera setup includes a 12.2 MP primary lens, and 16 MP telephoto lens. Selfies are handled by an 8 MP front camera. The 3700 mAh battery with 18W fast charging supported. The Google Pixel 4 XL price in Bangladesh is BDT 43,000 for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. It is an unofficial variant. This model comes in Clearly White, Just Black, and Oh So Orange colors.
