Google has officially confirmed that the Pixel 10 will be unveiled at the Made by Google event in New York City on August 20, 2025 at 1 pm. The event will feature the Pixel 10 smartphone along with three other new devices. According to the leaked report, the Pixel 10 will have a similar design to the previous model, but will feature significant improvements, such as a new triple-lens camera system with a telephoto lens and improved AI features. The phone will be equipped with Google's new Tensor G5 chip, which will be manufactured using TSMC's 3nm process, which will ensure improved performance and efficiency.
