The Google Pixel 8 Pro (512GB), unveiled in October 2023, represents a significant leap in smartphone technology, particularly in the realm of artificial intelligence. Here we will share Google Pixel 8 Pro price in Bangladesh. Also share its all features. It boasts a 6.7-inch Super Actua display, with 120Hz refresh rate. The device has a triple camera setup, 50MP main sensor, allowing for 10x optical-quality zoom. 48MP telephoto sensor, and 48MP ultrawide sensor. The selfie camera is 10.5 MP. The device is powered by the Google Tensor G3 chip. It runs Android 14, promises an extended software lifecycle, with seven years of updates. It has 12GB of RAM and 256GB of internal storage.This phone has 2 more variants 12GB+128GB and 12GB+256GB. All the features are the same, only the ROM changes with the price change. It is powered by a 5050 mAh battery with 30W wired charger and 23W wireless fast charging supported.
