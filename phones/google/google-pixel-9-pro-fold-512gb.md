The Google Pixel 9 Pro Fold (16GB+512GB) variant is a cutting-edge foldable smartphone that combines high performance with ample storage. It features a massive 8-inch internal display, offering an immersive viewing experience, and a 6.3-inch external cover display for quick access and usability. Powered by the latest Tensor G3 chip and equipped with 16GB of RAM, this variant ensures smooth multitasking and efficient performance. The 512GB storage capacity provides ample space for apps, media, and files, making it ideal for power users who need both speed and storage. Additionally, the device boasts advanced camera capabilities, leveraging Google’s AI for enhanced photography and videography. Another variant of the Pixel 9 Pro Fold is the 16GB+256GB model. While it shares the same powerful Tensor G3 processor and 16GB of RAM, the primary difference lies in its storage capacity. The key difference between the two variants is the storage capacity, which impacts the price and the amount of data you can store on the device. The 512GB variant is ideal for users who need extensive storage for apps, games, photos, and videos, while the 256GB variant offers a more budget-friendly option without compromising on performance or features. The Google Pixel 9 Pro Fold price in Bangladesh is BDT 290,000 (Unofficial) with 16GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage.
