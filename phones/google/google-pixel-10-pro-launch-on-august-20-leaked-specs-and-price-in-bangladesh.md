The Google Pixel 10 Pro will be launched at the Made by Google event in New York City on August 20, 2025 at 1 pm. It is reported that the event will include 3 other smartphones in the Pixel 10 series along with the Pixel 10 smartphone. According to the leaked report, the design of the Pixel 10 Pro will not be much different from the previous model, but will be significantly improved in several areas, such as the battery, charger, etc. It will feature the new Google Tensor G5 chip, which will be manufactured on TSMC's 3nm process, which will ensure better performance and efficiency.
