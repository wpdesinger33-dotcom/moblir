The Google Pixel 4a, released in August 2020, is a mid-range Android smartphone. It features a compact design with a 5.81-inch OLED display (1080 x 2340 pixels) and Corning Gorilla Glass 3 protection. The phone runs on Android 10, upgradable to Android 13. Keep reading this article to know about the Google Pixel 4a price in Bangladesh and its overall details. In terms of performance, you’ll find a Qualcomm Snapdragon 730G chipset with an octa-core CPU and Adreno 618 GPU. It comes with 6GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage (UFS 2.1), without a microSD card slot. The camera setup includes a single 12.2 MP rear camera (f/1.7, dual pixel PDAF, OIS) with features like LED flash, Pixel Shift, and Auto-HDR. It can record 4K videos at 30fps and 1080p videos at various frame rates. For selfies, there’s an 8 MP front camera (f/2.0). Other notable features include stereo speakers, a 3.5mm headphone jack, Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac, Bluetooth 5.0, NFC, and USB Type-C 3.1. This phone has a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor and sensors for accelerometer, gyro, proximity, compass, and barometer. Powering the Pixel 4a is a non-removable 3140 mAh Li-Po battery with 18W wired charging support. It’s available in two colors: Just Black and Barely Blue. The Google Pixel 4a price in Bangladesh is BDT 24,000 (Unofficial) for its 6GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage.
