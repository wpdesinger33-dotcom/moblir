The Google Pixel 7 Pro (12GB+256GB) is a top-tier mobile device. The other model of this Google Pixel 7 Pro is 12/128GB and the 12/256GB internal storage respectively. The differences are mainly with the memory attributes and the storage levels of the different variants. All the high-performance aspects including display, chipset, and thecamerasystem remain consistent with both variants. This one includes a 6.7-inch LTPO AMOLED with 1440 x 3120 pixels resolution and a 120 Hz refresh rate. The phone is operated by Google Tensor G2 chipset with Mali-G710 MP7 GPU.
