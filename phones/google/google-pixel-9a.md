The Google Pixel 9a is a flagship device that will further the lineage of the past models. This device features a 6.3-inch P-OLEDdisplaycapable of delivering stunning visuals even in direct sunlight thanks to a peak brightness of 2700 nits. It is powered by Google's Tensor G4 chipset containing an Immortalis-G715 MP7 GPU and runs the Android 15 operating system. The 5,100mAhbatteryallows for all-day usage with the additional convenience of 23W fast charging for on-the-go situations. High quality photography is supported by a 48MP rearcamerautilizing optical image stabilization for sharp and detailed photos. Selfies and video chats benefit from a 13MP front-facing camera. The Google Pixel 9a price in Bangladesh is BDT 57,000 (Unofficial) for 8GB of RAM and 128GB of ROM. It comes in Obsidian, Porcelain, Iris, and Peony colors.
