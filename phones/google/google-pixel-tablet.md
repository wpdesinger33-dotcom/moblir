The June 2023 launch of the Google Pixel Tablet provides users with a smooth and effective system that delivers productivity alongside entertainment benefits. The device presents a 10.95-inch IPS LCDdisplaythat displays content at 2560 x 1600 pixels resolution. The device operates on Google Tensor G2 chipset that features an octa-core processor coupled with Mali-G710 MP7 GPU. The Pixel Tablet comes with a 7020mAhbatterythat provides 12 hours of video streaming and supports charging. The device provides an 8MP rearcameratogether with an 8MP front camera for photography capabilities. Users can start with Android 13 on the Pixel Tablet but the operating system will update to Android 15.  Available in colors like Porcelain, Hazel, and Rose. The Google Pixel Tablet price in Bangladesh is BDT 62,000 (Unofficial) with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage.
