Allview A10 Plus is an entry-level smartphone designed for budget-conscious users. If you’re looking for an affordable device with basic features, the Allview A10 Plus might be worth considering. It was released in November 2018. It features a 5.34-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 480 x 960 pixels and an 18:9 aspect ratio. The phone runs on Android 8.1 Oreo (Go edition) and is powered by the MediaTek MT6580 chipset, and Mali-400MP2 GPU. The main camera is an 8 MP shooter with autofocus and LED flash, capable of recording 720p videos. For selfies, 5 MP front camera with an LED flash as well. It weighs 150 grams and packs a 2200 mAh Li-Ion battery. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 4.0, GPS, and FM radio.  The Allview A10 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,600 (official) for 1GB of RAm and 8GB of phone storage. It comes in Gray color.
