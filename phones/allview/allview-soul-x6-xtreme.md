The Allview Soul X6 Xtreme is a feature-rich smartphone designed to offer a premium user experience. Launched in April 2019, this device boasts a 6.39-inch AMOLED FullHD+ display with a resolution of 1080 x 2340 pixels. The screen is protected by Corning Gorilla Glass 5, ensuring durability and resistance to scratches. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the dual-camera setup on the back, featuring a 16MP main sensor with an f/1.8 aperture and a 5MP depth sensor. The front houses a motorized pop-up 8MP selfie camera. Under the hood, the Soul X6 Xtreme is powered by the Mediatek Helio P70 chipset, an octa-core processor clocked at 2.1 GHz. The 3450 mAh battery supports 18W fast charging. The Allview Soul X6 Xtreme price in Bangladesh is BDT 32,990 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 64 GB of phone storage. It comes in Urban Violet color.
