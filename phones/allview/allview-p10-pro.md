The Allview P10 Pro is an Android smartphone that was announced in April 2019. It features a 5.99-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720x1440 pixels. The phone runs on Android 8.1 (Oreo) and is powered by the MediaTek MT6739 chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.3 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU. It comes with 3 GB of RAM and 32 GB of internal storage (expandable via microSDXC). Keep reading this article to know about the Allview P10 Pro price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of photography, the P10 Pro sports a dual-camera setup on the back, consisting of a 13 MP main camera with autofocus and a QVGA sensor. For selfies, there’s an 8 MP front camera with an LED flash. The device is equipped with a 3000 mAh Li-Po battery. Other notable features include a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor, accelerometer, proximity sensor, and support for Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n and Bluetooth 4.2. However, it lacks NFC capability. Its dimensions are 161.5 x 76.5 x 8.8 mm, and it weighs 182 grams. The Allview P10 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 13,990 (Official) for its 3GB of RAM and 32GB of internal storage.
