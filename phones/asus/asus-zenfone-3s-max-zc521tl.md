The Asus Zenfone 3s Max ZC521TL, released in February 2017, is a mid-range smartphone known for its impressive battery life and solid performance. It features a 5.2-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels, providing decent clarity and color reproduction. Powered by a MediaTek MT6750 chipset. One of the standout features of the Zenfone 3s Max is its massive 5000mAh battery, which offers exceptional longevity, making it ideal for users who need a phone that can last through heavy usage without frequent recharging. The phone runs on Android 7.0 Nougat with Asus’s ZenUI 3.0, providing a user-friendly interface with various customization options. In terms of photography, the Zenfone 3s Max is equipped with a 13MP rear camera with an f/2.0 aperture, autofocus, and dual-LED dual-tone flash, capable of capturing decent photos in various lighting conditions. The front camera is an 8MP. The Asus Zenfone 3s Max ZC521TL price in Bangladesh is BDT 16,790 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 32GB of storage.
