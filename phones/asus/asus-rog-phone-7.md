The Asus ROG Phone 7 was announced in May 2023, and is a gaming smartphone. It has a 6,78-inch Samsung AMOLED display with 165 Hz refresh rate and support of HDR10+. The power of the device is charged by the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset working with an octa-core CPU and Adreno 740 GPU, in order to provide the highest performance. The phone also has a triple-camera setup at the back with the main wide lens of 50 MP and an ultrawide lens of 13 MP with a 5 MP macro lens, and a front camera of 32 MP. The battery capacity is 6000mAh and the charging is fast with 65 watt. The Asus ROG Phone 7 price in Bangladesh is BDT 85,999 (Unofficial) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of ROM.
