The Asus Zenfone 11 Ultra is among the top of the line smartphones that was unveiled in early March 2024 and officially unveiled to the market in April 2024. The device comes with a 6.78-inch LTPO AMOLED, and 144Hz refresh rate on this display. The aspect ratio of display resolution is 1080 x 2400 pixels. The screen and rear camera are shielded by Corning Gorilla Glass Victus 2. At its core, Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset and Adreno 750 graphic processing unit. The Zenfone 11 Ultra packs a triple lens system at the back that offers 50 MP main sensor, 32 MP telephoto with 3x optical zoom and 13 MP ultrawide. The front camera is a 32MP. The phone records 8K UHD video recording at 24fps, 4K UHD at 30/60 fps and 1080p Full HD at 30/60/120/240 fps. The battery is 5500mAh and charges with 65W through the cable, 15W wirelessly, and 10W if being charged as a power bank. The Asus Zenfone 11 Ultra price in Bangladesh is BDT 79,900 (Unofficial) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage. This phone comes in Eternal Black, Skyline Blue, Misty Gray, and Desert Sand colors.
