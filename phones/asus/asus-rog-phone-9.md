The Asus ROG Phone 9 is a powerhouse designed for gaming enthusiasts, featuring the latest Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite processor. It boasts a 6.78-inch Full HD+ AMOLED display with a refresh rate of up to 185Hz, ensuring smooth and immersive gaming experiences. The ROG Phone 9 is equipped with a 50MP primary camera, along with a 13MP ultra-wide camera and a 5MP macro camera. For selfies, it has a 32MP front camera.With a 5800mAh battery supporting 65W fast charging and 15W wireless charging. It also includes Air Triggers on the sides for enhanced gaming controls and an IP68 rating for dust and water resistance. The phone runs on Android 15 with Asus's ROG UI. The Asus ROG Phone 9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 140,000 (Unofficial) for 16GB of RAM and 512GB of ROM. The device comes in Phantom Black, and Storm White colors.
