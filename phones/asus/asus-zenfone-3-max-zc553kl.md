The Asus Zenfone 3 Max ZC553KL is a well-rounded smartphone that emphasizes battery life and design, released in November 2016. It features a 5.5-inch IPS LCD display and resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. The phone is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 430 chipset, coupled with an octa-core 1.4 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and Adreno 505 GPU, ensuring smooth performance for everyday tasks. One of the standout features of the Zenfone 3 Max is its impressive 4100 mAh battery, which offers extended usage times, making it ideal for users who need a reliable device throughout the day. The phone also supports 18W fast charging and reverse charging capabilities. In terms of photography, the Zenfone 3 Max sports a 16 MP primary camera with f/2.0 aperture, phase detection autofocus (PDAF), and laser autofocus, along with a dual-LED dual-tone flash. The front camera is an 8 MP shooter with an f/2.2 aperture, suitable for selfies and video calls. The device runs on Android 6.0.1 Marshmallow, upgradable to Android 8.1 Oreo, with Asus’s ZenUI 4 on top, offering a user-friendly interface. The Asus Zenfone 3 Max ZC553KL price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,490 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 32GB of ROM.
