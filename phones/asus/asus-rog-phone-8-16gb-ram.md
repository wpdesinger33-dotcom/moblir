The Asus ROG Phone 8 (16GB+256GB) is a smartphone for those who like to play games. It has another variant 12GB+256GB. The two variants are made to offer the best performance and gaming features possible. The only key differences between the 2 variants are RAM, ROM and price. It comes with a 6.78-inch LTPO AMOLED screen with 165Hz refresh rate. The phone is backed by the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset. The ROG Phone 8 also comes with a 5500mAh battery alongside supporting 65W fast charging. There is a three-camera system on the back with 50 MP main camera, 5 MP macro camera and 13 MP ultra-wide camera. The Asus ROG Phone 8 price in Bangladesh is BDT 107,999 (Unofficial) for 16GB of RAM and 256GB of ROM.
