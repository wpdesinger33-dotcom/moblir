The Asus Memo Pad ME 172 V was launched in January 2013 in Bangladesh and is actually a budget Android tablet which promises acceptable performance for basic operations. It employs a 7.0-inch IPS LCDdisplaywhich has a resolution of 600 x 1024 pixels. Available for the VIA WM8950 chipset with a 1.0 GHz ARM Cortex-A9 processor and Mali-400 GPU. It has a 1MP rear capture device and 720p video recording It is endowed with a 4270mAhbatteryand supports wireless fidelity and Bluetooth networks. The tablet comes in Sugar White, Titanium Gray, and Cherry Pink. The Asus Memo Pad ME 172 V price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,000 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
