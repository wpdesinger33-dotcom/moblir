The specifics for the new Asus ROG Phone 9 Pro are currently promising a whole new level of mobile gaming. Equipped with the Snapdragon 8 Elite lines. The device comprises a Samsung LTPO AMOLED display with the size of 6.78 inches and a resolution of 1080 X 2400 PPI, a maximum refresh rate of up to 185 Hz, and a maximum brightness of 2500 nits. In terms of photography, it includes a three-camera setup at the back – main camera 50 MP, telephoto camera 32MP, and ultrawide 13 MP. Smart selfies are taken by a front lens of 32MP. The phone has 5800mAh battery for power support, for recharge it also has fast charging of 65W through wires and wireless charging of 15W. This one could probably come in two flavors, namely the Phantom Black and the Storm White. The Asus ROG Phone 9 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 156,000 (Unofficial) for the 16GB of RAM and 512GB of ROM.
