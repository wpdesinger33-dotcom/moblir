The Asus Zenfone 2 Laser ZE601KL, released in December 2015, is a mid-range smartphone known for its impressive camera capabilities and robust performance. It features a 6.0-inch Full HD IPS display with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels, protected by Corning Gorilla Glass 4, ensuring durability and clarity. Powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 616 chipset, the device includes an octa-core processor with four cores clocked at 1.7 GHz and four at 1.2 GHz, paired with an Adreno 405 GPU. One of the standout features of the Zenfone 2 Laser ZE601KL is its camera system. The rear camera boasts a 13 MP sensor with an f/2.0 aperture and laser autofocus, which enhances focusing speed and accuracy, especially in low-light conditions. It also includes dual-LED dual-tone flash, panorama, and HDR capabilities, and can record 1080p videos at 30fps. The front camera is a 5 MP shooter with an f/2.0 aperture, suitable for selfies and video calls. It is equipped with a removable 3000 mAh Li-Po battery, providing decent battery life. The Zenfone 2 Laser ZE601KL runs on Android 5.0 Lollipop, upgradable to Android 6.0 Marshmallow, with Asus’s ZenUI on top, offering a user-friendly interface and additional features. The Asus Zenfone 2 Laser ZE601KL price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,990 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage. Available in Glacier Gray, Sheer Gold, and Glamour Red.
