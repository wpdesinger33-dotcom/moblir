Asus ROG Phone 8 Pro (24GB+1TB) variant is a newly released gaming smartphone which was released in January 2024. However, this model has another variant with 16GB of RAM and 512GB of internal storage, the design and hardware of the two phones are almost the same: screen type, chipset, camera layout and battery capacity. The main difference is the memory and storage that are designed to meet the needs and preferences of the user. This one has a 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED screen with the operating refresh rate of 165Hz. The device is run by the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset. The phone also has a triple rear camera with a 50 MP wide lens, 32 MP telephoto lens, 13 MP ultrawide lens and a 32 MP front camera. The battery capacity of the 5500mAh supports charging at a rate of 65W and gives up to 2 days of high power usage. The Asus ROG Phone 8 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 170,999 (Unofficial) for 16GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage.
