The Asus Zenfone Go ZB552KL, released in May 2017, is a budget-friendly smartphone designed to offer a balanced performance for everyday use. It features a 5.5” IPS LCD display and resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. Powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 410 chipset, the device includes a quad-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and Adreno 306 GPU, ensuring smooth operation for basic tasks and light gaming. Running on Android 6.0 Marshmallow with Asus’s ZenUI 3.0, the Zenfone Go ZB552KL provides a user-friendly interface and a range of customization options. The device is equipped with a 13MP rear camera featuring an f/2.0 aperture, autofocus, and dual-LED dual-tone flash, capable of capturing decent photos and 1080p videos. For selfies, it has a 5MP front camera with an f/2.0 aperture. Its 3000mAh battery, which offers up to 16 hours of talk time on 3G and up to 480 hours of standby time. The Asus Zenfone Go ZB552KL price in Bangladesh is BDT 11,490 (Official) with 2GB of RAM and 16GB of ROM. The device is available in various colors, including Charcoal Black, Pearl White, Glamor Red, Sheer Gold, Glacier Gray, and Silver Blue, catering to different aesthetic preferences.
