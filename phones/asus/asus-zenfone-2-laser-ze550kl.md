The Asus Zenfone 2 Laser ZE550KL, released in September 2015, is a budget-friendly Android smartphone that packs some noteworthy features. Its standout attribute is the laser autofocus system, which ensures rapid and precise focus, especially in dim lighting conditions. With a 5.5-inch IPS LCD display, it offers a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels and a pixel density of approximately 267 ppi. Display is protected by Corning Gorilla Glass 4. Under the hood, the Zenfone 2 Laser ZE550KL houses a Qualcomm Snapdragon 410 chipset, featuring a quad-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and an Adreno 306 GPU. In the camera department, the rear camera boasts a 13 MP sensor with laser autofocus technology, enabling lightning-fast focus in just 0.03 seconds. On the selfie camera is a 5 MP with an f/2.0 aperture. The device runs Android 5.0 Lollipop, which is upgradable to Android 6.0 Marshmallow with Asus’s custom ZenUI on top.Powering the Zenfone 2 Laser ZE550KL is a removable 3000 mAh Li-Po battery. The Asus Zenfone 2 Laser ZE550KL price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,390 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in various colors, including Black, White, Red, Silver, and Gold.
