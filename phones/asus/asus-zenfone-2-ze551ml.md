The Asus Zenfone 2 ZE551ML delivers good performance and a feature-rich experience. Released in March 2015, is an Android smartphone that packs impressive features. With a 5.5-inch display and a Corning Gorilla Glass 3 protection, it strikes a balance between size and usability. Under the hood, it boasts an Intel Atom Z3580 chipset, which powers a quad-core 2.3 GHz CPU (for the 4 GB RAM model) or a quad-core 1.8 GHz CPU (for the 2 GB RAM model). The 13 MP primary camera captures decent photos, and the 5 MP front camera handles selfies. The 3000 mAh non-removable battery supports 18W wired charging, promising a quick boost.  The Asus Zenfone 2 ZE551ML price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,490 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage. It comes in silver color.
