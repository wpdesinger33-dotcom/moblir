The Asus Transformer TF101 was launched in Bangladesh in April of the year 2011 and is an amazing tablet with the touch of a computer. It has a 10.1inch IPS LCDdisplay, with resolution of 1280 x 800 pixels. It is equipped with Nvidia Tegra 2 T20 chipset and is dual core, 1.0 GHz Cortex-A9 CPU and ULP GeForce GPU. It has the rearcameraof 5MP and has a 1.2MP front camera and has built-in stereo speakers for the sound output. The Transformer TF101 came with Android 3.0 (Honeycomb) out of the box but is currently available with an upgrade to Android 4.0 (Ice Cream Sandwich). The Asus Transformer TF101 price in Bangladesh is BDT 44,000 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
