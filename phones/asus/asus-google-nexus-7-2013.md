The Asus Google Nexus 7 (2013) is a small but high performing tablet released in July of 2013 in Bangladesh. Front face has got a 7.0-inch IPS LCD display, offering a pixel resolution of 1200 x 1920. Featuring the Qualcomm Snapdragon S4 Pro chipset backed with a 1.5 Ghz quad core Krait CPU and Adreno 320 GPU.  It has a 5MP rear endcameraand a 1.2MP selfie camera. Nexus 7 (2013) launched with Android 4.3 (Jelly Bean) out of box and officially upgradable to Android 6.0.1 (Marshmallow).  It’s equipped with this tab powered by a 3950 mAhbattery. The Asus Google Nexus 7 (2013) price in Bangladesh is BDT 21,500 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
