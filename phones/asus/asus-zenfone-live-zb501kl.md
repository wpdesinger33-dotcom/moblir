The Asus Zenfone Live ZB501KL is a budget-friendly smartphone that was released in March 2017. It features a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels, providing clear and vibrant visuals. This device is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 400 chipset. The phone is equipped with a 13MP rear camera and a 5MP front camera, both of which are capable of capturing decent photos in good lighting conditions. The device is powered by a 2650mAh battery, which provides adequate battery life for a full day of moderate usage. The Asus Zenfone Live ZB501KL price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,990 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of ROM.
