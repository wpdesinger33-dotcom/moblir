The Asus Zenfone 3 Max ZC520TL remains a reliable choice for users seeking a budget-friendly smartphone with exceptional battery life. It was released in August 2016. The device has a 5.2” IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. This smartphone is powered by a MediaTek MT6737M chipset. One of the standout features of the Zenfone 3 Max is its 4100 mAh battery, which offers excellent longevity, capable of lasting up to two days on a single charge with moderate use. The device runs on Android 6.0.1 Marshmallow, upgradable to 7.0 Nougat, with Asus’s ZenUI 3.0 overlay, which adds a range of customization options and features. In terms of photography, the Zenfone 3 Max sports a 13 MP rear camera with an f/2.2 aperture, capable of capturing decent photos in good lighting conditions. The 5 MP front camera for selfies.
