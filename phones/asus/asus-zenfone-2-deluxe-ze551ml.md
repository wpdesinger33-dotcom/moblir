The Asus Zenfone 2 Deluxe ZE551ML is a mid-range smartphone which combines performance and features for an affordable price in the Bangladesh market. It contains a screen size of 5.5 inches which is an IPS LCD supported by a pixel density of 1080 x 1920. The phone uses the Intel Atom Z3580 chipset and has a 2.3 Quad core GHz processor accompanied by a PowerVR G6430 GPU. The 13MP rear camera and dual-LED flash, while the 5MP front camera. The battery capacity of 3000mAh supports fast charging of 18W. Available for Android 5.0 (Lollipop) and comes with the ZenUI skin. The Asus Zenfone 2 Deluxe ZE551ML price in Bangladesh is BDT 18,590 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 128GB of ROM. It comes in Blue, Red, and White colors.
