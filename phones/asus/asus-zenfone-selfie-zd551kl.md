The Asus Zenfone Selfie ZD551KL, released in August 2015, is a smartphone designed with a strong emphasis on photography, particularly selfies. It features a 5.5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels, protected by Corning Gorilla Glass 4. It is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 615 chipset. The standout feature of the Zenfone Selfie is its dual 13-megapixel cameras, both front and rear, equipped with dual-LED dual-tone flash and laser autofocus. The front camera, with an f/2.2 aperture and an 88-degree wide-angle lens, is particularly notable for its selfie capabilities, including a Selfie Panorama mode that allows for wide-angle shots up to 140 degrees. Running on Android 5.0 Lollipop, upgradable to 6.0 Marshmallow with Asus’s ZenUI, the phone offers a smooth user experience. The 3000 mAh removable battery provides decent endurance, its octa-core processor and Adreno 405 GPU. The Asus Zenfone Selfie ZD551KL price in Bangladesh is BDT 16,290 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. Available in colors like Pure White, Chic Pink, and Aqua Blue.
