The Asus Zenfone GO ZB500KG is a budget-friendly smartphone that was released in 2016. It features a 5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels, providing decent clarity for everyday use. Powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 200 processor, it includes a dual-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU and an Adreno 302 GPU, which together offer basic performance suitable for light tasks and casual gaming. The Zenfone GO ZB500KG runs on Android 5.1 Lollipop, which, while outdated, still supports many essential apps and functions. Its camera setup includes an 8 MP rear camera with autofocus and LED flash, capable of capturing decent photos in good lighting conditions, and a 2 MP front camera for selfies and video calls. The phone is equipped with a 2600 mAh removable battery, providing adequate battery life for a full day of moderate use. The  Asus Zenfone GO ZB500KG price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,990 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. Available in various colors, including Glacier Gray, Sheer Gold, and Glamour Red.
