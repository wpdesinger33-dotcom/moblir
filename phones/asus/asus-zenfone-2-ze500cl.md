The Asus Zenfone 2 ZE500CL offers a good balance of features and performance for its price range. It was released in March 2015, is a mid-range smartphone that offers a balanced mix of performance and affordability. It features a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels, providing clear and vibrant visuals. The device is powered by an Intel Atom Z2560 dual-core processor clocked at 1.6 GHz. The Zenfone 2 ZE500CL is equipped with an 8-megapixel rear camera with an f/2.0 aperture, autofocus, and LED flash, capable of capturing detailed photos and 1080p videos. For selfies and video calls, there is a 2-megapixel front camera. The device runs on Android 5.0 Lollipop with Asus’s ZenUI, providing a user-friendly interface and additional features. It also includes a non-removable 2500 mAh battery, which offers a decent battery life, supporting up to 28 hours of talk time on 3G networks. It also includes Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.0, GPS, and an FM radio. The phone’s build is solid, with dimensions of 148.1 x 71.5 x 10.9 mm and a weight of 155 grams, making it comfortable to hold and use. The  Asus Zenfone 2 ZE500CL price in Bangladesh is BDT 18,500 (official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. Available in Charcoal Black, Pearl White, and Cherry Red.
