The Asus Fonepad was launched in Bangladesh in April of the year 2013, it is a multifaceted tablet at a quite reasonable price level. Thedisplayis an IPS LCD of 7.0-inch size with 800 x 1280 pixels resolution. Equipped with Intel Atom Z2420 System chipset used with 1.2 GHz CPU and PowerVR SGX540 GPU. It comes with a 3.15 MP rearcameraand 1.2 MP front camera. It is with 4270mAh which provides long battery time, and the device supports Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GPS operation. The tab comes in Titanium Gray and Champagne Gold. The Asus Fonepad price in Bangladesh is BDT 21,000 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
