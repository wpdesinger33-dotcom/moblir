The Asus Zenfone Go ZB500KL, released in October 2016, is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers a balanced mix of features for everyday use. It sports a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720x1280 pixels, providing decent clarity and color reproduction for its class. Powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 410 chipset, the device includes a quad-core 1.0 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and Adreno 306 GPU. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the 13 MP rear camera with an f/2.0 aperture, autofocus, and LED flash, capable of capturing 1080p videos at 30fps. The 5 MP front camera, also with an f/2.0 aperture, is suitable for selfies and video calls. Running on Android 6.0 Marshmallow, the Zenfone Go ZB500KL offers a user-friendly interface and access to a wide range of apps and features. The device is equipped with a removable 2600 mAh battery, which provides adequate standby and talk time for moderate usage. . The phone’s build includes a glass front, plastic back, and plastic frame, weighing in at 150 grams and measuring 143.7 x 70.9 x 11.3 mm.  The Asus Zenfone Go ZB500KL price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,990 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
