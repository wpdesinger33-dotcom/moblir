The Asus Zenfone 12 Ultra exists as a smartphone that delivers high-performance abilities to users who seek premium functions. This smartphone provides a 6.78-inch LTPO AMOLEDdisplaywhich reaches 1080 x 2400 pixels resolution. This phone enables 144Hz refresh rate operation on its display. The smart device receives its processing capability from Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite. The device operates with Android version 15. The Zenfone 12 Ultra features three cameras at the back that consist of a 50MP wide lens together with a 32MP telephoto lens and a 13MP ultrawide lens. The phone features a 32MP wide-angle front sensor.  The device features a 5500mAhbatterythat can be recharged through 65W wired charging while also offering support for 15W wireless charging. The Asus Zenfone 12 Ultra price in Bangladesh is BDT 140,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage. This phone is available in Black, Green, and Pink colors.
