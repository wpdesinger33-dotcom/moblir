The Asus Google Nexus 7 was released in July 2013 in Bangladesh remains to be one of the small but mighty tablets in the market, currently available at friendly prices. It comes with a screen size of 7.0 inches with an IPS LCD screen, 1280 x 800 pixels resolution. Being equipped with Nvidia Tegra 3 chipset, the quad-core 1.2 Ghz Krait CPU and ULP GeForce GPU. It offers a 5MP rear camera and 1.2MP front facingcamera. The Nexus 7 came with Android, version 4.3 (Jelly Bean) and can be upgraded to Android version 6.0 (Marshmallow). This tablet is powered by a 4325 mAhbattery. The Asus Google Nexus 7 price in Bangladesh is BDT 30,000 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
