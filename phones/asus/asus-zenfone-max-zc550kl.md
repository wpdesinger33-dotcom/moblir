The Asus Zenfone Max ZC550KL is a reliable choice for those seeking extended battery life. It was released in January 2016, boasts a massive 5000mAh battery, making it a standout feature. Its sturdy build includes Gorilla Glass 4 on the front and a plastic back. The 5.5-inch IPS LCD display offers a resolution of 720x1280 pixels. It has a Qualcomm Snapdragon 410 processor. It runs Android 5.0.1 with the ZenUI skin. The 13MP rear camera with laser autofocus captures decent photos, while the 5MP front camera handles selfies. The Asus Zenfone Max ZC550KL price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,300 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in Black, and White colors.
