The Asus ROG Phone 6D which Asus released in October 2022, and it is primarily a gaming phone. It comes out with 6.78 inches of the AMOLEDdisplay, which undergoes a 165Hz refresh rate and supports HDR10+. It runs on MediaTek Dimensity 9000+ processor,  with an operation frequency up to 3.35 GHz and Mali-G710 MC10 GPU that guarantees superior performance for gaming. The ROG Phone 6D runs on Android 12. The phone features a 3-camera rear system: 50-megapixels lens with OIS, an Ultrawide at 13-megapixels, and a 5-megapixels Macro. For selfies, there is a 12 MP front lens installed. It is also the use of a 6000mAhbattery, and includes 65W fast charging. The Asus ROG Phone 6D price in Bangladesh is BDT 79,999 (Unofficial) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
