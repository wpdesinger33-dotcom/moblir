The Itel A60S price in Bangladesh reflects its position as a budget-friendly smartphone that doesn’t skimp on features. Launched in 2023, the Itel A60S comes with a 6.6-inch IPS LCD display, offering a resolution of 720 x 1612 pixels. It runs on Android 12 and is powered by a Unisoc SC9863A1 chipset, paired with 4GB of RAM. Users have the option to choose between 64GB or 128GB storage variants, ensuring ample space for apps and media. It also supports microSDXC for additional storage expansion. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the Itel A60S’s 8MP main camera, complemented by an auxiliary lens for depth sensing, and a 5MP selfie camera. Both cameras are capable of capturing clear images and videos. The smartphone’s design is trendy, with a lenticular texture and right-angle edges that offer a comfortable grip. Battery life is a strong suit for the Itel A60S, thanks to its 5000mAh battery which supports 10W wired charging. This large battery capacity ensures that users can go through their day without worrying about frequent recharging. With its competitive price point in the Bangladeshi market, the Itel A60S is a compelling choice for those seeking a reliable and stylish smartphone experience.
