The Itel A50C is an entry-level smartphone designed to offer essential features at an affordable price. This device is equipped with a 6.6-inch display. The screen boasts a 90% screen-to-body ratio. It is powered by an octa-core processor. Runs on Android 14 (Go edition), which is optimized for devices with lower specifications, providing a user-friendly and efficient experience. In terms of photography, the Itel A50C features an 8MP rear camera with an auxiliary lens and a 5MP front camera. The device is equipped with a 4000mAh battery. The Itel A50C price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,990 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage. It comes in Champagne Gold, Dawn Blue, and Magic Black colors.
