The itel A70 sports a single 13 MP rear camera along with an auxiliary 0.08 MP lens. For selfies, 8 MP front camera. The 5000 mAh non-removable battery, with 10W wired charging. The itel A70 price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,990. It has 4GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. Also this model has a different variant 4GB+64GB.
