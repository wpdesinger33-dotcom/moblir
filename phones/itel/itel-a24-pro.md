The Itel A24 Pro is a budget-friendly smartphone that packs some impressive features. It comes with a quad-core Unisoc SC9832E SoC and a 5-inch IPS LCD display. Its compact form factor makes it easy to handle, and the screen provides decent clarity for everyday tasks. The phone runs on Android 12 Go edition, ensuring smooth performance even with limited resources. Additionally, it features face unlock for added convenience. With 32 GB internal storage, you’ll have ample space for apps, photos, and files. Plus, there’s a dedicated microSD card slot for further expansion. The rear camera is a 2-megapixel shooter, while the front-facing camera is a VGA sensor. While not groundbreaking, these cameras are sufficient for casual photography and video calls. Its 3020 mAh battery ensures all-day usage without frequent recharging. The Itel A24 Pro Price in Bangladesh is BDT 5,990 for its 2/32GB variant. It comes with two different colors which are Blue, Green. You can purchase this smartphone from Itel's official showroom, authorized shop and online marketplace.
