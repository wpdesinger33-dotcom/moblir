The Itel S25 is an Android mid- end smartphone in the Bangladesh market. It runs with a 6.78-inch size AMOLED panel at 1080 x 2436 pixels resolution and 120 Hzrefresh rate. It is running on a Unisoc T620 chipset. The Itel S25 also comes with a 50 MP AI rear lens and 32 MP AI front lens. The phone also comprises a 5000mAhbatterywith 18W fast charging capability. Itel S25 runs on Android 14 with itel’s own OS 14.5. Network support comprises WiFi, Bluetooth, GPS, NFC and a USB type C port. It also provides under-display fingerprint scanning for security reasons and is also IP54 dust and splash resistant. The Itel S25 price in Bangladesh is BDT 13,990 (Official) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of ROM. This phone comes in Bromo Black, Meteor Titanium, and Komodo Ocean colors.
