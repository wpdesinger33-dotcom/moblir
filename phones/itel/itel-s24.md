The Itel S24 is a budget-friendly smartphone that comes with lots of great features. It was launched in the Bangladesh market on 23 April 2024. Are you interested to know about this smartphone? Keep reading this article to know about the Itel S24 price in Bangladesh and its overall details. It comes with a sleek design with dimensions of 163.5 x 75.5 x 8.3 mm and a weight of 192 g. Its 6.6-inch IPS LCD display offers a resolution of 720 x 1612 pixels, resulting in a screen-to-body ratio of approximately 84.8%. The 90Hz refresh rate ensures smoother visuals, and the peak brightness reaches 480 nits. The S24 runs on Android 13 and features the MediaTek Helio G91 Ultra chipset. This octa-core chipset combines two Cortex-A75 cores with six Cortex-A55 cores at 1.8 GHz. The primary camera setup includes a 108 MP wide lens (f/1.6) with autofocus and an auxiliary 0.08 MP lens. Capture moments in 1440p at 30fps or 1080p at 30fps.
