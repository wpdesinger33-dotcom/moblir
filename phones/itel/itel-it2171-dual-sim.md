The Itel it2171 Dual SIM entered the Bangladesh market as a cost-effective feature phone during April 2019. The phone comes with a 1.77-inch TFTdisplaywhich shows 128 x 160 pixels resolution. The Itel it2171 Dual SIM contains a 0.3MP rearcamerathat allows photo capture at 640 x 480 pixels. This phone has a 1000mAh Li-Ionbatterywhich provides users with 10 hours of talk time and 220 hours of standby time. This phone offers three connectivity options which include Bluetooth 2.0 and microUSB 2.0 and FM radio. This phone provides 2G, 3G and 4G network capabilities which enable users to utilize dual SIM simultaneously. The Itel it2171 Dual SIM price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,049 (Official).
