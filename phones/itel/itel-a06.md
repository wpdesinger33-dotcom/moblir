The Itel A06 is an entry-level smartphone designed to offer essential features at an affordable price. It boasts a 6.56-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1600 pixels. In terms of photography, the Itel A06 features an 8MP rear camera with LED flash and a 5MP front camera, both capable of recording 720p videos at 30fps. The device runs on Android 14 (Go edition). The phone is equipped with a 4000mAh non-removable Li-Po battery. Connectivity options include dual SIM support, 4G LTE, Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.2, and GPS with A-GPS. The Itel A06 price in Bangladesh is BDT 7,000 (Expected) for 2GB of RAM and 32G of phone storage. Available in Champagne Gold, Dawn Blue, and Magic Black.
