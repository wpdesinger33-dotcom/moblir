The Itel it5617 Dual SIM, launched in April 2019 in Bangladesh, is a feature phone designed for simplicity and functionality. It features a 2.4-inch TFTdisplaywith a resolution of 320 x 240 pixels. The phone supports dual SIM functionality. Equipped with a 0.3 MP primarycamera. It also includes a 2500 mAhbattery, offering extended talk time and standby hours. The phone supports expandable storage up to 32 GB via a microSD card, and it comes with a 3.5mm headphone jack, FM radio, and torchlight for added convenience. Available in colors like Black, Deep Blue, and Light Blue. The Itel it5617 Dual SIM price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,899 (Official).
