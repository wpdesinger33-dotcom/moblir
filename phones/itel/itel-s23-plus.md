The main highlight of Itel S23 Plus is its stunning and large 6.78” Full HD+ AMOLED curved screen. It is also protected by a Corning Gorilla Glass 5 to ensure maximum security of this precious screen. The backside has two color variants, of which the Cyan Lake color gives the shiny gradient look. There is also a more plain and dark Elemental Blue color. For those who like design and display, it is hard to leave this lower-budget masterpiece. But that wasn’t the end of it. It comes with a very nice front and back camera, which can capture excellent shots in daylight conditions. There is also a so-called super steady video recording with the back camera for stable footage.
