The Itel A56 is an entry-level Android smartphone that offers budget-friendly features. It was released in June 2020. Running on Android 9 Pie, it is powered by a quad-core UniSoC Spreadtrum SC7731E chipset. The device features a 6-inch IPS LCD display and dual cameras at the back, including an 8-megapixel primary sensor and a 0.3-megapixel secondary sensor. For a selfie, 5MP. It also supports a 4000mAh battery. The Itel A56 price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,000 (official) for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in Aqua Blue, Champagne Gold, and Deep Grey colors.
