Itel City 200 is a low-priced Android smartphone that was launched on a large scale on 08 February 2026 in Bangladesh. Immediately you are attracted by its large 6.78-inch IPS LCD screen that has HD + resolution and a nice 120Hz refresh rate. It has a Unisoc T7250 octa-core processor under the hood that is coupled with Android 15. The photography is surprisingly decent at this price: the rear camera is 50 MP, and the front one is 8 MP, which allows taking clear photos on a sunny day. The phone also has a long battery of 5200mAh and supports 18Wfast charging, meaning that it lasts a long time. Itel did not rest on specifications, the City 200 has a slim unibody of 7.45 mm, IP65 water and dust resistant, and military grade. It has a side-mounted fingerprint sensor, dual-SIM 4G capability, and other handy sensors, such as a compass and gyro, as well. The Itel City 200 price in Bangladesh is BDT 13,990 (Official) with 6GB RAM and 128GB storage.
