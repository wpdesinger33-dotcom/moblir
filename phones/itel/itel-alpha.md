The Itel Alpha is a budget-friendly smartphone that was released in the Bangladesh market in Mar 2019. It is the latest addition to iTel’s lineup. It shares features with other recent iTel devices, such as the S13. Notably, the Alpha focuses on photography, boasting dual rear camera sensors. Unlike most iTel phones with limited internal storage (usually 8GB), the Alpha offers a more generous 16GB for your photos and multimedia. Keep reading this article to know aout the Itel Alpha price in Bangladesh and its overall details. The device sports a 5.5-inch FullView display, providing a decent viewing experience. Under the hood, you’ll find a Quad-core Mediatek MT6580 CPU clocked at 1.3GHz, paired with a Mali-400MP2 GPU. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the dual 8 + 0.8-megapixel rear cameras and a respectable 5-megapixel front camera. Additional features include Dual SIM support, a rear fingerprint scanner, FaceID, and expandable storage up to 32GB via microSD card. The Alpha runs Android 8.1 (Oreo, Go Edition). It has a  non-removable 2400 mAh Li-Ion battery. The Itel Alpha price in Bangladesh is BDT 5,550 for its 1GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
