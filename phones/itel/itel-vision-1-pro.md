Itel Vision 1 Procomes with 6.5 inches HD+ IPS screen. It has a Full-View waterdrop notch design. The back camera is of triple with 8 MP main camera, autofocus, LED flash etc. features and Full HD video recording. The front camera is of 8 MP. Itel Vision 1 Pro comes with 4000 mAh battery. It has 2 or 3 GB RAM, 1.4 GHz quad-core CPU and Mali T820 MP1 GPU. It is powered by a 28 nm UniSoC SC9832E chipset. The device comes with 32 GB internal storage and up to 128 GB dedicated MicroSD slot. There is a back-mounted fingerprint sensor in this phone. Among other features, there is FM Radio, Dual 4G SIM, Face Unlock etc.
