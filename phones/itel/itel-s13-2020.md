The Itel S13 (2020) is an entry level smartphone that comes with decent features in this price segment. It was released in September 2020 in Bangladesh. The Itel S13 boasts a 5.5-inch IPS TFT display with a resolution of 480 x 960 pixels. The phone’s dimensions are 152.2 x 77.6 x 9.4 mm, and it weighs 167 grams. Under the hood, you’ll find a MediaTek MT6580 quad-core processor. The rear camera setup consists of a 5 MP primary camera . On the front,a 13 MP selfie camera. The 2400 mAh Li-Ion battery provides. Running on Android 8.1 Oreo (Go Edition), the S13 offers a clean and lightweight user experience. The Itel S13 (2020) price in Bangladesh is BDT 7,490 (official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. It comes in Champagne Gold, Elegant Black, and City Blue colors.
