The Itel 2176 Dual SIM is a budget-friendly feature phone released in September 2023. It features a 2-inch QVGAdisplaywith a resolution of 240 x 320 pixels. It is equipped with a 0.3MP rearcamera, capable of capturing images at 640 x 480 pixels. The phone includes a 1000 mAh Li-Ionbattery, offering a talk time of up to 10 hours and a standby time of 220 hours. Connectivity options include Bluetooth 2.0, microUSB 2.0, and FM radio. The Itel 2176 Dual SIM supports 2G, 3G, and 4G networks, allowing for dual SIM functionality. The Itel 2176 Dual SIM price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,249 (Official).
