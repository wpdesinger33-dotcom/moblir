The Itel it5621 Dual SIM operates as a cost-effective feature phone available from October 2021. Users can enjoy clear images through its 2.8-inch TFTdisplaythat shows 240 x 320 pixels resolution. It is equipped with a 0.3MP rearcamera, capable of capturing images at 640 x 480 pixels It runs on a 2500 mAh Li-Ionbatterythat delivers 10 hours of talk time and 220 hours of standby duration. Users can connect through Bluetooth version 2.0 and microUSB version 2.0 as well as receive FM radio signals. The phone features 4MB of RAM together with 4MB of internal storage which users can extend to 32GB through a microSD card. The Itel it5621 Dual SIM price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,799 (Official).
