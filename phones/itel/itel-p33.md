Itel P33 is a budget-friendly smartphone released in March 2019. It features a 5.5-inch HD+ display with a resolution of 720 x 1440 pixels. The standout feature of this device is its built-in 4000mAh battery. The phone runs on Android 8.1 Oreo (Go Edition) and is powered by a MediaTek MT6580 chipset. The rear camera setup consists of an 8MP + 0.8MP dual sensor, while the front houses a 5MP selfie camera. The Itel P33 price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,150 for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It is available in three colors: Aqua Blue, Champagne Gold.
