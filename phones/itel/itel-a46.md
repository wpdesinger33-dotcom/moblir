The itel A46 offers a decent smartphone experience at an affordable price point. In Bangladesh, it was launched in May 2019. It features a 5.5-inch IPS display with a resolution of 720 x 1440 pixels. Powered by a Unisoc SC9863A octa-core processor (4x 1.6 GHz ARM Cortex-A55 and 4x 1.2 GHz ARM Cortex-A55). The device runs on Android 9.0 Pie and features a dual 5 MP rear camera setup. The 2350 mAh Li-Polymer battery. The itel A46 price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,990 (official) for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in Aqua Blue, Champagne Gold, Deep Grey, and Red colors.
