The Itel 5361 Dual SIM represents a cost-effective feature smartphone option which came out for the Bangladesh market during December 2023. The phone uses a 2.8-inch LCDdisplaywhich operates at 240 x 320 pixels resolution. The phone contains a 0.3MP rearcamerawhich produces images at 640 x 480 pixels resolution. The Itel 5361 Dual SIM has a 1900 mAh Li-Ionbatterywhich enables users to make calls for 10 hours and maintain standby mode for 220 hours. This phone supports Bluetooth 2.0 together with microUSB 2.0 and FM radio as its connectivity options. The Itel 5361 Dual SIM operates with 2G, 3G, and 4G networks to enable users to connect with dual SIM capability. The Itel 5361 Dual SIM price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,899 (Official).
