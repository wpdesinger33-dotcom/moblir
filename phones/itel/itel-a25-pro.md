The Itel A25 Pro is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers practical features for everyday use. It was released in June 2020 in Bangladesh. With its 5-inch IPS HD display with 480 x 854 pixels. The device runs on Android Go. Under the hood, you’ll find a quad-core UniSoC Spreadtrum SC9832E processor. The 3-in-1 slot supports dual 4G VoLTE for faster connectivity. It offers a single rear camera for taking pictures, which includes a 5MP main sensor. Also 5MP for selfies. It supports a 3020 mAh battery. The Itel A25 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 5,290 (official) for 2GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage. It comes in Gradient Black, and Gradient Blue colors.
