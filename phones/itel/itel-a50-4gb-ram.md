The Itel A50 (4GB+64GB) was launched in May 2024, and as an entry-level smartphone, offers a balance between cost and performance. On the other hand, the Itel A50 version that was launched at the same time with the 4GB+64GB model included 2GB+64GB. However, the primary difference may be found in the internal storage and RAM, so it seems that 4GB+64GB would be the better choice for the consumers in need of more storage space. It has a dimension of 163.9 x 75.7 x 8.7 mm. Also it has a 6.6” IPS LCD display of 720 x 1612 pixels resolution. The device is powered by Unisok T603 Soc chip. The phone also has a 5000mAh battery with 10W fast charging. Lime Green and Misty Black are the colors of this phone. The Itel A50 price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,990 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage.
