Released in December 2023 the Itel 5031 Dual SIM features practical design with stylish characteristics as a feature phone. The 2.4-inch LCDdisplayof the Itel 5031 Dual SIM offers 240 x 320 pixels resolution. The Itel 5031 Dual SIM has a 0.3MP rearcamerawhich produces 640 x 480 pixel resolution images. This phone operates on a 1000 mAh Li-Ionbatterywhich provides 10 hours of talk time and 220 hours of standby time. The Itel 5031 Dual SIM runs on three mobile network types: 2G, 3G and 4G to enable its dual SIM capabilities. The phone offers three connectivity features: Bluetooth version 2.0 along with microUSB version 2.0 and FM radio. The device features 4MB of RAM together with 4MB of built-in storage that expands to 32GB through a microSD card addition. The Itel 5031 Dual SIM price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,489 (Official).
