This phone is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 6080 chipset. The Itel Color Pro works on Android 13 with Itel OS 13. The main lens is a 50MP wide lens, and there is an 8MP selfie and video call camera on the front. It has a 5000mAhbatterywith 18W fast charging.
