The Itel S31 is an affordable smartphone with a focus on essential features. It boasts a 5.5-inch FullView display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. Powered by a 1.3GHz quad-core CPU, it runs on Android 6.0 (Marshmallow). The 5MP rear camera with LED flash, while the 5MP front-facing camera. Connectivity options include dual SIM, 3G/2G, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth 4.0. Powering the device is 2400 mAh battery. The Itel S31 price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,200 for 1GB of RAM and 32GB of ROM. It comes in colors like Champagne Gold, Ice Blue, and City Blue colors.
