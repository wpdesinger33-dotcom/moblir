The Itel A70 emerges as a budget-friendly smartphone that doesn’t skimp on functionality, offering a variant with 4GB of RAM and 64GB of internal storage. This device is designed to cater to the needs of users looking for a phone that can handle daily tasks without breaking the bank. Itel has equipped the A70 with a Unisoc T603 chipset and an Octa-core processor, ensuring smooth performance for the average user. The phone runs on Android 13 (Go Edition), which is optimized for budget devices with less memory, yet it still provides a seamless Android experience. One of the notable features of the Itel A70 is its large 6.6-inch IPS LCD display, which boasts a resolution of 720 x 1612 pixels. This screen is ideal for media consumption and provides ample space for users to navigate apps and the web. The phone also comes with a dual-camera setup, including a 13 MP primary sensor, and an 8 MP front camera for selfies. The battery life is impressive, with a 5000 mAh capacity that should last most users a full day on a single charge. Also, the Itel A70 includes a side-mounted fingerprint sensor, adding an extra layer of security. The Itel A70 official price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,990 for 4GB RAM and 64GB ROM. It has different variants 4GB+128GB. This phone comes in 4 different colors: Brilliant Gold, Starlish Black, Field Green, and Azure Blue.
