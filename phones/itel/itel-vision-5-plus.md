The Itel Vision 5 Plus runs on Android OS version 12 and is powered by an octa-core processor, ensuring smooth performance across applications. Storage-wise, the phone boasts an internal memory of 128 GB paired with 4 GB of RAM, which is quite generous for its price range. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the triple rear camera setup, featuring a 16 MP main sensor complemented by two additional lenses, and a 5 MP front camera for selfies.
