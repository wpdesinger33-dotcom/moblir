The Itel A49 emerged as a budget-friendly smartphone from the Itel smartphone brand. It was launched in the Bangladesh market in March, 2022. Are you curious to know about the Itel A49? If yes, then this article is for you. Check out this article to know the whole details of this smartphone. With a 6.6-inch IPS LCD display, it offers a resolution of 720x1600 pixels, ensuring clear visuals for its users. The device is powered by a Unisoc SC9832E chipset and runs on the efficient Android 11 Go edition, tailored for smoother performance on entry-level hardware. Photography needs are met with a 5-megapixel rear camera, accompanied by an LED flash for better low-light shots, and a 5-megapixel front camera for selfies. The Itel A49 is designed to last through the day with a 4000mAh battery, and it supports essential connectivity options including Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and GPS.
