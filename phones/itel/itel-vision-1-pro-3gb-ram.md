The Itel Vision 1 Pro is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers a variety of features suitable for users looking for a reliable device without breaking the bank. This model comes with a 3GB RAM variant paired with 32GB of internal storage, which can be expanded up to 128GB via a microSD card, catering to the needs of multitasking and ample storage space for apps and media. Notable for its 6.52-inch HD+ IPS display, the Itel Vision 1 Pro provides a clear and vibrant viewing experience for its users. The device runs on Android 10 (Go Edition) and is powered by a 1.4GHz Quad-core processor, ensuring smooth operation. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the triple 8MP rear camera setup and an 8MP front camera, capable of capturing decent photographs. The phone also includes modern features such as face unlock and a fingerprint sensor, enhancing security and convenience. The Itel Vision 1 Pro official price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,690 for 3GB RAM and 32GB ROM. This phone has another official variant 2GB+32GB. Also, it comes in appealing colors like Aurora Blue and Ocean Blue, offering a touch of personal style to the device.
