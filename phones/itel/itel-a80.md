The Itel A80 is an entry-level officially smartphone in the Bangladesh market. It runs on Android 14 with Itel’s custom OS 14 overlay. The phone is powered by a Unisoc T603 chipset. The device features a 6.7-inch IPS LCDdisplaywith a resolution of 720 x 1600 pixels and a refresh rate of up to 120Hz, providing a smooth visual experience. The display is bright, with a peak brightness of 500 nits, making it usable even in outdoor conditions. In terms of photography, the Itel A80 is equipped with a single 50MP rearcamerathat supports HDR and is accompanied by a dual-LED flash. The front camera is an 8MP shooter, suitable for selfies and video calls. The phone supports 1080p video recording at 30fps for both the front and rear cameras. The Itel A80 also includes a side-mounted fingerprint sensor for added security and convenience. It is powered by a 5000mAh non-removablebattery, which supports 10W wired charging. The Itel A80 price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,990 (Official) with 4GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. The Itel A80 is available in three colors: Sandstone Black, Glacier White, and Wave Blue.
