The Itel A50 (3GB+64GB) is one of the new budget smartphones that come with both affordable prices and basic performance features. Its release is set for April 2024 in Bangladesh. It also has 2 more variants, 2GB+64GB, and 4GB+64GB. All the features of the three variants are the same. The main difference is only the ROM. The phone packs a 6.6 inches IPS LCD with 720 x 1612 pixels resolution of the screen. To the heart of the phone lies the Unisoc T603 octa-core processor. The battery capacity of this Itel A50 is 5000mAh. It has 10W wired charging. Colors existing from the design are Lime Green and Misty Black. The Itel A50 price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,090 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage.
