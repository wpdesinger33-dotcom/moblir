The Itel A05s, a budget-friendly smartphone, offers a blend of practical features at an affordable price. The base variant of the Itel A05s boasts 2 GB of RAM and 32 GB of internal storage. This configuration ensures smooth multitasking and ample space for your apps, photos, and files. The phone’s Unisoc SC9863A1 (28nm) chipset powers its performance, making it a reliable companion for everyday tasks. Additionally, the massive 4000mAh battery provides extended usage without frequent charging. It’s a 4G-enabled device, ensuring good network connectivity for calls, browsing, and streaming. The dual-camera setup includes an 8 MP primary camera, allowing you to capture moments with decent clarity. The Itel A05s Price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,490 (Official) for its 2/32GB variant. It comes in four different colors which are Nebula Black, Meadow Green, Crystal Blue, and Glorious Orange.
