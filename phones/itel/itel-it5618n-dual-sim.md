The Itel it5618N Dual SIM brings budget-friendly features to the market through its May 2020 Bangladesh release. This phone model comes equipped with a 2.4-inch QVGAdisplaythat has 240 x 320 pixel resolution. The Itel it5618N Dual SIM contains a 0.3MP rearcamerathat records images at 640 x 480 pixels resolution. The 2500 mAh Li-Ionbatteryinstalled in the phone enables 10 hours of talk time and 220 hours of standby operation. This device supports Bluetooth 2.0 and microUSB 2.0 in addition to FM radio as its connectivity features. This phone supports dual SIM functionality through 2G, 3G and 4G networks. The Itel it5618N Dual SIM price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,799 (Official).
