Currently using Android 14 with HyperOS 1.0 interface. It also has a 5000mAhbattery. Charging is backed with 10W wired chargers. It comes with an 8 megapixel AI dual rear camera and a 5 megapixel front camera.  It features a sleek design with a modern flat-edge look and comes in two stylish colors: Phantom Crystal and Opal Purple.
