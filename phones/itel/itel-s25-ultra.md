The Itel S25 Ultra is an upper mid-range smartphone neatly packed with some of the best features you can find at the price Itel is asking. It features a 6.78-inch  AMOLEDdisplaywith a 120Hz refresh rate. The display also has Corning Gorilla Glass 7i screen protection to the device. The cellular network used in the phone relies on the Unisoc T620 chipset. The camera is equipped with a 50MP Samsung S5K2P3SX primary sensor and A 32MP Galaxy S10 front camera. The rear camera is capable of capturing videos at 2k at only 30fps whereas the frontcamerais equally powerful capturing 1080p videos at only 30 fps. Another important aspect of the Itel S25 Ultra is 5000mAh capacity and it is possible to charge it with 18W. The Itel S25 Ultra price in Bangladesh is BDT 17,990 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 128GB of ROM. The device available colors Bromo Black, Meteor Titanium, and Komodo Ocean.
