Itel Vision 2 Plus comes with 6.8 inches HD+ IPS screen. It has a Full-View waterdrop notch design. The backcamerais of dual 13+0.3 MP with autofocus, LED flash etc. features and Full HD video recording. The front camera is of 5 MP. Itel Vision 2 Plus comes with 5000 mAhbattery. It has 2 GB RAM, 1.6 GHz octa-core CPU and PowerVR IMG8322 GPU. It is powered by a UniSoC SC9863A (28 nm) chipset. The device comes with 32 GB internal storage and dedicated MicroSD slot. There is a back-mounted fingerprint sensor in this phone. Among other features, there is FM Radio, Dual 4G SIM, Face Unlock etc.
