Itel A33 is one of the low budget phones in Bangladesh. It launched in Bangladesh in Mar 2019. The Itel A33 offers basic features. It offers a 5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1440 pixels and runs on Android 8.1 (Oreo: Go Edition). Under the hood, you’ll find a quad-core MediaTek MT6580A processor. The camera setup includes a 5 MP main camera and a 2 MP front-facing shooter, both equipped with single LED flashes. It also supports a 2200 mAh battery. The Itel A33 price in Bangladesh is BDT 5,390 (official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of ROM. It comes in Champagne Gold, City Blue, and Piano Black
