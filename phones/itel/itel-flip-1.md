The Itel Flip 1 functions as a basic phone that provides users with both simplicity and functionality. It comes with a 2.4-inch QVGAdisplaydelivering 240x320 pixels resolution. The phone operates on a Unisoc SC6531E chipset that combines 4MB of RAM with 4MB of internal storage space that users can increase to 32GB with a microSD card. This phone has a built-in 0.3 MP primary camera. The Itel Flip 1 enables users to connect through dual Nano-SIM slots and functions on both 2G and 3G cellular networks. The 1200mAh non-removable Li-Ionbattery. The device supports Bluetooth v4.0 along with a 3.5mm audio jack and a USB Type-C port which handles both charging operations and data sharing. The Itel Flip 1 features an FM radio and loudspeaker and vibration alert functions. Available in black, blue, and orange. The Itel Flip 1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 3,000 (Unofficial).
