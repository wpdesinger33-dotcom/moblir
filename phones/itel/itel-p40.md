The Itel P40 3/32GB is a budget-friendly smartphone that packs impressive features. It comes with a 6.6-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1612 pixels. The water-drop notch design ensures an immersive viewing experience. Under the hood, it runs on Android 12 (Go edition) and is powered by the Unisoc SC9863A chipset, featuring an octa-core CPU (4x1.6 GHz Cortex-A55 & 4x1.2 GHz Cortex-A55) and the IMG8322 GPU. The camera setup includes a dual rear camera system with a 13 MP main lens and a 0.3 MP secondary lens, complemented by an LED flash. For selfies, there’s a 5 MP front camera. While it may not compete with flagship devices, it captures decent photos and videos. What sets the itel P40 apart is its massive 6000mAh battery. With 18W fast charging, you can stay connected all day without worrying about running out of juice. Additionally, the phone features a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor for added security.
