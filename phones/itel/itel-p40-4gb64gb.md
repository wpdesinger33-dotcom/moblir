The Itel P40 (4GB+64GB) is a budget-friendly Android smartphone that packs some impressive features. It comes with a 6.6-inch IPS LCD screen with a resolution of 720 x 1612 pixels, providing ample real estate for browsing, gaming, and multimedia consumption. Powered by the Unisoc SC9863A chipset, the P40 runs on Android 12 (Go edition). Its octa-core CPU (4x1.6 GHz Cortex-A55 & 4x1.2 GHz Cortex-A55) ensures decent performance for everyday tasks. With a massive 6000mAh battery, this phone ensures extended usage without frequent recharging. Plus, it supports 18W fast charging. The dual rear camera setup features a 13 MP primary lens and a 0.3 MP secondary lens with LED flash. For selfies, there’s a 5 MP front camera. The rear-mounted fingerprint sensor provides added security and convenience. The Itel P40 Price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,990 for its 4/64GB variant. It comes with three different colors which are Yellow, Blue, and Black. You can purchase this smartphone from Intel's official showroom, authorized shop and online marketplace.
