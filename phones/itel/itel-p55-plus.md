The Itel P55 Plus stands out as a dependable smartphone for daily use, all while maintaining an affordable price tag. It comes with a sleek design and comes in attractive colors. Its 6.6-inch IPS display offers a smooth experience with a 90Hz refresh rate, ensuring vibrant visuals for your content consumption. Under the hood, the P55+ packs a punch with 4GB or 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of internal storage. Powered by the Unisoc T606 (12 nm) chipset, it handles everyday tasks with ease. Capture moments with the 50MP main primary camera. While it may not be a photography powerhouse, it delivers decent results for casual photography. One of its standout features is the massive 5000mAh battery, which ensures extended usage without frequent charging. Additionally, the 45W fast charger speeds up refueling.
