The Itel A23 Pro, a budget-friendly Android 10 smartphone, packs essential features into its compact design. Sporting a 5-inch HD display with a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels, it offers a modest viewing experience. Under the hood, you’ll find a Quad-core Spreadtrum SC9832E CPU paired with an ARM Mali T820 MP2 GPU, 1GB of RAM, and 16GB of internal storage (expandable up to 64GB via an SD card). This smartphone caters to basic photography needs with its 2MP rear camera and a 0.3MP selfie camera. It supports dual SIM functionality and microSDXC cards. The 2400mAh battery ensures decent longevity for everyday tasks. While it may not be a powerhouse, the itel A23 Pro appeals to those seeking an affordable device for essential communication and light usage. Its Sapphire Blue and Lake Blue color options add a touch of vibrancy.
