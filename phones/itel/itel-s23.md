Its dual-camera setup, featuring a 50 MP primary sensor, captures high-quality images, while the 8 MP front camera ensures crisp selfies and video calls. One of the compelling reasons to consider the Itel S23 is its robust 5000mAh battery, which promises extended usage times, reducing the need for frequent charging.
