The Itel P55 is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers impressive features for its price. It comes with a 6.6-inch IPS LCD display with a smooth 90Hz refresh rate, ensuring fluid visuals. Its 720 x 1612 pixel resolution provides decent clarity for everyday use. Powered by the Unisoc T606 (12 nm) chipset, the octa-core processor (2x1.6 GHz Cortex-A75 & 6x1.6 GHz Cortex-A55) handles tasks efficiently. The Mali-G57 MP1 GPU contributes to a satisfactory gaming experience. On the rear, the 50 MP primary camera (along with an auxiliary 0.08 MP lens) captures detailed shots. Dual-LED flash and panorama mode enhance photography. The 8 MP front camera handles selfies and video calls. The 5000 mAh non-removable Li-Po battery ensures extended usage. With 18W fast charging, you won’t be tethered to the charger for long. The Itel P55 Price in Bangladesh is BDT 11,990 (Official) for its 8/128GB variant. It comes with three different colors which are Moonlit Black, Aurora Blue, Brilliant Gold. You can purchase this smartphone from Itel's official showroom, authorized shop and online marketplace.
