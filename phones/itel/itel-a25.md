The Itel A25 can be the best choice for those who are looking for a small size smartphone, it is a low budget phone. The Itel A25 was released in November 2020. It features a 5.0-inch display with a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels. Powered by the Unisoc SC9832E chipset. The phone boasts a 5 MP rear camera with LED flash and a 2 MP front camera. Running on Android 9.0 (Pie), the A25 is equipped with a 3020 mAh battery. The Itel A25 price in Bangladesh is BDT 4,990 (official) for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes with Gradient Black, and Gradient Blue colors.
