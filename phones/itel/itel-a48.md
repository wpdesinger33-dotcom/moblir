itel A48 comes with 6.1 inches HD+ IPS screen. It has a Full-View waterdrop notch design. The back camera is of dual 5+0.3 MP with autofocus, LED flash, portrait mode etc. and Full HD video recording. The front camera is of 5 MP. itel A48 comes with 3000 mAh battery. It has 2 GB RAM, 1.4 GHz quad-core CPU and PowerVR GPU. It is powered by a 28 nm UniSoC SC9832E chipset. The device comes with 32 GB internal storage and up to 128 GB dedicated MicroSD slot. There is a back-mounted fingerprint sensor in this phone. Among other features, there is FM Radio, Dual 4G SIM, Face Unlock etc.
