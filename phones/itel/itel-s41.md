The Itel S41 is a budget-friendly smartphone that was launched in September 2017. It features a 5.00-inch touchscreen display with a resolution of 720x1280 pixels. Powered by a 1.25GHz quad-core MediaTek MT6737 processor and 3GB of RAM, it runs on Android 7.0 Nougat. The non-removable 2700mAh battery keeps it going throughout the day. Keep reading this article to know about the Itel S41 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of cameras, the Itel S41 sports an 8-megapixel rear camera and an 8-megapixel front camera for selfies. It offers 16GB of inbuilt storage, expandable via a microSD card (up to 32GB). The phone supports dual SIM cards (Micro-SIM) and includes connectivity options like Wi-Fi, GPS, USB OTG, FM radio, 3G, and 4G. The Itel S41 price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,490 for its 3GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage.
