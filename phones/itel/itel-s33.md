The iTel S33 is the first smartphone from iTel Mobile to feature a larger 6.0-inch display. Prior to this, iTel smartphones had not ventured into the 6-inch arena. This phone belongs to iTel’s premium phone line, aiming to improve upon the previous iTel S32 in terms of specs and build quality. Keep reading this article to know about the iTel S33 price in Bangladesh and its details. It comes with a 6.0-inch IPS LCD capacitive touchscreen with a resolution of 480 x 960 pixels. While the display size is impressive, the pixel density (around 176 PPI) is lower compared to its predecessor, the iTel S32. In terms of performance, you’ll find a MediaTek MT6580 quad-core CPU clocked at 1.3 GHz, paired with 1 GB of RAM and 16 GB of internal storage (expandable up to 32 GB via microSD card). The S33 features a dual 5 + 5-megapixel rear camera setup and a 13-megapixel front-facing sensor.
