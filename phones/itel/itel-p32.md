Itel has launched their low budget phone Itel P32. It was launched in June 2018 in Bangladesh. It offers practical features for everyday use. With a 5.5-inch HD+ IPS LCD display (720 x 1440 pixels). Running on Android 8.1 Oreo Go Edition. The quad-core 1.3GHz MediaTek MT6580M processor ensures reliable performance. The dual rear cameras (5MP + 5MP) capture decent photos, while the 5MP front camera handles selfies. The 4000mAh non-removable battery keeps you powered throughout the day. The Itel P32 price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,990 (official) for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in Champagne Gold, City Blue, and Ice Blue colors.
