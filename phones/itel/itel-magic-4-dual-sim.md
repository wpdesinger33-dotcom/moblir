Launched in October 2022 the Itel Magic 4 Dual Sim operates as a practical feature phone which delivers both good performance and low price points. The Itel Magic 4 Dual Sim phone shows its screen content through a 2.8-inch QVGAdisplaywhich operates at 240x320 pixels resolution. The phone provides a 2MP rear-facingcameraenabled with LED flash. It supports dual SIM functionality. It has a built-in 2000mAh Li-ionbatterythat powers the device. This device supports Bluetooth 2.0 together with FM radio and features a 3.5mm headphone jack. Users can purchase the Itel Magic 4 Dual Sim in three distinct color options which comprise Black together with Blue and Red. The Itel Magic 4 Dual Sim price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,499 (Official).
