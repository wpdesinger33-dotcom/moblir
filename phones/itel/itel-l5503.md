The itel L5503, also marketed as the itel A46, is an entry-level smartphone that offers basic features at an affordable price. This phone has a slim profile with a thickness of 8.5 mm and weighs 155 grams. It comes in colors like blue, gray, red, and green, featuring a plastic body construction. Keep reading this article to know about the Itel L5503 price in Bangladesh and its details. It features a 5.45” IPS display with a resolution of 720 x 1440 pixels. While not the sharpest screen, it provides decent visuals for everyday use. In terms of performance, the L5503 is powered by the Unisoc SC9863A chipset, featuring an octa-core CPU (4x 1.6 GHz ARM Cortex-A55 and 4x 1.2 GHz ARM Cortex-A55) and a PowerVR GE8322 GPU clocked at 550 MHz. It comes with 2 GB of RAM and 16 GB of internal storage, expandable via microSD cards. The rear camera setup consists of a 5 MP primary sensor, while the front houses a 5 MP selfie camera. The image quality is suitable for basic photography needs. Its 2350 mAh Li-Polymer battery provides moderate endurance, enough to get you through a day of light usage. The Itel L5503 price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,990 (Unofficial) for its 2GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage.
