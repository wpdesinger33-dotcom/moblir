The Itel A44 pro was released to the Bangladesh market in March 2018 and is an affordable smartphone that was designed for basic use. A 5.45 inches IPS LCD display and has a resolution of 720 x 1440 pixels. Its operating system is supported by a MediaTek MT6737M system chip that has a quad core up to 1.3 GHz and Mali-T720 MP2 GPU. It has a variety of cameras with an 8MP rear camera and 5MP front camera for selfies. The A44 Pro has Android 7.0 Nougat which is easy to use. It also has a 2400mAh battery. The Champagne Gold, Midnight Black, and City Blue colors are offered. The Itel A44 pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,490 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
