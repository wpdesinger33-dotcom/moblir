The Itel 5262 Dual SIM arrived on the Bangladeshi market during December 2023 to serve users who require simple communication devices. This phone comes installed with a 2.4-inch QVGAdisplaywhich shows 240 x 320 pixel resolution. This phone uses a MediaTek chipset for processing and contains 4MB of RAM memory that users can extend to 8GB by adding a microSD card. Users benefit from dual SIM functionality on the Itel 5262 Dual SIM because it enables them to maintain two different phone numbers from a single device. The phone provides a 0.3 MP maincamerathat includes an LED flash. The phone enables connectivity through Bluetooth 2.0 and USB Type-C interfaces and FM radio along with a 3.5 mm audio port. With a 2000 mAh Li-Ionbattery, the Itel 5262 Dual SIM offers decent battery life for basic usage. The device presents three color options: black, green and blue. The Itel 5262 Dual SIM price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,799 (Official).
