The itel P65 is a budget-friendly smartphone that recently made its debut in the Philippines. Designed with a gaming-inspired aesthetic, it features a unique mech-like back panel adorned with a color-changing LED ring light. It boasts a  6.7-inch HD+ display with a resolution of 720 x 1600 pixels. Also the display supports a smooth 120Hz refresh rate. Under the hood, the P65 runs on a UNISOC Tiger T615 processor. The device offers Android 14. The main rear camera features a 50-megapixel sensor, promising decent photo quality in well-lit conditions. On the front, an 8-megapixel selfie camera handles your video calls and self-portraits. It packs a substantial 5,000mAh battery and supports 18W fast charging. The itel P65 price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,000 (Expected) for 4GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. It comes in Cyber Black and Cyber Titanium colors.
