The Alcatel One Touch Pop C9 smartphone costing low range Android was published in January 2014. This smartphone comes equipped with a 5.5 inches of IPS LCD and resolution of 540 x 960 pixels. The device is also supported by a 1.3GHz quad-core processor. It has 1GB RAM and 4GB built in memory and supports expansion with the microSD card. It has an 8 MP rear camera with autofocus and LED flash, that allows for 1080 p video recording, and 2 MP front facing. The phone has a 2500mAh removable battery. Other features include dual SIM capabilities, Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.0, GPS, and FM radio. The device also has a better look on the outer part, which is also available in any colors like Blue, White, and Gray. The Alcatel One Touch Pop C9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,999 (Official).
