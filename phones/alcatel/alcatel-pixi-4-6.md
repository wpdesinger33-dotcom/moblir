The Alcatel Pixi 4 (6) is an Android smartphone that was released in June 2016. It features a 6.0-inch display with an IPS LCD panel, offering a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The phone is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 210 chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.1 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU and an Adreno 304 GPU. In terms of photography, the Pixi 4 (6) sports an 8 MP primary camera with autofocus, LED flash, HDR, and panorama capabilities. For selfies, there’s a 5 MP front camera with an LED flash as well. The device runs on Android 5.1 (Lollipop), which is upgradable to 6.0.1 (Marshmallow). Other notable features include a 2580 mAh removable battery. It measures 165 x 83.8 x 8.3 mm and weighs approximately 186 grams. The Alcatel Pixi 4 (6) price in Bangladesh is BDT  6,799 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of ROM. Available color options include Silver,Gold.
