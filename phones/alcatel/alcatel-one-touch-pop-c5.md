The Alcatel One Touch Pop C5 is an Android touch phone for the basic user. The phone has a 4.5 inches TFT display that is capable of a resolution of 480 X 854 pixels. It is a 1.3GHz dual-core processor for the device to operate smoothly.. It is equipped with 512MB of RAM and packs 4GB of internal storage expanded by a microSD up to 32GB. It features a 5MP rear camera with LED flash and 0.3MP front camera to enable users to take selfies. The phone comes with a 1800 mAh lithium ion removable battery. Others include 2 SIM slots support, Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.0, GPS etc, FM-radio for communication. The Alcatel One Touch Pop C5 price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,199 (Official).
