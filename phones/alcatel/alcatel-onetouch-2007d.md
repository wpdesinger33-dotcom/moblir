The Onetouch 2007D was made and released in 2013, is a basic Alcatel model of a feature phone. It supports a display with a dimension of TFT 2.4 inches and resolution at 240x320 pixels. This device has internal memory of 16MB which is available for users 16MB with external memory that supports up to 8GB via Micro SD card. It has a 3MP back camera. The device comes with a 750mAh battery. The other special features are availability of two SIM card slots, Bluetooth version 3.0, FM radio, loudspeaker. The Onetouch 2007D price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,599 (Official).
