The Alcatel Idol 2 provides a simple and budget-friendly option for users seeking a basic smartphone experience. It was released in July 2014 in Bangladesh, is an Android smartphone that features a 5.0-inch display with a resolution of 540 x 960 pixels. It is powered by a quad-core 1.3 GHz chipset. The primary camera is 8 MP, while the front camera is 2 MP. The device has a 2000 mAh non-removable battery. Although it originally ran Android 4.2 (Jelly Bean), it is upgradable to Android 4.4 (KitKat). The Alcatel Idol 2 price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,499 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in  Slate, Chocolate, White, and Hot pink colors.
