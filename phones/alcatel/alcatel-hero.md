The Alcatel Hero, released in January 2014, is a 6-inch Android smartphone. It features an IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels and an oleophobic coating. Under the hood, it runs on a Mediatek MT6589 chipset with a quad-core 1.5 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU. The primary camera is 13 MP, while the front camera is 2 MP. The phone packs a 3400 mAh non-removable battery. The Alcatel Hero price in Bangladesh is BDT 17999 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in Black, and White colors.
