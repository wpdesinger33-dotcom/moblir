The Alcatel Idol 2 Mini S offers a budget-friendly option for those seeking a compact smartphone experience. It was released in June 2014, is a compact Android smartphone. It features a 4.5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 540 x 960 pixels. The phone is powered by a quad-core 1.2 GHz processor. For photography, the Idol 2 Mini S sports an 8 MP primary camera. The selfie camera is a 2 MP. The device runs on Android 4.3 (Jelly Bean), which is upgradable to Android 4.4 (KitKat). This smartphone has a 2000 mAh battery that provides up to 9 hours of talk time on 2G and up to 13 hours on 3G. The Alcatel Idol 2 Mini S price in Bangladesh is BDT 4,999 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage. Available colors include Slate, Chocolate, White, Hot Pink, and Cloudy.
