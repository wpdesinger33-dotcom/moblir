The Alcatel Idol X Plus, released in June 2014, is a notable entry in the low-range smartphone market. It features a 5-inch Full HD IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels, offering vibrant and sharp visuals. Powered by an octa-core 2.0 GHz Cortex-A7 processor. The phone runs on Android 4.2.1 Jelly Bean, upgradable to 4.4.4 KitKat, providing a decent user experience despite its slightly outdated software. One of the standout features of the Idol X Plus is its 13.1 MP rear camera, capable of capturing detailed photos and 1080p videos at 30fps. The 2 MP front camera also supports 1080p video recording, making it suitable for video calls and selfies. It has a 2500 mAh battery and offers reasonable endurance, supporting up to 20 hours of talk time on 2G networks. Design-wise, the Idol X Plus is sleek and lightweight, weighing just 125 grams and measuring 7.9mm in thickness. The  Alcatel Idol X Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,500 (Official) for 1.5GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in Black, and White colors.
