The Alcatel One Touch Flash was officially released on 20 November 2014. It has a 5.5inch IPS LCD capacitive touch screen with 720x1280 pixels. The phone is not very heavy, weighing 150 grams, and has a thin thickness of 8.1mm. It comes with an octa-core MediaTek MT6592M processor with a speed of 1.4GHz, 8GB of in-built memory and with the external micro SD card support of up to 32GB. The phone has a built-in 13MP rear camera with autofocus and LED flash, and has a 5MP front camera. The battery capacity of the smartphone is 3200mAh. The phone supports two SIM card slots, Wi-Fi connectivity, Bluetooth 4.0, GPS connectivity. The Alcatel One Touch Flash price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,888 (Official) for 1GB of RAM.
