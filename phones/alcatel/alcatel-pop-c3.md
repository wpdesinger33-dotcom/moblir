The Alcatel Pop C3, released in March 2014, is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers basic features suitable for everyday use. It comes with a 4.0-inch TFT display with a resolution of 480 x 800 pixels, providing decent clarity for its size. Powered by a dual-core 1.3 GHz Cortex-A7 processor and a MediaTek MT6572 chipset. It runs on Android 4.2 (Jelly Bean), which, while outdated, supports essential applications and functionalities. The Alcatel Pop C3 features a 5 MP rear camera capable of recording 480p videos, and a VGA front camera for basic selfies and video calls. Its 1300 mAh removable battery provides adequate battery life, with up to 13.5 hours of talk time on 2G networks and up to 9.5 hours on 3G networks. The Alcatel Pop C3 price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,999 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
