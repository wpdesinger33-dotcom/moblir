The Alcatel Flash 2 may not be the most cutting-edge device, it offers a reliable experience for its time. It was released in September 2015, is an Android smartphone that offers a balanced set of features. It comes with a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display, providing a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The phone is powered by the MediaTek MT6753 chipset, featuring an octa-core 1.3 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and a Mali-T720MP3 GPU. In terms of photography, the Alcatel Flash 2 boasts a 13 MP primary camera with f/2.0 aperture and PDAF (Phase Detection Autofocus). For selfies, a 5 MP front camera with an LED flash. The phone runs on Android 5.1 (Lollipop) and is equipped with a non-removable 3000 mAh Li-Ion battery.  The Alcatel Flash 2 price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,499 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. Available in Volcanic Grey and Mica White colors.
