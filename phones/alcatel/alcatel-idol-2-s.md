The Alcatel Idol 2 S is offered a decent package for its time, especially for users seeking a budget-friendly option with LTE connectivity. It was released in June 2014, is an Android smartphone with a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display. It features a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels, resulting in a pixel density of approximately 294 ppi. The phone runs on Android 4.3 (Jelly Bean), which is upgradable to Android 4.3 (KitKat). Under the hood, it houses a quad-core 1.2 GHz CPU. For photography enthusiasts, the Idol 2 S boasts an 8 MP rear camera with autofocus. The front-facing camera is a modest 1.3 MP shooter. Video recording is supported at 1080p@30fps for the rear camera and 720p for the front camera. Its non-removable 2150 mAh Li-Ion battery provides standby times of up to 520 hours (2G) or talk times of up to 10 hours 30 minutes (2G) and 16 hours 30 minutes (3G). The Alcatel Idol 2 S price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,999 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. It comes in colors like Slate, Chocolate, and White.
