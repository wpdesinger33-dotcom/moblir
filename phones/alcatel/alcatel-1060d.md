The Alcatel 1060D, another released in 2013 is an affordable basic feature phone. It has a 1.77 TFT colour display. The Alcatel 1060D includes 4MB of RAM and 4MB ROM and micro SD card slot that is firmly upgradable to 8GB. It has a low-end VGA rear camera, and a microUSB 2.0 for charging and data downloading. With a claimed back-up time of 650mAh. Other features are twin SIM support, an FM radio, an in-built loudspeaker, and vibration alert options. The Alcatel 1060D price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,249 (Official).
