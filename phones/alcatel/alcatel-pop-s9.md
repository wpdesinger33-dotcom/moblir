The Alcatel Pop S9, released in the second quarter of 2014, is a mid-range smartphone that offers a blend of functionality and affordability. It features a 5.9-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720x1280 pixels. This device is powered by a 1.2 GHz quad-core processor. The Pop S9 runs on Android 4.3 (Jelly Bean). In terms of photography, the Alcatel Pop S9 is equipped with an 8-megapixel rear camera that supports 1080p video recording, and a 2-megapixel front camera for selfies. The phone’s 3400 mAh non-removable battery offers substantial battery life, with up to 36 hours of talk time on 2G networks and up to 23 hours on 3G networks. The  Alcatel Pop S9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 13,999 for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. It also offers a range of color options, including Midnight Blue, Dark Chocolate, and Dark Aubergine, among others.
