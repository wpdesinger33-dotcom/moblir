The Alcatel A5 LED, released in June 2017, features a 5.2-inch IPS display with 720 x 1280 pixels. Its sleek design, with dimensions of 146 x 72.1 x 7.7 mm and a weight of 134 grams, makes it relatively lightweight and easy to handle. Under the hood, the phone is powered by a Mediatek MT6753 chipset. Photography-wise, the Alcatel A5 LED sports an 8-megapixel auto-focus camera on the back. Also, there’s a 5-megapixel selfie camera on the front for self-portraits. The 2800 mAh non-removable battery. The Alcatel A5 LED price in Bangladesh is BDT 16,499 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
