The Alcatel Pop 3 (5.5) caters to budget-conscious users looking for a basic smartphone experience. It was released in April 2016, is an Android smartphone with a 5.5-inch TFT display with 720 x 1280 pixels. It comes in a 13 MP primary camera, along with a 8 MP front camera. The device is powered by a quad-core processor running at either 1.3 GHz or 1.1 GHz.  The 2910 mAh battery keeps things running. The Alcatel Pop 3 (5.5) price in Bangladesh is BDT 7,299 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
