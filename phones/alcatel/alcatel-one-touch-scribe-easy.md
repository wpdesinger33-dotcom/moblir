The Alcatel One Touch Scribe Easy, released in May 2013, is an Android smartphone that offers a blend of basic functionality and affordability. It features a 5-inch TFT display with a resolution of 480x800 pixels. Powered by a dual-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A5 processor and Qualcomm’s Snapdragon S4 Play chipset. Weighing 163 grams and measuring 143 x 78.4 x 9.8 mm, it is relatively lightweight and easy to handle. In terms of photography, the device sports a 5 MP rear camera with autofocus and LED flash, capable of recording 720p videos at 24fps. The front VGA camera is suitable for basic video calls. The phone also features a 2500 mAh removable battery, offering up to 9 hours of talk time on 2G networks. Running on Android 4.1.2 Jelly Bean, the Alcatel One Touch Scribe Easy provides a user-friendly interface and access to a wide range of apps through the Google Play Store. The Alcatel One Touch Scribe Easy price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,499 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage. Available in vibrant colors like Flash Red, Flash Yellow, Black, and Flash Blue.
