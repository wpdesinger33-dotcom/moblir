The Alcatel One Touch Star 6010D was launched in March, 2013 is a low-end Android smartphone. It comes with a 4 inches AMOLED screen, supporting a resolution of 480x800 pixels. The phone operates from a 1GHz dual core MediaTek MT6577 processor. The Star 6010D has an internal memory of 512MB RAM and 4 GB built in and you can expand the memory by a microSD card of up to 32GB. It comes equipped with a rear 5MP auto focus camera with LED flash and on the front side 0.3MP camera. The phone  has a removable battery of 1500mAh. Other key highlights include dual SIM, Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.0, GPS and FM Radio connectivity. It also has a nice sleek look with 9.7mm thickness and it weighs 119.5 grams. The Alcatel One Touch Star 6010D price in Bangladesh is BDT 7,888 (Official).
