The Alcatel Fire C 2G, released in October 2014, is a compact smartphone with a focus on simplicity. It features a 3.5-inch TFT display, which offers a resolution of 320 x 480 pixels. The phone’s dimensions are 112.2 x 62 x 12 mm, and it weighs just 93 grams. Under the hood, you’ll find a Spreadtrum 6821 chipset. The 1.3 MP primary camera captures basic photos and videos at 320p resolution. For selfies, VGA front camera. The device runs on Firefox OS 1.3, which provides a straightforward and lightweight user experience. Powering the Alcatel Fire C 2G is a removable 1000 mAh Li-Ion battery, offering up to 160 hours of standby time and around 7 hours of talk time. The Alcatel Fire C 2G price in Bangladesh is BDT 3,299 (Official) for 128MB of RAM and 256MB of phone storage. It comes in two color variants: Bluish Black and Dark Chocolate.
