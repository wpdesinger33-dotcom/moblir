The Alcatel Pixi 4 (5), released in June 2016, is an Android smartphone that offers a compact and budget-friendly experience. It features a 5.0” IPS LCD display and Resolution of 480 x 854 pixels. The phone’s design is slim and straightforward, measuring 140.7 x 72.7 x 9.5 mm and weighing 148 grams. Under the hood, you’ll find a quad-core 1.3 GHz CPU paired. The camera setup includes an 8 MP primary camera with autofocus. For selfies, a 5 MP front camera with an LED flash, which is handy for low-light situations. The 2000 mAh removable battery. The Alcatel Pixi 4 (5) price in Bangladesh is BDT 5,190 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. It comes in Black colors.
