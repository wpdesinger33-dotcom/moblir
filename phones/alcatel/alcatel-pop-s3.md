The Alcatel Pop S3 was available in September, 2014 with TFT capacitive technology having a display size of 4.0" and a screen resolution of 480 x 800 pixels. The phone has a Qualcomm Snapdragon 400 MSM8926 chipset with a 1.2 Ghz quad core CPU and Adreno 305 GPU. The phone also comes with a 5MP rearcamerafor taking photos and 0.3MP front camera for taking selfies. Alcatel Pop S3 runs Android Operating System up to version 4.3 jelly bean. It has an embedded Li-ionbatteryof 2000mAh with the ability to talk for 12 hours and possible to standby for 400 hours. The Alcatel Pop S3 price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,999 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
