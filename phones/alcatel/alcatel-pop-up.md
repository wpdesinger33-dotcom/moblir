The Alcatel Pop Up is an Android smartphone that was released in September 2015. It offers a 5.0” IPS LCD display and resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The phone is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 610 (MSM8929) chipset, which includes an octa-core 1.4 GHz CPU and Adreno 405 GPU. In terms of photography, the Alcatel OneTouch Pop Up sports a 13 MP autofocus camera on the rear. For selfies, 5 MP front-facing camera. This device runs on Android 5.0 Lollipop. The Alcatel Pop Up is powered by a removable 2000 mAh Li-Ion battery. The Alcatel Pop Up price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,499 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in various color options, including Black, White, Red, Orange, and Green.
