The Alcatel Pop D5, an Android smartphone that was released in September 2014. This compact device offers a balance of features suitable for its time. It boasts a 4.5-inch TFT display with a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels, resulting in a pixel density of approximately 218 ppi. Under the hood, you’ll find a quad-core 1.3 GHz processor. The rear camera setup is a 5 MP primary camera with LED flash and HDR capabilities. It can record videos at 720p resolution. On the front, there’s a VGA (0.3 MP) selfie camera. Powering the Pop D5 is a removable 1800 mAh Li-Ion battery. The phone runs on Android 4.4.2 KitKat. The Alcatel Pop D5 price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,999 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage. It comes in various color options, including Black/White, Platinum Silver, Ash Gray, Volcano Black, Cozy Red, Lemon Yellow, Aqua Blue, and Jade Green.
