The Alcatel Pop D3 is an affordable budget smartphone with basic features, suitable for users looking for a simple and functional device. It is an Android smartphone that was released in August 2014. It features a 4.0-inch display with a resolution of 480 x 800 pixels. The phone is powered by a dual-core 1.3 GHz processor. In terms of photography, the Pop D3 sports a 5 MP primary camera with LED flash and HDR capabilities. For selfies, there’s a VGA front-facing camera. The device runs on Android 4.4.2 KitKat. The removable 1400 mAh Li-Ion battery provides up to 9 hours of talk time on 2G networks and up to 6 hours on 3G networks. The Alcatel Pop D3 price in Bangladesh is BDT 3,499 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage. It is available in various colors, including Midnight Blue, Dark Chocolate, Dark Aubergine, White, Red, Yellow, Orange, Green, and Purple.
