Alcatel 1011D that was launched in 2013 is an entry-level features phone. It comes with a 1.77-inch TFT display with a pixel dimension of 128X160. This phone offers consumers 4MB of RAM and 3MB of memory, an expansion slot to add up to 8GB microSD card. It has a 0.3 MP rear camera. The device has a battery power of 650mAh. The presence of a second SIM card, an FM radio, a loudspeaker, and vibration alerts. The Alcatel 1011D price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,249 (Official).
