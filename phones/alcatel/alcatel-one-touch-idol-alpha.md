The Alcatel One Touch Idol Alpha which was released in September 2013 in Bangladesh. It has a screen size of 4.7 inch, IPS LCD screen with a pixel density of 312 ppi and a resolution of 720x1280 pixels. The device is driven by a 1.2GHz quad-core MediaTek MT6589 processor. The Idol Alpha comes with 1GB of RAM and 16GB built-in memory which can be expanded with micro SD cards. It has 13MP rear camera, autofocus LED Flash and 1.3MP front facing camera. It also has a 2000mAh battery. The Alcatel One Touch Idol Alpha price in Bangladesh is BDT 11,999 (Official).
