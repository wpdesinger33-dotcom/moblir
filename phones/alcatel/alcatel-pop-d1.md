The Alcatel Pop D1 is an entry-level Android smartphone that was released in September 2014. It features a compact 3.5-inch TFT display with a resolution of 320 x 480 pixels. The phone is powered by a dual-core 1.0 GHz CPU. In terms of photography, the Pop D1 sports a 2 MP primary camera capable of capturing basic photos and videos. The device runs on Android 4.4.2 KitKat and is equipped with a 1300 mAh removable battery.  The Alcatel Pop D1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 5,999 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage. It comes in Midnight Blue, Dark Chocolate, Dark Aubergine, White, Red, Yellow, Orange, Green, and Purple colors.
