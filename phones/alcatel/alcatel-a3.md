The Alcatel A3 offers a budget-friendly option for users seeking essential features. It was released in July 2017, is an Android smartphone with a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display, offering a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. Powered by the MediaTek MT6737 chipset, it features a quad-core 1.25 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and a Mali-T720MP2 GPU. On the camera front, the Alcatel A3 boasts a 13 MP primary camera with features like LED flash. For selfies,  5 MP front camera with an LED flash. The device runs on Android 6.0 Marshmallow and includes a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor, and compass. Its 2460 mAh non-removable battery keeps it going. The Alcatel A3 price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,500 (Official) for 1.5GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. Available in white and gray colors.
