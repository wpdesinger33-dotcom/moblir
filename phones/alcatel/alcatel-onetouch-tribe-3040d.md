Alcatel OneTouch Tribe 3040D is an affordable smartphone for those who don’t need complex gadgets and high performance at the same time. This android version was released in August 2013 and boasts of a TFT touchdisplaypanel of 3.5 inches and has a resolution of 320 X 480. The phone is armed with one core 1.0GHz single operating processor. It also has a 2MP rear camera for basic photography with no selfiecamera. This phone is endowed with a 1000mAhbatterythat gives you a talk time of up to 8 hours and has an ability to standby for 560 hours. The available communication technologies are GSM, GPRS, EDGE, WLAN 3 (IEEE 802.11 a/b/g) and Bluetooth 3.0. The Alcatel OneTouch Tribe 3040D price in Bangladesh is BDT 3,499 (Official) for 128MB of RAM, and 128MB of phone storage.
