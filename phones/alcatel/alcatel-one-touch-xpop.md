The Alcatel One Touch X’Pop, released in May 2013, is an Android smartphone that was designed to cater to the mid-range market. It features a 4.5-inch TFT display with a resolution of 540 x 960 pixels, providing a decent visual experience for its time. The device is powered by a dual-core 1.0 GHz Cortex-A9 processor. The One Touch X’Pop is equipped with a 5-megapixel rear camera with autofocus and LED flash, capable of recording 720p videos at 30fps. For selfies, it has a VGA front camera. It runs on Android 4.1 Jelly Bean. The phone is powered by a removable 1800 mAh battery, which offers up to 16 hours of talk time on 2G networks and up to 8.5 hours on 3G networks. The One Touch X’Pop price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,949 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
