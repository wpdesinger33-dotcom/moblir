The Alcatel One Touch Pop C1, released in September 2013 in Bangladesh. It features a 3.5-inch TFT display with a resolution of 320x480 pixels. The phone is powered by a 1GHz dual-core processor. The Alcatel One Touch Pop C1 comes with 512MB of RAM and 4GB of internal storage, expandable via microSD card. It includes a 2MP rear camera. The device boasts a 1300mAh battery. The phone is available in various colors, including Pink, Black, Turquoise, White, Gray, and Red. The Alcatel One Touch Pop C1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,999 (Official).
