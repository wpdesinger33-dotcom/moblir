The Alcatel Idol 4, released in June 2016, is a mid-range Android smartphone. It features a sleek metal and glass design that’s uniquely reversible, ensuring it’s never upside-down. The phone boasts stereo speakers, a selfie flash, NFC, and a convenient shortcut button. Its key specs include a 5.2-inch full-HD display with 1080 x 1920 pixels. It has a 13MP primary camera. The front camera is an 8MP. Running on Android 6.0 Marshmallow, the Idol 4 is powered by a Snapdragon 617 CPU. Also it has a 2610 mAh with 18W fast charging. The Alcatel Idol 4 price in Bangladesh is BDT 22,999 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in Gold, Dark Gray, Rose Gold, and Metal Silver colors.
