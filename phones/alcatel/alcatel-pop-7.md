The Alcatel Pop 7, a budget-friendly Android tablet that was released in February 2014. It features a 7.0” TFT display and resolution of 600 x 1024 pixels. It’s powered by a Mediatek MT8312 chipset, dual-core 1.3 GHz Cortex-A7 processor. The 2 MP rear camera allows you to capture basic photos and record 720p videos, while the VGA front camera is suitable for video calls and selfies. Runs Android 4.2 Jelly Bean, which is quite dated but functional for basic usage. The tablet is powered by a non-removable 3240 mAh Li-Ion battery, providing standby times of up to 300 hours (2G) or up to 70 hours (3G). The Alcatel Pop 7 price in Bangladesh is BDT 9999 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage. It's available in a white color variant.
