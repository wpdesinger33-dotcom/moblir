The phone is powered by a MediaTek MT6572M chipset, containing a dual-core 1.0GHz ARM Cortex-A7 processor and an ARM Mali-400 MP1 graphic processing unit. The Alcatel One Touch Pop D1 operates with Android 4.4.2 KitKat and comes with Dual SIM. The phone also housed a 2-megapixel rearcamerawhich supports 1600 x 1200 digital pixels for picture taking and United Video 480p at 15fps. Powered by a 1300mAh Li-Ionbatteryit has up to 6 hrs 50 min of talk time. The Alcatel One Touch Pop D1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 5,999 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
