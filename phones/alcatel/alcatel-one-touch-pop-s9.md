The Alcatel One Touch Pop S9 was released in August 2014 and is available in Bangladesh. This model comes with 5.9 Inches IPS LCDdisplaywith a pixel density of 720 × 1280. The power of the device comes from the Qualcomm Snapdragon 400 MSM8926, that includes quad core 1.2 GHz ARM Cortex A7 and Qualcomm Adreno 305 graphics processing unit.  The Alcatel One Touch Pop S9 has Android 4.3 Jelly Bean as its operating system and the device has a dual SIM. The smartphone features a rear camera of 8MP for capturing of videos at 1080p HD resolution and front selfiecamerawith 2MP. This device measures 162.4 x 84.4 x 8.6 mm and thebatteryis Li-Ion 3400 mAh and may provide up to 36 h of talk time in 2G or up to 23 h talk time in 3G.  The Alcatel One Touch Pop S9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 3,999 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
