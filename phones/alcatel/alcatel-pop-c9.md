The Alcatel Pop C9 was a popular choice for budget-conscious consumers looking for a large-screen smartphone with basic features. It was released in June 2014, is an Android smartphone that offers a blend of functionality and affordability. It has a 5.5” IPS LCD display and resolution of 540 x 960 pixels. Powered by a quad-core 1.3 GHz processor, the device runs on Android 4.2 Jelly Bean. In terms of photography, the Alcatel Pop C9 is equipped with an 8 MP rear camera capable of recording 1080p videos, and a 2 MP front camera for selfies and video calls. The phone’s 2500 mAh non-removable battery offers reasonable standby and talk time, catering to the needs of average users. The Alcatel Pop C9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,999 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage. It was available in various colors, including Bluish Black, Cherry Red, Full White, Hot Pink, and Fresh Turquoise.
