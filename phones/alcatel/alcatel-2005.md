The Alcatel 2005 was first released in 2014 and is a primary point and affordable feature phone. It has a 2.4-inch TFT color display with 240 x 320 pixels resolution. The Alcatel 2005 comes with internal memory of 128MB and provides an expandable memory slot for microSD card. It possesses a 2 MP lens for rear camera. The device comes with an 850mAh battery. Other special components are the Dual SIM capability, FM radio, a loudspeaker, Vibration alert kinds. The Alcatel 2005 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,499 (Official).
