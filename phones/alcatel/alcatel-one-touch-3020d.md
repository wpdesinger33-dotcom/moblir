Although the Alcatel company released it recently on the 15th of August 2014, the phone is categorized under old generation phones, Alcatel One Touch 3020D. It has a 2.2" TFTdisplaywith resolution of 176x220 pixels. The device’s photographic feature comes with a 2-megapixel primary camera but does not have a front facingcamera. It  has 16MB of ROM that is expandable to 16GB by an external microSD card. The Alcatel One Touch 3020D listed above is fitted with an 850mAh Li-Ion battery with abatterytalk time of up to 7 hours and a battery standby time of up to 550 hours. It also contains multimedia communication tools for instance the FM radio and a document reader. The Alcatel One Touch 3020D price in Bangladesh is BDT 3,049 (Official).
