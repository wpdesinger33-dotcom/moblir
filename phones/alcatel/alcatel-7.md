The Alcatel 7 was released in August 2018, is a low budget Android smartphone with several noteworthy features. It boasts a 6.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 2180 pixels and an 18:9 aspect ratio. It’s powered by the Mediatek MT6763T Helio P23 chipset, featuring an octa-core 2.5 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and Mali-G71 MP2 GPU. It has dual rear cameras: A 12 MP primary lens (f/2.2) and a 2 MP depth sensor (f/2.4) for portrait mode. Selfie camera: An 8 MP front camera (f/2.8). The 4000 mAh non-removable battery with 10W wired charging. The Alcatel 7 price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,500 (official) for 2GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage. It comes in Black color.
