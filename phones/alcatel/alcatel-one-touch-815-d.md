The Alcatel One Touch 815 D was launched in 2013 as an affordable and simple feature phone for voice calls and SMS. The phone has an LCD screen of 2.4-inches with a pixel area of 320x240. One special feature of the One Touch 815 D is that it includes 64MB of RAM and 128MB of internal memory which is also supplemented by a microSD card up to 16GB. It has a 2-megapixel rear camera. The device has an 850 mAh battery. The other features for the communication are Dual SIM, Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 3.0, GPS and FM Radio. The Alcatel One Touch 815 D price in Bangladesh is BDT 3,499 (Official).
