The Alcatel Idol Mini, released in December 2013, is a low-range Android smartphone. It features a compact 4.3” IPS LCD display and resolution of 480x854 pixels. Powered by a dual-core 1.3 GHz Cortex-A7 processor (Mediatek MT6572). The 5 MP rear camera includes LED flash and panorama mode, while the front camera is VGA. The device runs Android 4.2 (Jelly Bean) and packs a 1700 mAh non-removable battery. The Alcatel Idol Mini price in Bangladesh is BDT 7,999 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. It comes in Cranberry Pink, Silver, and Slate colors.
