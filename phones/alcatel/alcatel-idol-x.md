The Alcatel Idol X offered a good balance of design, performance, and features for its time, making it a notable choice in the mid-range smartphone segment. It was released in July 2013. It featured a sleek design with a thickness of just 6.9mm. The phone boasted a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels, offering sharp visuals. Under the hood, it ran on Android 4.2.1 (Jelly Bean) and was powered by a Mediatek MT6589 quad-core processor clocked at 1.5 GHz. The camera setup included a 13.1 MP rear camera with autofocus and LED flash. For selfies, a 2.1 MP front camera.The 2000 mAh non-removable battery provided decent usage time. The Alcatel Idol X price in Bangladesh is BDT 12799 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in Blue, Black, Red, and Yellow colors.
