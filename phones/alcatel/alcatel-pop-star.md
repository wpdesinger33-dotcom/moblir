The Alcatel POP Star served as a budget-friendly option for users seeking basic functionality and a straightforward Android experience. It was released in February 2015, is an Android smartphone that offers a balanced set of features. With a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display and a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels, it provides decent visual clarity. The phone is powered by a MediaTek MT6580 chipset, featuring a quad-core 1.3 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU and a Mali-400MP2 GPU. In terms of photography, the POP Star sports an 8 MP primary camera with autofocus and an LED flash. For selfies, 5 MP front camera. The device runs on Android 5.0 Lollipop, which might feel a bit dated now, but it was standard at the time of its release. The removable 2000 mAh Li-Ion battery provides standby times of up to 566 hours (2G) or 500 hours (3G) and talk times of up to 12 hours (2G) or 10 hours (3G). The Alcatel POP Star price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,499 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. It is available in colors like Black, White, Silver, and Gold.
