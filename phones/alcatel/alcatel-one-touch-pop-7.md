The Alcatel One Touch Pop 7 launched in Bangladesh in January 2014. This is a fairly good android tablet which comes with the budget price, and performs reasonably well for general usage. It has dimensions of 192 x 113 x 8.9mm and its display is a 7.0 inch TFTdisplayof 1024 x 600 resolutions. Available in a 1.3GHz dual-core MediaTek processor variation. It also avails a rear camera of 2MP for Photo and video calling and another 0.3MP selfiecamera. It is equipped with a 3240mAhbattery. It comes in two colours which are white and black colours. The Alcatel One Touch Pop 7 price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,999 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
