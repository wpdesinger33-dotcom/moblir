The Alcatel Pop C1, released in March 2014, is a compact and budget-friendly smartphone designed for basic mobile needs. It offers a 3.5” TFT display and resolution of 320x480 pixels. Powered by a 1GHz dual-core processor. Running on Android 4.2 (Jelly Bean). The phone is equipped with a 2MP back camera. The device is powered by a removable 1300mAh battery, providing up to 6 hours and 48 minutes of talk time on 2G networks and up to 2 hours and 48 minutes on 3G networks. The Alcatel Pop C1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,999 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
