The Alcatel One Touch Pixi launched at a very cheap price in August 2013. There is a small 3.5-inch TFT display that supports the resolution of 320 x 480 pixels. The phone is packed with 1.0 GHz processors and it comprises 256 MB RAM and has 512 MB of Internal memory which can be expanded with the help of micro SD card. It has an effective rear camera of 2MP which supports 480p videos. There is a 3.5mm headphone jack, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 4.0, and a user-accessible 1300 mAh battery that offers a 13 hours’ talktime on 2G networks. It has Android 2.3 Gingerbread. The Alcatel One Touch Pixi price in Bangladesh is BDT 4,999 (Official).
