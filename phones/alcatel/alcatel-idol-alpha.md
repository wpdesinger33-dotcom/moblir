The Alcatel Idol Alpha remains a stylish and functional device that catered well to its market segment at the time of its release. It was released in June 2014, is a high-end smartphone that combines sleek design with robust functionality. It features a 4.7-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels, providing clear and vibrant visuals. This smartphone is powered by a 1.2 GHz quad-core Cortex-A7 processor. The Idol Alpha runs on Android 4.2 (Jelly Bean), which, while outdated by today’s standards, was quite capable at the time of its release. One of the standout features of the Alcatel Idol Alpha is its camera setup. It boasts a 13 MP rear camera with autofocus and LED flash, capable of recording 1080p videos at 30fps. The front camera, though modest at 1.3 MP, is sufficient for basic selfies and video calls. The phone’s design is another highlight, with a slim profile of just 7.5mm and a weight of 117.4 grams, making it both lightweight and easy to handle. The 2000 mAh non-removable battery provides decent battery life, with up to 10 hours of talk time on 3G networks. The Alcatel Idol Alpha price in Bangladesh is BDT 11,999 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
