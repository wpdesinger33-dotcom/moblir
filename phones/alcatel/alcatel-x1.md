The Alcatel X1 was discontinued but remains a budget-friendly option for those seeking a compact smartphone. It was released in June 2016, features a 5.0-inch Super AMOLED display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. It’s powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 415 chipset, which includes an octa-core CPU (4x1.4 GHz Cortex-A53 and 4x1.0 GHz Cortex-A53) and Adreno 405 GPU. In terms of photography, the Alcatel X1 sports a 13 MP primary camera with dual-LED dual-tone flash. For selfies, 5 MP front camera with an LED flash. The device runs Android 5.1 Lollipop and is equipped with a 2150 mAh non-removable battery. It weighs 140 grams and features a sleek design with Gorilla Glass 4 protection on both the front and back. The Alcatel X1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 13,999 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in White, and Black colors.
