With some little discrepancy, the Alcatel 2040D boasts 4MB of RAM and only 4MB of internal memory expandable up to 8GB through microSD. Beside, the VGA rear camera for simple photography. This device comes with an 850mAh battery. It comes with other features such as dual SIM, FM radio, loudspeaker, and vibration types. The Alcatel 2040D price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,799 (Official).
