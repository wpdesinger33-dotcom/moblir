The Helio S2 provides a reasonable blend of capabilities and functionality given its price point. It includes a 5.5-inch IPS display and is powered by a quad-core 1.3 GHz processor chip. The phone sports a 13 MP rear camera and an 8 MP front camera. Its 3150 mAh Li-po battery keeps it running throughout the day. In terms of network connectivity, the Helio S2 supports GSM, WCDMA, and LTE bands. It has a dual SIM configuration (Micro + Nano/SD Card). The phone’s dimensions are 154.5 mm x 75.6 mm x 8.15 mm, and it weighs 159 grams with the battery included. The Helio S2 in Bangladesh is BDT 14,990 for 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage.
