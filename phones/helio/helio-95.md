Helio’s latest smartphone, the Helio 95, feels like a bold step for a brand that’s trying to mix style with sensible specs, without making your wallet weep. It was launched on 5 February 2026 in Bangladesh. This model packs a 6.67″ AMOLED screen with a silky smooth 120Hz refresh rate that makes everything from scrolling to gaming look slick. Under the hood sits theMediaTek Helio G100chipset paired, so day-to-day tasks and light gaming don’t feel sluggish. Photography buffs get a 50MP IMX752 main camera and a solid 32 MP selfie shooter, while the 5000mAh battery keeps it running all day and 33W charges back up reasonably fast. This phone brings modern touches like in-display fingerprint unlock, IP64 dust and water resistance, and infrared remote support. The Helio 95 price in Bangladesh is BDT 19,999 (Official) with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage. It comes in Carbon Black and Titanium Fog.
