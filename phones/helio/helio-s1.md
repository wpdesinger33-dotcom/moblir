The Helio S1 is a smartphone that offers a blend of style and functionality. It features a 5.5-inch AMOLED display with a resolution of 1280 x 720 pixels. Under the hood, it runs on a quad-core 1.3 GHz processor and comes with 2 GB of RAM and 16 GB of internal storage, which can be expanded up to 64 GB using external storage. Keep reading this article to know about the Helio S1 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of photography, the Helio S1 boasts a 13 MP primary camera on the back and an 8 MP front-facing camera for selfies. The device is powered by a 2400 mAh Li-po battery. Additional features include dual SIM support, 4G connectivity, and Android 5.1 (Lollipop) as the operating system. The Helio S1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,990 (Official) for its 2GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage.
