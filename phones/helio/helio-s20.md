The Helio S20 is a smartphone that offers a blend of performance, visual quality, and sleek design. It features a 5.5-inch AMOLED display. Powered by a quad-core 1.9 GHz processor. On the camera front, the Helio S20 boasts a 16 MP Single camera setup on the back, capturing detailed photos and allowing for creative depth effects. For selfies, 8 MP front-facing camera. The device’s 3,000mAh battery life. Available in Gold and Grey colors, the Helio S20 combines functionality with style. The Helio S20 price in Bangladesh is BDT 20,990 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of internal storage.
