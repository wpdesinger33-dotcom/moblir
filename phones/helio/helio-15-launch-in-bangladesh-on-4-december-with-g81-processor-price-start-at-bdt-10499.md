Chinese tech giant Helio has launched another budget-friendly model in Bangladesh, the Helio 15. The company has earned a reputation for its low-budget phones in Bangladesh. The phone was launched on December 4, 2025, as revealed on its Facebook page. The Helio 15 brings a new dimension to the everyday smartphone experience. Designed for users who want smooth performance and reliable durability, the Helio 15 is packed with features that set it apart in its segment. It runs on a G81 octa-core processor, supported by 8GB (4GB Virtual) RAM with 128GB of storage. The device features a 6.75” HD+ display with a refresh rate of 120Hz, which delivers vibrant visuals, while its dual rear camera system and 8MP front camera. With a 5,000mAh battery, IP54 dust and water resistance, and a dual-view camera, the Helio 15 is ready for adventure and everyday use.
