The Helio S10 is a smartphone that was released in 2017. Powered by the Mediatek Helio P10 MT6755 chipset, it features 4 GB of RAM and 32 GB of internal storage. The phone’s 5.5-inch IPS display offers vibrant visuals with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. Keep reading this article to know about the Helio S10 price in Bangladesh and its details. Running on Android OS v7.0 (Nougat), the Helio S10 provides a seamless user interface and improved performance. Its octa-core CPU ensures smooth operation, while the Mali-T860 MP2 GPU handles tasks like 3D rendering and video playback. For photography enthusiasts, the Helio S10 offers a 13 MP rear camera and a 16 MP front camera. This device weighs 182 grams and comes in various colors. The Helio S10 price in Bangladesh is BDT 18,990 (Official) for its 4GB of RAM and 32GB of internal storage.
