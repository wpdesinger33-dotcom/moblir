Symphony Mobile’s sub-brand Helio introduced Helio S5 with some interesting features. The device comes with a 3900 mAh battery with 10W Fast Charging. The charging time is about 2.5 hours. 3 GB RAM, quad-core CPU with MediaTek chipset (unspecified version) offers decent performance for everyday usage. Light gaming should be fine but this device is not made for the high-end smooth gaming experience. The 5.99 inches full-view HD+ display is protected by a Panda Glass. We have got 32 GB internal storage and a shared MicroSD slot. The camera is decent for everyday casual photography. There are fingerprint and face ID security options. The device also comes with a notification light.
