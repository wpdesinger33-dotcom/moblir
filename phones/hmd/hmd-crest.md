The HMD Crest is a smartphone designed for self-repairability, allowing users to replace components like the battery, charging port, and display at home with a toolkit and tutorial. It features a 50 MP selfie camera, and dual rear camera 50MP main camera and 2MP depth sensor. Powered by the Unisoc T760 Octa Core Processor, it offers impressive performance with an Antutu score of over 510,000. It boasts a 6.67-inch FHD+ OLED display, a 60Hz refresh rate. The HMD Crest price in Bangladesh is BDT 18,000 (Expected) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. It comes in Midnight Blue colors.
