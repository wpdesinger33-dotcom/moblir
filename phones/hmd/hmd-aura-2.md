The HMD Aura 2 implements both powerful performance capabilities along with elegant design features in its budget-friendly smartphone range. This smartphone displays content on its 6.52-inch IPS LCDdisplayscreen which provides 576 x 1280 pixel resolution. The Unisoc SC9863A chipset equips the phone with its Octa-core CPU and IMG8322 GPU. This smartphone accepts 10W fast charging through its 5000mAhbatteryto provide rapid recharging. The HMD Aura 2 includes two camera systems comprising a primary 13 MP camera and a front-facing 5 MP camera. The IP54 rating makes the HMD Aura 2 resistant against dust and water penetration. The phone runs on Android 14. The HMD Aura 2 price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,000 (Expected) for 4GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
