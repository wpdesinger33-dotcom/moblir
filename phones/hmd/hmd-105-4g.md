The HMD 105 4G is designed to offer essential functionalities with modern enhancements, making it a practical choice for users seeking simplicity and reliability. The phone features a 2.4-inch TFT LCD display with a resolution of 240 x 320 pixels, providing clear and readable text and images. It is powered by the Unisoc T127 chipset. One of the standout features of the HMD 105 4G is its support for 4G LTE networks, ensuring better connectivity and call quality. Additionally, it includes Bluetooth 5.0 for wireless connectivity and a USB Type-C port for charging. The phone is equipped with a 1450mAh removable battery, offering long-lasting usage on a single charge. It also supports wireless FM radio and comes with a 3.5mm headphone jack for audio needs.
