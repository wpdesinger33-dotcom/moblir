The newHMDArc is an Android Smartphone. It has the Furiosa 6.52″ IPS LCD panel with a resolution of 576 x 1280 pixels. The phone is equipped with Unisoc SC9863A chipset and an octa-core processor powers the phone. The HMD Arc comes with a rear view camera of 13 Mega pixels and the front facingcamerafor taking selfies is 5 Megapixels. It has a 5000mAhbattery, with 10W fast charging capability. The HMD Arc comes in Shadow Black in a bid to deliver an Android 14 Go Edition. The HMD Arc price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,000 (Expected) for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage.
