The HMD Fusion is an innovative smartphone introduced by Human Mobile Devices (HMD) that emphasizes modularity and customization. This smartphone is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 chipset. It has a 6.56” IPS LCD display and its refresh rate is 90Hz. The phone also has a dual-rear camera setup with a 108MP main camera and a 50MP front camera. One of the standout features of the HMD Fusion is its repairability. Users can easily replace components like the display, back cover, battery, and charging port, promoting sustainability and longevity. The device runs on Android 14 and promises up to two major Android upgrades. The HMD Fusion price in Bangladesh is BDT 35,000 (Expected) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. It comes in Noir color.
