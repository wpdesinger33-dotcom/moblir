The HMD 110 4G is a feature phone designed for durability and ease of use, making it a reliable choice for those who need basic functionalities with modern connectivity. Announced in September 2024, this phone is equipped with a 2.4-inch TFT LCD display, offering a resolution of 240 x 320 pixels. It runs on the Unisoc T127 chipset. One of the standout features of the HMD 110 4G is its robust 1450mAh removable battery, which ensures extended usage throughout the day. The phone supports 4G VoLTE for better call quality and faster data speeds. It also includes a QVGA main camera, Bluetooth 5.0, and a USB Type-C port for charging. Multimedia features are well-covered with an MP3 player, wireless FM radio. Additionally, the HMD 110 4G is designed with user comfort in mind, featuring a lightweight body at 93.5 grams and dual SIM support. It also offers practical tools like a flashlight and is splash-resistant, making it a versatile and practical choice for everyday use.
