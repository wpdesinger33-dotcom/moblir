The HMD Skyline was launched in the market in July 2024 . It is utilizing a 6.55-inch P-OLED display with a 144Hz refresh rate. This device offers a display resolution of 1080 x 2400 pixels of resolution.  The HMD Skyline is backed by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 chipset and Adreno 710 GPU. An HMD Skyline has a rear panel with triple rear camera configuration with 108 MP main sensor, 50 MP Telephoto lens with 2x zoom and 13 MP ultrawide lens. The front camera is a 50MP sensor. The phone comes with the rear and front cameras that support 4K video recording at 30fps and 1080p at 30fps. The battery is also extraordinary with a capacity of 4600mAh charging via a 33W wired, 15W wireless, and it also supports 5W reverse wireless charging. The HMD Skyline price in Bangladesh is BDT 60,000 (Unofficial) for 8GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage.
