The HMD Hyper is an upcoming Android smartphone from HMD Global. Although not officially announced yet, leaked details suggest an intriguing mid-range device. The design draws inspiration from Lumia phones, featuring a punch-hole cutout on the front. Keep reading this article to know about the HMD Hyper price in Bangladesh and its details. This phone comes with an OLED screen with FHD+ resolution and a smooth 120Hz refresh rate. It will be powered by Qualcomm’s Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 chipset, paired with 8GB of RAM. The device offers 256GB of internal storage, expandable via a microSD card slot. On the back, expect a 50MP main camera with optical image stabilization (OIS), along with unspecified 13MP and 8MP shooters. The front houses another 50MP sensor within the punch-hole cutout. A 4,700 mAh battery fuels the HMD Hyper, supporting 33W fast charging. The HMD Hyper price in Bangladesh is BDT 22,500 (Expected) for its 8GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage.
