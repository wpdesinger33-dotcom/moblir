Doogee V Max Plus is one of the rugged smartphones that is made with a blend of toughness and performance. It is equipped with a very large battery of 22,000 mAh. Equipped with the Dimensity 7050 an octa-core CPU. The device comes with the most current Android 14 Operating System. It has a waterdrop screen with FHD+ 120Hz and 6.58 inches in dimension. Is also rather powerful and consists of a 200MP ultra-clear main camera. Also, it has support for 33W fast charging. The Doogee V Max Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 70,000 (Expected) for 16GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage. It comes in Obsidian Black, and Pewter Gray colors.
