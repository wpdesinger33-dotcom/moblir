The Doogee V30T provides users with a smartphone offering dual purposes of rugged construction alongside high performance abilities. This phone comes with a 6.58-inch IPS LCD screen that offers 1080 x 2408 pixel resolution with a 120Hz refresh rate. The device operates under the MediaTek Dimensity 1080 chipset. The phone runs on Android 12. This smartphone model comes with three back cameras that include a 108MP primary lens and dual 20MP night vision and 16MP ultra-wide sensors. The Doogee V30T offers a 32MP sensor as its frontcamera. This device carries a 10,800mAhbatterywhich allows 66W wired charging and 15W wireless charging capabilities. The rugged phone design provides IP68/IP69K dust and water resistance together with MIL-STD-810H drop resistance certification. The device comes in Marble Black, Rose Gold and Galaxy Gray color options. The Doogee V30T price in Bangladesh is BDT 50,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage.
