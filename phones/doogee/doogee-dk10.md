The Doogee DK10 is a high performing smartphone that has been made to be resistant to extreme conditions. They are equipped with a 6,67-inch AMOLED screen with a refresh rate of 120Hz and the Corning Gorilla Glass covering. Equipped with the MediaTek Dimensity 8020 system on a chip. On the back of the DK10 it has four cameras: the main camera with a 50-megapixel primary camera, a telephoto lens of 16 megapixels with 4x optical zoom, the ultrawide lens with 50 megapixels, and the night vision camera of 64 megapixels. The 50MP front camera. It has 5150mAh battery with fast charging of 120W through the wire and 50W through wireless charging. The phone is MIL-STD-810H certified, IP68 water and dust resistant, and can survive drops from up to 1.8m, so it’s perfect for the great outdoors. Running on Android 13.
