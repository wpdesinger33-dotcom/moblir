The V40 Pro has a large battery of 8680mAh and the battery also supports 33W fast charging. It is in IP68/IP69K with dust and water resistance. The Doogee V40 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 70,000 (Expected) with 16GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage. It comes in Blue, and Silver colors.
