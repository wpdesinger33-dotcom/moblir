The Doogee Blade10 Max is in development now as a rugged smartphone. It has an IPS LCD, 6.56 inches with both touch and the corning gorilla glass, 720×1612 pixels, and 90 Hz refresh rate. Equipped with Unisoc T606 SoC. Perhaps, the most striking feature of the Blade10 Max is a truly enormous battery 10,300mAh. It is an Android 14 phone and comes with dual SIM capability. For photography, it has a 50MP for the main camera and the 8MP for the front camera. Other trimmings include a side-mounted fingerprint scanner, NFC, and a second tiny 1.54-inch screen for notifications. The Doogee Blade10 Max price in Bangladesh is BDT 40,000 (Expected) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
