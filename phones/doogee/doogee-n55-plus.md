The Doogee N55 Plus serves as an affordable smartphone which merges performance elements with cost-efficient features. The device comes with a 6.56-inch IPS LCDdisplaythat supports 720 x 1612 pixel resolution at a 90Hz refresh rate. The smartphone operates with the Unisoc T606 chipset. The phone runs on Android 14. The Doogee N55 Plus features one 13MP camera on its rear side. The front of the device has an 8MPcamerasensor. The device carries a 5150mAhbatterythat enables users to charge it through a 10W wired connection. In terms of connectivity, the Doogee N55 Plus supports Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac, Bluetooth 5.0, and USB Type-C 2.01. Its elegant build consists of a glass front combined with plastic back and aluminum frame which creates an upscale appearance. The device comes in two color variations including Phantom Black and Lightweave Silver. The Doogee N55 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 35,000 (Expected) for 8GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage.
