The Doogee V Max Pro smartphone is built with a higher level of functionality and durability than other phones to cater for the needs of users who need durability and high performance. It includes a huge battery of 22,000mAh. The phone runs on an octa-core MediaTek Dimensity 7050 processor that supports connectivity with the 5G network. The Doogee V Max Pro comes with a 6.58 inches IPS LCD panel with 120Hz refresh rate. There is Corning Gorilla Glass 5 to protect the screen. The phone has a three-lens system back camera that consists of 108 mp main camera, 20 mp night vision camera with infrared light and 8 mp ultra wide lens. Front facing camera is 16MP. It is built on Android 14 and has Doogee’s own OS 4.0 on top. The Doogee V Max Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 70,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage.
