The Doogee Blade10 Ultra is a smartphone that has been built with powerful and durable features. It comes with an IPS LCD screen of the size of 6.56-inches with 720 x 1612 pixels resolution and a screen refresh rate of 90 Hz. As for design solutions, the Blade10 Ultra has IP68/IP69K protection against dust and water ingress. The back of the phone has a single camera that is a 50MP main camera. The built-in battery is 5150mAh, and now the device supports 18W fast charging. The Doogee Blade10 Ultra price in Bangladesh is BDT 35,000 (Expected) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
