The FreeYond F9 is an affordable smartphone introduced by the Chinese startup FreeYond. It features a 6.52-inch IPS display with a resolution of 720 x 1600 pixels, providing a bright and clear visual experience. The device is powered by the Unisoc SC9863A1 chipset, which includes an octa-core CPU composed of four 1.6 GHz ARM Cortex-A55 cores and four 1.2 GHz ARM Cortex-A55 cores, along with a PowerVR GE8322 GPU. This configuration ensures smooth performance for everyday tasks and basic gaming. Running on Android 12 Go edition. It is equipped with a 13 MP rear camera for capturing decent photos and an 8 MP front camera for selfies. It houses a substantial 5000 mAh battery, ensuring long-lasting usage on a single charge. The phone’s dimensions are 163 x 75.2 x 8.7 mm, and it weighs 185 grams, making it relatively lightweight and easy to handle. The FreeYond F9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 11,499 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. Available in blue and black colors.
