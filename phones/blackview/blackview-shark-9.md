The Blackview Shark 9 is a rugged smartphone with focus on the durability and efficacy of the system. The device boasts of 6.67 inches IPS LCD, with a resolution of 720 x 1604 pixels of display and a 90Hz refresh rate. It has a Unisoc T820 anchored by an octa-core CPU and the Mali-G57 GPU. The Shark 9 digital weighing alleviator comes with a strong construction coupled with IP68/IP69K dust and water resistant feature. The smartphone boasts a triple camera setup at the back that boasts a 50 MP primary sensor, 2 MP macro, and a 2 MP depth sensor on the back end. Battery is 5000mAh and the device is capable of fast charging at 18W. The Blackview Shark 9  price in Bangladesh is BDT 20,000 (Expected) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
