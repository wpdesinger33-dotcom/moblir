The Blackview BL9000 Pro is a rugged smartphone designed to withstand harsh environments while offering high-end features. This device boasts a 6.78-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 2460 pixels, protected by Corning Gorilla Glass Victus. It runs on Android 14 with Doke-OS 4.0, powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 8020 chipset. One of the standout features of the BL9000 Pro is its advanced camera system. It includes a triple rear camera setup with a 50MP main sensor, a 13MP ultrawide lens. The front camera is also impressive, featuring a 50MP sensor capable of capturing high-quality selfies and video at 1080p. The phone’s ruggedness is certified by its IP68/IP69K dust and water resistance and MIL-STD-810H compliance. The BL9000 Pro also excels in battery life and charging capabilities, featuring an 8800mAh battery that supports 120W fast charging and 5W reverse wired charging. The Blackview BL9000 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 50,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage. It comes in Splendid Gold, and Interstellar Black colors.
