This rugged smartphone from Blackview N6000 provides users with features to support their need for dependable sturdy products. The device operates using a 4.3-inch IPS LCD display which delivers 540 x 1200 pixel resolution. The N6000 possesses strong durability because it possesses IP68 and IP69K certifications which ensure complete dust protection and full water resistance. The N6000 device spans 208 grams through an 18.4mm thickness profile. Inside the Blackview N6000 users will find the MediaTek Helio G99 integrated as its processing chipset. A sleek software interface powered by Android 13 delivers smooth operations as well as delightful usability features. Through its dual camera system of 48MP primary camera and 16MP front-facing camera the N6000 delivers reliable photography functionality for standard pictures and selfies. Fast-charging at 18W is enabled by the 3880mAh battery in this model. The Blackview N6000 price in Bangladesh is BDT 38,900 (Unofficial) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
