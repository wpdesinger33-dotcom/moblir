The Blackview BV8200 exists as an industrial smartphone that brings together reliability with powerful performance capabilities. The phone delivers visual excellence through its IPS LCDdisplaywith 1080 x 2400 pixels resolution across its 6.5-inch display. The smartphone display operates at 120Hz refresh rate with protection from Corning Gorilla Glass 5. Inside the BV8200 runs the MediaTek Helio G100 processor equipped with an octa-core CPU along with Mali-G57 MC2 GPU. The device operates on Android 14 together with Doke OS 4.0. The BV8200 combines dual rear cameras having a 50MP wide-angle main lens and a 13MP ultra-wide lens and a 32MP front camera. The BV8200 stands out with its 8800mAhbatterythat allows 45W fast wired charging together with 5W reverse wired charging. The Blackview BV8200 price in Bangladesh is BDT 30,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
