The Blackview Wave 8 emerged on the market in November 2024 bringing together smartphone durability and high-performance capabilities. Users obtain 720x1612 pixel resolution from its 6.56-inch IPS LCDdisplay. The device operates on Unisoc T606 chipset together with its octa-core CPU alongside Mali-G57 MP1 GPU configuration. The Blackview Wave 8 delivers a 5000mAh battery alongside its 10W fast charging capability. The smartphone combines 13MP rear and 8MP frontcameramodules. DokeOS 4.0 operates with Android 14 to create a user-friendly interface on the phone device. Available in colors like Sunset Purple, Dawn Blue, and Dusk Black. The Blackview Wave 8 price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,000 (Expected) for 4GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
