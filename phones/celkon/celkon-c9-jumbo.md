The Celkon C9 Jumbo arrived on the market during 2014 as a feature phone which serves basic communication functions. The Celkon C9 Jumbo shows its display on a 2.4-inch TFT interface that delivers 240 x 320 pixels resolution. It has a built-in 1.3MP maincamera. The device provides dual SIM capabilities through which users can operate two phone numbers at once. It includes a 2000mAh removablebattery. The Celkon C9 Jumbo connects through 2G networks and operates with Bluetooth 2.0 and microUSB 2.0. This model has an FM radio together with a loudspeaker function and a 3.5mm audio jack that allows users to listen to music and radio. This phone comes with a plastic construction and users can choose between black/red, black/blue and white/red color options. The Celkon C9 Jumbo price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,800 (Unofficial).
