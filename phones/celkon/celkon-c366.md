The Celkon C366 serves as a basic feature phone designed to meet communication requirements because it launched in January 2014. The device includes a 1.8-inch TFTdisplaywhich offers 128 x 160 pixels resolution. The phone contains a 1.3MP primarycamera. Users can operate two phone numbers at once thanks to the dual SIM feature of this device. Users can replace the 1000mAh battery that comes with the Celkon C366. The Celkon C366 maintains connection through its 2G networks and Bluetooth 2.0 and microUSB 2.0 features. The device comes equipped with an FM radio as well as loudspeaker functionality and supports music and radio playback through its 3.5mm audio jack. The plastic build of the phone can be found in black, blue, red and white color options. The Celkon C366 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,000 (Unofficial).
