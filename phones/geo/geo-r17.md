Geo R17 is a feature phone which has the functionality of a traditional phone but at a very low price. Launching in Bangladesh in June 2024. It has a 2.4 Inch QVGAdisplay. The phone has two SIM card slots hence the user can easily operate the two numbers at the same time. It has a QVGAcamera, wireless FM radio with record option and micro SD card slot supported up to 32 GB. The battery capacity in Geo R17 is a removable 2000 mAhbattery. It also has Bluetooth for transferring files, 3.5mm headphone jack and a micro USB port as well. Available in colors such as black, blue and grey. The Geo R17 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,200 (Official).
