The Geo R44 is a conventional and basic mobile phone with functionality. Available in Bangladesh market from June, 2024. It comes with a 2.4 inch QVGAdisplay. The phone has dual SIM card slots whereby a user can use two lines at the same time. The device offers a 0.03MPcamera, wireless FM radio with recording facility and a micro SD card slot for external memory up to 32GB. The Geo R44 is run by a rechargeable 1000 mAhbattery. It also supports Bluetooth connectivity and also has a 3.5 mm headphone jack and micro USB port for the ease of file transfers. It comes in colours such as Magic Red, Midnight Blue, and Olive Green. The Geo R44 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,425 (Official) for 16MB of RAM and 16 MB of phone storage.
