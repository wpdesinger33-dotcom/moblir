The Geo R27 is a mobile phone which is mainly a feature phone, can be described as practical and affordable. Available in September 2024, it has a 2.4-inch TFT LCDdisplay. The phone comes with a dual SIM slot meaning the user can have two different numbers at the same time. It is equipped with a 0.03camerafor shooting moments, Wireless FM Radio with recording and Micro SD Card Slot expandable up to 32 GB. It has a removablebatteryof 1200 mAh. Phone available in black, blue and green. The Geo R27 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,280 (Official).
