The Geo R23 is a feature phone with some practicality as well as affordability. It was launched in Bangladesh in June 2024. It also has a small 2.4 inches QVGdisplay. It is dual SIM support which enables one to run two different numbers at the same time. It has VGAcameraand Wireless FM Radio with recording, and has a dedicated micro Card Slot for external Storage up to 32 GB. The Geo R23 is built with a removable 2500 mAhbattery. It also has Bluetooth capability, 3.5 mm headphone jack, and has a micro USB port for an easier sharing of files. Available in colours like Red Blue and Green. The Geo R23 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,350 (Official) for 16MB of RAM and 16MB of phone storage.
