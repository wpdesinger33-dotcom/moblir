The Geo R40 is a model of a feature phone, which was released on 22 June 2024 in Bangladesh. It has a 2.4 inch QVGAdisplayand could handle up to four SIMs at the same time. The phone has an internal memory of 32 MB and the phone is capable of supporting up to 500 contacts. Further, it also has an external memory slot on a microSD card up to 32 GB for additional memory. Geo R40 has a 2500mAhbatterywhich gives talk time up to 15 hours and Standby time up to 620 hours. Incorporated in the phone is 0.03camera, auto call recording, wireless FM radio, MP3-MP4 player and a very big torch light. The Geo R40 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,700 (Official).
