This phone model is called Geo R25 and it is a basic model of a feature phone. It has adisplaysolution which is 2.4 inches QVGA and has an optimum resolution of 240 x 320 pixels. It also allows for the total utilization of two sim cards, thus, the ability to use two different numbers on one phone. It has a 0.03MP Digital zoomCamera. It is powered by a 1200 mAh removablebattery. The connectivity feature supported comprises Bluetooth and USB Type C making it easier to transfer data from one device to another. The features of the phone also include loudspeaker, 3.5mm jack, and an FM radio with the feature of recording. The Geo R25 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,100 (Official).
