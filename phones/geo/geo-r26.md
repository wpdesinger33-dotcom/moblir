The Geo R26 is a feature phone which is quite practical and cost effective. Available in Bangladesh from September 2024. It has the capacity of 2.4 inch TFT LCD in builtdisplay. The device features dual SIM operation that enables users to handle two numbers on a single device. It is fitted with a 0.03camera, an FM wireless radio with recording option, and a micro SD card slot that supports up to 32 GB storage. The Geo R26 is kept running by a 2000mAh removablebattery. It also features Bluetooth technology and micro USB, 3.5 mm headphone jack for sharing of files. Comes in styles with black, blue and green as some of the readily available colors. The Geo R26 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,390 (Official).
