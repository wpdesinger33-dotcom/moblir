The Geo R2 is an entry-level non-internet connected handset that was launched in June, 2024 in Bangladesh. It has a 1.77 inches QVGAdisplaywith resolution of 128 x 160 pixels. It has the functionalities of the dual SIM meaning one can have two phone numbers on the same phone. Geo R2 comes with a built-in 1000mAh, Li-ion non removablebattery. Bluetooth and USB Type-C are the available connectivity options. In addition, this phone has got the loudspeaker, 3.5mm jack and the built-in FM radio with recording function. The Geo R2 price in Bangladesh is BDT 980 (Official).
