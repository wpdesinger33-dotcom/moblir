The Coolpad Legacy SR is an entry-level smartphone available through Boost Mobile. It was released in July, 2020. It offers a 5.45” TFT LCD display with a resolution of 720 × 1440 pixels and an 18:9 aspect ratio. The phone runs on Android 9.0 (Pie) and is powered by the MediaTek MT6761 Helio A22 processor. The 2,450mAh battery provides up to 17 hours of calling, texting, and entertainment, ensuring it lasts throughout the day and into the night. Camera-wise, the phone features an 8MP rear-facing camera and a 5MP front-facing camera. The Coolpad Legacy SR price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,000 (Unofficial) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of ROM. But it has now not available in the Bangladesh market. It comes in Steel color.
