Coolpad Legacy was released in May 2019. It is a mid-range device with a modern design. The Legacy runs Android 9.0 (Pie) and has a 6.36 inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 2160 pixels. The screen has an 18:9 aspect ratio and a pixel density of approximately 380 PPI. The dual rear camera system includes a 16MP primary camera and a 5MP secondary camera, along with an LED flash. A 13MP front-facing camera . Video can be recorded at 1080p resolution and 30 frames per second. Powering the Legacy is a 1.8GHz quad-core Cortex-A53 processor and Qualcomm's Snapdragon 450 (14nm) chipset. Connectivity features include 4G LTE, Wi-Fi 5 (802.11 a/b/g/n/ac) (dual-band), Bluetooth 4.2, MicroUSB, Wi-Fi Direct, and mobile hotspot capabilities. A 4000mAh battery provides power. The Coolpad Legacy price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,000 (official) for 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage. But it has now not available in the Bangladesh market. It comes in Silver color.
