The Coolpad Cool 5, announced in October 2019, is a budget-friendly Android smartphone. It features a 6.22-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1520 pixels, resulting in a pixel density of approximately 270 ppi. The screen-to-body ratio is around 80.9%, providing a decent viewing experience. Keep reading this article to know about the Coolpad Cool 5 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of performance, the Cool 5 is powered by the MediaTek Helio P22 chipset, which includes an octa-core CPU clocked at 2.0 GHz (Cortex-A53 cores) and a PowerVR GE8320 GPU. It comes with 4 GB of RAM and 64 GB of internal storage (expandable via microSDXC card) using eMMC 5.1 technology. It comes with a dual-camera setup on the rear. The primary camera is a 13 MP sensor with autofocus, accompanied by a 2 MP secondary sensor. Features include LED flash, panorama, and HDR. For selfies, there’s a 16 MP front-facing camera with an f/2.2 aperture and a wide-angle lens.
