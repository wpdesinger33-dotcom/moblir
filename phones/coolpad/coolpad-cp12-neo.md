The Coolpad CP12 Neo is a simple and budget-friendly smartphone made for daily use. It was released on 30 January 2026 in Bangladesh. This smartphone has a 6.56-inch IPS LCD and 120Hzrefresh rateand Gorilla Glass protection. It has a Unisoc SC9863A1 octa-core processor in-house. The main camera with 13MP and a small secondary sensor and a 5MP selfie shooter can take good photos to post on social media, and the 1080p video can preserve memories clear enough. The CP12 Neo has a 5000mAhbattery, 10W charger, fingerprint sensor on the side, FM radio, dual SIM, and Android with CoolOS on top, which makes it feel like a well-considered low-end package.  The Coolpad CP12 Neo price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,500 (Official) for 4GB RAM and 64GB storage. It comes in Platinum Black, Dream Blue, Cherry Pink, and Space Gray colors.
