Coolpad released a budget smartphone called the Cool 3 Plus. Despite its affordable price, the device offers impressive features. It runs Android 9.0 (Pie) and has a 5.71 inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1520 pixels and a pixel density of 295 PPI. The Cool 3 Plus is equipped with a 13MP rear camera that includes an LED flash. The front-facing camera has 8MP. Video can be recorded at 1080p resolution and 30 frames per second. Powering the phone is a 2.0 GHz quad-core Cortex-A53 processor and a Mediatek MT6761 Helio A22 chipset built on a 12 nm process. Connectivity options consist of 4G LTE, Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n), Bluetooth 4.2, MicroUSB, Wi-Fi Direct, mobile hotspot and more. A 3000 mAh lithium-polymer battery. The Coolpad Cool 3 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 7,500 (official) for 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage. But it has now not available in the Bangladesh market. It comes in Ocean Blue, and Cherry Black colors.
