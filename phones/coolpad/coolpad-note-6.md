The Coolpad Note 6, released in May 2018, features a 5.5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. It runs on Android 7.1 Nougat and is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 435 chipset, which includes an octa-core CPU (4x 1.4 GHz Cortex-A53 and 4x 1.1 GHz Cortex-A53) and the Adreno 505 GPU. Keep reading this article Coolpad Note 6 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of photography, the Coolpad Note 6 boasts a 13 MP primary camera with autofocus and LED flash. For selfies, it offers a dual front camera setup: an 8 MP lens paired with a 5 MP depth sensor. The device also includes a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor for added security. Powering the Coolpad Note 6 is a non-removable 4070 mAh Li-Ion battery. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n), Bluetooth 4.0, GPS, and FM radio. However, it lacks NFC support. The phone is available in a gold color variant. The Coolpad Note 6 price in Bangladesh is BDT 11,400 (Official) for its 4GB of RAM and 64GB of internal storage.
