The Coolpad Cool 2 is an Android smartphone that was announced in April 2018. It features a 5.7-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1440 pixels, offering a decent visual experience. The phone is powered by the MediaTek MT6750 chipset, which includes an octa-core processor (4x1.5 GHz Cortex-A53 and 4x1.0 GHz Cortex-A53) and an ARM Mali-T860 MP2 GPU clocked at 520 MHz. Keep reading this article to know about the Coolpad Cool 2 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of photography, the Coolpad Cool 2 boasts a dual-camera setup on the back: a 13 MP primary camera and a 0.3 MP depth sensor. The rear cameras support features like LED flash, panorama, and HDR, allowing users to capture decent photos. For selfies, there’s an 8 MP front-facing camera. The phone runs on Android 7.0 Nougat, which might feel a bit dated considering the current Android versions available. However, it still provides a functional user experience. The Coolpad Cool 2 also features a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor, accelerometer, and proximity sensor.
