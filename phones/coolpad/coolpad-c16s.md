The Coolpad C16s is one of the affordable smartphones. It comes with a 6.75 inches IPS LCD and has a pixel density of 271 PPI with 720 X 1600 pixels resolution. The Helio G36 processor, an octa-core chip and PowerVR GE8320 GPU. The Coolpad C16s has 4GB RAM and 128GB built-in storage but can utilize external microSD card storage. This one is equipped with a set of two 50MP default cameras with 2MP macro camera, the frontal 8MP camera. The phone comes with 5000mAh battery capacity and only 10W wired charging is supported. Other features include; Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac, Bluetooth v5.0 and NFC for wireless communications. The device is available in three stylish colors: Midnight Black, Woodland Green and Sunset Orange. The Coolpad C16s price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,000 (Expected).
