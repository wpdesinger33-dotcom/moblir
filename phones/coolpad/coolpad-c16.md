The Coolpad C16 is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers a balanced mix of features for everyday use. It sports a 6.75-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1600 pixels and a 90Hz refresh rate, providing a smooth visual experience. The device is powered by the Unisoc T606 chipset. Running on Android 14, the Coolpad C16 ensures a modern software experience with the latest features and security updates. In terms of photography, the Coolpad C16 is equipped with a dual-camera setup on the back, featuring a 50MP primary sensor and a 2MP macro lens. For selfies and video calls, there is an 8MP front-facing camera. The device also includes a side-mounted fingerprint sensor for added security and convenience. One of the standout features of the Coolpad C16 is its large 5000mAh battery, which supports 10W charging. The Coolpad C16 price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,000 (Expected) for 4GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. The phone is available in three colors: Midnight Black, Woodland Green, and Gulf Blue, catering to different aesthetic preferences.
