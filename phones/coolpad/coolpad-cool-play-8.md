The Coolpad Cool Play 8, released in December 2018, features a 6.2-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 2246 pixels. Its slim profile measures 155.5 x 74.8 x 8.5 mm, weighing 177 grams. The phone runs on Android 8 (Oreo) with Cool UI 9. Keep reading this article to know about the Coolpad Cool Play 8 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of performance, it packs a MediaTek MT6750 chipset with an octa-core CPU (4x1.5 GHz Cortex-A53 and 4x1.0 GHz Cortex-A53) and a Mali-T860MP2 GPU. The device offers 4 GB of RAM and 64 GB of internal storage (expandable via microSDXC). For photography, the Cool Play 8 boasts a dual rear camera setup: a 16 MP main camera with autofocus and a 2 MP depth sensor. The front-facing camera is an 8 MP shooter. It supports 1080p video recording at 30 fps. Other notable features include a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor, accelerometer, proximity sensor, and a 4000 mAh non-removable battery with 10W wired charging. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.2, GPS, GLONASS, BDS, and FM radio. The phone uses a USB Type-C 2.0 port. Available in Black and Red colors, the Coolpad Cool Play 8 offers a budget-friendly option for users seeking a decent smartphone experience. The Coolpad Cool Play 8 price in Bangladesh is BDT 13,990 for its 4GB of RAM and 64GB of internal storage. But it has now not available in the Bangladesh market.
