The Coolpad X100 smartphone delivers an impressive combination of advanced features along with elegant design alongside powerful capabilities. This device arrives with a 6.78-inch IPS LCDdisplaythat uses 1080 x 2460 pixel resolution and includes a 120Hz refresh rate. The front display uses Gorilla Glass for protection. The Coolpad X100 has a three-camera system on its back which includes an 108MP main sensor and two additional lenses consisting of a 5MP ultrawide and a 2MP macro sensor. The 32MP front camera. The device operates using the MediaTek Helio G99 chipset. The Coolpad X100 operates under Android 14 through CoolOS. The Coolpad X100 includes a 5000mAhbatterywhich supports 33W fast charging technology. The device comes in four color options such as Starlit Black, Polar Silver, Palm Blue and Fairy Pink. The Coolpad X100 price in Bangladesh is BDT 21,990 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
