The Coolpad Mega 5A is a budget-friendly Android smartphone that was announced in August 2018. It features a 5.45-inch display with HD+ resolution. Under the hood, it runs on a Spreadtrum SC9850K quad-core processor, paired with 2GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage. The phone comes with Android 8.1 Oreo, customized with a few tweaks by Coolpad. Keep reading this article to know about the Coolpad Mega 5A price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of camera capabilities, the Mega 5A sports a dual-camera setup on the back, consisting of an 8 MP primary camera with an f/2.0 aperture and PDAF, along with a 0.3 MP depth sensor. For selfies, there’s a 5 MP front camera with an LED flash. This phone is powered by a 2500 mAh non-removable battery. It also includes features like a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor, accelerometer, and support for Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n and Bluetooth 4.0. However, it lacks 4G LTE support.
