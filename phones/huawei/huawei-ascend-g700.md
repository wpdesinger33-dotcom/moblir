The Huawei Ascend G700, released in 2013, is a mid-range smartphone that offers a solid balance of performance and features. It comes with a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720x1280 pixels. The Ascend G700 is powered by a 1.2 GHz quad-core MediaTek MT6589 processor. Runs on Android 4.2 (Jelly Bean). The device features an 8 MP rear camera with autofocus and LED flash, capable of recording 1080p videos, and a 1.3 MP front camera for selfies and video calls. The phone is equipped with a 2150 mAh battery. The Huawei Ascend G700 price in Bangladesh is BDT 17,500 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
