The Huawei G Play Mini is a low-range smartphone that offers a balanced mix of performance and features. It is powered by the HiSilicon Kirin 620 octa-core processor, clocked at 1.2 GHz. The 5-inch IPS display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. In terms of camera capabilities, the Huawei G Play Mini features a 13 MP rear camera with autofocus and LED flash, capable of capturing detailed photos and 1080p videos. The 5 MP selfie camera. It runs on Android 4.4.2 KitKat OS. The 2550 mAh battery provides sufficient power to last through a typical day of usage. The design of the Huawei G Play Mini is practical, with dimensions of 143.3 x 71.9 x 8.8 mm and a weight of 162 grams, making it comfortable to hold and use. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 4.0, GPS, and micro USB 2.0. The Huawei G Play Mini price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,490 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. It comes in Black, White, and Gold colors.
