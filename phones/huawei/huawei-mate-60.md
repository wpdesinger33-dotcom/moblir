Huawei Mate 60 is one of the latest mobile phones that was launched in the market on 10 September 2023. Equipped with a 6.69-inch LTPO OLED screen, the pixel density of 1216x2688 pixels. It is running on Kirin 9000S chipset, octa-core CPU and Maleoon 910 MP4. There is a 4750mAh battery supporting 66W fast wired charging and 50W wireless fast charging. It also has a 50MP main camera, a 12MP periscope telephoto lens and a 12MP ultra-wide-angle lens. This phone has a 13MP selfie camera. It was released with HarmonyOS 4.0 and it is also offered in Black, White, Green, and Purple lineup. The Huawei Mate 60 price in Bangladesh is BDT 130,000 (Unofficial) for 12GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage.
