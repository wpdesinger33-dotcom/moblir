The Huawei P9, released in 2016, is an Android smartphone that builds upon the design of its predecessor, the Huawei P8. While maintaining a similar appearance, the P9 introduces a significant upgrade: a dual-camera setup co-engineered with Leica. This collaboration enhances photography capabilities, particularly in low-light conditions. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei P9 price in Bangladesh and its details. It features a sleek design with a glass front (protected by Corning Gorilla Glass 3), an aluminum back, and an aluminum frame. Its compact dimensions—145 x 70.9 x 7 mm—make it comfortable to hold. The 5.2-inch IPS-NEO LCD display offers a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels, resulting in a pixel density of approximately 423 ppi. In terms of performance, it  runs on Android 6.0 (upgradable to 7.0) with EMUI 4.1. It houses a Kirin 955 chipset (16 nm) with an octa-core CPU (4x2.5 GHz Cortex-A72 & 4x1.8 GHz Cortex-A53) and a Mali-T880 MP4 GPU. The standout feature of the P9 is its dual-camera system. Its primary camera consists of a 12 MP color sensor (f/2.2 aperture) and a 12 MP monochrome sensor (also f/2.2 aperture).
