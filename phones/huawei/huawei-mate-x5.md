The Huawei Mate X5 competes in the foldable market with its impressive specs and innovative design. It was released in September 2024. It features a large 7.85-inch OLED display with a resolution of 2224 x 2496 pixels. The phone runs on HarmonyOS 4.0 (in China) or EMUI (in Europe), depending on the region, and lacks Google Play Services. Under the hood, it’s powered by the Kirin 9000S chipset, which is built on a 7nm process. In terms of photography, the Mate X5 boasts a 50MP primary camera with f/1.8 aperture, a 12MP periscope telephoto lens with 5x optical zoom, and a 13MP ultrawide lens. For selfies, there’s an 8MP front camera. The phone also supports stylus input on both displays. The battery capacity is 5060mAh, and it supports 66W wired charging and 50W wireless charging. The Huawei Mate X5 price in Bangladesh is BDT 195,000 (Unofficial) for 16GB of RAM and 512GB of ROM. It comes in Black, White, Purple, Dark Green, and Gold colors.
