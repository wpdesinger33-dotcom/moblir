The Huawei Ascend Y300, released in March 2013, is an entry-level smartphone that offers a balance of basic functionality and affordability. It features a 4.0” IPS LCD display with a resolution of 480 x 800 pixels. The device is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon S4 Play chipset, with a dual-core 1.0 GHz Cortex-A5 CPU and Adreno 203 GPU. The phone runs on Android 4.1 Jelly Bean, overlaid with Huawei’s Emotion UI 1.0. The 5MP rear camera, equipped with autofocus and LED flash, while the VGA front camera is suitable for basic video calls. The phone’s design is straightforward, with a plastic body that feels solid in hand. It includes a removable 1730 mAh battery. The Huawei Ascend Y300 price in Bangladesh is BDT 5,000 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage. It comes in Black, and White colors.
