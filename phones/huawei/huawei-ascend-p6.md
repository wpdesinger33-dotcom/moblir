The Huawei Ascend P6, released in June 2013, was a standout in the smartphone market for its time, primarily due to its ultra-slim design. Measuring just 6.2mm in thickness and weighing 120 grams. The device featured a 4.7-inch IPS+ LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The Ascend P6 ran on Android 4.2.2 Jelly Bean, upgradable to 4.4.2 KitKat, with Huawei’s EMUI 1.6 skin on top, providing a user-friendly interface. Under the hood, the Ascend P6 was powered by a quad-core 1.5 GHz Cortex-A9 processor. The phone’s camera capabilities were notable, featuring an 8MP rear camera with LED flash and HDR, capable of recording 1080p videos, and a 5MP front camera, which was quite advanced for selfies at the time. The Huawei Ascend P6 price in Bangladesh is BDT 24,990 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
