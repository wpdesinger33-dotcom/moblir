The Huawei Mate 70 Pro Plus is the pinnacle of Huawei's smartphone lineup. It features a 6.9-inch LTPO OLED display with a resolution of 1316x2832 pixels and a 120Hz refresh rate. The phone is powered by the Kirin 9020 chipset. The Mate 70 Pro Plus boasts a triple-camerasetup on the back, including a 50MP main sensor, a 40MP ultra-wide lens, a 48MP periscope telephoto lens. The front camera features a 13MP selfie shooter. The device includes a 5,700mAhbatterywith 100W wired charging, 80W wireless charging, and 20W reverse wireless charging. Other notable features include an in-display fingerprint sensor, IP68/IP69 dust and water resistance, and HarmonyOS 4.3 as the operating system. It is available in colors like Black Ink, Flying Azure, Gold Silk, and Silver Brocade. The Huawei Mate 70 Pro Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 220,000 (Unofficial) for 16GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage.
