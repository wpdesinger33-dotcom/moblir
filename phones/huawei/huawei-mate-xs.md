The Huawei Mate Xs is a remarkable foldable smartphone that was released in March 2020. It is a foldable OLED display that measures 8.0 inches when unfolded, with a screen-to-body ratio and a high resolution of 2200x2480 pixels. When folded, the device presents a practical 6.6-inch cover display. The Mate Xs is powered by the Kirin 990 5G chipset, which is built on a 7nm+ process and includes an integrated 5G modem. In terms of storage and memory, the Mate Xs comes with a generous 512GB of internal storage paired with 8GB of RAM. It features a triple camera system with a 40MP main sensor, an 8MP telephoto lens with 3x optical zoom, and a 16MP ultrawide lens. It is a robust 4500mAh battery supported by 55W fast charging, which promises to recharge the battery to 85% in just 30 minutes. The device’s build quality is enhanced by a new Falcon Wing hinge design and a quad-layer construction that aims to improve durability.
