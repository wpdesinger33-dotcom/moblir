The Huawei Y3 II is a budget-friendly smartphone in Bangladesh. It was released in June 2016, features a 4.5-inch TFT display with a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels. Runs on Android 5.1 Lollipop with Huawei’s EMUI 3.1 interface. Under the hood, the Y3 II is powered by a quad-core MediaTek processor, with two variants: the MT6735M for the 4G model and the MT6582M for the 3G model. In terms of photography, the Huawei Y3 II is equipped with a 5 MP rear camera featuring autofocus and dual-LED flash, capable of recording 1080p videos. The front camera is a basic 2 MP shooter, sufficient for video calls and simple selfies. The device includes essential connectivity options such as Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.0, GPS, and an FM radio. It is powered by a removable 2100 mAh Li-Po battery. The Huawei Y3 II price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,990 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. It is available in various colors like Rose Pink, Obsidian Black, Arctic White, Sand Gold, and Sky Blue.
