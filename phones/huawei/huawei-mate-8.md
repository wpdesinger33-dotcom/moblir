The Huawei Mate 8, released in November 2015, is an Android smartphone that boasts a large 6.0-inch display. Its design features a glass front (protected by Corning Gorilla Glass 3) and an aluminum back and frame. The phone is powered by the Kirin 950 chipset, which includes an octa-core processor (4x2.3 GHz Cortex-A72 and 4x1.8 GHz Cortex-A53) and a Mali-T880 MP4 GPU. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Mate 8 price in Bangladesh and its details. Its display offers a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels with a 16:9 aspect ratio, resulting in a pixel density of approximately 368 ppi. On the rear, you’ll find a 16 MP camera with an f/2.0 aperture, PDAF, OIS, and dual-LED dual-tone flash. The front-facing camera is an 8 MP sensor with an f/2.4 aperture. Video recording capabilities include 1080p at 60fps, 1080p at 30fps, and 720p at 120fps. The 4000 mAh non-removable battery supports 18W wired charging, promising 37% charge in just 30 minutes. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac, Bluetooth 4.2, GPS, GLONASS, and NFC. Additionally, the Mate 8 features a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor for secure unlocking. The Huawei Mate 8 price in Bangladesh is BDT 49,990 (Official) for its 4GB of RAM and 64GB of internal storage.
