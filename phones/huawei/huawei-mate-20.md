The Huawei Mate 20 offers premium features. It was officially released  on 28 November 2018 in Bangladesh. The phone comes with a 6.53-inch Full HD+ IPS LCD display that delivers vivid colors and good brightness yet falls short of high-resolution standards in flagship devices. The Mate 20 uses Kirin 980 chipset. The phone operates on Android 9 Pie with EMUI 9. The Mate 20 through its triple camera system combining 12MP main, 8MP telephoto and 16MP ultra-wide lenses. The phone has a 24MP selfie camera. The Mate 20 thanks to its 4,000mAh battery capacity. The device includes 22.5W fast charging capabilities. The Huawei Mate 20 price in Bangladesh is BDT 76,990 (Official) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage.
