The Huawei Ascend Y511, announced in October 2013, is a budget-friendly Android smartphone. It features a 4.5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels. The phone is powered by the MediaTek MT6572 chipset, which includes a dual-core 1.3 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU and 512 MB of RAM. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Ascend Y511 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of storage, the Ascend Y511 offers 4 GB of internal storage, expandable via a microSDHC card slot. The main camera is a modest 3.15 MP shooter, while the front-facing camera is a VGA unit. The device runs Android 4.2 Jelly Bean. Other notable features include Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.0, and FM radio. The 1730 mAh removable battery provides up to 300 hours of standby time and up to 5 hours of talk time. It comes in two color options: Black and White. The Huawei Ascend Y511 price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,990 (Official) for its 0.5GB of RAM and 4GB of internal storage.
