The Huawei Ascend G630 is a mid-range smartphone that offers a balanced mix of performance and affordability. It was released in March 2014, features a 5” IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The device is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 200 chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU and an Adreno 302 GPU.The phone runs on Android 4.3 Jelly Bean with Huawei’s EMUI Lite 2 on top. In terms of photography, the Huawei Ascend G630 is equipped with an 8-megapixel rear camera with autofocus and LED flash, capable of capturing decent photos in various lighting conditions. The front-facing camera is a modest 1-megapixel shooter, suitable for basic selfies and video calls. The device is powered by a 2000 mAh non-removable battery. The Huawei Ascend G630 price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,490 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
