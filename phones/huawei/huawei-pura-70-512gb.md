The Huawei Pura 70 combines aesthetics, performance, and innovative features. This phone has 2 different variants 12GB+256GB, and 12GB+512GB. The features of the 2 variants are same, only RAM, and price are different. It boasts a stunning 6.6-inch LTPO OLED display with HDR support and a high refresh rate of 120Hz. Its Kirin 9010 chipset, built on a 7nm process, ensures smooth performance. The phone comes with three rear cameras: a 50MP primary camera, a periscope telephoto lens with 5x optical zoom, and an ultrawide lens. The retractable 13MP front camera. Battery life is impressive, thanks to the 4900 mAh battery, which supports 66W wired and 50W wireless charging. The phone runs either EMUI 14.2 (for international markets) or HarmonyOS 4.2 (for China).
