Huawei MatePad Pro 13.2 (2024) is a powerful tablet, which will appeal to customers seeking the highest performance. It possesses a 13.2-inch OLED screen with 144Hz refresh rate. The back of the tablet features a 50MP main camera and an 8MP ultrawide camera and the front has a 16MP camera. The tablet operates HarmonyOS 4.3. It also includes the 3rd generation M-Pencil stylus for improved and accurate drawing and even writing. There is a 10,100mAH battery that supports 100W of Turbo charger. The Huawei MatePad Pro 13.2 (2024) price in Bangladesh is BDT 90,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
