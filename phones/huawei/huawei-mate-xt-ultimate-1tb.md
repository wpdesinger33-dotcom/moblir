Huawei Mate XT Ultimate (16GB+1TB) is one of the most compact and powerful foldable smartphones with a new tri-fold design. In contrast, the Huawei Mate XT Ultimate has 2 more variants: 16GB+256GB and 16GB+512GB. All the variants share the same high-performance features including design, processor and camera. It also has a 10.2-inch LTPO OLED display with a density of 2,232 by 3,184 pixels, and more. Supported by the Kirin 9010 chipset. The camera department comprises 50 MP main, 12 MP periscope telephoto, and 12 MP ultrawide lenses. The battery that is 5600mAh and comes with the device supports 66W fast charging through the wire and 50W fast charging wirelessly. Running on HarmonyOS 4.2. The Huawei Mate XT Ultimate price in Bangladesh is BDT 490,000 (Unofficial) for 16GB of RAM and 1TB of ROM.
