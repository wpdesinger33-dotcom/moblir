The Huawei Y6 Pro is a budget-friendly smartphone that has seen multiple iterations since its initial release. The 2015 version, launched in October, features a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels, offering a decent visual experience for its price range. The device is powered by a Mediatek MT6735P chipset. In terms of photography, the Huawei Y6 Pro (2019) is equipped with a single 13 MP rear camera with an f/1.8 aperture, capable of capturing decent photos in good lighting conditions. The selfie camera is a 5 MP shooter with an f/2.0 aperture. The phone runs on Android 5.1.1 (Lollipop) with Huawei’sEMUI 3.1 Lite OS. It is powered by a 4000 mAh non-removable battery, which offers a reasonable battery life for a day of moderate use. The Huawei Y6 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 11,690 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. Available in several colors, including Gold, Grey, and White colors.
