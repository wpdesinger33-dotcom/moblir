The Huawei P9 Lite Mini is an Android smartphone that was announced in August 2017. It features a compact 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The phone is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 425 chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.4 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and an Adreno 308 GPU. It comes with 2 GB of RAM and 16 GB of internal storage, expandable via a microSDXC card. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei P9 Lite Mini price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of photography, the P9 Lite Mini sports a 13 MP rear camera with autofocus and LED flash, capable of recording 1080p videos at 30 fps. For selfies, there’s a 5 MP front camera. The device also features a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor for added security. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth v4.1, and GPS with GLONASS support. It lacks NFC functionality. The phone runs on Android 7.0 Nougat with Huawei’s EMUI 5.1 skin. Its 3020 mAh non-removable battery ensures decent usage time. The Huawei P9 Lite Mini price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,990 (Official) for its 2GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage.
