The Huawei MediaPad T3 8.0 is a compact and versatile tablet released in May 2017. It features an 8.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 800 x 1280 pixels, offering a pixel density of 189 ppi. The tablet is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 425 chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.4 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and a Mali-450MP4 GPU. The MediaPad T3 8.0 runs on Android 7.0 (Nougat) with Huawei's EMUI 5.1 interface. It has a 5MP rearcamerawith autofocus and a 2MP front camera. The tablet is equipped with a 4800mAhbattery. The Huawei MediaPad T3 8.0 price in Bangladesh is BDT 16,999 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
