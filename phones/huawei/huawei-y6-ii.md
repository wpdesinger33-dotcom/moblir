The Huawei Y6 II, released in 2017, is a versatile smartphone that offers a blend of functionality and affordability. It features a 5.5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The device is powered by the Huawei HiSilicon Kirin 620 octa-core processor, clocked at 1.2 GHz. It runs on Android 6.0 Marshmallow and Huawei’s EMUI 4.1. In terms of photography, the Huawei Y6 II is equipped with a 13 MP rear camera that includes features like autofocus, HDR, and panorama, allowing users to capture detailed and high-quality images. The front-facing camera is 8 MP, making it suitable for selfies. It is powered by a 3000 mAh battery. The Huawei Y6 II price in Bangladesh is BDT 13,900 (Official) with 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It is available in various colors such as black, white, and gold.
