The Huawei Mate 10 Pro, announced in October 2017, is an Android smartphone that boasts a 6.0-inch OLED display. Its sleek design features a glass front and back, framed by an aluminum chassis. The phone is IP67 dust and water-resistant, capable of surviving submersion up to 1 meter for 30 minutes. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Mate 10 Pro price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of performance, the Mate 10 Pro packs a Kirin 970 chipset with an integrated neural processing unit (NPU). This AI-enhanced processor impacts various aspects of the phone, including photography and energy efficiency. The dual-camera system on the rear consists of a 12 MP primary lens (f/1.6 aperture) and a 20 MP monochrome lens (also f/1.6). Leica optics, dual-LED dual-tone flash, panorama, and HDR enhance the photography experience. The front-facing camera is an 8 MP shooter (f/2.0). Other notable features include stereo speakers, USB Type-C 3.1, NFC, and an infrared port. The 4000 mAh battery, and supports 22.5W fast charging, reaching 58% in just 30 minutes. Unfortunately, there’s no 3.5mm headphone jack. It runs Android 8.0 (Oreo) out of the box, upgradable to Android 10 with EMUI 10.2. The Huawei Mate 10 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 80,900 (Official) with 4GB of RAM and 64GB of internal storage.
