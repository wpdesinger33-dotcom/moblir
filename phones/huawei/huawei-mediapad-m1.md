Huawei MediaPad M1 is a small straightforward multiple purpose tablet unveiled in May 2014 in Bangladesh. It has an 8.0-inch IPS LCD display, which smoothes 800 x 1280 pixels resolution and pixel density of 189 ppi. The tablet is driven by the Kirin 910 chipset that has a quad core 1.6 GHz Cortex-A9 processor and a Mali-450MP4 graphics processor unit. The MediaPad M1 now comes with Android 4.2.2 (Jelly Bean). It has 5 MegaPixel rear autofocuscamerasand 1 MegaPixel front facing camera. Thebatteryof this tablet is 4800mAh. The Huawei MediaPad M1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 27,990 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
