The Huawei P10 Plus, released in April 2017, is a flagship smartphone that stands out with its sleek design and powerful features. It features a 5.5-inch QHD display with a resolution of 1440 x 2560 pixels, offering vibrant and sharp visuals. The device is powered by the Kirin 960 chipset. The P10 Plus runs on Android 7.0 Nougat, upgradable to Android 9.0 Pie, with Huawei’s EMUI 9.1 interface providing a user-friendly experience. One of the key highlights of the Huawei P10 Plus is its dual-camera setup, developed in collaboration with Leica. The rear camera system includes a 20MP monochrome sensor and a 12MP RGB sensor, both with f/1.8 apertures. The front camera is an 8MP shooter with an f/1.9 aperture, perfect for high-quality selfies. The device also features a 3750mAh battery, which supports 18W fast charging. The Huawei P10 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 66,900 (Official) with 4GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage. It is available in several colors, including Ceramic White, Dazzling Blue, and Graphite Black.
