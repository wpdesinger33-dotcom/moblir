The Huawei G7 Plus, launched in November 2015, is an upper mid-range smartphone that offers a blend of solid performance and elegant design. It features a 5.5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels, providing clear and vibrant visuals. The device is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 615 chipset, an octa-core processor that ensures smooth multitasking and efficient performance. In terms of camera capabilities, the Huawei G7 Plus is equipped with a 13MP rear camera featuring optical image stabilization (OIS) and a dual-LED dual-tone flash, which enhances photo quality in various lighting conditions. The selfie camera is a 5MP shooter. The device runs on Android 5.1 Lollipop, upgradable to Android 6.0 Marshmallow, with Huawei’s EMUI 3.1 skin on top. The smartphone also includes a 3000mAh non-removable battery, which provides decent battery life for daily use. The Huawei G7 Plus price in Bangladesh nis BDT 27,990 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage. It is available in multiple colors, including gray, silver, and gold.
