The Huawei GR3 (2017) is a mid-range smartphone. It was launched on April 9, 2017, features a sleek design with a 5.2-inch Full HD IPS display, offering a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. The device is powered by the HiSilicon Kirin 655 octa-core processor, which includes four Cortex-A53 cores clocked at 2.1 GHz and another four Cortex-A53 cores at 1.7 GHz. The phone runs on Android 7.0 Nougat with Huawei's EMUI 5.0 on top, providing a smooth and user-friendly experience. In terms of photography, the Huawei GR3 (2017) is equipped with a 12-megapixel rear camera that features phase detection autofocus and an LED flash, ensuring clear and vibrant photos even in low light conditions. The front camera is an 8-megapixel shooter, suitable for selfies. Connectivity options are robust, with support for 4G LTE, Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.1, and GPS. The phone is powered by a 3000mAh non-removable battery, which provides ample power for a full day of use. It is housed in a metal frame, giving it a premium look and feel. It measures 147.2 x 72.94 x 7.6 mm and weighs 147 grams, making it comfortable to hold and use. The Huawei GR3 (2017) price in Bangladesh is BDT 17,900 (Official) with 3GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. Available in colors like black, gold, and white, the device offers a good balance of performance, design, and features at an affordable price point.
