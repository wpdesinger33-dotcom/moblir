Huawei MatePad T8 is a slim, sleek and portable tablet primarily focused for general-purpose usage. It has an 8.0 inch High Definition IPS screen with a resolution of 1280 x 800 pixels. It is easily portable and lighter in weight, having a weight of approximately 310 grams and thickness is 8.6mm. The supported chipset is MediaTek MT8768 . The tablet comes with Android 10 and with the EMUI 10. The MatePad T8 comes with the 5MP rear camera and 2MP front camera. The tablet also has two speakers, which have been tuned by Harman Kardon. The Huawei MatePad T8 price in Bangladesh is BDT 16,999 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
