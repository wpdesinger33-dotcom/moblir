The Huawei Y3 (2018) is an entry-level smartphone released in May 2018, designed to cater to users seeking basic functionality at an affordable price. It features a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels, providing a modest visual experience suitable for everyday tasks. This smartphone is powered by a MediaTek MT6737M chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.1 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and a Mali-T720MP2 GPU. Running on Android 8.0 Oreo (Go edition), the Huawei Y3 (2018) is optimized for low-end hardware. The device’s camera setup includes an 8 MP rear camera with an f/2.0 aperture and LED flash, capable of capturing decent photos in good lighting conditions. The front-facing camera is a 2 MP shooter, suitable for basic selfies. It is equipped with a 2280 mAh non-removable battery, which offers sufficient power to get through a day of moderate use. The Huawei Y3 (2018) price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,890 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. The phone is available in multiple colors, including Gold, White, and Gray.
