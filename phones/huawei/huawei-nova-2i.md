The Huawei Nova 2i features a 5.9-inch IPS display with a resolution of 1080 x 2160 pixels. It’s powered by the Huawei HiSilicon KIRIN 659 chipset, which includes an octa-core CPU (4x 2.36 GHz ARM Cortex-A53 and 4x 1.7 GHz ARM Cortex-A53) and an ARM Mali-T830 MP2 GPU. It comes with 4 GB of RAM and 64 GB of storage, expandable via microSD cards. Running Android 7.0 Nougat, it boasts a dual-camera setup on the back (16 MP + 2 MP) and a 13 MP front camera. The 3340 mAh battery is non-removable. Overall, the Huawei Nova 2i offers a balanced combination of features for its price. The Huawei Nova 2i price in Bangladesh is BDT 24,990 (Official) for its 4GB of RAM and 64GB of internal storage.
