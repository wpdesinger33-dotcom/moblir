The Huawei P8, released in April 2015, is a sleek and stylish smartphone that marked a significant step forward for Huawei in the competitive mobile market. It features a 5.2-inch IPS-NEO LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. The device is powered by the HiSilicon Kirin 930 chipset. The P8 runs on Android 4.4.2 KitKat, upgradable to Android 6.0 Marshmallow, with Huawei’s EMUI 3.1 skin on top. The camera setup on the Huawei P8 includes a 13 MP rear camera, capable of capturing detailed photos and 1080p videos. The front-facing camera is an 8 MP shooter, suitable for selfies and video calls. The device is equipped with a non-removable 2680 mAh battery. The Huawei P8 price in Bangladesh is BDT 49,990 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
