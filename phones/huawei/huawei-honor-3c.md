The Honor 3C, released in December 2013, is a mid-range Android smartphone produced by Huawei. It features a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The phone runs on Android 4.2.2 (Jelly Bean) with EMUI Lite 2. It is powered by a Mediatek MT6582 quad-core processor clocked at 1.3 GHz, paired with 2 GB of RAM and 8 GB of internal storage (expandable via microSD). Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Honor 3C price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of photography, the Honor 3C sports an 8 MP primary camera with an f/2.0 aperture and LED flash, capable of recording 1080p videos at 30 fps. For selfies, there’s a 5 MP front camera with an f/2.4 aperture. The phone supports dual SIM cards (Micro-SIM) and offers Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 4.0, GPS, and FM radio connectivity. Its 2300 mAh removable battery provides decent usage time. While the Honor 3C impressed with its design, power, and camera capabilities, it did have some downsides. The software was based on the older Android version, and the graphics performance wasn’t stellar. Overall, it offered good value for its budget-friendly price point.
