The Huawei GR5 Mini is a compact smartphone with a focus on balanced performance. It features a sleek design with dimensions of 73.76 mm (width) x 147.1 mm (height) x 8.3 mm (thickness) and weighs 156 grams. Its body is crafted from an aluminum alloy, and it comes in silver, gold, and gray color options. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei GR5 Mini price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of performance, it packs a Huawei HiSilicon KIRIN 650 system-on-chip (SoC). This octa-core processor consists of four 2.0 GHz ARM Cortex-A53 cores and four 1.7 GHz ARM Cortex-A53 cores. The graphics are handled by the ARM Mali-T830 MP2 GPU, clocked at 900 MHz. It offers 2 GB of RAM and 16 GB of internal storage, expandable via microSD cards. The display is a 5.2-inch IPS panel with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels (Full HD) and 24-bit color depth. The phone runs on Android 6.0 Marshmallow. For photography, it features a 4160 x 3120 pixels rear camera and a 1920 x 1080 pixels front camera, both capable of recording video at 30 fps.
