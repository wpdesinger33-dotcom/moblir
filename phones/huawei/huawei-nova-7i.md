The Huawei Nova 7i is an upper mid-range smartphone that offers a blend of performance and affordability. It was released in February 2020, features a 6.4-inch LTPS IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 2310 pixels, providing vibrant and sharp visuals. The device is powered by the Kirin 810 chipset, an octa-core processor that ensures smooth performance for everyday tasks and moderate gaming. One of the standout features of the Huawei Nova 7i is its quad-camera setup on the rear, which includes a 48 MP main sensor, an 8 MP ultra-wide lens, a 2 MP macro lens, and a 2 MP depth sensor. The front camera is a 16 MP sensor, housed in a punch-hole design, which supports HDR and 1080p video recording. Despite lacking Google Play Services, the phone runs on Android 10 with EMUI 10, offering a range of Huawei's own apps and services. It is equipped with a 4200 mAh battery that supports 40W fast charging, capable of charging up to 70% in just 30 minutes. This ensures that the phone can keep up with a busy lifestyle without frequent recharges. The Huawei Nova 7i price in Bangladesh is BDT 29,999 (Official) with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. Available in colors like  Crush Green, Midnight Black, and Sakura Pink.
