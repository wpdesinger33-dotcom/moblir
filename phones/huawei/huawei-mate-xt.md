The Huawei Mate XT is a groundbreaking addition to the foldable smartphone market, featuring a unique tri-fold design. This innovative device boasts two hinges, allowing it to unfold into a large 10.1-inch display with a resolution of 2780 x 3200 pixels and a 120Hz refresh rate. The Mate XT’s design enables part of the main display to double as the outer screen when folded, providing versatility and convenience for users. The device runs on Android 15, further enhancing its user experience with the latest features and updates. It is powered by a Kirin 9000S chipset, and Maleoon 910 GPU. For photography, it has triple rear cameras, A 50 MP main sensor, and 8 MP selfie camera. It has a 5060 mAh battery. The Huawei Mate XT price in Bangladesh is BDT 70,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage. It comes in Dark Green, Black, White, Purple, and Gold colors.
