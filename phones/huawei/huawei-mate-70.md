Huawei mate 70 is one of the luxury smartphones. This one is supported by a 6.7 inch LTPO OLED with 1216 x 2688 pixels resolution screen. The phone runs on the Kirin chipset which guarantees the phone to be efficient in its operations. The Mate 70 has introduced a triple-camera setup as well, with a 50 MP main sensor and lens, a 40 MP ultra wide sensor and lens, and a 12 MP periscope telephoto lens. Its selfie camera is 13MP. It also integrated with a 5300mAh battery with 66W wired charging and 50W wireless charging solution. It comes in Black, Purple, White, and Green colors. The Huawei mate 70 price in Bangladesh is BDT 90,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
