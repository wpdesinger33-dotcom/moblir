The Huawei P10, launched in March 2017, is a flagship smartphone that builds on the success of its predecessor, the P9. It features a sleek design with a 5.1-inch IPS-NEO LCD display, offering a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. The device is powered by the Kirin 960 chipset, an octa-core processor that ensures smooth performance. The P10 runs on Android 7.0 Nougat, upgradable to Android 9.0 Pie, with Huawei’s EMUI 9.1 interface. One of the standout features of the Huawei P10 is its dual-camera system, developed in collaboration with Leica. The rear camera setup includes a 12MP RGB sensor and a 20MP monochrome sensor, which work together to produce high-quality images with excellent detail and dynamic range. The camera also supports 4K video recording and various modes like portrait and monochrome. The front camera is an 8MP shooter, capable of capturing clear selfies and 1080p video. It also includes a 3200mAh battery that supports fast charging. The Huawei P10 price in Bangladesh is BDT 56,900 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of ROM. It comes in Rose Gold, Mystic Silver, Graphite Black, Dazzling Gold, Dazzling Blue, and Greenery colors.
