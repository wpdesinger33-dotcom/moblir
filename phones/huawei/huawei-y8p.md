The Huawei Y8p is an Android smartphone that packs several noteworthy features. Here we will share Huawei Y8p price in Bangladesh and specs. The Huawei Y8p boasts a sleek design with dimensions of 157.4 x 73.2 x 7.8 mm and a weight of 163 g. Its 6.3-inch OLED display offers a Full HD+ resolution (1080 x 2400 pixels). For photography, 48 MP Main Camera, 8 MP Ultrawide Camera, and 2 MP Depth Sensor. The notch houses a 16 MP selfie camera. Under the hood, the Y8p runs on a Kirin 710F chipset (12 nm) with an octa-core CPU (4x2.2 GHz Cortex-A73 & 4x1.7 GHz Cortex-A53). A non-removable 4000 mAh Li-Po battery powers the Y8p, supporting 10W wired charging. The Huawei Y8p price in Bangladesh is BDT 25,499 (Official) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. This model comes in Breathing Crystal, and Midnight Black colors.
