The Huawei P8lite, released in May 2015, is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers a blend of style and functionality. It offers a 5” IPS LCD display and resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The phone is powered by the Kirin 620 chipset, an octa-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU. The device runs on Android 5.0.2 Lollipop, upgradable to Android 6.0 Marshmallow, with Huawei’s EMUI 3.1 interface. In terms of design, the P8lite sports a sleek and lightweight build, weighing just 131 grams. It has a glass front protected by Corning Gorilla Glass 3, a plastic back, and a plastic frame. The camera setup includes a 13 MP rear camera with an f/2.0 aperture, autofocus, and dual-LED flash, capable of capturing detailed photos and 1080p videos. The front-facing 5 MP camera is for selfies. It also includes a 2200 mAh non-removable battery, which offers decent battery life for moderate use. The Huawei P8lite price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,490 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in Black, White, and Gold colors.
