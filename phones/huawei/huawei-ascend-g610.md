The Huawei Ascend G610 is a budget-friendly mid-range smartphone notable for its dual SIM card slots. Released in August 2013, it features a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 540 x 960 pixels. Powered by a quad-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A7 processor (Mediatek MT6589) and 1 GB of RAM, the G610 runs on Android 4.2 (Jelly Bean). Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Ascend G610 price in Bangladesh and its details. Its 5 MP rear camera includes an LED flash and can record 720p videos, while the front VGA camera is suitable for basic selfies. The device packs a removable 2150 mAh Li-Ion battery. Available in black and white, the Huawei G610s was priced around 150 EUR. The Huawei Ascend G610 price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,980 (Official) for its 1GB of RAM and 4GB of internal storage.
