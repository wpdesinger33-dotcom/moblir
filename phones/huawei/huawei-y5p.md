The Huawei Y5p is an entry-level Android smartphone released in May 2020. Here we will share Huawei Y5p price in Bangladesh and specs. It features a compact 5.45-inch HD+ IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1440 pixels. Powered by the MediaTek Helio P22 chipset. The phone runs on Android 10 with EMUI 10.1. In terms of photography, the Y5p sports an 8 MP single rear camera with features like LED flash, HDR, and panorama. For selfies, 5 MP front camera. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 5.0, GPS, GLONASS, and BDS. It supports 4G LTE and has a 3020 mAh non-removable battery. The Huawei Y5p price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,999 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage. It comes in colors like Mint Green, Midnight Black, and Phantom Blue.
