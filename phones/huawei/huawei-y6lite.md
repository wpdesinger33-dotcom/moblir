The Huawei Y6 Lite, also known as the Huawei Y6, is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers a balanced mix of essential features for everyday use. Released in September 2017, it sports a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels, providing a decent visual experience for its price range. The phone is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 210 chipset. In terms of photography, the Huawei Y6 Lite is equipped with a 8MP rear camera, which allows for decent low-light photography. The front camera is a 2MP shooter, suitable for selfies. It runs on Android 6.0  with EMUI 4.1. It is powered by a 3000mAh non-removable battery, which provides adequate battery life for a full day of moderate use. The Huawei Y6 Lite price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,990 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. Available in colors like White, Gold, and Gray.
