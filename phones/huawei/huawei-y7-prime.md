The Huawei Y7 Prime is an Android smartphone featuring a 5.5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720x1280 pixels. It runs on Android N OS 7.0 and is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 435 chipset. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Y7 Prime price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of photography, the Y7 Prime (2019) features a dual rear camera setup with a 12 MP primary lens (f/1.8, PDAF) and a 8 MP depth sensor. The device is equipped with a 4000 mAh non-removable battery, which supports 10W wired charging. Other notable features include a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor, Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.2, GPS, and FM radio. It’s available in colors like Aurora Blue, Midnight Black, Coral Red, and Brown. The Huawei Y7 Prime price in Bangladesh is BDT 19,900 (Official) for its 3GB of RAM and 32GB of internal storage.
