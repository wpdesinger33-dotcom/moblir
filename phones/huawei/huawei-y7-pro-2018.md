The Huawei Y7 Pro (2018) is a mid-range smartphone that was released in April 2018. It features a sleek design with a 5.99-inch IPS LCD display, offering a resolution of 720 x 1440 pixels. This gives the device a modern 18:9 aspect ratio, making it suitable for media consumption and everyday use. The phone is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 430 chipset, an octa-core processor clocked at 1.4 GHz. The device runs on Android 8.0 Oreo with Huawei’s EMUI 8.0 skin on top, providing a user-friendly interface and additional features. In terms of photography, the Huawei Y7 Pro (2018) is equipped with a dual-camera setup on the back, consisting of a 13 MP primary sensor with PDAF and a 2 MP depth sensor. This setup allows for decent photo quality and depth effects in portrait shots. The front camera is an 8 MP shooter, which is adequate for selfies and video calls. Both cameras can record 1080p videos at 30fps. The phone also includes a 3000 mAh non-removable battery. The Huawei Y7 Pro (2018) price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,990 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage. Available in black, gold, and blue.
