Huawei MatePad T 10s is the best tablet that is sleek in design and very powerful in performance. It includes a 10.1 inch Full High Definition IPS panel and has a resolution of 1920 x 1200 pixels. It comes at 450 grams, and has a thickness of 7.9mm. The internals of this gadget is driven by the Kirin 710A chipset. This android tablet has android 10 with EMUI 10.1 as its operating system. The MatePad T 10s also features a 5MP rear camera and 2MP front camera. The tablet is also endowed with dual speakers supported by the Harman Kardon-the sound quality is deep. The Huawei MatePad T 10s price in Bangladesh is BDT 16,999 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage.
