The Huawei Y625, announced in April 2015, is an Android smartphone featuring a 5.0-inch display. Its screen resolution is 480x854 pixels, resulting in a pixel density of approximately 196 ppi. The phone is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 200 chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU and Adreno 302 GPU. It comes with 1 GB of RAM and 4 GB of internal storage (expandable via microSDHC). Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Y625 price in Bangladesh and its details. It comes with an 8 MP primary camera with autofocus and LED flash, capable of recording 720p videos at 30 fps. For selfies, there’s a 2 MP front camera. The device runs on Android 4.4.2 KitKat with EMUI Lite 2.3. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.0, and GPS. It also features a 3.5mm headphone jack and FM radio. Its removable 2000 mAh Li-Po battery provides standby times of up to 310 hours (2G) or 250 hours (3G) and talk times of up to 7 hours 30 minutes (2G) or 8 hours 20 minutes (3G). Available in black and white, the Huawei Y625 was priced around 130 EUR. The Huawei Y625 price in Bangladesh is BDT 7,990 (Official) for its 1GB of RAM and 4GB of internal storage.
