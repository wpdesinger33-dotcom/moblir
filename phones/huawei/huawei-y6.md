The Huawei Y6, released in July 2015, is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers a balanced mix of essential features. It sports a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The device is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 210 chipset, featuring a quad-core 1.1 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU and an Adreno 304 GPU. Running on Android 5.1 Lollipop with Huawei’s EMUI 3.1, the Y6 offers a user-friendly interface and basic smartphone functionalities. In terms of photography, the Huawei Y6 is equipped with an 8 MP rear camera with an f/2.0 aperture, autofocus, and LED flash, capable of capturing reasonable photos in good lighting conditions. The front-facing camera is a modest 2 MP, suitable for basic selfies and video calls. It is powered by a removable 2200 mAh battery. The Huawei Y6 price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,990 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. Available in white, black, and gold.
