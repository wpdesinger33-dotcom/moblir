The Huawei Ascend P7, announced in May 2014 and released in June 2014, is a high-end smartphone running the Android operating system. Its slim glass construction garnered attention, and it features a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display. The phone is powered by the Kirin 910T chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.8 GHz Cortex-A9 CPU and an ARM Mali-450MP4 GPU. With 2 GB of RAM and 16 GB of internal storage, the Ascend P7 offers decent performance for its time. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Ascend P7 price in Bangladesh and its details. Its 13 MP primary camera captures photos with an f/2.0 aperture and a 28mm wide lens. It supports features like LED flash, panorama, and HDR, while the 8 MP front camera is ideal for selfies. The display boasts a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels and is protected by Corning Gorilla Glass 3. The phone’s 2500 mAh non-removable battery provides standby time of up to 422 hours (3G) and talk time of up to 22 hours (2G) or 14 hours (3G). Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.0, GPS, and NFC. The Ascend P7 also features an FM radio and a microUSB 2.0 port. Sensors like accelerometer, proximity, and compass enhance its functionality. Available in colors like black, white, and pink, the Huawei Ascend P7 was a visually appealing handset that left a positive impression despite its slower processor.
