The Huawei P30 Pro is a flagship Android smartphone that was released in March 2019. It boasts impressive features and a sleek design. It features a glass front and back, with an aluminum frame. It’s both elegant and sturdy. The 6.47-inch OLED display offers vibrant colors and HDR10 support, making it great for multimedia consumption. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei P30 Pro price in Bangladesh and its overall details. In terms of performance, the P30 Pro is powered by the Kirin 980 chipset, an octa-core processor with impressive performance.The standout feature of the P30 Pro is its Leica Quad Camera systems which are 40MP main cameras. 8MP periscope telephoto lens, 20MP ultra-wide lens and TOP 3D depth sensor. The 4200mAh battery ensures all-day usage, and the 40W wired charging can juice up the phone quickly. Additionally, it supports 15W wireless charging and even reverse wireless charging. It comes with 7 color options which are Aurora, Amber Sunrise, Breathing Crystal, Black, Pearl White, Misty Lavender, or Mystic Blue. The Huawei P30 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 89,999 (Official) and 57,000 (Unofficial) for its 8GB of RAM and 256GB of internal storage.
