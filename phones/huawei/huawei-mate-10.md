The Huawei Mate 10 juxtaposes superior technology with an elegant design. It possesses a 5.9-inch IPS LCDdisplaywith a resolution of 1440 x 2560 pixels. The chipset powering the phone is HiSilicon Kirin 970 which contains an Octa-core processor. Running on Android 8.0 (Oreo) with Huawei's EMUI 10.2. Long-lasting power is provided by a 4000mAhbattery, whereas 22.5W fast charging. The phone offers dual 20MP + 12MP rear camera, and  8MP camera in front. The Huawei Mate 10 price in Bangladesh is BDT 68,000 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage. This model comes in Mocha Brown, Black, Champagne Gold, and Pink Gold colors.
