The Huawei Y7p is an Android smartphone that was announced in February 2020. Here we will share Huawei Y7p price in Bangladesh and specs. It features a 6.39-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1560 pixels. The phone is powered by a Kirin 710F chipset. The Y7p boasts a triple rear camera setup consisting of a 48MP primary lens, an 8MP ultrawide lens, and a 2MP depth sensor. For selfies, 8MP front camera. The device runs on Android 9.0 (Pie) with EMUI 9. Other notable features include a 4000mAh non-removable battery. The Huawei Y7p price in Bangladesh is BDT 18,999 for 4GB of RAM and 64GB. It is an official variant. It is available in colors like Aurora Blue and Midnight Black.
