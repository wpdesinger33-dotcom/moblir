The Huawei Y5II is another entry level Smartphone that was released in June 2016 in Bangladesh. This one comes with an 5.0 inch IPS LCD display offering pixel resolution of 720 x 1280. The powering technology for the device is MediaTek MT6735P chipset that consist of a 1.3 GHz quad core cortex A53 processor with Mali T720MP2 GPU. Looking at the aspect of design, the Y5II has received the aspect of a sleek and lightweight body that weighs 135 grams with dimensions of 143.8 x 72 x 8.9mm. The phone has an 8MP rear facing and 2MP front facing camera. The 2200mAh battery provides. The Huawei Y5II price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,990 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
