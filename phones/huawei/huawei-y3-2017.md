The Huawei Y3 (2017) is an entry-level smartphone that was released in June 2017. It features a compact design with a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display, offering a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels. The phone is powered by a MediaTek MT6737M chipset, paired with a quad-core 1.1 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and a Mali-T720MP2 GPU. Runs on Android 6.0 Marshmallow with Huawei’s EMUI 4.1 Mini on top. In terms of photography, the Huawei Y3 (2017) is equipped with an 8MP rear camera with an f/2.0 aperture and autofocus, capable of recording 720p videos at 30fps. For selfies, it has a 2MP front-facing camera. It is powered by a 2200 mAh non-removable Li-Ion battery. The Huawei Y3 (2017) price in Bangladesh is BDT 7,390 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. The device is available in several colors, including Gold, Pink, Blue, White, and Gray.
