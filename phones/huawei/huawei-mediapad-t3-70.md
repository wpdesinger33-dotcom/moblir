Huawei MediaPad T3 7.0 is a small and inexpensive tablet in a line of Huawei presented in May 2017. It is a 7.0 inches, IPS LCD touchscreen with resolution of 600 x 1024 pixels. The operating system of the tablet is MediaTek MT8127 chipset and it is powered with a quad-core 1.3 GHz Cortex-A7 Processor with Mali-450MP4 GPU. It has a 2 megapixel rear camera and a 2 megapixel front facing camera. The tablet has Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) as its operating system, with Huawei’s EMUI 4.1. It features a 3100mAh battery. Available connectivity includes: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 4.0, and USB 2.0. The Huawei MediaPad T3 7.0 price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,690 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
