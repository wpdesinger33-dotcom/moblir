The Huawei Pura 70 Ultra is a flagship smartphone that combines impressive camera capabilities with sleek design. Announced in April 2024, this device boasts a 50-megapixel triple camera system, including a retractable lens for optical zoom. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Pura 70 Ultra price in Bangladesh and its overall details. Its 6.8-inch LTPO OLED display offers vibrant colors and HDR support, while the Kirin 9010 chipset powers smooth performance. With up to 512GB of storage and 16GB of RAM, it caters to storage-intensive tasks. Its 5200mAh battery supports 100W wired charging and 80W wireless charging. However, its lack of Google app support and high price tag may limit its appeal to a broader audience. For photography enthusiasts, the Pura 70 Ultra’s innovative camera setup makes it a compelling choice. The Huawei Pura 70 Ultra price in Bangladesh is BDT 120,000 (Unofficial) for its 16GB of RAM and 512GB of internal storage.
