The Huawei Ascend Mate, unveiled at the 2013 International CES and released in March 2013, is a notable entry in Huawei’s lineup of phablets. This device features a large 6.1-inch IPS+ LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The screen is protected by Corning Gorilla Glass, ensuring durability against scratches and minor impacts. Under the hood, the Ascend Mate is powered by a Huawei K3V2 chipset with a quad-core 1.5 GHz Cortex-A9 CPU and Vivante GC4000 GPU. It runs on Android 4.1 Jelly Bean, upgradable to 4.2.2, with Huawei’s EMUI 1.5 skin on top. The device is equipped with an 8MP rear camera capable of recording 1080p videos and a 1MP front camera for basic selfies and video calls. One of its standout features is the large 4050 mAh non-removable battery, which offers impressive battery life, with up to 23 hours of talk time on 3G. The Huawei Ascend Mate price in Bangladesh is BDT 22,990 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. It comes in Crystal Black, and Pure White colors.
