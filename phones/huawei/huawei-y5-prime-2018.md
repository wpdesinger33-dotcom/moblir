Huawei Y5 Prime (2018) smartphone is an entry-level device meant for calling and messaging alongside using social networking apps and listening to music. It was launched in June 2018. It consists of a 5.45 inches IPS LCD type touchscreen display with a resolution of 720 x 1440 pixels. The phone is portable and easy to handle since it has a size of 146.5mm in width, 70.9mm in breadth and thickness of 8.3mm and a weight of 142 grams. Being driven by MediaTek MT6739 processor and processor that includes Quad-core 1.5GHz Cortex-A53 CPU. It also has a 13MP rear camera and recording videos with 1080p resolution. There is a battery capacity of 3020 mAh in the device. The Huawei Y5 Prime (2018) price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,599 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. This phone comes in Black, Blue, and Gold colors.
