The Huawei Mate 30 Pro 5G, announced in September 2019, boasts a 6.53-inch curved OLED display with HDR10 support, offering an immersive visual experience. Powered by the Kirin 990 5G chipset, it ensures smooth performance and connectivity. The phone features a robust 4,500 mAh battery with 40W wired charging and 27W wireless charging capabilities. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Mate 30 Pro 5G price in Bangladesh. Photography lovers will appreciate the versatile camera setup. The rear camera system includes a 40 MP primary lens (f/1.6), an 8 MP telephoto lens (3x optical zoom), and a 40 MP ultrawide lens (f/1.8). Additionally, there’s a 3D depth-sensing camera for improved portrait shots. On the front, a 32 MP selfie camera captures detailed images. Despite its impressive hardware, the Mate 30 Pro 5G faced challenges due to the absence of Google Play Services. However, Huawei’s EMUI 10 interface compensates with useful features. The Huawei Mate 30 Pro 5G price in Bangladesh is BDT 64,000 (Unofficial) for its 8GB of RAM and 256GB of internal storage.
