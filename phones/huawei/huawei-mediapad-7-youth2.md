The Huawei MediaPad 7 Youth2 presents itself as a stylish small tablet that hit the Bangladesh market in March 2014. The  Huawei MediaPad 7 Youth2 displays Full HD resolution at 1024 x 600 pixels through its 7-inch screen that delivers 170 ppi pixel density. The phone uses a quad-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU. The Huawei MediaPad 7 Youth2 operates on Android 4.3. Its rear and front cameras capture 3.15MP and 0.03MP pictures. The tablet operates with a 4100 mAh battery system. This device supports Wi-Fi 4 and Bluetooth 4.0. The Huawei MediaPad 7 Youth2 price in Bangladesh is BDT 13,990 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
