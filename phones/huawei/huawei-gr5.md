The Huawei GR5, also known as the Honor 5X in some markets, is a mid-range smartphone that was first released in October 2015. It features a sleek aluminum body design, giving it a premium look and feel despite its mid-range positioning. The device is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 616 processor, ensuring smooth performance for everyday tasks and moderate gaming. The Huawei GR5 sports a 5.5-inch Full HD IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. The phone runs on Android 5.1 Lollipop with Huawei’s EMUI 3.1 on top, offering a user-friendly interface and a range of customization options. In terms of photography, the Huawei GR5 is equipped with a 13-megapixel rear camera and a 5-megapixel front camera. The 3000mAh battery provides sufficient power to last through a day of typical use. The Huawei GR5 price in Bangladesh is BDT 18,990 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
