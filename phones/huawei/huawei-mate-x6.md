Huawei Mate X6 is one of the brand new foldable smartphones that belongs to the highest class. It includes a LTPO 2.0 folding 7.93 inches display with a resolution of 2240 by 2440 p and a frequency of 120 Hz. When folded, it measures 6.45 inches in its cover screen with the same PPI and refresh rate. The Huawei Mate X6 is built around Hauwei’s very own Kirin 9000 chipset. On the back, it has a triple camera of the 50MP primary camera, the 40MP ultra-slim aperture lens, and the 48MP telephoto lens. For selfies, it entails a front facing 8 MP camera. The battery power of the Mate X6 ranges at 5,110mAh with the support of 66W fast charging, wired, and 50W wireless. It also comes with IPX8 for water resistance up to 30 minutes under 2 meters submerging depth. The Huawei Mate X6 price in Bangladesh is BDT 220,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of internal storage.
