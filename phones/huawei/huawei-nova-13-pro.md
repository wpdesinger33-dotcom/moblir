Huawei Nova 13 Pro is a flagship smartphone of this year with a screen size of 6.76 inches LTPO OLED with a resolution of 1224 x 2776 pixels at 120 Hz refresh rate. The Kirin 8000 is Huawei’s proprietary silicon, and gives the phone a good deal of its processing power. The primary night camera uses a 50MP sensor, the telephoto bionic uses a 12MP and there is a third 8MP ultra-wide camera. The front camera has a 60-megapixel sensor and an 8-megapixel telephoto lens for nice photographs when taking selfies. It comes with a  5000mAh battery and fast charging 100W Wired. The Huawei Nova 13 Pro comes with preloaded HarmonyOS 4.2. Available in colors including Feather Sand Purple, Feather Sand White, Loddon Green, and Star Black. The Huawei Nova 13 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 65,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
