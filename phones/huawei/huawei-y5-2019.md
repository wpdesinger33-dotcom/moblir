The Huawei Y5 (2019), announced in April 2019, is an Android smartphone that offers a balance of features at an affordable price. Here we will share Huawei Y5 (2019) price in Bangladesh and specs. It is a 5.71-inch IPS LCD display and a resolution of 720 x 1520 pixels. The phone is powered by the Mediatek MT6761 Helio A22 chipset, which includes a quad-core 2.0 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and a PowerVR GE8320 GPU. The Huawei Y5 (2019) features a 13 MP single rear camera with an f/1.8 aperture, PDAF, LED flash, HDR, and panorama capabilities. For selfies,  5 MP front camera with an f/2.2 aperture. The phone runs on Android 9.0 (Pie) with Huawei’s EMUI 9.1 skin. Other notable features include a 3020 mAh non-removable battery. The Huawei Y5 (2019) price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,999 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage. The Huawei Y5 (2019) is available in colors like Midnight Black, Modern Black, Sapphire Blue, and Amber Brown.
