The Huawei Y9 Prime (2019) is a budget-friendly smartphone that was released in the Bangladesh market on 05 August 2019. It features a large 6.59-inch display with a resolution of 1080 x 2340 pixels, providing a pixel density of approximately 391 ppi. The phone is powered by the Kirin 710F chipset, which includes an octa-core CPU (4x2.2 GHz Cortex-A73 and 4x1.7 GHz Cortex-A53) and a Mali-G51 MP4 GPU. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Y9 Prime (2019) price in Bangladesh and its details. Its standout feature is the motorized pop-up selfie camera, which houses a 16 MP sensor. On the rear, it boasts a triple-camera setup with a 16 MP primary lens (f/1.8), an 8 MP ultrawide lens (13mm), and a 2 MP depth sensor (f/2.4). The camera system supports features like LED flash, HDR, and panorama, allowing users to capture diverse scenes. Its 4000 mAh non-removable battery ensures decent endurance, and the phone runs on Android 9.0 (Pie), upgradable to Android 10 with Magic UI 2.1. The Huawei Y9 Prime (2019) price in Bangladesh is BDT 23,999 (Official) for its 4GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage.
