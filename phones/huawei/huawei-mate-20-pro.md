The Huawei Mate 20 Pro, released in October 2018, is a flagship smartphone that stands out for its advanced features and sleek design. It boasts a 6.39-inch OLED display with a resolution of 1440 x 3120 pixels. It is powered by the Kirin 980 chipset, which was one of the first to be built on a 7nm process. One of the standout features of the Mate 20 Pro is its triple camera setup, co-engineered with Leica. This includes a 40MP primary lens, a 20MP ultra-wide-angle lens, and an 8MP telephoto lens. Additionally, the 24MP front camera ensures high-quality selfies. The Mate 20 Pro also excels in battery life, with a 4200mAh battery that supports 40W fast charging, 15W wireless charging, and even reverse wireless charging. The Huawei Mate 20 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 89,990 (Official) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. It comes in Emerald Green, Midnight Blue, Twilight, Pink Gold, and Black colors.
