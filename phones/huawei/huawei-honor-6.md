The Huawei Honor 6 is an upper mid-range smartphone that was released in August 2016, it is affordable but delivers a good performance. It has 5.0-inch IPS LCD screen resolution of 1920 x 1080 pixels. The phone is portable and light in nature, measuring approximately 139.6 x 69.7 x 7.5 mm in dimension and its weight is130 grams. Supported by Huawei HiSilicon Kirin 920 chipset with Octa Core 1.7 GHz Cortex A15 & 1.3 GHz Cortex A7 processor and Mali T628 MP4 Image Signal Processor. The back side of the mobile consists of a 13MP back facing camera to capture images and videos and a front facing 5MP camera. This phone battery is 3100 mAh. The Huawei Honor 6 price in Bangladesh is BDT 33,500 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
