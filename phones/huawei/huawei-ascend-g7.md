The Huawei Ascend G7 is an Android smartphone that was announced in September 2014. It features a 5.5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels and a 70.3% screen-to-body ratio. It’s powered by a 1.2GHz quad-core Qualcomm Snapdragon 410 processor, paired with 2GB of RAM. The 16GB internal storage can be expanded using a microSDHC card. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Ascend G7 price in Bangladesh and its details. Connectivity options include 4G LTE, Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.0, GPS, GLONASS, and NFC. The phone also features an FM radio and a microUSB 2.0 port with OTG support. Its 3000mAh non-removable battery provides decent standby time, and the phone runs on Android 4.4.2 (KitKat), upgradable to Android 6 (Marshmallow) with EMUI 3. It comes in colors like black and white. The Huawei Ascend G7 price in Bangladesh is BDT 25,990 (Official) for its 2GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage.
