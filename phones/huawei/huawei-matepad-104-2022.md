The Huawei MatePad 10.4 (2022) arrived in Bangladesh during May 2022 as a tablet which serves both professional work needs and recreational purposes. The device presents a 10.4-inch IPS LCDdisplaythat operates at 1200 x 2000 pixels resolution. The MatePad 10.4 (2022) operates using the Kirin 820 5G chipset that contains an octa-core CPU together with a Mali-G57 GPU. The Huawei MatePad 10.4 has a 8 MP rearcamerathat includes an LED flash for photography. The camera system of this device can record videos at 1080p resolution. The front section of the MatePad 10.4 features an 8 MP camera. The device comes with a built-in 7250 mAh battery that can be charged at 22.5W fast speed. The MatePad 10.4 runs on Android 10 with EMUI 10.1. Customers can purchase the device in Midnight Grey finish. The Huawei MatePad 10.4 (2022) price in Bangladesh is BDT 37,000 (Unofficial) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage.
