Huawei Nova 13 is one of the best smartphones in the market today with elegant design and great performance potential. It comes with a 6.7-inch OLED screen with a 1084 x 2412 pixels resolution and the 120Hz refresh rate. Their main processor used in the phone includes Huawei’s Kirin 8000 chipset. The rear camera comprises a 50 MP main sensor, an 8 MP ultrawide lens, and the frontside camera comes with 60 MP. The device comes with a 5000mAh battery power and it supports 100W wired fast charging. The Huawei operated on Nova 14 runs on the latest version of HarmonyOS, only that its version is 4.2. Some of the colors include Feather Sand Purple, Feather Sand White, Loddon Green and even Star Black. The Huawei Nova 13 price in Bangladesh is BDT 50,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
