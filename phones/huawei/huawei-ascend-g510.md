The Huawei Ascend G510 is a mid-range Android smartphone that was announced in January 2013. It features a 4.5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels, resulting in a pixel density of approximately 218 ppi. The phone is powered by a dual-core 1.2 GHz Qualcomm Snapdragon S4 Play processor. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Ascend G510 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of photography, the Ascend G510 sports a 5 MP rear camera with autofocus and an LED flash. The camera is capable of recording 720p videos. For selfies, there’s a VGA front-facing camera. The device runs on Android 4.1 Jelly Bean, which was a popular version of the operating system at the time of its release. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 2.1 with A2DP and EDR, GPS, and FM radio. The phone also supports NFC (market dependent). It comes with a 1700 mAh removable Li-Ion battery, providing up to 320 hours of standby time on 2G and up to 340 hours on 3G. The Huawei Ascend G510 price in Bangladesh is BDT 7,500 (Official) for its 512MB of RAM and 4GB of internal storage.
