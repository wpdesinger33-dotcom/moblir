Huawei Y9 (2018) is a mid-range smartphone that Huawei launched in April 2018 with a competent price tag. It supports a 5.93 inch IPS LCD type touchscreen with a pixel density of 403 ppi, 1080 x 2160 pixels resolution. The phone is portable and easy to carry around due to its thin profile of 157.2mm in width, 75.3 mm in breadth and 7.9mm in thickness and it also weighs 170 grams. Supported by Kirin 659 chipset and an octa-core CPU: 4 x 2.36GHz Cortex-A53, 4 x 1.7 GHz Cortex-A53 with Mali-T830 MP2 GPU. This also assures an advanced dual 16MP + 2MP rear camera and a dual 13MP + 2MP front camera. The device runs on a 4000 mAh battery. The Huawei Y9 (2018) price in Bangladesh is BDT 17,990 (Official) with 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage. It comes in Black, Gold, and Blue colors.
