The Huawei GR5 (2017) Premium boasts a sleek metal unibody design, exuding a premium feel. Its 5.5-inch Full-HD screen delivers bright and detailed visuals, thanks to its high resolution. The curved edges and ‘2.5D’ glass design ensure comfortable handling despite the large display size. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei GR5 (2017) Premium price in Bangladesh and its details. Huawei stands out by equipping the GR5 (2017) Premium with dual cameras. These allow for faster focusing and create the coveted ‘bokeh’ effect, blurring the background like professional photographs. Reviewers have praised its detailed shots and adjustable post-shoot image settings. On the front, an 8-megapixel selfie camera captures impressive self-portraits, complete with beauty mode enhancements. Powered by Huawei’s Kirin 655 Octa-core processor (a departure from the usual Snapdragon chips in budget phones), the GR5 (2017) Premium handles basic tasks smoothly. With 4 GB of RAM and 64 GB of internal storage (expandable via microSD card), it strikes a balance between performance and affordability. It packs a substantial 3340mAh battery, promising up to 1.5 days of usage. The Huawei GR5 (2017) Premium price in Bangladesh is BDT 24,400 (Official) for its 4GB of RAM and 64GB of internal storage.
