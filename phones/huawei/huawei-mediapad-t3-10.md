Huawei MediaPad T3 10 is an ultraportable tablet released in June 2017 in Bangladesh. It sports 9.6 inches IPS LCD display with support of 800 x 1280 pixels that makes the pixel density of about 157ppi. The operating system of the tablet is the Qualcomm Snapdragon 425, which has a support of a quad-core 1.4 GHz Cortex-A53 processor and Mali-450MP4 GPU. Huawei’s MediaPad T3 10 comes preinstalled with Android 7.0 Nougat with a skin on top known as EMUI 5.1. It is equipped with a 5MP rearcamerawith 2MP front camera. The tablet is powered by a 4800mAhbattery. The Huawei MediaPad T3 10 price in Bangladesh is BDT 20,499 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
