The Huawei P9 lite is an Android smartphone that was announced in April 2016. It features a 5.2-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. The phone is powered by the Kirin 650 chipset, which includes an octa-core CPU (4x2.0 GHz Cortex-A53 and 4x1.7 GHz Cortex-A53) and a Mali-T830MP2 GPU. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei P9 lite price in Bangladesh and its details. The rear camera is a 13 MP shooter with an f/2.0 aperture, LED flash, panorama, and HDR capabilities. For selfies, there’s an 8 MP front camera. The P9 lite runs Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) out of the box, upgradable to 7.0 (Nougat), with Huawei’s EMUI 4.1 skin on top. It also features a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor, accelerometer, proximity sensor, and compass. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.1, GPS, GLONASS, and NFC (available on specific models). The phone has a 3000 mAh non-removable battery. It weighs 147 grams and measures 146.8 x 72.6 x 7.5 mm. Available colors include Black, White, and Gold. The Huawei P9 lite price in Bangladesh is BDT 17,520 (Official) for its 3GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage.
