The Huawei P30 was released in March 2019, features a sleek design with a glass front and back, framed by aluminum. It weighs 165 grams and boasts a 6.1-inch OLED display with a resolution of 1080 x 2340 pixels. The screen-to-body ratio is approximately 85.8%, providing an immersive viewing experience. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei P30 price in Bangladesh and its overall details. It runs on Android 9.0 (upgradable to Android 10) with Huawei’s EMUI 10 skin. It’s powered by the Kirin 980 chipset, an octa-core processor (2x2.6 GHz Cortex-A76 & 2x1.92 GHz Cortex-A76 & 4x1.8 GHz Cortex-A55), and Mali-G76 MP10 GPU. You’ll find different memory configurations: 64GB with 8GB RAM, 128GB with 6GB RAM, 128GB with 8GB RAM, and 256GB with 8GB RAM. The standout feature is its triple-camera setup co-engineered with Leica. The rear cameras include a 40 MP wide lens (f/1.8), an 8 MP telephoto lens (3x optical zoom), and a 16 MP ultra-wide lens. The front-facing camera is a 32 MP. Expect impressive photo and video capabilities, including 4K recording at 30fps.
