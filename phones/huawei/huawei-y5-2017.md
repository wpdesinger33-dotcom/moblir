The Huawei Y5 (2019) is an entry-level Android smartphone that was announced in April 2019. It features a 5.71-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1520 pixels, resulting in a pixel density of approximately 295 ppi. The phone is powered by the Mediatek MT6761 Helio A22 chipset, which includes a quad-core 2.0 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and a PowerVR GE8320 GPU. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Y5 (2019) price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of photography, the Huawei Y5 (2019) sports a single 13 MP rear camera with an f/1.8 aperture, PDAF, LED flash, HDR, and panorama capabilities. For selfies, there’s a 5 MP front camera with an f/2.2 aperture. The phone runs on Android 9.0 (Pie) with Huawei’s EMUI 9.1 skin on top. Other notable features include a 3020 mAh non-removable battery, support for 4G LTE connectivity, Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 5.0, GPS, GLONASS, BDS, FM radio, and a microUSB 2.0 port. It is available in colors like Midnight Black, Modern Black, Sapphire Blue, and Amber Brown. The Huawei Y5 (2019) price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,990 (Official) for its 2GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage.
