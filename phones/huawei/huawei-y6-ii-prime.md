The Huawei Y6 II Prime is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers a balanced mix of performance and features. It features a 5.5” IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The phone is powered by a HiSilicon Kirin 620 chipset. The device runs on Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) with Huawei’s EMUI skin, offering a user-friendly interface and a range of customization options. The phone features a 13MP rear camera with PDAF for quick focusing and an 8MP front camera, both equipped with LED flash. The 3000mAh battery provides adequate power to last through a typical day of usage. Connectivity options on the Huawei Y6 II Prime include Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n), Bluetooth 4.2, GPS, and a microUSB 2.0 port. The Huawei Y6 II Prime price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,900 (Official) with 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. With its sleek design, available in black, gold, and blue colors.
