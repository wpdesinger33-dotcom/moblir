Huawei Y6s was released in May, 2020 and is an economy smartphone with standard features and a good processor. The handset comes with 6.09 inches IPS LCD, and the resolution of the screen is 720 X 1560 pixels. The smartphone operates on MediaTek Helio P35 chip which is an octa-core processor and accompanied by up to PowerVR GE8320 GPU. On the appearance area, the Y6s is well designed by coming in a modern looking smartphone that has a glass front, a body that is made of black or pale blue color plastic and aluminum frame. The phone comes with LED flash with a 13MP rear camera, and a front camera of 8MP. It is powered by a 3020mAh battery that guarantees truly-long usage. The Huawei Y6s price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,500 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage.
