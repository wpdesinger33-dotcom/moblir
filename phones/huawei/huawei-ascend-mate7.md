The Huawei Ascend Mate7, released in October 2014, is a notable entry in the phablet category, distinguished by its large 6-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. This device features a sleek design with an aluminum back and frame, giving it a premium feel. The screen-to-body ratio is impressive at approximately 77.6%, thanks to its narrow bezels. The phone is powered by the HiSilicon Kirin 925 chipset, an octa-core processor that combines four Cortex-A15 cores clocked at 1.8 GHz and four Cortex-A7 cores at 1.3 GHz, paired with a Mali-T628MP4 GPU. It originally ran on Android 4.4.2 KitKat, upgradable to Android 6.0 Marshmallow, with Huawei’s EMUI 3.1 skin on top. It is equipped with a 13 MP rear camera featuring an f/2.0 aperture and autofocus, capable of recording 1080p videos at 30fps. The front camera is 5 MP, suitable for selfies. One of the standout features of this device is its large 4100 mAh non-removable battery, which offers substantial battery life, making it ideal for heavy users. The Huawei Ascend Mate7 price in Bangladesh is BDT 52,750 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in Obsidian black, Moonlight silver, and Amber gold colors.
