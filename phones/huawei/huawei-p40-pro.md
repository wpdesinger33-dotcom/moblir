The Huawei P40 Pro is a flagship smartphone that boasts impressive features. Here we will share Huawei P40 Pro price in Bangladesh and specs. The P40 Pro features a 6.58-inch Quad-Curve OLED display with a resolution of 1200 x 2640 pixels and a smooth 90Hz refresh rate. Its sleek design includes a glass front and back with an aluminum frame, and it’s IP68 dust and water-resistant. Under the hood, the Kirin 990 5G chipset. While it runs on Android 10 with EMUI 10.1. The quad-camera setup on the rear includes a 50 MP main sensor, a 40 MP ultrawide lens, a 12 MP periscope telephoto lens. The front camera is a 32 MP. Powering the P40 Pro is a 4200 mAh battery, which supports 40W wired charging, 27W wireless charging. The Huawei P40 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 109,999 for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage. It is an official variant. This phone has an unofficial variant, its price at BDT 68,000. It’s available in colors like Silver Frost, Blush Gold, and Deep Sea Blue.
