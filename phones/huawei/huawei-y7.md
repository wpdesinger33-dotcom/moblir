Huawei Y7 is an affordable Smartphone that aims to strike a balance within their specifications and functionality. It was introduced in July 2017 in Bangladesh. It offers a body built and comes with a 5.5-inch full high definition IPS LCD display with a pixel density of 720 x 1280. It runs a Qualcomm Snapdragon 435 SoC that is an octa-core 1.4 GHz Cortex-A53 processor. Regarding design the Y7 is slim and lightweight it has a weight of 165 gm and dimensions of 153.6 x 76.4 x 8.4 mm. The phone sports a 12MP + dual LED flash rear camera while the frontal part packs an 8MP front facing camera. The battery power placed on the phone is 4000mAh and this allows for all day usage. The Huawei Y7 price in Bangladesh is BDT 13,990 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
