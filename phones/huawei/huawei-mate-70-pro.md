Huawei Mate 70 Pro is one of the best smartphones, having an impressive display of 6.9-inches LTPO OLED screen with the resolution of 1316 x 2832 pixels and 120 Hz refresh rate. The phone is running on the Kirin 9000S chipset. The Mate 70 Pro through the inclusion of a triple-lens setup that consists of a 50MP main rearcamera, a 40MP super-wide angle lens, and 48MP telephoto periscope lens. For selfie taking, it comprises a 13MP front facing camera. The phone has 5500mAhbatterywith 100W fast wired charging, 80W wirelessly charging, as well as wireless reverse charging at 18W. Others being in-display fingerprint sensor, IP68 rated dust/water resistant, operating system HarmonyOS 4.0. It comes in colours such as Obsidian Black, Snow White, Spruce Green and Hyacinth Purple. The Huawei Mate 70 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 195,000 (Unofficial) for 12GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage.
