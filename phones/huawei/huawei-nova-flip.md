The Huawei nova Flip is the latest addition to Huawei’s foldable lineup, and it brings a stylish and compact design to the table. On the outside, you’ll find a square 2.14-inch OLED cover display, which serves as a viewfinder for the main camera and can run first-party apps like weather, music, and calendar. But the real magic happens when you open it up—the 6.94-inch LTPO OLED display with FHD+ resolution and a 1-120Hz refresh rate. Speaking of cameras, the nova Flip boasts a 50MP main camera, along with an 8MP ultrawide lens and a 32MP front-facing camera. It’s powered by the Kirin 8000 chip. HarmonyOS 4.2 handles the software side. Battery life won’t be a concern—the nova Flip packs a 4,400mAh battery with 66W wired charging. The Huawei nova Flip price in Bangladesh is BDT 90,000 (Exp) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage. It comes in New Green, Sakura Pink, Zero White, and Starry Black.
