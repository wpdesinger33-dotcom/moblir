The Huawei Nova 3e is a mid-range smartphone that offers a blend of style and functionality. The design of the Nova 3e is reminiscent of the more premium Huawei P20, with a narrow 19:9 display ratio that makes it comfortable to hold. It measures 71.2 mm in width, 148.6 mm in height, and has a thickness of 7.4 mm. The device weighs 145 g and comes in colors like black, blue, pink, and gold. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Nova 3e price in Bangladesh and its details. It packs a Huawei HiSilicon KIRIN 659 chipset with an octa-core CPU (4x 2.36 GHz ARM Cortex-A53 and 4x 1.7 GHz ARM Cortex-A53) and an ARM Mali-T830 MP2 GPU. The display is a 5.8-inch IPS LCD panel with a resolution of 1080 x 2280 pixels. The phone runs on Android 8.0 Oreo with Huawei’s EMUI 8.0 skin. For photography, it features a 16 MP main camera and a 16 MP selfie camera. Its 3000 mAh battery keeps the Nova 3e powered throughout the day. The Huawei Nova 3e price in Bangladesh is BDT 27,990 (Official) for its 4GB of RAM and 64GB of internal storage.
