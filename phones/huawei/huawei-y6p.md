The Huawei Y6p is a budget-friendly Android smartphone that offers a balance of features and affordability. Here we will share Huawei Y6p price in Bangladesh and specs. Under the hood, it’s powered by the MediaTek Helio P22 (MT6762) SoC, which includes an octa-core CPU (4x 2.0 GHz ARM Cortex-A53 and 4x 1.5 GHz ARM Cortex-A53) and an IMG PowerVR GE8320 GPU clocked at 650 MHz. The display is a 6.3-inch IPS panel with a resolution of 720 x 1600 pixels. The 13 MP rear camera captures acceptable photos, and the 8 MP front camera. The standout feature is the massive 5000 mAh Li-Polymer battery, ensuring extended usage without frequent charging. The Y6p runs on Android 10 with Huawei’s EMUI 10.1. The Huawei Y6p price in Bangladesh is BDT 16,999 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage. This model comes in Phantom Purple, Emerald Green, and Midnight Black colors.
