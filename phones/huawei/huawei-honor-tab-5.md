The Huawei Honor Tab 5 launched in March 2019 is one of the tablets which are the perfect amalgamation of high performance and daily usage. It has an 8.0-inch IPS LCDdisplaywith the resolution of 1920 x 1200 pixels. The tablet runs on the Hisilicon Kirin 710 processor equipped with the support for expansion through microSD up to 512GB. The Honor Tab 5 has a 5100mAhbatterywhich supports 10W fast charging. The device has both an 8MP rear camera and an 8MP frontcamera. This tablet operates on Android 9(Pie) with Huawei's custom Magic 2 U. Available in Silver and Blue colors. The Huawei Honor Tab 5 price in Bangladesh is BDT 19,990 (Unofficial) for 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage.
