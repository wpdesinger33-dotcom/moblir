The Huawei Mate XT Ultimate is a groundbreaking smartphone that has captured attention with its innovative design and advanced features. As the world’s first triple-foldable phone, it offers a unique experience by transforming from a standard phone into a dual or triple screen mode. When fully unfolded, the device boasts a 10.2-inch display with resolution of 2232x3184 pexels, providing a tablet-like experience. Under the hood, the Mate XT Ultimate is powered by HarmonyOS 4.2. The phone features a triple camera setup with a 50MP main sensor, a 12MP periscope telephoto lens, and a 12MP ultrawide lens, allowing for high-quality photography in various scenarios. The 5600mAh battery supports 66W wired and 50W wireless charging, ensuring that the device can be quickly recharged. The Huawei Mate XT Ultimate price in Bangladesh is BDT 430,000 (Unofficial) for 16GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage. It comes in Red, and Black colors.
