The Huawei MediaPad X1 entered Bangladesh markets in April 2014 as a stylish tablet device. It features a 7-inch IPS LCD display which displays 1200 x 1920 pixel resolution at 323 ppi density. This device runs on the HiSilicon Kirin 910 chipset which contains a quad-core 1.6 GHz Cortex-A9 processor together with a Mali-450MP4 GPU. The MediaPad X1 enables users to connect through Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n and Bluetooth 4.0 together with 3G and 4G LTE networks. It has a 13MP rear camera alongside a 5MP front camera in the device. The key selling point of this device lies in its 5000mAh battery capacity. The MediaPad X1 operates with Android 4.2.2 Jelly Bean software but consumers can upgrade it to Android 4.4.2 KitKat. The Huawei MediaPad X1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 39,990 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
