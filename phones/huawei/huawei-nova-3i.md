The Huawei Nova 3i is an upper mid-range Android smartphone that was launched in July 2018. Here we will share Huawei Nova 3i price in Bangladesh and features. It features a 6.3-inch display with a 1080 x 2340 pixel resolution. Powered by the Huawei Kirin 710 processor. It comes with 4GB of RAM, and 128GB of internal storage. The quad-camera system, with a 16 MP primary lens and a 2 MP depth sensor on the back, along with a 24 MP wide-angle lens and another 2 MP depth sensor for selfies. The 3340mAh battery with 10W charging is supported.
