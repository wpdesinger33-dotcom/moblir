Huawei has officially introduced Mate 80 in China On 25 November 2025 with a combination of improved performance and stunning power-saving technology to the flagship line. Some leaks suggest that the phone may launch in Global, but the company has not officially confirmed it yet. The phone operates on HarmonyOS 6.0 and carries on with Huawei impetus of its own Kirin chipset. Among the most notable features is the new Outdoor Mode which is aimed at the user who spends a lot of time without a charger. According to Huawei, this mode can last several days, and it boasts of 14 days on a full charge. The 50MP rear camera on the Mate 80 is also present and the phone comes as a part of the new collection of Huawei with a smoother software experience and greater efficiency.
