The Huawei Nova 5T is an Android smartphone that was announced in August 2019. It features a 6.26-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 2340 pixels, resulting in a pixel density of approximately 412 ppi. The phone’s design includes a glass front and back with an aluminum frame, giving it a sleek appearance. Keep reading this article to know about the Huawei Nova 5T price in Bangladesh and its overall details. In terms of performance, the Nova 5T is powered by the Kirin 980 chipset, built on a 7nm process. It boasts an octa-core CPU configuration, including two high-performance Cortex-A76 cores clocked at 2.6 GHz, two additional Cortex-A76 cores at 1.92 GHz, and four power-efficient Cortex-A55 cores at 1.8 GHz. The GPU is the Mali-G76 MP10. If you look at its camera, it comes with a quad-camera setup on the rear that includes 48MP primary lens, 16MP ultra-wide angle, 2MP macro lens, and 2MP depth sensor. For selfies, there’s a 32 MP front-facing camera with an f/2.0 aperture. The phone supports 4K video recording at 30fps and 1080p video at 30/60fps, with gyro-based electronic image stabilization (EIS).
