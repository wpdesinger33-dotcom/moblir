The Huawei Mate 40 Pro stands as a testament to Huawei’s commitment to pushing the boundaries of smartphone technology, despite the challenges posed by the absence of Google Play Services. Here we will share Huawei Mate 40 Pro price in Bangladesh and all spec. The device is powered by Kirin 9000 5G chipset with a Mali-G78 MP24 GPU. It offers 512 GB of storage and 8 GB of RAM. The phone features a 6.76-inch OLED display with HDR10 support and a 90Hz refresh rate. The screen’s resolution is 1344x2772 pixels. The Mate 40 Pro also boasts IP68 dust and water resistance. It has a triple-camera setup with a 50 MP main sensor, 12 MP periscope telephoto lens offering 5x optical zoom, and a 20 MP ultrawide lens. For selfies 13 MP front-facing camera. It has 4400 mAh battery with 66W fast charging.
