The Huawei Ascend Y520, released in December 2014, is an entry-level Android smartphone designed to cater to basic mobile needs. It features a 4.5-inch TFT display with a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels, providing a modest visual experience. The device is powered by a dual-core 1.3 GHz Cortex-A7 processor. In terms of photography, the Huawei Ascend Y520 is equipped with a 5-megapixel rear camera with autofocus and LED flash, capable of recording 480p videos. The front-facing camera is a VGA shooter, suitable for basic video calls and selfies. The device runs on Android 4.4 KitKat OS. It is powered by a removable 1750 mAh Li-Ion battery, which offers decent battery life for its specifications. The Huawei Ascend Y520 price in Bangladesh is BDT 7,990 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage. It is available in multiple colors, including black, white, blue, and pink.
