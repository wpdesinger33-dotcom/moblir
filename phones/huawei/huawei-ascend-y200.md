Huawei Ascend Y200 was released in May 2012 as an entry level smartphone that focuses on the users’ essentials of calling, texting and entertainment. It has a 3.5 Inches of IPS LCD Capacitive Touchscreen with a pixel density of 215 pixel per inch density for your screen resolution of 320 x 480 pixels. The phone has a small and sleek body and its dimensions are 116.9 x 60.4 x 11.7 mm and the phone has a weight of 120 grams. This handset is operated by a Qualcomm Snapdragon S1 processor with an 800 MHz. It also has a 3.2MP rear facing camera for taking pictures and making video calls at 480p. It has a 1400 mAh battery. The Huawei Ascend Y200 price in Bangladesh is BDT 3,000 (Official) with 256MB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage.
