The Cubot KingKong ES serves the market as a rugged smartphone which presents durability alongside reliable performance capabilities. The 6.56-inch IPS LCD display of the KingKong ES shows high-resolution 720 x 1612 pixels. The KingKong ES safety relies on Gorilla Glass which protects both the display from scratches and impact. This rugged smartphone performs via the Unisoc T606 processor. The Cubot KingKong ES standout is its powerful 5100mAh battery with 33W fast charging capabilities. With an IP68/IP69K certification the device demonstrates its ability to withstand both dust penetration and submergence in water. The device maintains total operational functionality when submerged to depths over 1.5 meters for periods lasting up to 30 minutes. The KingKong ES features a triple-camera system consisting of a 48MP main camera together with 2MP macro lens and 0.08MP camera. The phone provides you with a 16MP front facing camera. The phone system operates on Android 14. The Cubot KingKong ES price in Bangladesh is BDT 33,000 (Unofficial) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage.
