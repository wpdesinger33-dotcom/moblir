The Cubot KingKong Power 3 is a powerful smartphone. This device was announced in September 2024 and is expected to be released in October 2024. This device is built to be strong with IP68/IP69K for dust and water resistance. It has a 6.72 inches FHD+ IPS LCD screen with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is also equipped with the Mediatek MT8781 octa-core processor although most of its functions are hidden under the hood. The smartphone utilizes Android 14 as its operating system. Among them, the highlight is the huge battery of 10200mAh, and it supports 33W flash charging. Camera lovers will especially love the two back cameras of the KingKong Power 3 which has a 100MP main camera and 5MP macro lens as well as a 32 MP selfie camera. The Cubot KingKong Power 3 price in Bangladesh is BDT 40,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage. It is available in Black.
