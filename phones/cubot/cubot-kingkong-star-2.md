The Cubot KingKong Star 2 is a powerful smartphone built for the harshest environment with the best performance. It has a large size of 6.72 inches IPS LCD display with 1080 x 2400 pixels resolution and Corning Gorilla Glass. The screen features a refresh rate of 144Hz and peak brightness of 1500 nits. Furthermore, there is a front facing 1.85 inch display on the phone. It runs on MediaTek Dimensity 8200 chip. Android 14 is the operating system on the device. It has a heavy build and is certified with IP68/IP69K, therefore, protected against water and dust. For those who like to take pictures, there is a main chamber with 100 MP and a macro chamber with 5 MP, and the front chamber is 32 MP. The 5100mAh battery is also capable of 33W fast charging. The Cubot KingKong Star 2 price in Bangladesh is BDT 40,000 (Expected) with 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
