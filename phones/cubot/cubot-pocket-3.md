The Cubot Pocket 3 brings together a miniaturized smartphone body with affordable purchase pricing. Designed with portability in mind. The smartphone displays its content on a 4.5-inch IPS LCD screen which reaches a resolution of 480 x 1170 pixels. The Pocket 3 operates via the MediaTek Helio G85 processor. Android 12 operates on the Pocket 3 to provide intuitive use for its users. This smartphone has a 20MP main camera paired with a 5MP front camera. This phone stands out through its combination of a large 3000mAh battery capable of receiving 18W fast charging speed. Dust and water resistance features are enabled through IP68 certification of the Cubot Pocket 3. The Cubot Pocket 3 price in Bangladesh is BDT 30,000 (Unofficial) for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage.
