The Cubot KingKong X Pro which was unveiled in November 2024 is tough. It is built with a 6.72 inches IPS LCD screen and has a 144Hz refresh rate. MediaTek Dimensity 8200 is the chipset which runs the device, and an octa-core CPU and Mali-G610 MC6 GPU. The device comes with a 10,200mAh battery capacity that is fast charge capable at 33 watts. The primary camera system comprises a 100MP wide lens and a 5MP macro lens and a 32MP selfie camera. The phone is also dust and water resistant up to the IP68 and IP69K standards as well as it is MIL-STD-810H certified. The Cubot KingKong X Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 40,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of ROM. It comes in only Black colors.
