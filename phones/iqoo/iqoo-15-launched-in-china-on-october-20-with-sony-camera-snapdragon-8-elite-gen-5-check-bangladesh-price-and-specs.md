The iQOO has officially released the flagship iQOO 15 in China on 20 October 2025, which is powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset. Rumor has it that this phone can officially launch in Bangladesh. The phone is available in four colors and five storage options, one of them being a special iQOO 15 Honor of Kings 10th Anniversary Collector's Edition with 16GB RAM and 512GB storage. It has a 6.85-inch Samsung M14 AMOLED display up to 144Hz refresh. The launch created an instant impression as more than 142,000 units were sold in the first four hours, which was 87 percent higher than the last generation, establishing a new sales record in the history of iQOO. The phone was immediately sold on the release, and it became one of the best-selling flagships in the history of the brand. So let's know the iQOO 15 price in Bangladesh and specifications.
