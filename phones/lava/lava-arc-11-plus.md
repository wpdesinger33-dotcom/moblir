The Lava ARC 11 Plus is a feature phone. It supports a basic 1.8inch TFT display with 128 x 160 pixel resolution. The phone is packed with a low-end processor coming with support of 32MB RAM and 24MB ROM and supporting a micro SD card. The ARC 11 Plus has a 0.3MP rear camera. The smartphone also comes with a removable 1000mAh battery. It has the flexibility of dual SIM to help users to use two different numbers on the same SIM card. The Lava ARC 11 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,190 (Official).
