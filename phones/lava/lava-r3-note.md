The Lava R3 Note released a budget-friendly smartphone. Despite its affordable price point, the R3 Note offers many features and an appealing design. It runs Android 8.1 (Oreo) and features a 6.22-inch IPS TFT display with a 720 x 1440 pixel resolution, 18:9 aspect ratio, and 197 PPI density. The R3 Note is equipped with a 13MP rear camera with LED flash and an 8-megapixel front-facing camera. Powering the phone is a 1.3GHz quad-core processor paired with an ARM-based chipset. Connectivity options include 3G, LTE, Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.2, MicroUSB, Wi-Fi Direct, and mobile hotspot. A non-removable 3260 mAh Li-Ion battery provides power. The Lava R3 Note price in Bangladesh is BDT 11,990 (official) with 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage. It comes in Illusion Blue, and Champagne Gold colors.
