Lava Iris 870 is another android smartphone which comes with the low price range for the Bangladeshi market which was launched in May 2016. It has a comfortable 5.0-inch IPS LCD display resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The device runs with MediaTek MT6735 chipset and has one quad-core 1.3 GHz Cortex-A53 Processor and Mali-400 GPU. The phone has a rear camera of 8MP with LED flash and front camera of 5MP. The 2700mAh battery life. The device is Android 6.0 Marshmallow. The device comes in black, white, silver, and gold colors, and is built to be robust with Corning’s Gorilla Glass 3. The Lava Iris 870 price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,990 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of ROM.
