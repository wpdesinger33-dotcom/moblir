The Lava Blaze 2 is an affordable mobile phone which poses no compromise in functionality. It has an IPS LCD panel of 6.5-inch in size with support for up to 90Hz refresh rate. The screen has a good definition of 720 x 1600 pixels. The Lava Blaze 2 engine is the Unisoc Tiger T616 that is good enough for regular usage. The phone measures just 154 x 75 x 7.7mm and weighs 203 grams. A non-removable 5000 mAh battery. Fast charging is possible at 18W. One will find a 50MP main camera and an 8MP front camera. The Lava Blaze 2 price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,999 (Official) with 6GB of RAM and 128GB of ROM.
