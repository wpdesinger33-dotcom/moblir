The Lava O3 Pro is one of the most basic smartphones with some added features. The device comes with a 6.56 inches HD+ LCD screen, and 90Hz refresh rate that gives a good quality and smooth visuals. The device comes with the Unisoc T606 as its processor. For photography, there is a 50MP main camera and an 8MP front facing camera. The device is based on Android 14. It also has a 5000mAh battery and comes with support for 10W fast charging. The Lava O3 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,000 (Expected) for 4GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage.
