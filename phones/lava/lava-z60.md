The Lava Z60 is an Android smartphone that was announced in September 2017. It features a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels. The phone is powered by a MediaTek MT6737M chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.1 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and an ARM Mali-T720MP2 GPU. In terms of cameras, the Z60 sports a 5 MP primary camera with an f/2.0 aperture. The front-facing camera is also 5 MP and has an LED flash for better selfies. The device runs on Android 7.0 Nougat and is equipped with a 2500 mAh non-removable battery. The Lava Z60 price in Bangladesh is BDT 7690 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 16G  of phone storage. It comes in two color variants: Gold and Black.
