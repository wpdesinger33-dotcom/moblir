The Lava A3 is a budget-friendly smartphone designed for everyday use. With its sleek design and compact form factor, it appeals to users seeking affordability without compromising on essential features. It comes with a minimalist design, featuring a polycarbonate body with rounded edges. Its 6.2-inch HD+ display provides vibrant colors and decent viewing angles, making it suitable for browsing, social media, and light multimedia consumption. Keep reading this to know about the Lava A3 price in Bangladesh and its details. It comes with a quad-core MediaTek processor clocked at 1.5 GHz, paired with 3 GB of RAM. While not a powerhouse, it handles basic tasks smoothly, such as web browsing, messaging, and casual gaming. The rear camera setup consists of a 13 MP primary lens and a 2 MP depth sensor. It captures decent photos in well-lit conditions, but struggles in low-light scenarios. The 5 MP front camera is adequate for selfies. A 4000 mAh battery powers the Lava A3, providing all-day usage on a single charge. It’s ideal for users who need a reliable device for work or entertainment without frequent recharging. It runs on Android 10 (Go Edition), optimized for smoother performance on entry-level hardware.
