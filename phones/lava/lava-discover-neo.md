The Lava Discover Neo is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers essential features for everyday use. Released in October 2013, it features a 3.5-inch TFT display with a resolution of 320 x 480 pixels. The phone is powered by a Cortex A7 processor. It includes a 1.3 MP rear camera. The device is equipped with a 1000 mAh Li-ion battery. The Lava Discover Neo supports dual SIM functionality, Bluetooth and GPS, offering versatile connectivity options. Additionally, it comes with a microSD card slot for expandable memory up to 8 GB. The phone comes in Black, and Grey colors. The Lava Discover Neo price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,200 (Official).
