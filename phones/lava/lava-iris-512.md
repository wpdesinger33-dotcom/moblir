The LAVA Iris 512 is an entry-level smartphone that offers basic features at an affordable price. It comes with a compact design with a 10.16 cm (4-inch) WVGA display. While the resolution is modest at 800 x 480 pixels, it provides decent clarity for everyday tasks. Keep reading this article to know about the LAVA Iris 512 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of performance, the Iris 512 houses a dual-core processor clocked at 1 GHz. It’s not a powerhouse, but it handles essential functions like web browsing, social media, and light apps without major hiccups. The 512 MB RAM ensures smooth multitasking. It features a 2 MP rear camera. While it won’t compete with flagship devices, it’s adequate for capturing everyday moments. The phone also includes a 2 MP front camera for selfies. With 4 GB of internal storage, you’ll need to manage your apps and media judiciously. However, the microSD card slot allows for expansion. The phone supports 3G connectivity, Wi-Fi, and GPS. A 1400 mAh Li-Ion battery powers the device. While it won’t last days on a single charge, it should get you through a day of moderate usage.
