The Lava Iris 400s, released in August 2014, is an Android smartphone with a compact 4.0-inch IPS LCD display. It features a 5 MP primary camera with LED flash and panorama capabilities. The phone runs on Android 4.4.2 (KitKat) and is powered by a dual-core 1.2 GHz processor. While the battery capacity is 1400 mAh, the phone’s design is sturdy, and it has dual SIM support. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 3.0, and GPS. The Lava Iris 400s price in Bangladesh is BDT 5990 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
