The Lava Pixel V2 Plus is a modest choice for those on a tight budget, especially if camera features are a priority. September 2015, it comes with a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display, featuring a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The phone runs on Android 5.1 (Lollipop) with the Android One program. Powered by MediaTek MT6735 quad-core processor clocked at 1.0 GHz. The camera setup includes a 13 MP back camera. For selfies, 8 MP front camera with an LED flash. The battery is a 2500 mAh Li-Po unit, providing up to 24 hours and 40 minutes of talk time on 2G networks. The Lava Pixel V2 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 10990 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. Available in Icy White and Royal Black colors.
