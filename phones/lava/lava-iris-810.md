The Lava Iris 810 is a budget-friendly smartphone that was announced in May 2016. It features a 5-inch IPS FWVGA touchscreen display with a resolution of 854 x 480 pixels. The phone weighs 154.7 grams and supports dual SIM cards. In terms of photography, the Iris 810 sports a 5-megapixel primary camera with LED flash and digital zoom, along with a 2-megapixel front-facing camera that also has an LED flash. The video recording capability is at HD resolution. Keep reading this article to know about the Lava Iris 810 price in Bangladesh and its details. It runs on Android Lollipop v5.1.1 and is powered by a quad-core 1.3 GHz CPU. The phone comes with 1 GB of RAM and 8 GB of internal storage, which can be expanded up to 32 GB using a microSD card. Connectivity options include GPRS, Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Wi-Fi Direct, hotspot, and Bluetooth v4.1. Its battery capacity is 2000 mAh, and the available colors are black and white. The Lava Iris 810 price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,290 (Official) for its 1GB of RAM and 8GB of internal storage.
