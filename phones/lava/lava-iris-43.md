The Lava Iris 43 was released in August, 2019 in Bangladesh. It is an entry-level smartphone. It has a 5.0-inch display with a resolution of 480 x 960 pixels and an 18:9 aspect ratio. The device is powered by Android 8.1 (Oreo) Go Edition. Camera specifications include a 5MP rear camera with LED flash and a 2MPl front camera. Video can be recorded at 720p resolution and 30 frames per second. The 1.3GHz quad-core SC7731E chipset provides connectivity options such as 3G, LTE, Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.2, microUSB, and mobile hotspot. A non-removable 2000 mAh lithium-ion battery powers the smartphone. The Lava Iris 43 price in Bangladesh is BDT 3,890 (official) for 1GB of GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in Red, Blue, and Black colors.
