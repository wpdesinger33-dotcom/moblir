The Lava Iris 250 is an Android smartphone that was announced in August 2014. It features a 4.0-inch TFT display with a resolution of 480 x 800 pixels. This smartphone runs on Android 4.4.2 (KitKat) and is powered by a dual-core 1.0 GHz processor. It comes with 512MB of RAM and 4GB of internal storage, which can be expanded using a microSDHC card. Keep reading this article to know about the Lava Iris 250 price in Bangladesh. In terms of photography, the Iris 250 sports a 3.15 MP primary camera with dual-LED flash and a VGA front-facing camera for selfies. The device supports Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n, dual-band Bluetooth 2.1, and FM radio. It also includes basic sensors like an accelerometer and proximity sensor. The removable 1450 mAh Li-Ion battery provides up to 250 hours of standby time and up to 11 hours and 30 minutes of talk time. Available in colors like Black, White, and Purple, the Lava Iris 250 was an entry-level smartphone with modest specifications. The Lava Iris 250 price in Bangladesh is BDT 5,290 for its 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
