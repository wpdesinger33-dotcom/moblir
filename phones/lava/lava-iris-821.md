The Lava Iris 821, released in June 2016, is a budget-friendly smartphone. It features a 5-inch TFT display with a resolution of 720x1280 pixels. The phone runs on the Android operating system. Under the hood, the Iris 821 is powered by an Octa-core processor. In terms of photography, the rear camera boasts an 8-megapixel sensor, while the selfie camera captures images at 5 megapixels. This device is equipped with a 2500 mAh battery. The Lava Iris 821 price in Bangladesh is BDT 6999 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
