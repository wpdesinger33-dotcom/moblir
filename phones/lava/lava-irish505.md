Lava Iris 505 is an entry level smartphone unveiled in August 2013 in the Bangladesh market. It has a screen size of 5.0 inches TFT and has screen resolution of 480 x 854 pixels. The operational system and other running functions rely on hardware including a dual core 1.2 GHz Cortex A7, and Mali-400 GPU. The phone has a 2MP rear camera with LED flash and 2MP front camera for selfie lovers. The battery power of 2000 mAh with up to 6 hours talktime on 3G. The gadget is Android 4.2 Jelly Bean. It is black and comes in a slim profile with Corning Gorilla Glass protection. The Lava Iris 505 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,945 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
