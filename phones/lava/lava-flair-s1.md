The Lava Flair S1 offers basic features suitable for everyday use, making it an affordable choice for users seeking a simple smartphone experience. It was launched in March 2016. It features a 4.50-inch touchscreen display with a resolution of 480x854 pixels. Under the hood, it is powered by a 1.3GHz dual-core processor. The phone runs on Android 5.1 and is equipped with a 1750mAh removable battery. In terms of photography, the Lava Flair S1 sports a 3.2-megapixel rear camera and a 0.3-megapixel front camera for selfies. The Lava Flair S1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 4690 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. It comes in Black and Champagne Gold color options.
