The Lava Iris Atom 2, announced in August 2015, features a compact 4.5-inch TFT display with a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels. Powered by a 1.3 GHz quad-core processor, it comes with 512 MB of RAM and 8 GB of internal storage (expandable via microSD card). The phone runs Android 4.4.2 (KitKat) and is equipped with a removable 1750 mAh Li-Ion battery. Keep reading this article to know about the Lava Iris Atom 2 price in Bangladesh and its details. On the camera front, the Iris Atom 2 sports a 5 MP primary camera with LED flash and a VGA front-facing camera. While the Lava Iris Atom 2 offers basic functionality, its modest specifications make it suitable for light usage. Overall, it’s an entry-level device with a focus on affordability.
