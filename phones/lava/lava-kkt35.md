The Lava KKT35+ is an affordable smartphone with basic features anyone would need in their day to day life. It has a 2.4inch TFT display with a graphic resolution of 240 x 320 pixels. It has 0.3 MP rear cameras and has a thickness of 8.5mm. The battery capacity of the device is 1400 mAh Li-ion battery. Also, it has a microSD card slot for a memory card of up to 8GB and it supports GSM, GPRS and EDGE. The Lava KKT35+ price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,500 (Official).
