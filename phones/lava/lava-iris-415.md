The Lava Iris 415, also known as the Lava 3G 415, is a budget-friendly smartphone running Android 4.2.2 (Jelly Bean). It features a compact design with dimensions of 125 x 64 x 10.7 mm and weighs 119 grams. The 4.0-inch  TFT display offers a resolution of 480x800 pixels, providing a pixel density of 233 PPI. Under the hood, the Lava Iris 415 is powered by a dual-core 1.0 GHz processor (MT6572 chipset). The 1500 mAh Li-ion battery provides moderate usage time. In terms of photography, the Lava Iris 415 features a 5 MP primary camera with LED flash, HD video recording, and digital zoom. There’s also a 0.3 MP front-facing camera. The Lava Iris 415 price in Bangladesh is BDT 5790 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage. It comes in Grey and Black colors.
