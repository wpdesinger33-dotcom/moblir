A high-performance smartphone was launched in October with 8 GB of RAM and 256 GB storage space named the Lava Agni 3 (8GB+256GB). There is another version called Lava Agni 3 which comes with an 8GB 128GB configuration. The prime change between the two versions is the amount of built in storage space. These two versions feature similar display, processor, RAM, camera and battery as the main components meaning that their performance won’t differ significantly. Its 6.78-inch AMOLED panel has a resolution of 1200 x 2652 pixels, besides a 120Hz refresh rate. It is run by the MediaTek Dimensity 7300X chipset. It has a 50MP OIS main camera, 8MP telephoto lens and a 8MP ultra-wide lens . The Agni 3 also packs a 16MP front facing camera.
