The Lava A7 Torch is a multimedia mobile phone with a lot of features. It was officially released on 02 September 2024 in Bangladesh. It has a 2.4 inches QVGA screen. It has a basic 0.3MP primary camera, and dual SIM option so that users can use two numbers on the same phone. Bluetooth, FM with recording, 3.5mm audio jack are also incorporated for better music output. It is equipped with a 2575mAh battery. This phone comes with Blue, and Black colors. The Lava A7 Torch price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,650 (Official) for 24MB of RAM and 32MB of ROM.
