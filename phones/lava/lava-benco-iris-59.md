The Lava Benco Iris 59 is an affordable smartphone that offers decent features. It comes with a 6.09-inch IPS touchscreen display with a resolution of 720 x 1520 pixels. The phone runs on Android 9.0 (Pie) (Go Edition) and is powered by a Quad-core Unisoc SC7731e processor clocked at 1.3 GHz. Keep reading this article to know about the Lava Benco Iris 59 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of cameras, the Benco Iris 59 features a dual 8 MP primary camera setup on the back, along with an LED flash. On the front, there’s a 5 MP selfie camera. While it won’t compete with flagship devices, it’s suitable for everyday photography and video calls. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth (v4.2), and GPS with A-GPS. The phone also supports dual SIM cards. It’s equipped with a 4000 mAh lithium-ion battery, which should provide decent battery life. The Lava Benco Iris 59 price in Bangladesh is BDT 5,999 for its 1GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage.
