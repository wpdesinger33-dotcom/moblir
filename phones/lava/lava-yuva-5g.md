The Lava Yuva 5G is a cheap smartphone that has stunning features. It comes with a 6.52 inches IPS LCD with a pixel density of 269 per inch with a resolution of 720 x 1600. It has a refresh rate of 90Hz on the screen of the display. The phone is portable and easy to handle as it measures 163.4mm in width, 76.2mm in breadth and 9.1mm in thickness and has a weight of 208 grams. Equipped with an Unisoc T750 chipset and an octa-core CPU of 2.0 GHz Cortex-A76 & 6x 1.8 GHz Cortex-A55 and Mali-G57 MC2 GPU. The rear is equally impressive, with a dual 50MP main camera and a 2MP macro camera, and 8MP selfie camera. It is powered by a 5000mAh battery with fast charging of 18W. The Lava Yuva 5G price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,000 (Expected) for 4GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage.
