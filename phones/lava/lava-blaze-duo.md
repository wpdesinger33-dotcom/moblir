Lava has released a new smartphone called Lava Blaze Duo. It comes with a 6.67 inches 3D curved AMOLED, which has a 120Hz refresh rate. Also, the phone has the 6.67-inch AMOLED screen on the back to answer a call, check notification, or even take a selfie using the 64 MP main camera. The secondary screen can also be used as a viewfinder. Supported by MediaTek Dimensity 7025 5G chipset. The device comes with Android 14 preinstalled and supports an upgrade to Android 15. The Lava Blaze Duo is built to last and is protected from dust and water by IP64 rating. It supports 33W wired fast charging. The phone comes in two colors: Arctic White along with Celestial Blue. The Lava Blaze Duo price in Bangladesh is BDT 20,000 (Expected) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage.
