The Lava Yuva 4 is one of the affordable mobiles phones that is well capable of delivering a good user experience. It has a 6.56-inch HD+ IPS display with a resolution of 720 x 1600 pixels with a 90Hz refresh rate. It is packed with an Unisok T606 chip with four cores and 1.6 GHz frequency as well as Mali-G57 MP1 GPU. The Lava Yuva 4 sports a 50MP rear camera, and 8 MP selfie camera. With 5,000mAh battery which provides long lasting battery backup and also the phone supports 10W wired charging through USB Type-C. The Lava Yuva 4 also has a side-mounted fingerprint sensor for added security. It comes in three color options: Eclipse Black, and Galaxy White. The Lava Yuva 4 price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,000 (Expected) for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage.
