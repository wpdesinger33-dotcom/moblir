The Lava Iris X1 Atom is a budget-friendly smartphone that was released back in 2015. While it may not boast cutting-edge specifications, it offers basic functionality for users seeking an affordable device. The Iris X1 Atom sports a 4-inch TFT display with a resolution of 480 x 800 pixels. Under the hood, it houses a Spreadtrum SC7731G system-on-chip (SoC). On the rear, you’ll find a 5-megapixel camera, while the front features a VGA selfie camera. It has a 1750 mAh Li-Ion battery. The phone runs on Android 4.4.2 KitKat. The Lava Iris X1 Atom price in Bangladesh is BDT 3990 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
