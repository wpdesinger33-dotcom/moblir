The Lava Iris X5 features a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. It’s powered by a quad-core 1.2 GHz processor and comes with 1 GB of RAM. The phone runs on Android 4.4.2 (KitKat) and has 8 GB of internal storage, which can be expanded using a microSD card. Keep reading this article to know about the Lava Iris X5 price in Bangladesh and its details. It boasts an 8 MP primary camera with autofocus and LED flash, capable of recording 1080p videos. For selfies, there’s a 5 MP front camera with an LED flash as well. The device is dual SIM and supports GSM and HSPA networks. Its 2100 mAh removable battery provides up to 200 hours of standby time (2G/3G) and up to 6 hours of talk time (2G) or 4 hours (3G). The Lava Iris X5 price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,990 for its 1GB of RAM and 8GB of internal storage.
