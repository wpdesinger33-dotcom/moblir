The Lava Blaze 3 5G is an upcoming smartphone in Bangladesh. It features a 6.56-inch HD+ IPS LCD display with a 90Hz refresh rate, providing a smooth visual experience. The device is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 6300 chipset. The phone runs on Android 14, with a planned upgrade to Android 15, offering a clean and up-to-date user interface. In terms of photography, the Lava Blaze 3 5G is equipped with a dual-camera setup on the back, featuring a 50MP primary sensor and a 2MP secondary sensor. This setup is capable of capturing detailed and vibrant photos. For selfies and video calls, there is an 8MP front camera. The device also includes a side-mounted fingerprint sensor for added security and convenience. It is powered by a 5000mAh battery, which supports 18W fast charging, ensuring that the phone can last through a full day of use with ease. Connectivity options include 5G, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.2, and a USB Type-C port. The Lava Blaze 3 5G price in Bangladesh is 15,000 (Expected) with 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. The phone is available in two color options: Glass Blue and Glass Gold.
