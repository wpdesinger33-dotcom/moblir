Lava Pixel V2 Plus (3GB+16GB) variant is a budget smartphone launched in September, 2015 in Bangladesh. However, the Lava Pixel V2 Plus is another variant that comes with 2GB of RAM, but has the same display, camera configuration, battery and operating system as the Lava Pixel V2. The first and most notable is the difference in RAM between the two models where the 2GB RAM version offers around 10-15 percent less multitasking and overall performance than the 3GB RAM. Lava Pixel V2 Plus (3GB+16GB) Variant is equipped with 5.0 inches IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The hardware component of the device is anchored on the MediaTek MT6735 chipset. The phone has a 13MP back camera and an 8MP front camera. The long lasting battery of 2500mAh Li-Po. It is operated with Android 5.1 Lollipop. The Lava Pixel V2 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 11,990 (Official) for the 3GB+16GB variant.
