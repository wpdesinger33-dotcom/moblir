The Lava Iris X8 offers a decent package for its time, with a focus on affordability. It features a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display and resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. It’s powered by an octa-core 1.4 GHz Cortex-A7 processor. The phone runs Android 4.4.2 (KitKat), which is upgradable to Android 5.1 (Lollipop). The main camera is an 8 MP shooter with dual-LED flash, HDR, and panorama capabilities. For selfies, a 3 MP front camera. It is equipped with a 2500 mAh non-removable battery, providing up to 291 hours of standby time and up to 14 hours of talk time on 2G networks. The Lava Iris X8 price in Bangladesh is BDT 9990 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in Black, and White colors.
