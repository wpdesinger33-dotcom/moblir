The Lava Hero Glow is an entry level and very inexpensive feature phone that will suit those who do not require high end features but require reliability. It comes with a 1.8-inch QVGA screen with a resolution of 128 x 160 pixels. It has a 3MB RAM and 4MB ROM which can be expanded with a micro SD card up to 32GB for storing contacts and other media files. This battery power is complimented by the 1000mAh removable Li-Ionbatterywhich is one of the device’s most important features. The Lava Hero Glow has the standard GSM + GSM dual SIM facility to provide the users with quick and easy access to two numbers. It also comes with Bluetooth 2.0 for basic communication and microUSB 2.0 for charging and data transfer. It makes up for this shortcoming with basic essentials like an FM radio, loudspeaker, and 3.5 mm audio jack. The Lava Hero Glow price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,130 (Official).
