The Lava KKT 27S is an entry level mobile that is best suited for the user who will require a mobile phone. Launched in August, 2013 it has 2.8 inches TFT screen, the resolution of 240 x 320 pixels. The phone can be expandable up to 32 GB via a microSD card slot. The KKT 27S has a single 0.3MP rear camera and it has a user accessible 1400mAh battery. One of them is dual SIM support which enables a user to use two numbers on a single phone. The Lava KKT 27S price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,590 (Official).
