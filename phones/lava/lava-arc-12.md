The Lava ARC 12 is a very simple talking phone targeted at the mass market and moreover a low budget market. It includes a 2.4-inch TFT display resolution of 240 x 320 pixels. It is equipped with a meager punch of a processor and contains 24MB RAM and 32MB of built-in memory, which can be expanded through a microSD card. The ARC 12 has a low-resolution rear camera with VGA, and a removable 1000mAh battery. It backs and blends Dual-SIM provision hence enabling a user to handle two contacts under one phone. The Lava ARC 12 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,190 (Official).
