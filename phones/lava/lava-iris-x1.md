The Lava Iris X1 is an Android smartphone that was announced in May 2014. It features a 4.5-inch IPS LCD display. Also it has a  resolution of 480 x 854 pixels. The phone is powered by the Broadcom BCM23550 chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.2 GHz CPU and the Broadcom VideoCore IV GPU. It comes with 1 GB of RAM and 8 GB of internal storage, expandable via a microSDHC card slot. Keep reading this article to know about the Lava Iris X1 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of photography, the Iris X1 boasts an 8 MP rear camera with autofocus and an LED flash, capable of recording 1080p videos at 30 fps. For selfies, a 2 MP front-facing camera. Runs on Android 4.4.2 KitKat. Other notable features include Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.0, GPS, FM radio, and a 3.5mm headphone jack. The removable 1800 mAh Li-Ion battery provides up to 9 hours and 45 minutes of talk time. Available in black and white colors. The Lava Iris X1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,990 (Official) for its 1GB of RAM and 8GB of internal storage.
