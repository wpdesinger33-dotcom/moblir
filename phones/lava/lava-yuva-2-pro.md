The Lava Yuva 2 Pro is a smartphone that comes with some fancy features well worthy of its price. The device boasts of a screen size of 6.52 inches, IPS LCD type screen, with a pixel density of 720 x 1600 pixels. It is backed by a MediaTek Helio G37 processor. The battery power is 5000mAh non-removable battery that is long lasting. The Lava Yuva 2 Pro also has a 5MP front camera for selfies and a 13MP rear camera. Comes in Glass Lavender, Glass Green and Glass White colors. The Lava Yuva 2 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,999 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 128GB of ROM.
