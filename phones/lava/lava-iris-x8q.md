Lava Iris X8Q was launched in February 2015, and it is an affordable smartphone with enough features to fulfill day to day operations. It comes with support for 5.0 inches IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The operating system of the device is MediaTek MT6582M chipset consisting of a quad core 1.3GHz Cortex A7 processor with Mali 400 MP GPU. The battery is 2500mAh and supports up to 17 hours of talk time and 500 hours standby time The device has an 8MP rear camera and a 5MP front camera. The Lava Iris X8Q price in Bangladesh is BDT 7,990 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. This phone comes in Black, and White colors.
