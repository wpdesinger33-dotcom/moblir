The Lava Iris 510 is one of the most affordable phones available in the market and hence a phone that offers even the basic requirements at an affordable price. Launched in June, 2015 in Bangladesh market. It has a 4.0 inches TFT display having a display resolution of 480 x 800 pixels. At its heart, it has a dual core 1.3 GHz MediaTek MT6572W processor, the graphics processing is managed by a Mali-400 GPU. It has a 5MP rear camera and a 2 MP front camera. The device operates from a 1400 mAh Li-ion battery which is built into the unit. The Lava Iris 510 has the capability of dual SIM, Bluetooth 2.0, GPS making it easier to connect the device to another device. Besides, it is integrated with a microSD card slot for a memory expansion up to 32 GB and can support GSM/GPRS/EDGE standards. The Lava Iris 510 price in Bangladesh is BDT 4,990 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of ROM.
