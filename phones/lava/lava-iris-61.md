The Lava Iris 61, released in October 2019, is an entry-level smartphone with a focus on affordability. It features a 5.0-inch FWVGA display with a resolution of 480 x 960 pixels and an 18:9 aspect ratio. The device runs on Android 9.0 (Pie) Go Edition, optimized for smoother performance on lower-end hardware. Keep reading this article to know about the Lava Iris 61 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of performance, the Iris 61 is powered by a quad-core Spreadtrum processor clocked at 1.4 GHz. It comes with 1 GB of RAM and 16 GB of internal storage, which can be expanded further using a microSD card (up to 32 GB). The 2500 mAh lithium-ion battery provides decent endurance for everyday use. It comes with a 5 MP primary camera on the back, complemented by a 2 MP selfie camera on the front. While not groundbreaking, these cameras are suitable for basic photography needs. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and microUSB 2.0. Additionally, the phone supports 3G and 4G LTE networks. The Lava Iris 61 price in Bangladesh is BDT 4,690 for its 1GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage.
