Easy to use smartphone with rather moderate specifications and affordable price, Lava Iris 88 is a model for every day use. It is a 5.45 inches HD IPS TFT display with resolution 720 х 1440. The device is driven by a MediaTek MT6739 quad-core processor with 1.5 GHz cpu frequency. The camera setup includes an 8MP rear camera with autofocus  and videos at 30fps. The front-facing camera is a 5MP sensor. The 3000mAh battery is given and the phone has 4G LTE, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GPS options. The Lava Iris 88 price in Bangladesh is BDT 7,490 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of ROM. This phone comes in Gold, and Black colors.
