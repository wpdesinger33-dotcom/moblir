The Lava Grand 2 is an Android smartphone that was released in January 2016. It features a 5.5-inch IPS display with a resolution of 720x1280 pixels. Under the hood, it’s powered by the MediaTek MT6735 chipset, which includes a quad-core Cortex-A53 processor. In terms of photography, the Lava Grand 2 sports a 13 MP primary camera on the back and an 8 MP front-facing camera. The battery capacity is 3000 mAh. The Lava Grand 2 price in Bangladesh is BDT 15500 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage.
