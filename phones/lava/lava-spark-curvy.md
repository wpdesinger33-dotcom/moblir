The Lava Spark Curvy is an affordable sleek smartphone that will suit those people who are looking for both fashion and functionality. The device is equipped with a 2.8-inch TFT display and an opportunity to display pictures in the resolution of 240 x 320 pixels. It comes with a 1.3 MP rear facing camera. The device is powered by a Li-ion 1400 mAh battery. The Lava Spark Curvy comes with dual SIM connectivity, Bluetooth 2.0, GPS, thus providing more flexibility. Also, it has a micro SD card slot that allows you to expand your memory up to a capacity of 4 GB’s. The Lava Spark Curvy price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,650 (Official).
