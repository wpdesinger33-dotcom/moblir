The Lava Iris 414 is a budget-friendly option for basic smartphone needs. It is a dual-SIM smartphone that was launched in black color. It measures 120.00 x 63.40 x 10.40mm and weighs 110.00 grams. The device features a 3.15-megapixel rear camera and Wi-Fi, GPS, FM radio, and 3G connectivity options. Powered by the MT6570N / Cortex-A7 chipset. Its 1400mAh battery provides reasonable usage time. The Lava Iris 414 price in Bangladesh is BDT 4190 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of internal storage.
