The Lava Yuva Star is a recent addition to Lava’s Yuva series, designed specifically for first-time smartphone buyers. It features a glossy back design and a large 6.75-inch HD+ notch display. The phone runs on Android 14 Go edition. It boasts a dual-camera setup with a 13MP main camera and a 5MP front camera. The device is powered by a substantial 5,000mAh battery with 10W wired charging support. The Lava Yuva Star price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,000 (Exp) for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage. It comes in White, Black, and Lavender colors.
