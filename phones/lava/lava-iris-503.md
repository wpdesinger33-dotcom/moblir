Lava Iris 503 is an affordable Android Smartphone which was launched in October 2013. It comes with an IPS LCD screen of 5.0 inches with 540X960 pixels should be considered. The device is run by a MediaTek MT6572 and a Mali-400 GPU. The phone is equipped with a 5MP rear camera with LED flash, and 1.2MP front facing camera for selfies. The battery power is 2000 mAh and the usage is up to 7 hours on 2G networks. The device is equipped with the operating system of Android 4.2 Jelly Bean. It comes in black and white, it is built to be sturdy with Corning Gorilla Glass coating. The Lava Iris 503 price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,300 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
