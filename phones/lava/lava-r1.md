The Lava R1 provides a decent user experience with its compact form factor, reliable performance, and budget-friendly price point. It is a 4G LTE smartphone with a 5.0" HD+ IPS touchscreen display. Powered by a quad-core MediaTek MT6739WA processor. The primary camera is 8MP, while the front-facing camera captures 5MP selfies. The phone runs on Android Marshmallow v6.0 and features a 2620 mAh battery. The Lava R1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,990 for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
