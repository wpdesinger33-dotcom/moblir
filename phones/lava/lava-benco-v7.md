The Lava Benco V7 is an affordable smartphone that caters to users seeking a trendy design without breaking the bank. Sporting a 6.1-inch HD IPS LCD display with a 19.5:9 aspect ratio, the device features a dewdrop notch housing a 5MP selfie camera. Keep reading this article to know about the Lava Benco V7 price in Bangladesh and its details. The back of the phone houses an 8MP primary camera. Under the hood, it runs on a 1.3 GHz quad-core processor with UniSoC SC7731E chipset, paired with 2GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage (expandable via a dedicated microSD slot). It supports 4G LTE, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and has a 4000 mAh battery. The Lava Benco V7 price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,890 for its 2GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage.
