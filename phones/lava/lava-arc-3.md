The Lava ARC 3 is yet another simple mobile phone which aims to serve its user for calls and basic purposes. It has a display of 2.4 inches TFT with a maximum resolution of 320 x 240 pixels. It has a 0.3MP camera and also possesses a 1,000mAh battery in the phone. Also, it has a dual SIM option and works with 2G GSM networks. It also possesses a microSD card slot for memory expansion up to 16GB and Bluetooth and FM radio for more connection types. The phone is only produced in black color. The Lava ARC 3 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,490 (Official) for 24MB of RAM and 32MB of ROM.
