The Lava Iris 800 model was unveiled in Bangladesh in April 2015 and is one of Lava’s low-priced devices for everyday use. It comes with a capacitive touch screen of 5.0 inches IPS LCD screen with screen resolution of 480×854 pixels. The availability of the device is with the MediaTek MT6572AW chipset and ARM Mali-400 MP GPU. The battery installed in the phone is 2500mAh and the stand by time is up to 20 hours of talk time.It has a 5MP rear camera with image resolution of 2592 x 1944 Pixels. And a 2MP selfie camera. phone measurement of 143 mm x 72 mm x 8.90 mm. Its weight is 148 grams. The Lava Iris 800 price in Bangladesh is BDT 6,290 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
