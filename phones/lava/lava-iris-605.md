The Lava Iris 605 is an affordable smartphone that was released in September 2016. It features a 4.5-inch TFT touchscreen display with a resolution of 267 PPI. The phone runs on Android Lollipop v5.0 and is powered by a dual-core 1.2 GHz processor (MediaTek MTK6572W) with 1 GB of RAM and 8 GB of internal storage. You can expand the storage further using a dedicated MicroSD slot, supporting up to 32 GB. Keep reading this article to know about the Lava Iris 605 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of photography, the Iris 605 comes equipped with a 5-megapixel primary camera with LED flash and digital zoom. For selfies, there’s a 2-megapixel front-facing camera. While it doesn’t support 4K video recording, it offers smooth performance for everyday tasks. The phone’s design is simple yet appealing, and it boasts decent battery life thanks to its 1750 mAh lithium-ion battery. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi Direct, hotspot, Bluetooth, and GPS.  There’is a glass protection for the display.
