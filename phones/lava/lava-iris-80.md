The Lava Iris 80 is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers a balanced set of features. It comes with a well-designed body, and its compact dimensions (144.8 x 71.8 x 9.0 mm) make it comfortable to hold. The fingerprint sensor adds a layer of security and convenience. Keep reading this article to know about the  Lava Iris 80 price in Bangladesh and its details. This phone features a 5.0-inch HD (1280 x 720 pixels) IPS display. While not the largest screen, it provides decent clarity and vibrant colors. Its rear camera setup consists of an 8MP lens with flash, while the front camera offers a wide-angle 5MP lens with flash. These cameras deliver satisfactory performance for everyday photography. In terms of performance, this phone is powered by the MediaTek MT6737 quad-core processor clocked at 1.3GHz. It comes with 1GB of RAM and 8GB of internal storage (ROM). While not a powerhouse, it handles basic tasks smoothly.
