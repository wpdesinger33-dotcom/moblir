TheLavaBlaze 5G is an affordable smartphone released on 24 October 2022 ,it is also a 5G smartphone. The phone includes a 6.5-inch IPS LCDdisplayresolution and a pixel density of 720 x 1600. The phone uses the MediaTek Dimensity 700 chipset and graphical performance a Mali-G57 MC2 GPU. In terms of thecamera, it has a 50MP main rear camera and 2MP macro camera plus an 8MP selfie camera. It is powered by a 5000mAhbatteryand supports 15W fast charging. Running on Android 12. The Lava Blaze 5G price in Bangladesh is BDT 18,999 (Official) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. This model comes in Green, and Blue colors.
