The Lava iris X8 pro is an android smartphone that costs a reasonable amount of money, and was released in Bangladesh in November 2015. It comes with a 5.0 inch screen IPS LCD type with a pixel density of 720 x 1280. The MediaTek MT6592 chipset is used as an engine for the device, it encompasses an octa-core 1.4 GHz Cortex-A7 processor and Mali-450 MP4 graphics. The Smartphone comes with an 8 MP rear camera with dual LED flash and 5 MP front camera. The phone offers users enough charge with a 2500mAh Li-Po battery that allows it to stay stand-by up to 291 hrs / 14 hrs talk-time on 2G only. Android 5.1 Lollipop is used in the device. Currently, it comes in colors such as white and black and is built to be tough with Asahi Dragontrail Glass protection. The Lava iris X8 pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,190 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
