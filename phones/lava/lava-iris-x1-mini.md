The Lava Iris X1 mini features a compact design with dimensions of 123.5 x 63.4 x 9 mm and a weight of 116 grams. Its 4.0-inch IPS LCD display offers a resolution of 480 x 800 pixels, providing a pixel density of approximately 233 ppi. Keep reading this article to know about the Lava Iris X1 Mini price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of performance, it is powered by a Spreadtrum 7731 chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.2 GHz CPU and a Mali-400 GPU. It comes with 512 MB of RAM and 4 GB of internal storage, expandable via a microSDHC card slot. The primary camera is a 5 MP shooter with an LED flash, capable of recording videos at 720p resolution. For selfies, there’s a VGA (0.3 MP) front-facing camera. The 1750 mAh removable battery provides up to 10 hours of talk time on 3G networks.
