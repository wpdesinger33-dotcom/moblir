The Lava Iris 405 is an Android based budget smartphone that was launched in May 2013. It also comes with a 4.0-inch TFT display with support for 480 x 800 pixels resolution. The operating clock of the device is 1GHz dual-core ARM Cortex-A9 CPU and Mali-400 GPU. This phone comes with a 5MP back camera with LED flash and with 0.3MP VGA front camera. This phone comes with a 1400mAh battery which has up to 6 hours’ talk time. Android 4.1 Jelly Bean is the operating system on which the device performs. It is available in black, although the bespoke design ensures that it is a durable phone with Corning Gorilla Glass. The Lava Iris 405 price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,490 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
