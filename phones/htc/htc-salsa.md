The HTC Salsa smartphone made its Bangladesh release in June 2011 to serve social media users specifically. This smartphone presents a 3.4-inch TFTdisplaywith pixel resolution set at 320 x 480 pixels. The Qualcomm Snapdragon S1 chipset inside the device contains an 800 MHz ARM 11 CPU together with an Adreno 200 GPU. The HTC Salsa contains a 5 MP camera as its main photographic feature. Thecamerasystem on this device supports 480p video recording capabilities. The camera on the front side of the device operates as a VGA shooter. The removablebatteryof the device has a capacity of 1520 mAh. The Salsa operates on Android 2.3 Gingerbread with Sense UI from HTC installed. The device exists in three color options: Black, Silver and Lilac. The HTC Salsa price in Bangladesh is BDT 23,500 (Official) with 512MB of RAM and 512MB of phone storage.
