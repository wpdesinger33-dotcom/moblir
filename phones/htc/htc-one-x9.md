The HTC One X9 released in Bangladesh during January 2016 belongs to the mid-range smartphone category by combining performance and design features. The device contains a 5.5-inch LCDdisplaythat reaches 1080 x 1920 pixels resolution. The device operates using the MediaTek MT6795 Helio X10 chipset that contains an octa-core 2.2 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU alongside PowerVR G6200 GPU. The camera system of the HTC One X9 includes a 13 MP rearcamerawhich works with dual-LED dual-tone flash. The device has the capability to record videos at 4K resolution. Users can take photographs with the 5 MP front camera through its f/2.8 aperture. It comes with a built-in 3000 mAhbatterythat can be charged using 18W fast charging technology. The phone operates with Android 6.0 Marshmallow through the combination of HTC's Sense UI. This device comes in Black, Silver, Gold, and Pink colors. The HTC One X9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 25,990 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 32G of phone storage.
