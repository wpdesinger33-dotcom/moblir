The HTC Desire S is a low-end phone released in early 2011 in Bangladeshi markets. It has an S-LCD 3.7 inches screen resolution of 480 x 800 pixels. The phone is run by a 1 GHz Qualcomm Snapdragon S2 processor of 1 GHz and it offers 768 MB of RAM. It has a 5MP main camera and has the ability to record videos in HD of 720p. This also features a 0.3MP VGA front facing camera. This phone is built with Android 2.3 Gingerbread operating system with an option to upgrade it to Android 4.0 Ice Cream Sandwich. The battery power provided in HTC Desire S is 1450mAh and using this battery consumer can talk for up to 9H 50M in 2G and 7H 10 M in 3G. The phone also has a microSDHC card slot in case you need to expand up to the phone’s memory and it supports up to 32GB. The HTC Desire S price in Bangladesh is BDT 26,990 (Official).
