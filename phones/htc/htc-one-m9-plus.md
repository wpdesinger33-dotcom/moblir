In May 2016 Bangladesh received the high-end HTC One M9 Plus smartphone which brings together sophisticated specifications and elegant design elements. The phone has a 5.2-inch LCDdisplaywhich offers 1440 x 2560 pixels resolution. The HTC One M9 Plus operates with the MediaTek MT6795T Helio X10 chipset that integrates an octa-core 2.2 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU together with PowerVR G6200 GPU. Having 20 MP rear cameras with dual-LED dual-tone flash. Thecamerasystem on this device enables users to record videos at 4K resolution. The front camera system uses a 4 MP resolution with f/2.0 aperture. The device integrates a fixed 2840 mAhbatterywithin its structure. The One M9 Plus operates with Android 5.0.2 Lollipop and HTC's Sense UI and users can upgrade to Android 6.0 Marshmallow. Customers can choose Gunmetal Gray or Silver Gold or Amber Gold when selecting their One M9 Plus device. The HTC One M9 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 33,900 (Official) with 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage.
