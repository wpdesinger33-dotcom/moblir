The HTC One Max is an Android smartphone designed and manufactured by HTC. Released in October 2013, it’s a larger variant of HTC’s flagship high-end smartphone, the HTC One. The standout feature of the One Max is its expansive 5.9-inch display. Keep reading this article to know about the HTC One Max price in Bangladesh and its details. It comes with a solid build with metallic properties, consistent with HTC’s design language. Its dimensions measure 164.5 x 82.5 x 10.3 mm, and it weighs 217 grams. The 5.9-inch Super LCD3 display offers a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. It resulted in a pixel density of approximately 373 ppi. Corning Gorilla Glass 3 protects this screen. In terms of performance, the One Max features a Qualcomm Snapdragon 600 chipset (APQ8064T) with a quad-core 1.7 GHz Krait 300 CPU and Adreno 320 GPU. Its rear camera is a 4 MP shooter with an aperture of f/2.0, 1/3.0" sensor size, and 2µm pixel size.
