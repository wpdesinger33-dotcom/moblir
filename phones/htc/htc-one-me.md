The HTC One ME is a mid-range smartphone designed and first released by HTC corporation in July 2015. It comes with a 5.2-inch, IPS LCDDisplaywith a higher resolution of 1440 x 2560 pixels. The phone operates with the help of MediaTek Helio X10 chipset that consists of octa-core 2.2 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and the PowerVR G6200 GPU. Housing a 20 Megapixel rearcamerawith dual-LED dual-tempo flash, the phone also has High Dynamic Range (HDR) and 4 Megapixel front-facing camera. It also has a phone that has abatterycapacity of 2840mAh and has the capacity of 23 hours talk time and 626 hours standby time. Operating system of Android 5.2 (Lollipop) and Sense UI 7. The HTC One ME price in Bangladesh is BDT 24,500 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage.
