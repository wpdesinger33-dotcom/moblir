The HTC U23 is considered to be in the higher-tier smartphones and the phone is a good mix of features given the Bangladesh market price. It comes with a 6.7-inch OLED screen that supports a 1080 x 2400 pixels resolution, and a 120Hz refresh rate.Internally, it is running on a Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 1 chipset, and an Adreno 644 GPU. The camera on the U23 comprises a main sensor of 64MP with an f/1.8 aperture, the second is an ultrawide lens of 8MP and the third one is a macro lens of 2MP. The front camera is a 32MP sensor. The device is backed by a 4600mAh Li-Polymer battery and the fast charging is 30W. The phone runs on Android 13. The HTC U23 price in Bangladesh is BDT 90,000 (Expected) with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. The device comes in Aqua Blue, and Roland Violet colors.
