The HTC ChaCha was launched in June 2011, is an Android based smartphone. It has a 2.6” TFT screen, resolution of 320 x 480 pixels. It supports a Qualcomm Snapdragon S1 processor of 800 MHz for its functioning. Its RAM is 512MB and internal storage is 512 MB. iT can increase it up to 32GB by using a microSD card. It has an integrated 5MP rear camera with autofocus and LED flash, and a VGA front facing camera. The device comes with a 1250mAh removable battery. Other features include Dual SIM, Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 3.0, FM Radio and a dedicated Facebook button. It has the Android 2.3(Gingerbread) Operating system with a touch of HTC’s Sense UI 2.1. The HTC ChaCha price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,000 (Official).
