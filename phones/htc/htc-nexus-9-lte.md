The HTC Nexus 9 LTE, released in November 2014 in Bangladesh, is a powerful tablet that combines performance and portability. It features an 8.9-inch IPS LCDdisplaywith a resolution of 1536 x 2048 pixels, providing clear and vibrant visuals. Powered by the Nvidia Tegra K1 chipset with a dual-core 2.3 GHz Denver CPU and Kepler DX1 GPU. It also includes an 8MP rearcameraand a 1.6MP front camera for capturing moments. With a 6700mAhbattery, it offers long battery life, and it supports Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and GPS connectivity. The Nexus 9 LTE is available in Indigo Black, Lunar White, and Sand. The HTC Nexus 9 LTE price in Bangladesh is BDT 49,000 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage.
