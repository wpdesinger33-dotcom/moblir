The HTC One mini 2, announced in May 2014, is a compact Android smartphone. It features a 4.5” Super LCD2 display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The phone is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 400 chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU and Adreno 305 GPU. In terms of photography, the One mini 2 boasts a 13 MP primary camera with LED flash, HDR, and panorama capabilities. The front-facing camera is a 5 MP. The non-removable 2110 mAh Li-Po battery provides up to 556 hours of standby time (3G) and up to 16 hours and 10 minutes of talk time (3G). The HTC One mini 2 price in Bangladesh is BDT 33900 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in Gunmetal Gray, Glacial Silver, and Amber Gold color variants.
