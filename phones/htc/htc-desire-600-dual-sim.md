The HTC Desire 600 Dual Sim is an Android smartphone that was announced in May 2013. It falls into the mid-range category and offers dual SIM functionality. Drawing inspiration from the HTC One series, it incorporates features like Sense 5, Beats Audio, and BoomSound front stereo speakers. It comes with a 4.5-inch Super LCD2 display with a resolution of 540 x 960 pixels (245 ppi). Its compact form factor and lightweight design make it easy to handle, weighing just 130 grams. In terms of performance, the Desire 600 is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 200 chipset with a quad-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A5 CPU. Its 8 MP primary camera captures decent photos, and there’s a 1.6 MP front camera for selfies and video calls. A removable 1860 mAh battery keeps the phone running, and it offers 8 GB of internal storage. The HTC Desire 600 Dual Sim price in Bangladesh is BDT 33,500 (Official) for its 1GB of RAM and 8GB of internal storage.
