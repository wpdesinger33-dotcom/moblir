The HTC One A9 was launched in Bangladesh in November 2015, combining cutting-edge design elements and reliable performance. The smartphone features a 5.0-inch AMOLEDdisplaywith a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. The device is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 617 chipset that includes an octa-core CPU and an Adreno 405 GPU. The HTC One A9 provides users with a 13 MP rearcamerapaired with dual-LED dual-tone flash. The camera system enables recording of videos at 1080p resolution. Users can capture images through the 4 MP front-facing camera which operates at f/2.0 aperture. The device has a built-in 2150 mAhbatterywhich enables 18W fast charging capabilities. It operates using Android 6.0 Marshmallow through the HTC Sense UI interface and it supports an Android 7.0 Nougat upgrade. The HTC One A9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 22,000 (Official) with 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
