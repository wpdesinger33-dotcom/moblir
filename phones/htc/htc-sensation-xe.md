The HTC Sensation XE is an Android smartphone that was announced in September 2011. It features a 4.3-inch S-LCD display with a resolution of 540 x 960 pixels. This phone is powered by a Snapdragon S3 processor. It runs on Android 2.3.4 (Gingerbread), which is upgradable to 4.0 (Ice Cream Sandwich) with HTC’s Sense UI. Keep reading this article to know about the HTC Sensation XE price in Bangladesh and its details. Its camera setup includes an 8 MP rear camera with dual-LED flash, capable of recording 1080p videos, and a VGA front-facing camera. The phone has 4GB of internal storage (with 1GB available for users) and supports microSDHC cards for expandable storage. Beats Audio integration is a standout feature of the Sensation XE. It includes a red speaker, buttons, rear camera, and the Beats logo, making it distinct from the original Sensation. The phone also features Corning Gorilla Glass for added durability.
