The HTC One (M8) which entered the Bangladesh market during March 2014 represents HTC's flagship smartphone. The M8 model of HTC One incorporates a 5.0-inch LCDdisplaydelivering 1080 x 1920 pixels resolution. The HTC One (M8) operates on the Qualcomm Snapdragon 801 chipset that offers a quad-core 2.3 GHz Krait 400 CPU combined with an Adreno 330 GPU. A 4MP UltraPixel rearcamera. The front section of the device has a  5MP camera. The device operates with Android 4.4.2 KitKat that can be upgraded to Android 6.0 Marshmallow while running under HTC's Sense UI 6. The 2600mAh non-removablebattery. The device supports Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac and Bluetooth 4.0 and GPS connectivity features. The device offers customers three color options: Gunmetal Gray, Glacial Silver and Amber Gold. The HTC One (M8) price in Bangladesh is BDT 47,500 (Official) with 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
