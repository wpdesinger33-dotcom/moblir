The HTC U19e, released in June 2019, is a flagship range smartphone. In this article we will share the HTC U19e price in Bangladesh and spec. It features a 6.0-inch OLED display with a resolution of 1080 x 2160 pixels. The device is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 710 chipset. The phone runs on Android 9.0 (Pie), with HTC SenseA. The HTC U19e has dual rear cameras, 12MP main sensor, and 20MP telephoto sensor. For selfie 24MP main sensor, and 2MP depth sensor. 3,930mAh battery with Quick Charge 4.0 via USB-C. The HTC U19e price in Bangladesh is BDT 39,990 with 6GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage. This model is officially available in Bangladesh. It comes in Extraordinary Purple, and Modest Green colors.
