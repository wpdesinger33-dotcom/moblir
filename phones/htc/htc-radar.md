HTC Radar was introduced in October 2011 and from HTC’s product portfolio it can be classified under upper mid-range smartphones with fine tune performance and features. Mobile has a screen size of 3.8 inches and exhibits LCD quality functioning at a resolution of 480 x 800 pixels. Its operating method is the Qualcomm Snapdragon S2, consisting of a 1.0 GHz Scorpion CPU and an Adreno 205 GPU. The Radar in relation to design is relatively small and lightweight; it is only 137 grams and has a dimension of 120.5 x 61.5 x 10.9mm. It has a 5MP rear camera, and VGA front camera. The battery capacity is 1520mAh and offers normal usage. The HTC Radar price in Bangladesh is BDT 31,500 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
