The HTC Desire 10 Pro, released in December 2016 in Bangladesh, is a mid-range Android smartphone. It features a metal chassis with a textured back, providing a comfortable grip. The 5.5-inch full HD display delivers vibrant colors and sharp text. As a dual-SIM phone, it supports 4G with VoLTE and offers expandable storage via the second SIM slot. Keep reading this article to know about the HTC Desire 10 Pro price in Bangladesh and its details. It packs an octa-core MediaTek Helio P10 processor paired with either 3GB or 4GB of RAM. It comes with 64GB of internal storage (expandable via microSD) and runs Android 6.0 Marshmallow. Its rear camera comes with a 20MP sensor with dual-LED dual-tone flash, while the front camera captures selfies at 13MP. A rear-mounted fingerprint sensor enhances security. Powering the device is a 3000mAh non-removable battery, which supports 10W wired charging. The HTC Desire 10 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 34,990 (Official) for its 4GB of RAM and 64GB of internal storage.
