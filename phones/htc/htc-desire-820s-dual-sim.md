The HTC Desire 820s dual sim, released in March 2015 in Bangladesh, is a mid-range smartphone that offers a balanced mix of performance and features. It sports a 5.5-inch IPS LCDdisplaywith a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The device is powered by the MediaTek MT6752 chipset, featuring an octa-core 1.7 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and Mali-T760MP2 GPU. The camera setup includes a 13MP rearcameracapable of recording 1080p videos at 30fps. The front-facing camera is an 8MP shooter, also capable of 1080p video recording. The device runs on Android 4.4.4 KitKat with HTC's Sense UI 6. The device is equipped with a 2600mAh non-removablebattery. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.0, GPS, and FM radio. Available in various colors, including Marble White, Milkyway Gray, Monarch Orange, Santorini White, and Tuxedo Gray. The HTC Desire 820s dual sim price in Bangladesh is BDT 23,050 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
