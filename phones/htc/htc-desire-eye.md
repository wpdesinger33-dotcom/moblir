The HTC Desire Eye is an Android smartphone that was announced in October 2014 and released in November 2014. It offers a 5.2” IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. The phone’s unibody design houses a Snapdragon 801 chipset, along with 2GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage. Keep reading this article to know about the HTC Desire Eye price in Bangladesh and its details. One of the standout features of the Desire Eye is its dual 13MP camera setup. The front-facing camera boasts an f/2.2 aperture and a wide 22mm lens, while the rear camera has an f/2.0 aperture. Both cameras are equipped with dual-LED dual-tone flash and can record 1080p videos. Other notable specifications include IPX7 water and dust resistance (up to 1 meter for 30 minutes), stereo speakers, and a 2400mAh non-removable battery. The Desire Eye runs Android 4.4.4 (KitKat) out of the box but is upgradable to Android 6.1 (Marshmallow) with HTC’s Sense UI 7.. The HTC Desire Eye price in Bangladesh is BDT 21,990 (Official) for its 2GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage.
