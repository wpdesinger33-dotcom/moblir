In October 2014 the HTC One M8 Eye made its appearance in the Bangladesh market. The HTC One (M8 Eye) shows a 5.0-inch LCD visual display which supports 1080 x 1920 pixels resolution. A combination of the Snapdragon 801 Qualcomm chipset runs the device by using a Krait 400 quad-core 2.3 GHz CPU and an Adreno 330 GPU. The HTC One (M8 Eye) comes with a 13MPcamerathat supports recording videos at 1080p along with 60fps capability. A 5MP front-facing shooter. The smartphone operates with Android 4.4.4 KitKat that can be upgraded to Android 6.0 Marshmallow using HTC's Sense UI 7. Thebatterythat comes with the HTC One (M8 Eye) operates through a 2600mAh non-removable power pack. The HTC One (M8 Eye) offers consumers three color options among Gunmetal Gray, Glacial Silver and Amber Gold. The HTC One (M8 Eye) price in Bangladesh is BDT 38,990 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
