Taiwanese tech brand HTC has officially expanded its budget smartphone lineup in Bangladesh with the launch of the new HTC Wildfire E7 Life. The device was released on January 10, 2026, and it is aimed at individuals who want to have a stylish and affordable smartphone to use in their daily life. The Wildfire E7 Life is unique because of its appealing design and provides useful hardware within the price range. It has a big battery capacity of 5000mAh, 6GB of RAM and internal storage of 128GB, which is expected to provide a smooth day to day performance. On the camera side, HTC has added a 50MP rear camera and it emphasizes on the sharp shots and social media captures. Through this launch, HTC is further working towards consolidating its market in Bangladesh with regard to the competitive budget smartphone market.
