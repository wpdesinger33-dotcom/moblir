It features a 3.2-inch TFT LCDdisplaywith a resolution of 240 x 320 pixels. The phone is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon S1 chipset with a 528 MHz processor. It runs on Android 2.1 Eclair with HTC's Sense UI, offering a user-friendly interface. The device includes a 5MP rearcamerawith autofocus and LED flash. With a 1300mAhbattery.
