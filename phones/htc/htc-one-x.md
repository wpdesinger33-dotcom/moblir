The HTC One X, released in May 2012 in Bangladesh, is a flagship smartphone that marked a significant step forward for HTC. It features a 4.7-inch IPS LCDdisplaywith a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The device is powered by the Nvidia Tegra 3 chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.5 GHz Cortex-A9 CPU and ULP GeForce GPU. The camera setup includes an 8MP rearcameraand LED flash, capable of recording 1080p videos at 24fps. The front-facing camera is a 1.3MP shooter, also capable of 720p video recording. The device runs on Android 4.0 Ice Cream Sandwich, upgradable to Android 4.1.1 Jelly Bean. The device is slim and lightweight, with a thickness of 8.9mm and a weight of 130g. It is equipped with a 1800mAh non-removablebattery. Available in various colors, including Gray and White. The HTC One X price in Bangladesh is BDT 48,000 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage.
