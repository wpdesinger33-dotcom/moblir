The HTC Wildfire X is an Android smartphone that was released in August 2019. features a 6.2-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1520 pixels. It has a triple rear camera setup, including a 12 MP wide-angle lens, an 8 MP telephoto lens with 2x optical zoom, and a 5 MP depth sensor. For selfies, there’s an 8 MP front camera. keep reading this article to learn about the HTC Wildfire X price in Bangladesh and its overall details. In terms of performance, it runs on Android 9 (Pie) and is powered by the Mediatek MT6762 Helio P22 chipset, which includes an octa-core 2.0 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and a PowerVR GE8320 GPU. It comes in a single variant option which is 128GB of storage and 4GB of RAM. It also supports microSDXC cards via a shared SIM slot. Other notable features include a 3300 mAh non-removable battery with 10W wired charging, a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor, and a USB Type-C 2.0 port. Unfortunately, it lacks Gorilla Glass protection for the display. The HTC Wildfire X price in Bangladesh is BDT 16,000 (Unofficial) for its 4GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage.
