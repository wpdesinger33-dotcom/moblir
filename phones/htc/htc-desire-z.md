The HTC Desire Z is also known as the T-Mobile G2 in the US. It was a slider-style smartphone developed by HTC Corporation. It featured a 3.7-inch S-LCD display with a resolution of 480 x 800 pixels and a QWERTY keyboard that slid out from underneath. Its Qualcomm MSM7230 chipset powered an 800 MHz Scorpion CPU and Adreno 205 GPU. The phone had 512MB RAM and 1.5GB internal storage, expandable via a microSDHC card slot. Keep reading this article to know about the HTC Desire Z price in Bangladesh and its details. Its 5-megapixel primary camera with autofocus and LED flash could capture 720p video. Other features included Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GPS, and FM radio. The Desire Z runs Android 2.2 (Froyo), upgradable to 2.3 (Gingerbread), with HTC’s Sense UI overlay. Its 1300 mAh removable battery provided up to 9 hours and 50 minutes of talk time on 2G networks.
