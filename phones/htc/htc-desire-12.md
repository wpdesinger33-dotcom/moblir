The HTC Desire 12 is an Android smartphone that was announced in March 2018. It features a 5.5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1440 pixels, resulting in a 18:9 aspect ratio. The phone’s design is sleek, with dimensions of 148.5 x 70.8 x 8.2 mm and a weight of 137 grams. Keep reading this article to know about the HTC Desire 12 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of performance, the Desire 12 is powered by a MediaTek MT6739 quad-core processor clocked at 1.3 GHz. It comes in two RAM and storage configurations: 2 GB RAM with 16 GB internal storage, and 3 GB RAM with 32 GB internal storage. The storage can be expanded further using a microSDXC card. In terms of photography, the Desire 12 sports a 13 MP primary camera with an f/2.2 aperture, phase detection autofocus (PDAF), LED flash, HDR, and panorama capabilities. The front-facing camera is a 5 MP sensor with an f/2.4 aperture. Both cameras can record videos at 1080p and 720p, respectively.
