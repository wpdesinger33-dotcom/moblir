The HTC One, released in April 2013 in Bangladesh, is a flagship smartphone that significantly contributed to HTC's reputation for quality and design. It features a 4.7-inch Super LCDdisplaywith a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. The device is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 600 chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.7 GHz Krait 300 CPU and Adreno 320 GPU. The camera setup is a standout feature, with a 4MP rearcamera. The front-facing camera is a 2.1MP shooter, capable of recording 1080p videos. The device runs on Android 4.1.2 Jelly Bean, upgradable to Android 5.0 Lollipop, with HTC's Sense UI 5. The 2300mAh non-removablebattery. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac, Bluetooth 4.0. Available in various colors, including Black, Silver, Red, Blue, and Gold. The HTC One price in Bangladesh is BDT 37,900 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage.
