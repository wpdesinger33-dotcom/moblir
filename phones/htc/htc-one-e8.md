The HTC One (E8), released in June 2014, is a high-end Android smartphone by HTC. It comes with a sleek design with dimensions of 146.4 x 70.7 x 9.9 mm and a weight of 145 grams. Its 5.0-inch Super LCD3 display offers a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels, resulting in a pixel density of approximately 441 ppi. Keep reading this article to know about the HTC One (E8) price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of performance, the HTC One (E8) packs a Qualcomm Snapdragon 801 chipset with a quad-core 2.5 GHz Krait 400 CPU and Adreno 330 GPU. The device runs Android 4.4.2 (KitKat), upgradable to 6.1 (Marshmallow), with HTC’s Sense UI 7. Its primary camera features a 13 MP sensor with an f/2.2 aperture, LED flash, panorama mode, and HDR support. It can record 1080p videos at 30 fps. For selfies, there’s a 5 MP front-facing camera that also records 1080p videos. The HTC One (E8) includes stereo speakers for enhanced audio quality.
