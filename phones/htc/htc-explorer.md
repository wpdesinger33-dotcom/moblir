The HTC Explorer was released in October 2011. It is an Android smartphone developed by HTC. Despite its modest specifications, it found its niche in the entry-level market. It comes with a compact form factor, measuring 102.8 x 57.2 x 12.9 mm and weighing 108 grams. Its 3.2-inch TFT display offers a resolution of 320 x 480 pixels, resulting in a pixel density of around 180 ppi. Keep reading this article to know about the HTC Explorer price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of performance, you'll find the Qualcomm MSM7225A Snapdragon S1 chipset, featuring a 600 MHz Cortex A5 CPU and Adreno 200 GPU. While not a powerhouse, it handles essential tasks reasonably well. The primary camera is a 3.15 MP shooter capable of recording 480p videos. It’s functional for basic photography needs. This phone comes with 512MB of internal storage and 512MB of RAM. You can expand storage using a microSDHC card. Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 3.0, GPS, and stereo FM radio are part of its connectivity suite. It runs Android 2.3 (Gingerbread) with HTC’s Sense UI 3.5. A removable 1230 mAh Li-Ion battery powers the device, providing up to 485 hours of standby time (2G) or up to 445 hours (3G).
