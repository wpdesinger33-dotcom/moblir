The HTC Desire 630 arrived in Bangladesh during June 2016 as an affordable smartphone which provides reasonable specifications for its cost. It sports a 5.0-inch LCDdisplaywith a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The Qualcomm Snapdragon 400 chipset of this device includes a quad-core 1.6 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU and Adreno 305 GPU. The HTC Desire 630 features a 13 MP rearcamerawith an LED flash for photography functions. The camera system can create 1080p resolution videos. The 5 MP front camera on this device uses an f/2.8 aperture. The device includes a removable 2200 mAhbattery. The Desire 630 operates on Android 6.0 Marshmallow under the control of HTC's Sense UI. The device features a product selection that includes Black and White color options. The HTC Desire 630 price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,500 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
