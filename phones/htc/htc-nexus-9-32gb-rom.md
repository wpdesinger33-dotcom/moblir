The HTC Nexus 9 (2GB+32GB) model that was launched in November 2014 in Bangladesh. On the other hand, HTC Nexus 9 has another variant 2GB+16GB version that comes with the similar features ofdisplay, chipset together with a camera portion with the 2GB+32GB version. It has an 8.9 inch IPS LCD display, with display resolution of 1536 x2048 pixels. It works with Nvidia Tegra K1 chipset, two Denver cores with the frequency of 2.3 GHz, and DX1 Kepler GPU. It features the same Android 5.0 (Lollipop) withAndroid 7.1.1 (Nougat) as an upgrade. It also has an 8MP back facingcameraand a 1.6MP front facing camera and it has a 6700mAhbattery. The HTC Nexus 9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 40,000 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage.
