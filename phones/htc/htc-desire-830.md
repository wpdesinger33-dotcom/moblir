The HTC Desire 830 is a mid-range smartphone that was released in 2016. It features a 5.5-inch display with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. Runs on Android 6.0 Marshmallow with HTC’s custom Sense UI on top. Keep reading this article to know about the HTC Desire 830 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of performance, the Desire 830 is powered by an octa-core MediaTek MT6753 processor clocked at 1.5 GHz, paired with 3 GB of RAM. It offers 32 GB of internal storage, which can be expanded via a microSD card slot. It comes with a 13 MP rear camera with an f/2.0 aperture, autofocus, and LED flash. The front-facing camera is also 13 MP, making it suitable for selfies and video calls. This phone is equipped with a 2800 mAh non-removable battery, whic  should provide decent battery life for everyday use. The HTC Desire 830 price in Bangladesh is BDT 22,500 (Official) for its 3GB of RAM and 32GB of internal storage.
