The HTC One mini entered the market in August 2013 as a scaled-down version of the HTC One flagship smartphone. The device shows a 4.3-inch LCDdisplaythat delivers 720 x 1280 pixels resolution. The Qualcomm Snapdragon 400 chipset from Qualcomm drives the device through its dual-core 1.4 GHz Krait 200 CPU combined with Adreno 305 GPU processing unit. The HTC One mini contains a 4 MPcamerasystem at the rear position. The camera of this device allows users to capture 1080p resolution videos. The One mini features a 1.6 MP front-facing shooter. The 1800 mAhbatterytype is integrated as a non-removable component. The One mini operates on Android 4.2.2 Jelly Bean with HTC's Sense UI while offering an upgrade ability to Android 5.0 Lollipop. The device exists in three different color options which are Glacial Silver, Stealth Black and Blue. The HTC One mini price in Bangladesh is BDT 28,900 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
