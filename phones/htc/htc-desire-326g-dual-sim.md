The HTC Desire 326G dual sim, released in June 2015, features a 4.5-inch TFT display with a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels. Powered by a quad-core 1.2 GHz Spreadtrum SC7731G processor. The phone runs Android 4.4.2 KitKat. It supports dual SIM cards (Micro-SIM, dual stand-by). In terms of cameras, the rear packs an 8 MP autofocus camera with LED flash. For selfies, 2 MP front camera. The device is equipped with a 2000 mAh removable battery, providing up to 20 hours of talk time on 2G networks and up to 11 hours on 3G networks. The HTC Desire 326G dual sim price in Bangladesh is BDT 10500 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. Available colors include White Birch and Black Onyx.
