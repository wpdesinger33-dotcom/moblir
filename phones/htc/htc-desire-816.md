The HTC Desire 816 an upper mid-end smartphone with reasonable specifications. Launched in May 2014 in Bangladesh.  It incorporates 5.5 inch super LCDDisplaysupport with 720 x 1280 pixel density. The Qualcomm Snapdragon 400 chipset  with a powerful 1.6Ghz slider quad core processor. The device also has a 13MP rear and 5 MP front-facingcamera. The phone has Android 4.4.2 KitKat with HTC’s Sense 6.0 skin. It is powered by a 2600 mAhbattery. The HTC Desire 816 price in Bangladesh is BDT 27,500 (Official) for 1.5GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. It comes in Black, White, Red, Gray, and Green colors.
