The HTC Butterfly S, released in July 2013 in Bangladesh, is a high-performance smartphone that combines robust features with a sleek design. It boasts a 5.0-inch Super LCDdisplaywith a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. The device is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 600 chipset, featuring a quad-core 1.9 GHz Krait 300 CPU and Adreno 320 GPU. The camera setup includes a 4MP rearcamerawith autofocus and LED flash, capable of recording 1080p videos at 30fps. The front-facing camera is a 2.1MP shooter, also capable of 1080p video recording. The device runs on Android 4.2.2 Jelly Bean, upgradable to Android 4.4.2 KitKat, with HTC's Sense UI 5. One of the standout features of the HTC Butterfly S is its 3200mAh non-removablebattery. Available in various colors, including Black, Black, White, Red, and Gray. The HTC Butterfly S price in Bangladesh is BDT 49,900 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
