The HTC Desire HD which was released October 2010 is a upper mid sized smartphone that holds a good level in performance and specification. It comes embedded with a 4.3 inches Super LCD display with a pixel density of 480 x 800. It operates on a Qualcomm Snapdragon S2 MSM8255 system chip assisted by a 1.0 GHz Scorpion processor and an Adreno 205 GPU. In regard to design, the handset is slim and light with no heavy extra appendages; it has a weight of 164 grams and dimensions of 123 x 68 x 11.8mm. The phone offers the rear camera of 8MP with the autofocus and dual-LED flash is also installed. The 1230mAh battery provides. The HTC Desire HD price in Bangladesh is BDT 31,500 (Official) for 768MB of RAM and 1.5GB of phone storage.
