It features a 5.5-inch LCDdisplaywith a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The device is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 615 chipset, which includes an octa-core CPU and Adreno 405 GPU. The camera setup includes a 13MP rearcamerawith autofocus and LED flash, capable of recording 1080p videos at 30fps. The front-facing camera is an 8MP shooter, also capable of 1080p video recording. The device runs on Android 4.4.2 KitKat with HTC's Sense UI 6. The device is equipped with a 2600mAh non-removablebattery. Available in various colors, including Marble White, Tuxedo Grey, Santorini White, Milky-way Grey, Tangerine White, Saffron Grey, Flamingo Grey, Blue Misty, and Monarch Orange.
