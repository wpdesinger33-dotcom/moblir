The HTC Desire 310 dual sim offered budget-friendly features, including dual SIM support. It is an Android smartphone that was released in April 2014. It features a 4.5-inch TFT display with a resolution of 480 x 854 pixels. The phone is powered by a MediaTek MT6582M chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.3 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU and a Mali-400MP2 GPU. In terms of photography, the Desire 310 sports a 5 MP primary camera. For selfies, there’s a VGA front-facing camera. It also has a 2000 mAh removable battery. The HTC Desire 310 dual sim price in Bangladesh is BDT 14900 (Official) for 512MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage.
