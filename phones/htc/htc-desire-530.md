The HTC Desire 530 made its Bangladesh release in March 2016 as a cost-effective smartphone which provides acceptable performance at its price point. It sports a 5.0-inch LCDdisplaywith a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The Qualcomm Snapdragon 210 chipset runs the device and contains a quad-core 1.1 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU combined with an Adreno 304 GPU. The rearcamerasystem of HTC Desire 530 includes an 8 MP sensor with autofocus functionality and LED flash capabilities. The camera system of this device allows users to record videos at 720p resolution. Users can find a 5 MP front camera equipped with an f/2.8 aperture on the device. The device contains a non-removable 2200 mAhbatteryas one of its features. It operates under Android 6.0 Marshmallow together with Sense UI from HTC. This device comes in Black, and White colors. The HTC Desire 530 price in Bangladesh is BDT 11,500 (Official) for 1.5GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
