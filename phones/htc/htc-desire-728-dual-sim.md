The HTC Desire 728 dual sim smartphone released in November 2015 presents users with a combination of practical functionality and decent performance capabilities at its mid-range level. The smartphone presents a 5.5-inch Super LCDdisplaythat operates at 720 x 1280 pixels resolution. The device operates using the MediaTek MT6753 chipset that contains an octa-core 1.3 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and Mali-T720MP3 GPU. The 13MP rearcameraon the device contains autofocus and LED flash capabilities while it records videos at 1080p resolution at 30fps. The smartphone has a 5MP camera on its front side that enables users to record 1080p videos. HTC Sense UI operates on Android 5.1.1 Lollipop. An integrated 2800mAhbatteryoperates within the device with no possibility to remove it. This device comes in White and Black color options.
