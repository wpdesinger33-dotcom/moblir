The HTC Desire 20 Pro was released in June 2020, is a feature-rich Android smartphone catering to both power and photography enthusiasts. With a 6.5-inch IPS LCD display with 1080 x 2340 pixels. It offers a Snapdragon 665 chipset, an Octa-core CPU (4x2.0 GHz Kryo 260 Gold & 4x1.8 GHz Kryo 260 Silver), and an Adreno 610 GPU. The quad-camera setup includes a 48 MP main sensor, an 8 MP ultrawide lens, a 2 MP macro lens, and a 2 MP depth sensor. For selfies, 25 MP front camera. This device boasts a substantial 5000 mAh battery and supports 15W wired charging. The HTC Desire 20 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 26,000 (unofficial) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. Available in colors like Smoky Black, Pretty Blue, and Crystal Green.
