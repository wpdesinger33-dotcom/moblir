HTC Wildfire E2 offers good value for its price, making it an attractive choice for budget-conscious users. It was released in August 2020, is an Android smartphone with a 6.22-inch IPS LCD display with 720 x 1560 pixels. It features a MediaTek MT6762D Helio P22 chipset, an octa-core 1.8 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU, and PowerVR GE8320 GPU. In terms of photography, dual-camera setup on the rear: a 16 MP wide lens and a 2 MP depth sensor. For selfies,  8 MP front camera. The device runs on Android 10. Its 4000 mAh non-removable battery supports 10W wired charging. The HTC Wildfire E2 price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,990 (unofficial) for 4GB of RAM and 64Gb of phone storage. It comes in Sapphire Blue colors.
