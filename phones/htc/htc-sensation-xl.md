The HTC Sensation XL is a flagship-range Android phone that was launched in November 2011 in Bangladesh. The screen size is 4.7 inches S-LCD touch screen with resolution of 480 x 800 pixels resolution. The phone has a 1.5 GHz Qualcomm Snapdragon S2 processor. The Sensation XL has 16Gb of internal memory and 768Mb of RAM, and you can increase the memory up to 32Gb with a Micro SD card. It has an 8MP autofocus, LED flash rear camera and a front facing 1.3MP camera. The device comes with the 1600mAh removable battery. Other features are Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 3.0, FM radio, loudspeaker. It is an Android 2.3 (Gingerbread) powered phone accompanied with HTC’s Sense UI 3.5. The HTC Sensation XL price in Bangladesh is BDT 43,700 (Official).
