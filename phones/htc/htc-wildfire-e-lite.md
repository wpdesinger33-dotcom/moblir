The HTC Wildfire E Lite strikes a balance between price and essential features, It was released in December 2020. It is an Android 10 (Go edition) smartphone. Also it features a 5.45-inch HD+ display, which provides 720x1440 pixels. Powered by the MediaTek Helio A20 Quad-Core 12nm. The 8.0 MP + VGA rear dual camera captures moments with Bokeh, Beauty, and Panorama effects. With a 3000 mAh battery. The HTC Wildfire E Lite price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,558 (Unofficial) for 2GB of ROM and 32GB of phone storage. It comes in Blue color.
