The HTC Desire 610 announced in February 2014 in Bangladesh, is a mid-range Android smartphone. It features a 4.7-inch qHD display with a resolution of 540 x 960 pixels. The phone is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 400 chipset, which includes a quad-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU and an Adreno 305 GPU. In terms of photography, the Desire 610 sports an 8 MP primary camera. For selfies, 1.3 MP front camera . The 2040 mAh non-removable battery provides up to 650 hours of standby time (3G) and up to 16 hours of talk time (3G). The HTC Desire 610 price in Bangladesh is BDT 23900 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. It is available in various colors, including Black, White, Red, Gray, and Green.
