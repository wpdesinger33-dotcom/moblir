The HTC Desire 200 is a low-range Android smartphone released by HTC in 2013. It features a compact 3.5-inch LCD display with a resolution of 480 x 320 pixels. It runs on a Qualcomm Snapdragon S1 system-on-chip with a single-core 1 GHz CPU. Keep reading this article to know about the HTC Desire 200 price in Bangladesh and its details. Its rear camera is a fixed-focus 5 MP shooter with an f/2.8 aperture and 34 mm focal length, capable of recording videos up to 480p. This phone weighs 100 grams and houses a 1230 mAh battery. The HTC Desire 200 price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,500 (Official) for its 512GB of RAM and 4GB of internal storage.
