The HTC U23 Pro is a flagship device that comes with lots of amazing features and it was released in the Bangladesh market on 18 May 2023. It has a sleek design with a glass front (protected by Gorilla Glass Victus) and a plastic frame and back. It’s IP67 dust and water-resistant, making it durable. The 6.7-inch OLED display offers a smooth 120Hz refresh rate, vivid colors, and a resolution of 1080 x 2400 pixels. Keep reading this article to know about the HTC U23 Pro price in Bangladesh and its overall details. In terms of performance, you’ll find the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 1 chipset, an octa-core CPU, and Adreno 644 GPU. The quad-camera setup includes a whopping 108MP primary lens, an 8MP ultrawide lens, a 5MP macro lens, and a 2MP depth sensor. Selfies are handled by a 32MP front camera. It packs a 4600mAh battery, supporting 30W wired charging and 15W wireless charging. It’s available in Snow White and Coffee Black colors. The HTC U23 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 99,500 (Unofficial) for its 12GB of RAM and 256GB of internal storage.
