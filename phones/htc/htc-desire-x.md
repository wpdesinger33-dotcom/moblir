The HTC Desire X was a mid-range offering that catered to users seeking a balance between performance and affordability. It was announced in August 2012, features a 4.0-inch Super LCD display with a resolution of 480 x 800 pixels. Powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon S4 Play chipset, it runs on Android 4.0 (Ice Cream Sandwich), upgradable to 4.1.1 (Jelly Bean) with HTC’s Sense UI 4. The device is equipped with a dual-core 1.0 GHz Cortex-A5 CPU and Adreno 203 GPU. In terms of photography, the Desire X sports a 5 MP primary camera. Its 1650 mAh removable Li-Ion battery provides up to 20 hours of talk time on 2G networks.  The HTC Desire X price in Bangladesh is BDT 19500 (Official) for 768MB of RAM and 4GB of phone storage. Available in colors like White/Black and Prussian Blue.
