The HTC Desire 620G dual sim became available in Bangladesh during December 2014 as a cost-efficient smartphone which provides reasonable performance at its price point. It sports a 5.0-inch LCDdisplaywith a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The smartphone runs through the MediaTek MT6592 chipset that uses an octa-core 1.7 GHz Cortex-A7 CPU together with Mali-450MP4 GPU. The rearcameraof the HTC Desire 620G has an 8 MP resolution with autofocus capabilities and LED flash. The camera system of this device allows users to record videos at 1080p resolution. The device includes a 5 MP camera on its front. Users can replace the built-in 2100 mAhbatteryof this device. HTC's Sense UI operates on top of Android 4.4.4 KitKat as the operating system for the Desire 620G. Users can choose from six different color options when purchasing the HTC Desire 620G including Marble White, Milkyway Gray, Saffron Gray, Santorini White, Tangerine White and Tuxedo Gray. The HTC Desire 620G dual sim price in Bangladesh is BDT 16,640 (Official) with 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
