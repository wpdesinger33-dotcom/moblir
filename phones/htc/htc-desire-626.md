The HTC Desire 626 made its Bangladesh market entry in March 2015 as a smartphone that combines performance capabilities with elegant design features. This smartphone includes a 5.0-inch IPS LCDdisplaythat operates at 720 x 1280 pixels resolution. The Qualcomm Snapdragon 410 chipset of the device contains a quad-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU alongside an Adreno 306 GPU. The HTC Desire 626 has a 13 MP rearcameraas its main photography component. The camera system on this device has 1080p video recording capability. The phone includes a 5 MP camera system on its front-facing position. The device contains a built-in 2000 mAhbatterythat cannot be removed. The Desire 626 features Android 4.4.4 KitKat with HTC's Sense UI and it supports an upgrade to Android 6.0 Marshmallow. Users can choose between Black and White as available color options for this device model. The HTC Desire 626 price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,500 (Official) with 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
