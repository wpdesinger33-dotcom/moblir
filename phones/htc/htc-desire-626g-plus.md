The HTC Desire 626G Plus that became available in Bangladesh during April 2015 positions itself as a mid-range smartphone delivering equal proportions of performance alongside specific features. The device comes with an IPS LCDdisplayof 5.0 inches that supports a 720 x 1280 pixel resolution. The phone operates Android 4.4.2 KitKat while using a MediaTek MT6592 octa-core processor operating at 1.7 GHz speed and Mali-450MP4 GPU. The phone has a thin yet lightweight design with 138 grams weight and a dimension of 8.2mm. The rearcameraof the HTC Desire 626G Plus delivers 13MP imaging while using an f/2.2 aperture and autofocus technology for 1080p video recording at 30fps. The front camera uses a 5MP camera with f/2.8 aperture capability while recording 1080p videos. The 2000mAh Li-Po non-removablebatteryin this device enables a satisfactory usage duration that includes 22 hours of 2G network talk time. Available in colors like White Birch, Purple Fire, and Blue Lagoon. The HTC Desire 626G Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,750 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
