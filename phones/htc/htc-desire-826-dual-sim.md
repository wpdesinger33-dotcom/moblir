The HTC Desire 826 dual sim released in the Bangladesh market in March 2015 delivers mid-range performance through its combination of design and functionality. The LCDdisplayof this phone measures 5.5 inches while offering 1080 x 1920 pixels resolution. The device operates on a Qualcomm Snapdragon 615 chipset that has an octa-core CPU together with Adreno 405 GPU. The HTC Desire 826 dual sim model comes with a 13 MP rearcamerasystem that includes autofocus and an LED flash. The device supports 1080p video recording. The camera specifications for the front-facing lens differ per region and it has a 4 MP shooter. The device incorporates a non-removable 2600 mAhbattery. The Desire 826 functions with Android 5.0.1 Lollipop and HTC's Sense UI and supports an update to Android 6.0.1 Marshmallow. The device becomes available in five distinct color options which include White Birch, Blue Lagoon, Marshmallow White, Dark Grey alongside Purple Fire. The HTC Desire 826 dual sim price in Bangladesh is BDT 22,300 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
