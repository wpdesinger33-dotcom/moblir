It is compatible with a refresh rate of up to 120Hz. It is a mobile phone with quite small dimensions of 167.1 x 74.9 x 9 mm and it is also quite lightweight 198 g. Equipped with the modern Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 processor. There is also a 50MP main camera and a 50MP front camera. It has a 4600mAh battery with fast charging of 60W.
