The HTC One V is a smartphone that was part of the HTC One series. Announced in February 2012 and released in April 2012, it featured Android 4.0.3 (Ice Cream Sandwich) with HTC’s Sense UI 4. The phone had a 3.7-inch Super LCD2 display with a resolution of 480x800 pixels. Its design, reminiscent of the HTC Legend from 2010, showcased an aluminum unibody construction. Keep reading this article to know about the HTC One V price in Bangladesh and its details. This phone packed a Qualcomm MSM8255 Snapdragon S2 chipset, a 1.0 GHz Scorpion CPU, and Adreno 205 GPU. It offered 512 MB of RAM and 4 GB of internal storage (with 1 GB available to users). The 5-megapixel rear camera featured autofocus and an LED flash, capable of recording 720p videos. Beats Audio technology enhanced the audio experience. The phone had a non-removable 1500 mAh Li-Ion battery. While it wasn’t upgradable to Android 4.1 (Jelly Bean), the HTC One V left its mark as a mid-range device with a stylish design and decent performance. The HTC One V price in Bangladesh is BDT 16,000 for its 512MB of RAM and 4GB of internal storage.
