An 8MP camera serves as the primarycameracomponent in this device. The device comes with a 2MP camera located on its front side. The device operates on Android 4.2.2 Jelly Bean while employing the HTC's Sense UI 5.5 interface. The removablebatteryinside the device holds a capacity of 2000mAh.
