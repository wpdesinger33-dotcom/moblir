The HTC One E9 Plus smartphone arrived on the market in May 2015 Bangladesh as a premium device that combines advanced features with an elegant body design. This smartphone comes with a 5.5-inch IPS LCDdisplaywhich supports 1440 x 2560 pixels resolution. The device operates with a MediaTek MT6795M Helio X10 chipset that integrates an octa-core 2.0 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU together with PowerVR G6200 GPU. The HTC One E9 Plus comes with a rearcameraequipped with autofocus and LED flash that has 20 MP resolution. The camera system of this device captures 4K resolution videos. Thet front-facing camera is a 4 MP camera. The device features a permanent 2800 mAhbatteryfor power delivery. The One E9 Plus operates on Android 5.0 Lollipop through HTC's Sense UI but users can update their software to Android 6.0 Marshmallow. Customers can choose between Meteor Gray and Classic Rose Gold and Gold Sepia variations for this device. The HTC One E9 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 22,900 (Official) with 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage.
