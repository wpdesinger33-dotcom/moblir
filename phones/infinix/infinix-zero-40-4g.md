The Infinix Zero 40 series is poised to be a compelling choice for vloggers and content creators. On the front, there’s a punch-hole selfie camera integrated into a curved 6.78-inch 144Hz 3D curved AMOLED display. This display promises a smooth and immersive viewing experience, perfect for content creators who prioritize high-quality visuals. The standout aspect is the camera setup tailored for vloggers. It includes: A 108MP main camera with Optical Image Stabilization (OIS), A 50MP ultra-wide lens with a 120° field of view, A 50MP front-facing camera Both front and rear cameras support 4K video recording at 60fps, a rare feature in this class. Also, adaptive anti-shake technology (OIS+EIS) ensures stable footage even in dynamic conditions. Powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 8200 Ultimate 5G processor, the Zero 40 promises robust performance. The phone supports 45W wired charging and 20W wireless charging. It launches with Android 14 and will receive updates up to Android 16, along with three years of security patches. The Infinix Zero 40 price in Bangladesh is BDT 25,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage. It will be available in Misty Aqua, Blossom Glow, and Rock Black color options.
