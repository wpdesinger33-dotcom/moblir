The Infinix Zero 40 is a highly anticipated smartphone Expected to release on September 8, 2024. It boasts a sleek design with dimensions of 164.3 x 74.8 x 7.9 mm and a weight of 195 grams. The device features a 6.78-inch AMOLED display with a resolution of 1080 x 2436 pixels, offering vibrant colors and sharp visuals. The screen supports HDR and a 144Hz refresh rate, making it ideal for gaming and multimedia consumption. Protected by Corning Gorilla Glass 5, the display is both durable and visually impressive. Under the hood, the Infinix Zero 40 is powered by the Mediatek Dimensity 8200 Ultimate chipset. The phone runs on Android 14 with XOS 14.5, promising up to two major Android upgrades. The device is equipped with a 5000 mAh battery that supports 45W wired charging, 20W wireless charging, and 10W reverse wired charging, ensuring long-lasting battery life and quick recharges. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the Infinix Zero 40’s camera setup, which includes a triple rear camera system featuring a 108MP main sensor, a 50MP ultra-wide lens, and a 2MP depth sensor. The front camera is a 50MP shooter, perfect for high-quality selfies and video calls. The Infinix Zero 40 price in Bangladesh is BDT 50,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage. Available in colors like Rock Black, Violet Garden, and Moving Titanium.
