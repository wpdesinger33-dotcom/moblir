The Infinix Note 40S is a feature-packed smartphone designed to cater to a wide range of users. It boasts a 6.78-inch AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate and a resolution of 2436x1080 pixels, ensuring vibrant colors and smooth visuals. The device is powered by the MediaTek Helio G99 Ultimate chipset. The phone runs on Android 14 with Infinix’s XOS 14 skin, offering a user-friendly interface and a host of customization options. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the Infinix Note 40S’s camera setup, which includes a 108MP main sensor, a 2MP macro lens, and a 32MP front camera for high-quality selfies. The rear camera supports various modes such as Super Night, Slow Motion, and Time-Lapse, allowing users to capture stunning photos and videos in different scenarios. The device also features dual speakers tuned by JBL, ensuring an immersive audio experience whether you’re watching videos or listening to music. Battery life is another strong point of the Infinix Note 40S, with its 5000mAh battery supporting 33W fast charging and 20W wireless charging. This ensures that the phone can easily last through a day of heavy usage and can be quickly recharged when needed. Additional features include an under-display fingerprint sensor, IP54 dust and splash resistance. The Infinix Note 40S price in Bangladesh is BDT 26,999 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage. Its sleek design available in colors like Obsidian Black and Vintage Green.
