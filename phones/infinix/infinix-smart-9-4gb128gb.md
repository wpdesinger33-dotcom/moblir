The Infinix Smart 9 (4GB+128GB) variant is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers a solid combination of performance and storage. Another variant of the Infinix Smart 9 is the 3GB+64GB model. The primary difference lies in its memory and storage capacity. The 3GB RAM may result in slightly less efficient multitasking compared to the 4GB variant, and the 64GB internal storage offers less space for apps, media, and files. It features a 6.7-inch IPS LCD display with a 120Hz refresh rate. Powered by the Mediatek Helio G81 chipset, it runs on Android 14, ensuring a modern and efficient user interface. The device is equipped with a dual rear camera setup, including a 50MP main sensor, which is capable of capturing detailed photos. Additionally, it has a 5000mAh battery that supports fast charging. The Infinix Smart 9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 11,999 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage.
