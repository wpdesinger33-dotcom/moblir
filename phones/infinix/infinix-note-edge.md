The Infinix Note Edge didn’t just arrive in early 2026 it made an entrance. Officially released on 23 January 2026, this smartphone lands as a bold mid-range contender with a vibe that feels premium without the premium price tag. The Note Edge shocks with a 6.78-inch curved AMOLED screen that glitters with sharp color and smooth images, a slight suggestion that Infinix is not toying with the idea of big-screen experiences. It is based on the MediaTek Dimensity 7100 5G chipset and runs onAndroid 16with XOS 16 on top, which guarantees fast daily performance and good future-proofing. However, the best part is the 6,500mAhbatterythat fits into its slender 7.2 mm frame, that is stamina without the size. Include 50 MP primary camera, smooth curved glass, waterproof construction, and large storage space. The Infinix Note Edge price in Bangladesh is BDT 29,999 (Official) for 8GB RAM and 128GB storage. This smartphone comes in Lunar Titanium, Silk Green, Stellar Blue, and Shadow Black.
