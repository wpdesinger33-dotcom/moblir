The Infinix Smart 8, with its 4GB RAM and 128GB storage variant, offers a compelling blend of features at a competitive price point in Bangladesh. The device boasts a 6.6-inch IPS LCD display with a 90Hz refresh rate, ensuring smooth visuals whether you’re browsing the web or watching videos. The display’s 500 nits peak brightness makes it easily readable even under direct sunlight. Under the hood, the Infinix Smart 8 is powered by the Unisoc T606 chipset, which, along with the ample memory configuration, provides a seamless experience for everyday tasks and moderate gaming. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the dual-camera setup on the rear, featuring a 13MP primary sensor complemented by a depth sensor for portrait shots. The 8MP front camera ensures your selfies are crisp and clear. The device runs on Android 13, offering the latest features and security updates. One of the key selling points of the Infinix Smart 8 is its large 5000mAh battery, which can easily last a full day on a single charge, and the 10W fast charging support means you won’t be tethered to an outlet for long. The dedicated microSDXC slot allows for storage expansion, ensuring you won’t run out of space for your photos, videos, and apps.
