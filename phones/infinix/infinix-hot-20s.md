The Infinix Hot 20S, with its 8GB RAM and 128GB storage variant, stands out in the competitive smartphone market. Equipped with a Mediatek MT6781 Helio G96 chipset and running on Android 12, the Infinix Hot 20S promises smooth multitasking and a user-friendly experience with its XOS 10.6 interface. The device boasts a large 6.78-inch IPS LCD display with a 120Hz refresh rate, ensuring crisp visuals and fluid navigation. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the triple camera setup, featuring a 50MP main sensor complemented by macro and depth sensors for versatile shooting options. An 8MP front camera ensures your selfies are clear and social media-ready. It is not just about looks and performance; it’s also practical. A robust 5000mAh battery supports extended usage, and with 18W fast charging, you won’t be tethered to an outlet for long. The side-mounted fingerprint sensor, accelerometer, proximity, and compass round out the feature set, making it a secure and convenient device. Whether you’re a power user or someone who values aesthetics and camera capabilities, the Infinix Hot 20S is a smartphone that delivers quality. The official price in Bangladesh is BDT 18,999 for 8GB RAM and 128GB ROM. It comes in vibrant colors, including Sonic Black, Tempo Blue, Fantasy Purple, Light-rider White, and Neon, catering to diverse aesthetic preferences. This smartphone is available for purchase through authorized Infinix retailers, Infinix brand shops, and online platforms.
