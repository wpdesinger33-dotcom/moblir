As the name suggests the Infinix hot 50 Pro 4G is on its way to being a mid-end smartphone with a beautiful design and rather robust features. It is equipped with a 6.78-inch AMOLED screen with a refresh rate of 120Hz. The phone will be running MediaTek Helio G100 chipset. The camera included a 50-megapixel main sensor and one or two other lenses. A 5000mAh battery that can support fast charging of 33W. Mentioned versions will also support an in-display fingerprint sensor, twin front-facing speakers with JBL enrichment, and an IP54 certificate for dust and water protection. The Infinix hot 50 Pro 4G price in Bangladesh is BDT 18,999 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. This phone comes in Sleek Black, Titanium Grey, and Glacier Blue colors.
