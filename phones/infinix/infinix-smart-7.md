The Infinix Smart 7, with its 3GB+64GB variant, is a noteworthy entrant in the budget smartphone market. Released in February 2023, this model boasts a 6.6-inch IPS LCD display, providing a decent screen size for media consumption. The resolution of 720x1612 pixels is standard for its price range, ensuring that users have a satisfactory visual experience. Under the hood, the device runs on Android 12, offering a contemporary operating system with the latest features from the Android ecosystem. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the dual-camera setup, featuring a 13MP main sensor complemented by a depth sensor for portrait shots. The selfie camera is a modest 5MP, but it’s equipped with an LED flash to brighten up those personal photos. The Infinix Smart 7 also includes a dedicated microSDXC slot, allowing users to expand storage if needed. A substantial 5000mAh battery powers the device, which should comfortably last a full day on a single charge, and it supports 10W wired charging. The official price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,400 for the 3GB+64GB variant. It comes in 4 different colors Polar Black, Coastal Green, Iceland White, and Peacock Blue. You can buy this model from authorized Infinix retailers, Infinix brand shops, and online marketplace.
