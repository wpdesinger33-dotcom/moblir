Infinix Note 12 Pro comes with 6.7 inches Full HD+ AMOLED screen. It has a waterdrop notch front camera design. The back camera is of triple 108+2+0.3 Megapixel with PDAF, depth sensor, 1/1.67″, f/1.8 aperture, quad-LED flash, HDR etc. and Quad HD video recording. The front camera is of 16 MP. Infinix Note 12 Pro comes with 5000 mAh battery with 33W fast charging. It has 8 GB RAM, up to 2.2 GHz octa-core CPU and Mali-G57 MC2 GPU. It is powered by a MediaTek Helio G99 (12 nm) chipset. The device comes with 256 GB internal storage and dedicated MicroSD slot. There is a side-mounted fingerprint sensor in this phone. Among other features, there is FM Radio, Dual SIM, face unlock etc.
