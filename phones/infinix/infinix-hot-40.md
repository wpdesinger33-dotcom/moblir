The Infinix Hot 40 is a smartphone that has been making waves in the tech community, particularly for its impressive specifications at an affordable price point. Launched in December 2023, the device comes in several variants, offering a choice between 8 GB of RAM and either 128 GB or 256 GB of internal storage. It’s available in a range of eye-catching colors: Palm Blue, Horizon Gold, Starlit Black, and Starfall Green, ensuring there’s a style to suit everyone’s taste. Notable features of the Infinix Hot 40 include a large 6.78-inch IPS LCD display with a 90Hz refresh rate for smooth scrolling and a peak brightness of 500 nits, making it easy to view even in bright conditions. The phone runs on Android 13 with Infinix’s own XOS 13.5 user interface, powered by the Mediatek Helio G88 chipset. Photography lovers will appreciate the dual 50 MP primary camera setup, complemented by a 32 MP front camera for high-quality selfies. Moreover, the device boasts a robust 5000 mAh battery supported by 33W fast charging, which promises to get you from 20% to 75% in just 35 minutes.
