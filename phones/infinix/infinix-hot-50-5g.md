The Infinix HOT 50 is a feature-packed smartphone designed to offer a balanced performance at an affordable price. It boasts a 6.7-inch IPS LCD display with a 120Hz refresh rate. The screen resolution is 720 x 1600 pixels, which, while not the highest, ensures decent clarity for everyday use. The device is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 6300 chipset. In terms of photography, the Infinix HOT 50 is equipped with a dual-camera setup on the rear, featuring a 48MP main sensor and a 2MP depth sensor. For selfies, 8MP front camera, which is adequate for video calls and social media updates. The phone runs on Android 14 with XOS 14.5, offering a user-friendly interface and a range of customization options. One of the standout features of the Infinix HOT 50 is its 5000mAh battery, which supports 18W fast charging. The Infinix HOT 50 price in Bangladesh is BDT 18,000 (Expected) for 4GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. Available in vibrant colors like Blue, Black, Green, and Purple.
