The Infinix GT 10 Pro is a compelling smartphone that packs impressive features into mid-range packages. It ensures smooth multitasking and ample space for your apps, photos, and files. Its 6.67-inch AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate delivers vibrant visuals and smooth animations. The 108MP triple-camera setup captures detailed photos, while the 32MP front camera excels in selfies. Additionally, the 5000mAh battery ensures all-day usage, and the 45W fast charging gets you back up and running swiftly. The Infinix GT 10 Pro Price in Bangladesh is BDT 30,000 (unofficial) for its 8/256 GB variant. It comes with two different colors which are Cyber Black, Mirage Silver.
