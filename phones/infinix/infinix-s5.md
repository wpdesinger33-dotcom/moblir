The Infinix S5 is an Android smartphone that was announced in October 2019. In this article we will share Infinix S5 price in Bangladesh and specs. It features a 6.6-inch display with an IPS LCD panel and a resolution of 720 x 1600 pixels. The phone is powered by a MediaTek MT6762 Helio P22 chipset, which includes an octa-core 2.0 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and a PowerVR GE8320 GPU. The rear camera setup consists of a triple camera system with a 16 MP primary lens, a 5 MP ultrawide lens, and a 2 MP depth sensor. For selfies, 32 MP front camera. This phone is equipped with a 4000 mAh non-removable battery. The Infinix S5 price in Bangladesh is BDT 13,990 for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage. This model comes in Quetzal Cyan, and Violet colors.
