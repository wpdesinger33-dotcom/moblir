The Infinix Note 12 2023 (8/256GB) is a budget-friendly mid-range smartphone, packs impressive features that cater to both productivity and entertainment. It comes with a 6.7-inch AMOLED display with a resolution of 1080 x 2400 pixels. This vibrant screen provides sharp visuals and an immersive viewing experience for multimedia content, gaming, and productivity tasks. Under the hood, the Mediatek Helio G99 chipset powers the Note 12 (2023). Its octa-core CPU ensures smooth multitasking and efficient performance, whether you’re gaming or handling everyday tasks. The 50 MP primary camera captures detailed photos, while the 2MP depth sensor adds artistic bokeh effects. Additionally, the QVGA camera enhances low-light performance. For selfies, the 16MP front camera delivers clear and well-lit self-portraits. With a 5000mAh non-removable battery, the Note 12 (2023) keeps you connected throughout the day. The 33W wired charging feature ensures quick top-ups when needed. Enjoy rich sound quality with the 24-bit/192kHz Hi-Res audio and dual speakers. Whether you’re streaming music or watching videos, the audio experience is enhanced.
