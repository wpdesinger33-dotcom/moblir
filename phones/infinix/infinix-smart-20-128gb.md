The arrival of the Infinix Smart 20 (128GB) in the Bangladesh market on February 22, 2026, was like a low-end phone that had picked up some tricks with more expensive handsets. The 4GB+128GB model makes the deal even more palatable as it doubles storage capacity to 128GB, allowing you to store even more photos, apps, and videos without immediately resorting to a microSD card, which is the only real difference between it and the 4GB+64GB model. Their hearts are similar: both models have a MediaTek Helio G81 Ultimate chipset and 4GB of RAM, a large 6.78-inch IPS LCD with a smooth 120Hzrefresh rate, and a massive 5200 mAhbatterythat is capable of sustaining a full day of social scrolling, music, and videos. Cameras remain simple at 8MP front and back and connectivity is available at 4G, NFC, FM radio and side-mounted fingerprint security. Simply put, the 128GB is ideal to any person who is a media hoarder or does not wish to carry around storage cards, and the 64GB gives the same experience but with a more affordable price tag. The Infinix Smart 20 price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,499 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 128GB of storage.
