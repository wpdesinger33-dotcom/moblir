Infinix Hot 30definitely shook and shaped the smartphone market after its release. Both 4 and 8 GB versions are highly attractive deals. Firstly the design looks nice and stylish, with color-shifting back panels (except the black color). This alone attracts a lot of potential buyers. Then just look at its overall specifications. You get almost everything that you desire at a price like this. Full HD display, good quality camera, 33W charger, Helio G88 chipset, stereo speakers and so on. But there is no screen protection or splash resistance. Also, a noise cancellation microphone is missing which are some of its drawbacks.
