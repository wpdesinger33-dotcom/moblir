The Infinix Hot 11 Play emerged as a budget-friendly smartphone, launched on November 15, 2021. If you want to know about the Infinix Hot 11 Play price in Bangladesh, then this article is for you. Keep reading this article to know more details of this smartphone. This smartphone comes with a large 6.82-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720x1640 pixels and a typical brightness of 440 nits. Under the hood, the device is powered by the Mediatek Helio G35 chipset. Running on Android 11 with XOS 7.6, the Infinix Hot 11 Play is equipped with a generous 6000mAh battery, supporting 10W charging to keep you connected throughout the day. Photography lovers will appreciate the dual rear camera setup, featuring a 13-megapixel primary sensor and a QVGA lens, complemented by a quad-LED flash. For selfies, an 8-megapixel front camera is available, ensuring bright and clear shots.
