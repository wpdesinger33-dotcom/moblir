The Infinix Smart 5 Pro Price in Bangladesh offers a balance of performance and affordability. This smartphone, released on 17 November 2021, features a 6.52-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720x1600 pixels, providing a clear and vibrant visual experience. Under the hood, the Infinix Smart 5 Pro is powered by the Unisoc SC9863A chipset, paired with 2GB of RAM and 32GB of internal storage, expandable via a dedicated microSD slot. It runs on Android 11 (Go edition) with XOS 7.6, ensuring a smooth user interface. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the dual 13MP rear camera setup, complemented by a QVGA sensor and quad-LED flash, capable of recording videos at 1440p. The front-facing 8MP camera with dual-LED flash is perfect for selfies and video calls. The device’s large 6000mAh battery supports prolonged usage, making it ideal for users who are on the go. Despite its robust features, the Infinix Smart 5 Pro does not support 5G connectivity, but it does offer reliable 4G LTE speeds for browsing and streaming. With its rear-mounted fingerprint sensor, users can enjoy enhanced security and quick access to their device.
