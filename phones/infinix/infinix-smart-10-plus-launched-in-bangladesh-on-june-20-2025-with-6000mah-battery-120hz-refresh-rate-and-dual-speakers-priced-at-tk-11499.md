A leading manufacturer of affordable smartphones, Infinix Mobility has unveiled its new device, the Smart 10 Plus, in Bangladesh. It was launched in the Bangladeshi market on June 20, 2025, entering the entry-level market by relying on attractive features and low price. The Infinix Smart 10 Plus seems to have found a niche among affordable consumers who want smooth performance, long battery life, and plenty of storage. Due to its potentially low price, it is also highly competitive in emerging markets. Let’s find out the Infinix Smart 10 Plus Price in Bangladesh and Specifications. Infinix Smart 10 Plus Update Price in Bangladesh The Infinix 10 Smart Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 11.499 (Official). It comes with 4GB RAM + 4GB extended RAM and 128GB phone storage, it can be expanded via SD card.
