The Infinix Note 7 is an Android smartphone that was announced in April 2020. Here we will share Infinix Note 7 price in Bangladesh and specs. It features a large 6.95-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1640 pixels. The phone is powered by the MediaTek Helio G70 chipset, which includes an octa-core processor (2x2.0 GHz Cortex-A75 and 6x1.7 GHz Cortex-A55) and the Mali-G52 2EEMC2 GPU. The Note 7 boasts a quad-camera setup on the back, including a 48 MP main camera, a 2 MP macro lens, a 2 MP depth sensor. For selfies, 16 MP front camera. The phone runs on Android 10 with the XOS 6.0 skin and is powered by a 5000 mAh non-removable battery with 18W fast charging support. The Infinix Note 7 price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,990 for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage. It is an official variant. Available colors include Forest Green, Aether Black, and Bolivia Blue.
