The Infinix Smart 8 Pro is a versatile smartphone designed for the modern user at a reasonable price tag. It was launched in the Bangladesh market on January 29, 2024. If you want to know about the Infinix Smart 8 Pro price in Bangladesh, then this article is for you. Keep reading this article to know the whole details of this smartphone. It features a 6.6-inch IPS LCD display with a 90Hz refresh rate, providing smooth visuals and a responsive touch experience. The screen resolution stands at 720x1612 pixels, with a pixel density of approximately 267 ppi, ensuring clear and vibrant images. In terms of performance, the Infinix Smart 8 Pro is powered by the Mediatek Helio G36 chipset.
