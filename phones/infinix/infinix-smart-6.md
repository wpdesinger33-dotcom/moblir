Infinix Smart 6 is a low-end smartphone that was launched in October 2021. It features a 6.6-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1600 pixels and a brightness of 500 nits. It runs on Android 11 (Go edition) with XOS 7.6 user interface. It is powered by an octa-core Unisoc SC9863A chipset with 2 GB or 3 GB of RAM and 32 GB or 64 GB of internal storage. It has a dual rear camera setup with an 8 MP main sensor and a 0.8 MP depth sensor, and a 5 MP selfie camera with LED flash.
