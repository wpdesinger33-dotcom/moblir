Infinix Smart 20 was officially released on February 22, 2026 in Bangladesh. It surprisingly ticks a lot of the contemporary boxes considering it is a budget phone. At the front, there is a massive 6.78-inch IPS LCD with a smooth 120Hzrefresh rateand a maximum of 700 nits brightness. The resolution is 720x1576 pixels, but the screen is made to be immersive with its thin bezels, and 90 percent screen-to-body ratio. The phone can comfortably run daily applications, social media and light gaming with the help of the MediaTek Helio G81 Ultimate chipset that has 4GB LPDDR4X RAM. Storage is up to 128GB and has a dedicated microSD slot (up to 2TB). The real hero in this situation is the 5200mAhbatterythat has long standby (933+hours on 4G) with 15W charging and even 5W reverse charging. Includes IP64 splash protection, side fingerprint sensor, NFC, FM radio, DTS-tuned audio, and Android 16  and the Smart 20 is smarter than its cost. The Infinix Smart 20 price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,999 (Official) with 4GB RAM and 64GB storage.
