The Infinix Note 50 exists as a smartphone that combines features from both performance segments and advanced technical capabilities. The Note 50 features a 6.78-inch FHD+ AMOLEDdisplaywhich operates at 1080x2436 pixels resolution with a 144Hz refresh rate. The device operates with the MediaTek Helio G100 Ultimate chipset as its power source. The smartphone operates with Android 15 combined with XOS 15 custom skin. The Infinix Note 50 has a triple-camera system at the back with a 50MP main sensor that includes OIS technology and a 2MP macro lens. The Note 50 features a 13MP camera located on its front side. The Note 50 allows users to record 1080p video at a frame rate of 30fps. Users can enjoy extended use from the device due to its 5200mAh non-removable Li-Polybatterywith 45W wired and 30W wireless fast charger. Customers can choose from four different color options for this device which includes Titanium Grey, Ruby Red, Mountain Shade and Shadow Black. The Infinix Note 50 price in Bangladesh is BDT 25,999 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
