The Infinix Note 10 Price in Bangladesh reflects its status as a budget-friendly yet feature-packed smartphone. Launched on 7 June 2021, the Infinix Note 10 boasts a large 6.95-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080x2460 pixels, providing an immersive viewing experience. Under the hood, it’s powered by the MediaTek Helio G85 chipset, coupled with 6GB of RAM, ensuring smooth performance across applications. Storage options are generous, with 128GB variants available, expandable via a dedicated microSDXC slot. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the Infinix Note 10’s triple camera setup, featuring a 48 MP main sensor complemented by a 2 MP depth sensor and a 2 MP monochrome sensor, along with a Quad-LED flash, HDR, and panorama capabilities. For selfies and video calls, a 16 MP front camera does the job adeptly. The device runs on Android 11 with XOS 7.6 and is kept alive by a robust 5000 mAh battery, supported by 18W fast charging to minimize downtime. Despite its plastic build, the phone includes a side-mounted fingerprint sensor, accelerometer, proximity, and compass for a comprehensive user experience. The Infinix Note 10 Price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,990 for the official variant. It has 6GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage. It comes in Black, Purple, and Emerald Green colors.
