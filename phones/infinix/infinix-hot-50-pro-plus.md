The most notable upper mid-tier smartphone which this article seeks to review is the Infinix Hot 50 Pro Plus, famous for its slim profile and performance to match. The phone has a display of 6.78 inches AMOLED with pixel density at 1080 x 2460. The screen has a facility for animations to run at 120 frames per second. On the inside, the Hot 50 Pro Plus is driven by the MediaTek Helio G100 processor. This device comes with a 5000mAh battery that can also be charged with a maximum of 33W. There is a 50MP main camera, and a 2MP depth sensor on the camera side of the device. The phone operates on Android, version 14 with Infinix’s own user interface, XOS 14.5. The Infinix Hot 50 Pro Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 22,999 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
