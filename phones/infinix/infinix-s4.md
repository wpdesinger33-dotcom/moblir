The Infinix S4, introduced in April 2019, boasts several notable features. It sports a 6.2-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1520 pixels. Under the hood, it runs on the MediaTek Helio P22 chipset, which houses an Octa-core 2.0 GHz Cortex-A53 processor and the PowerVR GE8320 GPU. Keep reading this article to know about the Infinix S4 price in Bangladesh and its overall details. In terms of photography, the triple rear camera setup includes a 13 MP main sensor, complemented by an 8 MP and a 2 MP lens. For selfies, there’s a 32 MP front camera with an f/2.0 aperture. The device is powered by a 4000 mAh non-removable battery. Design-wise, the Infinix S4 features a glass front and a plastic back and frame. It supports dual SIM cards (Nano-SIM) and offers connectivity options like Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac, Bluetooth 5.0, and microUSB 2.0. Additionally, it includes sensors such as fingerprint (rear-mounted), accelerometer, gyro, proximity, and compass. The Infinix S4 price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,000 for its 3GB of RAM and 32GB of internal storage.
