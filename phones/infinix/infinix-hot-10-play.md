The Infinix Hot 10 Play, announced in January 2021, boasts a large 6.82-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1640 pixels. Its screen-to-body ratio is approximately 82.4%, providing ample real estate for your content. Under the hood, it runs on Android 10 (Go edition) with the XOS 7 skin. Keep reading this article to know about the Infinix Hot 10 Play price in Bangladesh and its details. Powering the Hot 10 Play is the MediaTek MT6762G Helio G25 chipset, an octa-core processor consisting of four Cortex-A53 cores clocked at 2.0 GHz and four Cortex-A53 cores running at 1.5 GHz. It comes in two memory configurations: 32GB storage with 2GB RAM or 64GB storage with 4GB RAM (eMMC 5.1). For photography enthusiasts, the Hot 10 Play features a dual-camera setup on the rear. The primary 13 MP lens with an f/1.8 aperture captures decent shots, while the QVGA sensor assists with depth information. On the front, there’s an 8 MP selfie camera with an f/2.0 aperture, accompanied by an LED flash.
