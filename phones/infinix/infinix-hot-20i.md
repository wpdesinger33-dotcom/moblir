Infinix Hot 20i comes with 6.6 inches HD+ IPS LCD screen. It has a waterdrop notch front camera design. The back camera is of triple 13+0.3+0.3 Megapixel with autofocus, f/1.8, dual-LED flash, etc. and Full HD video recording. The front camera is of 8 MP. Infinix Hot 20i comes with 5000 mAh battery with 10W fast charging. It has 4 GB RAM, 2.0 GHz octa-core CPU and PowerVR GE8320 GPU. It is powered by a Mediatek Helio A22 (12 nm) chipset. The device comes with 64 GB internal storage and dedicated MicroSD slot. There is a back-mounted fingerprint sensor in this phone. Among other features, there is FM Radio, Dual SIM, face unlock etc.
