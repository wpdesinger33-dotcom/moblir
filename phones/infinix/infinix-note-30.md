Infinix Note 30 comes with 6.78 inches Full HD+ IPS LCD screen. It has a center punch-hole front camera design. The back camera is of triple 64+2 Megapixel+ QVGA with PDAF, macro depth sensor, quad-LED flash, HDR etc. and Quad HD video recording. The front camera is of 32 MP. The phone comes with 5000 mAh battery with 45W fast charging and reverse charging facility. It has 8 GB RAM, up to 2.2 GHz octa-core CPU and Mali-G57 MC2 GPU. It is powered by a MediaTek Helio G99 (6 nm) chipset. The device comes with 128 or 256 GB internal storage and dedicated MicroSD slot. There is a side-mounted fingerprint sensor in this phone. Among other features, there is FM Radio, Dual SIM, face unlock, dual speakers etc.
