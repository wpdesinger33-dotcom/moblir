The Infinix Hot 50 Pro Plus 5G is a mid-range smartphone with perfect integration of performance and features for an affordable price point. It comes with a 6.78 inches AMOLED screen with 1080 x 2426 pixels and 120 Hz screen refresh rate. The phone is built on MediaTek Helio G100 chip. Specs for the camera are: 50 MP for the main image; 2 MP for depth; a front lens for taking selfies is 13 MP. The battery capacity of 5000mAh supports 33W wired charging and 50% charge in 26 minutes to offer long battery durability and fast charging. It currently runs on Android 14 with XOS 14.5 on top. The Infinix Hot 50 Pro Plus 5G price in Bangladesh is BDT 25,000 (Expected) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of internal storage. The phone comes in Sleek Black, Titanium Grey, and Dreamy Purple colors.
