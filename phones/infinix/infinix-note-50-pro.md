The Infinix Note 50 Pro combines multiple advanced features to fulfill present-day user requirements. The device features a 6.78-inch AMOLEDdisplaywhich operates at 1080x2436 pixels resolution and supports 144Hz refresh rate. The device operates with the MediaTek Helio G100 Ultimate chipset. The phone functions with Android 15 along with XOS 15 as its operating system. The Infinix Note 50 Pro has two rear cameras that include a 50MP primary sensor with OIS and an 8MP ultra-wide-angle lens. The front camera of this device uses a 32MP shooter. This device enables users to record videos in 1440p resolution at 30fps and in 1080p resolution at multiple frame rates. This device comes with a 5200mAh non-removable Li-Polybatterywhich supports 90W wired fast charging and 30W wireless charging and 10W reverse wired charging. The device offers four color options including Titanium Grey, Enchanted Purple, Racing Edition and Shadow Black. The Infinix Note 50 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 29,999 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
