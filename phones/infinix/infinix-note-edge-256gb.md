Infinix Note Edge was officially released in Bangladesh on 23 January 2026, and immediately attracted attention due to its ability to provide large features at a reasonable price. The phone has a big 6.78-inch curved AMOLEDdisplay, which is bright, smooth, and high-end to use on a daily basis such as watching videos or browsing social media. Infinix has two storage variants, which are 8GB RAM + 128GB storage and 8GB RAM + 256GB storage. Storage space is the only difference. Both models share the same MediaTek Dimensity 7100 5G processor. The 8GB+256GB is the superior option in case you have many photos, videos, or applications to store. The other advantage is the 6,500mAhbattery, which can last a full day, and 45W fast charging to save time. The Infinix Note Edge price in Bangladesh is BDT 31,999 (Official) for 8GB RAM and 256GB storage. This smartphone comes in Lunar Titanium, Silk Green, Stellar Blue, and Shadow Black.
