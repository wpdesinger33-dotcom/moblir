Are you looking for a smartphone that offers a large display, a long-lasting battery, and a decent performance at an affordable price? If yes, then you might want to check out the Infinix Smart 6 Plus, a new entry-level device from the Chinese brand Infinix. In this article, we will try to share full details on Infinix Smart 6 Plus. So, keep reading this article. The Infinix Smart 6 Plus is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers a big screen and battery, along with a decent performance and camera. The phone has a glass front, a plastic back, and a plastic frame, and comes in four color options: Tranquil Sea Blue, Miracle Black, Purple, and Ocean Blue. The phone has a rear-mounted fingerprint scanner, a 3.5mm headphone jack, a microUSB 2.0 port, and a dedicated microSD card slot. The phone supports dual SIM, 4G LTE, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GPS, and NFC (in some regions). The phone also has a DTS audio processing with Beez 2.0, which enhances the sound quality and clarity. The Infinix Smart 6 Plus is a budget-friendly smartphone with a 6.6-inch HD+ display, a 5000mAh battery, a dual rear camera, and a MediaTek Helio G25 processor. It has 2GB or 3GB of RAM and 64GB of internal storage, which can be expanded up to 256GB. The phone runs on Android 12 (Go edition) with XOS 7.6 skin. The Infinix Smart 6 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,999.
