The Infinix Smart 7 (4GB+64GB) variant is an affordable smartphone that has an adequate operating capacity for basic tasks. Other models of the Infinix Smart 7 include the 3GB+64GB. The 3GB+64GB model is similar to the former but with 3GB of RAM which is less power for multitasking as compared to 4GB of RAM. It comes with a 6.6 IPS LCD touchscreen display with 280ppi and 720 x 1612 pixels resolution. The device is built on the Unisoc SC9863A platform. The battery capacity is 5000mAh and it is fast charge capable at 10W. It also has a 13MP dual rear camera with 0.3MP depth sensor, and 5MP front facing camera. Comes in Polar Black, Coastal Green, Iceland White, and Peacock Blue. The Infinix Smart 7 price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,200 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of ROM.
