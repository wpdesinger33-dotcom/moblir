The Infinix Note 12 is an Android smartphone that was announced in April 2022. It features a 6.7” AMOLED display with a resolution of 1080 x 2400 pixels. The phone is powered by the MediaTek Helio G88 chipset, which includes an octa-core CPU and the ARM Mali-G52 MC2 GPU. Keep reading this article to know about the Infinix Note 12 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of photography, the Note 12 boasts a triple-camera setup on the back, including a 50 MP main sensor with an f/1.6 aperture and PDAF, a 2 MP depth sensor, and a QVGA camera. For selfies, a 16 MP front-facing camera. The phone supports video recording at 1440p@30fps and 1080p@30fps. Its 5000 mAh non-removable battery supports 33W wired charging. The Infinix Note 12 price in Bangladesh is BDT 18,499 (Official) for its 6GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage.
