Infinix released a smartphone, the Note 12 (G96). This mid-range device offers premium features at an affordable price point. It runs on Android 12 with XOS 10.6. The Note 12 (G96) sports a 6.7-inch AMOLED display with a 1080 x 2400 resolution and 20:9 aspect ratio. The screen has a density of approximately 393 pixels per inch. The triple rear camera system includes a 50+2 megapixel primary sensor with an LED flash. An additional 16 megapixel selfie camera allows for fixed focus self-portraits. Video can be recorded at 1440p resolution and 30 frames per second. Powering the device is an octa-core Mediatek MT6781 Helio G96 processor with a maximum 2.05GHz clock speed. Connectivity features LTE, WiFi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac, Bluetooth 5.0, USB-C, and WiFi Direct. A 5,000mAh lithium polymer battery supports 33W fast charging. The Infinix Note 12 (G96) price in Bangladesh is BDT 19,999 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. It comes in 3 different colors: Snowfall, Force Black, and Sapphire Blue.
