The Infinix Note 12 2023 edition is a compelling choice for smartphone enthusiasts looking for a blend of performance and style. This model comes in two variants, offering either 128GB or 256GB of internal storage, both equipped with a generous 8GB of RAM to ensure smooth multitasking and gaming experiences. Notable features of the Infinix Note 12 include its vibrant 6.7-inch AMOLED display, providing an immersive viewing experience with rich colors and deep contrasts. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the triple camera setup, led by a 50MP main sensor complemented by a 2MP depth sensor and a QVGA camera for capturing detailed images and videos. The 16MP front camera ensures high-quality selfies and video calls. Security is enhanced with a side-mounted fingerprint sensor, and the device runs on the efficient MediaTek Helio G99 chipset, powered by a robust 5000mAh battery supporting 33W fast charging. The Infinix Note 12 2023 official price in Bangladesh is BDT 18,999 for the 8GB+128GB variant. It has another official variant 8GB+256GB. The device boasts a sleek design available in three colors: Volcanic Grey, Tuscany Blue, and Alpine White, catering to different tastes and preferences. You can buy this model from authorized Infinix retailers, Infinix brand shops, and online platforms.
