The Infinix Smart 9 is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers a range of features suitable for everyday use. Released in September 2024, it comes with a 6.7-inch IPS LCD display that supports a 120Hz refresh rate, providing a smooth visual experience. The phone is powered by the MediaTek Helio G81 chipset. It runs on Android 14. The camera setup on the Infinix Smart 9 includes a dual rear camera system with a 50MP main sensor, capable of capturing decent photos in good lighting conditions. The front camera is an 8MP shooter, suitable for selfies and video calls. One of the standout features of this phone is its 5000mAh battery. The Infinix Smart 9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,499 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage. It comes in Gold, White, and Blue colors.
