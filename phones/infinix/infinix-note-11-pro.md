The Infinix Note 11 Pro is a mid-range smartphone that brings a blend of refinement and performance to the table. It comes with a sleek design, with a matte finish on the plastic back. Available in colors like Haze Green, Mist Blue, and Mithril Grey, it exudes quiet elegance. The rear houses a triple camera setup, while the front features a stunning 6.95-inch Super Fluid display with a 120Hz refresh rate. This display is perfect for multimedia consumption, offering deep blacks and richly saturated colors. Under the hood, the Note 11 Pro packs a punch. It runs on the Helio G96 chip paired with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage. Infinix even includes the MemFusion feature, extending the RAM to 11GB for enhanced productivity and creativity. The phone ships with Android 11 and XOS 10, ensuring a smooth user experience. The camera department receives an upgrade too. The Note 11 Pro features a 64MP main camera, a 13MP telephoto lens with 30X digital zoom, and a 2MP depth sensor. Selfies are handled by a 16MP front camera housed in the center punch-hole cutout. Expect competent performance across various shooting scenarios. Powering the Note 11 Pro is a hefty 5000mAh battery, which keeps you going throughout the day. And when it’s time to recharge, the 33W fast charging ensures you’re back up and running swiftly.
