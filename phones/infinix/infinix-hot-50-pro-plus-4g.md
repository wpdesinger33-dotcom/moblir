Infinix Hot 50 Pro Plus 4G is a slim and high performing upper mid-tier smartphone for the Bangladeshi market. This features a 6.78-inch AMOLED display, with an overall refresh rate of 120Hz. The device is driven by MediaTek’s Helio G100 SoC. The camera setup includes a 50-megapixel primary sensor and other lenses are also there. And its selfie camera is 13MP. A 5000mAh battery with charging in 33W. The phone can come with an in-display fingerprint scanner as well as dual front-firing stereo speakers tuned by JBL, as well as they will be IP54 dust and water resistant. The Infinix Hot 50 Pro Plus 4G price in Bangladesh is BDT 22,999 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage. The phone comes in Sleek Black, Titanium Grey, and Dreamy Purple colors.
