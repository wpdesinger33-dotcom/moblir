The Infinix Hot 50i is a recently unveiled smartphone that aims to offer a blend of performance and affordability. It features a 6.7-inch IPS LCD screen with a 120Hz refresh rate, providing a smooth and responsive display experience. The device is powered by the MediaTek Helio G81 processor, which is built on a 12nm processor. This combination ensures that the phone can handle everyday tasks with ease and maintain performance over time, as indicated by its 48-month TÜV Fluency certification. The rear of the phone features a squircle camera module housing a 48MP primary camera and an LED flash, while the front has an 8MP selfie camera. The device also includes dual speakers that offer 300% ultra volume, enhancing the audio experience for media consumption and calls.The Infinix Hot 50i is equipped with a 5000mAh battery that supports 18W fast charging, ensuring long-lasting usage and quick recharges. It runs on Android 14 with Infinix’s XOS skin, providing a user-friendly interface and additional features.
