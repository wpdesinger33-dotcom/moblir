The Infinix Note 40 Pro 4G 12/256GB is a smartphone that packs a punch in terms of features and performance. Its sleek design, measuring 164.4 x 74.6 x 7.8 mm, houses a vibrant 6.78-inch AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate and a peak brightness of 1300 nits. The Corning Gorilla Glass protection ensures durability against scratches and accidental drops. At the heart of this device lies the MediaTek Helio G99 Ultimate chipset, which powers its performance. The 108MP triple camera setup on the rear captures stunning photos, while the 32 MP front camera handles selfies with finesse. The Note 40 Pro 4G can withstand splashes and dust, making it suitable. Its 5000mAh battery charges rapidly, reaching 50% in just 20 minutes. The 70W wired charging keeps you connected without long downtimes.
