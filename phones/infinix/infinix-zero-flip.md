The Infinix Zero Flip is the latest addition to the foldable smartphone market, marking Infinix’s first venture into this innovative segment. This clamshell foldable phone features a 6.9-inch full-HD+ AMOLED main screen and a 3.64-inch AMOLED cover display, both supporting a 120Hz refresh rate. The device is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 8020 chipset. The Zero Flip is designed with a zero-gap hinge, ensuring minimal screen crease and a sleek, modern look. In terms of camera capabilities, the Infinix Zero Flip boasts a dual-camera setup on the rear, including a 50-megapixel primary sensor with optical image stabilization (OIS) and a 50-megapixel ultra-wide shooter. The front camera is equally impressive, featuring a 50-megapixel sensor capable of 4K video recording.It is equipped with a 4,720mAh battery that supports 70W wired fast charging, ensuring long-lasting performance and quick recharges. The Infinix Zero Flip price in Bangladesh is BDT 80,000 (Expected) with 8GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage. Available in Blossom Glow and Rock Black color options.
