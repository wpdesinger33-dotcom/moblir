The Infinix Hot 11s is the successor to the Infinix Hot 10s smartphone. The Hot 10s was well received in its price range for providing a good gaming experience. The Infinix Hot 11s differs from the Hot 10s in its camera capabilities. It has a 50MP primary sensor with an F1.6 aperture. A 2MP depth sensor and AI camera are also included. On the front is an 8MP selfie camera with a f/2.0 aperture to enable good selfies. A dual LED flash is present as well. Video can be recorded at 1080p resolution at 30 frames per second. This phone features a large 5000mAh battery for all-day usage. An 18W fast charger quickly replenishes the battery. The 6.82-inch IPS LCD screen has a Full HD+ resolution and a high 90Hz refresh rate. Powering the device is an octa-core MediaTek Helio G88 gaming chipset consisting of two Cortex-A75 cores clocked at 2GHz and six Cortex-A55 cores at 1.8GHz. The  Infinix Hot 11s price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,990 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. It comes in Polar Black, Green Wave, and Purple colors.
