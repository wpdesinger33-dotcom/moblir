The Infinix S3X is a mid-range smartphone that was announced in 2018. It’s part of Infinix’s lineup and follows a familiar formula. While it lacks some premium features found in flagship phones, it offers good value for its price point. It measures 156.7 x 75.3 x 8 mm and weighs 150 grams. It features a 6.2-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1500 pixels. The screen-to-body ratio is around 82%, and there’s a notch at the top. Keep reading this article to know about the Infinix S3X price in Bangladesh and its overall details. In terms of performance, the Infinix S3X runs on the Qualcomm Snapdragon 430 chipset, which includes an octa-core CPU clocked at 1.4 GHz. It comes in a single variant which is 3GB of RAM and 32GB of internal storage. The dual rear camera setup consists of a 13 MP primary lens with PDAF (Phase Detection Autofocus) and a 2 MP depth sensor. For selfies, there’s a 16 MP front camera. It comes with a 4000 mAh non-removable battery, which supports 10W wired charging. It runs on X OS Hummingbird 3.3 Lite based on Android 8.1 Oreo. Other features include a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor, accelerometer, gyro, proximity sensor, and compass. The Infinix S3X price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,000 (Official) for its 3GB of RAM and 32GB of internal storage.
