Infinix Zero 30 4G is an Infinix mid-range smartphone in Bangladesh. It has a 1080 x 2400 pixels AMOLED screen of 6.78 inches which has a 144Hz refresh rate. It runsMediatek Helio G99, which is an octa-core CPU and Mali-G77 MC9 GPU. Equipped with a triple 108 MP main camera + 2MP macro Camera + 2 MP depth camera  and a 50 MP front camera. Charging is also quite fast with the 45W fast wired charging and thebatterycapacity of 5000mAh. It runs on  Android 13 with XOS 13 as the skin. The Infinix Zero 30 4G price in Bangladesh is BDT 25,990 (Unofficial) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
