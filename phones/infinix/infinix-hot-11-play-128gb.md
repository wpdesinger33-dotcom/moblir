The Infinix Hot 11 Play emerged in the tech market on November 15, 2021, offering a blend of performance and endurance. This device boasts a 6.82-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720x1640 pixels and a typical brightness of 440 nits. It’s powered by the Mediatek Helio G35 chipset, paired with 4GB of RAM. Keep reading this article to know about the Infinix Hot 11 Play price in Bangladesh. Running on Android 11 with XOS 7.6, the Infinix Hot 11 Play is equipped with a 6000mAh battery, supporting 10W charging to keep you connected throughout the day. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the dual rear camera setup, featuring a 13-megapixel main sensor complemented by a QVGA camera. The front-facing 8-megapixel selfie camera ensures your social media game stays strong. The phone’s design is practical with a glass front, plastic back, and plastic frame, available in colors like Sunset Gold, Exploratory Blue, Polar Black, and Haze Green. It measures 171.8 x 78 x 8.9 mm and supports dual Nano-SIMs.
