The Infinix Hot 30i (8/128GB) is a budget-friendly smartphone with a sleek glass and plastic design. The 6.56-inch IPS LCD display has a 90Hz refresh rate and 500-nit brightness for smooth visuals and vibrant colors. Its Unisoc T606 chipset provides efficient multitasking and app performance. Capture moments using the dual rear cameras including a 13MP wide-angle lens and 2MP depth sensor. Photography is enhanced with dual-LED flash, panorama, and HDR features. The Hot 30i runs Android 12 with the XOS 10.6 interface for a clean experience. A 5000mAh battery provides all-day power while 18W fast charging reduces charging time.
