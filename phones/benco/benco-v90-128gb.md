The Benco V90 is a 6.82-inch smartphone that boasts a large HD+ waterdrop display. With its 16MP triple rear cameras featuring AI capabilities, you can capture stunning photos. The phone packs a hefty 5000mAh battery for extended usage, and it comes with 7GB RAM (including 3GB Virtual RAM). One of its standout features is the generous 128GB internal storage. Available in three fashionable colors, the Benco V90 offers a compelling package for mobile lovers. The Benco V90 (128GB) price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,599 (Official) for its 4GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage.
