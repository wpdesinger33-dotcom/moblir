The Benco V91 Plus (8GB+256GB) is packed with features that really makes a smartphone a very budget friendly but high performing device. Available in the market of Bangladesh in September 2024. While the Benco V91 Plus can be availed in 6GB RAM and 128GB internal storage, the company’s version of the smartphone is slightly downgraded with 6GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage. The first and most obvious distinction is RAM and internal storage . All these two variants have the same screen, the same camera, the same battery power and to some extent, the same design. It has an 6.56 inches, IPS LCD display, an up-to 720 x 1612 pixels, and a refresh rate of 90 Hz. It has Unisoc T606 as the chipset for the phone. It has a 50 MP main camera, 2 MP depth sensor and 8 MP front facing camera. The gadget has a 5000mAh battery which supports 18W fast charging. The Benco V91 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,899 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of ROM.
