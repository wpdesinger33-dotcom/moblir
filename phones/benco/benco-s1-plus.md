The Benco S1 Plus is one of the cheapest smartphones in the Bangladesh market that has the competency of performance and features. The smartphone has a water drop notch 6.8 Inches IPS LCD with 1080 x 2460 pixels resolution and 90Hz refresh rate. This phone is built with the UniSOC T616 octa-core processor. For photography, there is a 48MP primary lens at the back, and a 2MP macro lens and the front has a 16MP selfie shooter. The battery is a 5000mAh with support for 33W fast charging. Running on Android 13. Comes in colors such as Dark Grey, Night Purple and Moon White. The Benco S1 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 17,499 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of ROM.
