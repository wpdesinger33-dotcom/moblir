The Benco V91 is an entry-level smartphone that was launched in August 2023. Designed for budget-conscious consumers, it offers practical features at an affordable price point. It comes with a striking design, combining aesthetics with functionality. Its slim profile and ergonomic build make it comfortable to hold. Keep reading this article to know about the Benco V91 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of performance, the V91 packs an upgraded UNISOC Tiger T606 octa-core processor. While it’s not a powerhouse, it handles everyday tasks smoothly. The phone features a 6.56-inch water-drop display with a resolution of 1640×720 HD+. The 60Hz screen refresh rate ensures smoother visuals. You have two RAM options: 4GB or 8GB, along with 128GB of internal storage. Additionally, there’s virtual RAM to enhance multitasking. It comes with a 5000mAh Li-poly battery, which provides ample juice for a day’s use. Plus, it supports 18W fast charging for quicker top-ups. The Benco V91 price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,999 (Official) for its 4GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage.
