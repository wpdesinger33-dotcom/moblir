The Benco V91c is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers a balanced mix of performance and features. It comes with a 6.56-inch IPS LCD display, boasting a resolution of 720 x 1612 pixels and a 90Hz refresh rate, ensuring smooth visuals and a decent viewing experience. The device is powered by the Unisoc T606 octa-core processor, clocked at 2.0 GHz. The phone runs on Android 13, offering a modern and user-friendly interface. The camera setup includes a dual rear camera system with a 13MP primary sensor and a 2MP secondary sensor, along with an 8MP front camera for selfies. These cameras are equipped with AI enhancements to improve photo quality. The device also features a large 5000mAh battery, which supports 18W fast charging. It is designed with practicality in mind, featuring a fingerprint sensor, face unlock. The Benco V91c price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,499 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. It is available in two color options: Rainbow White and Space Grey.
