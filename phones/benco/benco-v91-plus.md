The Benco V91 Plus is a feature-rich smartphone designed to offer a balanced performance for everyday use. It boasts a 6.56-inch punch-hole display with a 720 x 1612 HD+ resolution, a 90Hz refresh rate, and a peak brightness of 500 nits, ensuring a smooth and vibrant visual experience. The device is powered by the Unisoc T606 octa-core processor. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the Benco V91 Plus’s camera setup, which includes a 50MP AI triple rear camera with a large sensor size for detailed and clear photos, and an 8MP AI front camera for high-quality selfies. The device runs on Android 13, offering a modern and user-friendly interface. It also features a 5000mAh battery with 18W fast charging, ensuring long-lasting usage and quick recharges. In terms of connectivity, the Benco V91 Plus supports 4G networks and includes dual Nano SIM slots and a Micro SD card slot for expandable storage. The Benco V91 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,499 (Official) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. It comes in Sunrise White, and Volcanic Grey colors.
