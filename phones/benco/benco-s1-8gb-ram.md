The Benco S1 is a budget-friendly smartphone that was released in the Bangladesh market on 01 November 2023. This phone comes with two variants which are 6GB and 8GB of RAM along with 128GB of internal storage. If you look at its specifications, then you can't find any differences between its two variants. Just a simple difference is on their price tag. This phone comes with 48MP high-resolution triple rear camera setup, allowing you to capture clear and bright photos in various scenarios. On the front, there’s a 16MP selfie camera for your self-portraits. It has a5000mAh battery that keeps you going throughout the day, and it supports 18W fast charging for quick top-ups. The Benco S1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,599 (Official) for its 8GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage.
