Benco recently released a mid-range smartphone, the S1 Pro, which offers impressive features at an affordable price point. The device runs on the Android 13 operating system and features a 6.82-inch IPS LCD display with 1080 x 2460 pixel resolution and a 20.5:9 aspect ratio. Its triple rear camera system consists of a 64MP main camera, 2MP depth sensor, and 2MP macro camera along with an LED flash. Selfie shots and video calls are supported by the 16MP front camera. Video can be recorded at 1080p resolution up to 30 frames per second. Hardware specifications include 8GB of RAM, 256GB of built-in storage, and an octa-core Unisoc T616 chipset clocked up to 1.6GHz. Connectivity options cover LTE, WiFi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 5.0, USB-C, and more. The 5000mAh battery supports 33W fast charging via the USB-C port for all-day power in a compact design. The Benco S1 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 13,650 (Official) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. This phone has a different variant 8GB+256GB. It comes in Pearl White, Iceberg Blue, and Gemstone Black colors.
