Apple Watch Series 10 which was released in September 2024 is made of the latest technology as a Smartwatch but with a trendy look. It comes with a 1.96-inch Retina LTPO3 OLED touch screen with a pixel density of 454 ppi, resolution 496 x 416. The screen is capable of having a max of 2000 nits brightness and comes with sapphire crystal glass. It is 46mm with weights and 41.7 grams respectively, and its movement is powered by the Apple S10 SiP, the 64-bit dual-core processor, and a 4-core Neural Engine. The watch offers LTE internet, GPS and is certified to be dust resistant with the IP6X rating and water resistant to 50 meters. Having a battery capacity of 327 mAh, it charges fast, getting to 80% charge within 30 minutes. The Apple Watch Series 10 price in Bangladesh is BDT 51,000 (Unofficial).
