The Apple iPhone 5c was a unique addition to Apple’s lineup, offering a more affordable and colorful alternative to the flagship iPhone models of its time. It was released in September 2013. It featured a 4-inch Retina display with a resolution of 640 x 1136 pixels. Powered by the Apple A6 chipset, running on iOS 7 and upgradable to iOS 10.3.3. The camera setup included an 8MP rear camera, and a 1.2-megapixel front-facing camera for selfies. The device supported various connectivity options, including 4G LTE, Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.0, and GPS. The 1510 mAh battery provided decent battery life. The Apple iPhone 5c price in Bangladesh is BDT 28,990 (official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
