The Apple iPhone 11 with 4GB RAM and 256GB storage is a remarkable blend of style and functionality. Released in September 2019, this model quickly became a favorite among users looking for a powerful yet affordable iPhone. It boasts a 6.1-inch Liquid Retina IPS LCD display, providing vibrant colors and sharp details for an immersive viewing experience. The dual-camera system, featuring a 12MP wide and ultra-wide lens, allows users to capture stunning photos and videos with ease. The Night mode and next-generation Smart HDR enhance photo quality in low-light conditions, making it a great choice for photography enthusiasts. Under the hood, the iPhone 11 is powered by Apple’s A13 Bionic chip, ensuring smooth performance whether you’re gaming or multitasking. The absence of a card slot is compensated by the generous internal storage, allowing ample space for apps, photos, and media. The device also supports fast charging, getting up to 50% charge in just 30 minutes, and Qi wireless charging for convenience. The Apple iPhone 11 official price in Bangladesh is 99,999 for 4GB RAM and 256GB ROM. Also it has 2 different variants 4GB+64GB, and 4GB+128GB. The iPhone 11 is available in 6 different colors: Black, Green, Yellow, Purple, Red, and White. You can purchase this smartphone from the official Apple brand shops, Apple authorized shop and online marketplace.
