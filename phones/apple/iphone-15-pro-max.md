Apple iPhone 15 Pro Max is Apple's top-of-the-line smartphone for 2023, featuring a titanium frame, A17 Pro chip, USB-C, and a 5× periscope telephoto camera for the first time on a large iPhone.

## Specifications

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Display | 6.7-inch Super Retina XDR OLED, ProMotion 120Hz, 2796 × 1290 px |
| Processor | Apple A17 Pro (3 nm) |
| RAM | 8 GB |
| Storage | 256 GB / 512 GB / 1 TB |
| Main Camera | 48 MP (main) + 12 MP (ultra-wide) + 12 MP (5× periscope telephoto) |
| Front Camera | 12 MP TrueDepth, autofocus |
| Battery | ~4422 mAh, USB-C 27W, MagSafe 15W |
| OS | iOS 17 (upgradable to iOS 18) |
| Dimensions | 159.9 × 76.7 × 8.3 mm, 221 g |
| Colors | Black Titanium, White Titanium, Blue Titanium, Natural Titanium |
| Network | 5G / LTE |
| SIM | Nano-SIM + eSIM |
| IP Rating | IP68 |

## Camera Details

- **Main**: 48 MP, f/1.78, sensor-shift OIS, 24 mm eq.
- **Ultra-wide**: 12 MP, f/2.2, 13 mm eq., macro photography
- **Periscope telephoto (5×)**: 12 MP, f/2.8, 120 mm eq., OIS
- **Selfie**: 12 MP TrueDepth, autofocus

## A17 Pro Performance

The A17 Pro is the industry's first 3 nm chip, delivering hardware ray tracing, a 6-core GPU and 16-core Neural Engine.
