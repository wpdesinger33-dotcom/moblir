The main varying factor in these versions is the internal storage, which means that users can pick the device according to its storage space. Every version has the same display, the same processor, and a similar design, which makes all models identical in terms of the user interface. It has a 9.7-inch IPS LCDdisplaywith a resolution of 2360 x 1640. Apple M1 brings an 8-core CPU, 8-core GPU for smooth multitasking and media consumption on an energy-efficient system architecture.Batterylife is 8,600 up to 10 hours of the web or watching videos.
