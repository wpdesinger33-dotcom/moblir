The Apple iPad Air (2022), also known as the fifth-generation iPad Air, was released in March 2022 and offers a blend of power and portability. It features a 10.9-inch Liquid Retina IPS LCDdisplaywith a resolution of 1640 x 2360 pixels. The device is powered by the Apple M1 chip, which includes an octa-core processor and an 8-core GPU. It boasts a 12MP rearcameraand a 12MP ultra-wide front camera. The device also includes a 7600mAhbattery. With its sleek design, available in colors like Space Gray, Starlight, Pink, Purple, and Blue. The Apple iPad Air (2022) price in Bangladesh is BDT 108,120 (Unofficial) for 8GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage.
