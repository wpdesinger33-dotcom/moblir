Released in September 2019.  It also has 2 different storage variants. All released simultaneously. There is no change in their features, all features are the same. However, the price varies based on storage. This model boasts a triple-camera system that captures stunning photos and 4K videos, complemented by a 6.5-inch Super Retina XDR display that brings visuals to life. Under the hood, the A13 Bionic chip ensures seamless operation, whether for gaming or professional tasks. Its robust build, featuring scratch-resistant glass and an IP68 rating for dust and water resistance, encapsulates Apple’s attention to durability and quality.
