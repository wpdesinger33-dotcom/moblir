The Apple iPad mini 2 (1GB+32GB) variant was officially released in November 2013 in Bangladesh. This tablet was also released in 2 more variants 1GB+16GB, and 1GB+64GB. All the features of the 3 variants are the same, the main difference between them is only the ROM. It has a Retina display with a diagonal of 7.9 inches and a resolution of 2048 by 1536 pixels. The main operations of the tablet are under the operation of Apple A7 chip, 64-bit and M7 motion coprocessor. It features iOS 7 with support for upgrading to iPadOS 12.5.6. It features an impressive 5MP rearcamerathat delivers 1080p video capture and 1.2 MP front facing camera. It’s installed with a 6470mAhbatterycharge for up to 10 hours of web surfing, video watching, or music listening. It is slim and light with space gray and silver coloured aluminum finishes. The Apple iPad mini 2 price in Bangladesh is BDT 54,900 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage.
