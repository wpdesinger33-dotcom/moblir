The Apple iPhone 13 mini is the smallest and most affordable price of Apple’s 2021 smartphone lineup. Here we will share Apple iPhone 13 mini price in Bangladesh and spec. It features a 5.4-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display with a resolution of 2340 x 1080. Powered by the A15 Bionic chipset. The iPhone 13 mini boasts a dual-camera system with a 12MP main lens (f/1.6) and a 12MP ultrawide lens (f/2.4). The front-facing camera is also 12MP (f/2.2). Video recording capabilities include up to 4K at 60fps and 1080p at 30fps. Battery life is 2438 mAh, providing approximately 8 hours and 41 minutes of usage. The phone supports 20W wired charging and 15W MagSafe wireless charging. The Apple iPhone 13 mini price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,03,999 for 4GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. It is an official variant. This model comes in Starlight, Midnight, Blue, Pink, Red, and Green colors.
