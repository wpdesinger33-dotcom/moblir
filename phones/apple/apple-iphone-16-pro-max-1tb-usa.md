The Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max (8GB+1TB) model is a high-performance device. In addition to the 8GB+512GB variant, the iPhone 16 Pro Max also comes in 8GB+256GB and 8GB+1TB variants. The 8GB+256GB variant is ideal for users who need high performance but can manage with less storage, making it a more budget-friendly option. On the other hand, the 8GB+1TB variant caters to those who require extensive storage for large files, such as high-resolution videos and extensive app libraries. While all variants share the same core features. Its expansive 6.9-inch OLED display, offering a stunning visual experience with a 120Hz refresh rate. Powered by the A18 Pro chipset, it ensures top-notch performance for all your tasks. The device also features a triple-camera setup, 48MP main camera, 48MP ultra-wide angle camera, and 12MP telephoto camera. For selfie, 12MP front camera. Its robust 4686 mAh battery life and Ceramic Shield glass make it a durable and reliable choice for heavy users. The Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max price in Bangladesh is BDT 190,000 (Unofficial) with 8GB of RAM and 1TB of internal storage.
