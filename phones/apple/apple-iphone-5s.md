The Apple iPhone 5s, released in September 2013, marked the seventh generation of iPhones. It succeeded the iPhone 5 and was unveiled alongside the iPhone 5c. Sporting a sleek design, the 5s featured a 4.0-inch LED-backlit IPS display with a resolution of 640x1136 pixels. Under the hood, it packed the Apple A7 system-on-chip, a 64-bit dual-core CPU. The 8 MP rear camera, powered by a custom Sony Exmor RS sensor. It ran on iOS 7 initially, with the last update being iOS 12.5.7. It has a 1560 mAh battery. The Apple iPhone 5s 19,999 for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. It comes in Space Gray, White/Silver, and Gold colors.
