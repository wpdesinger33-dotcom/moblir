The Apple iPhone XR (3GB+128GB) stands out as a noteworthy model in Apple’s lineup. Released in October 2018. This model also has 2 different storage variants. Only the storage and price have changed, all the features are the same. The iPhone XR features a vibrant 6.1-inch Liquid Retina IPS LCD display, offering a comfortable viewing experience with true-to-life colors. It’s powered by the efficient Apple A12 Bionic chip, ensuring smooth performance across apps and games. The device boasts 3GB of RAM, which, while not the highest in modern standards, works seamlessly with iOS to provide a fluid user experience. Photography enthusiasts can appreciate the iPhone XR’s 12MP rear camera, capable of capturing high-quality images and 4K videos, complemented by a 7MP front camera for selfies and video calls. Its design is both stylish and durable, with an aerospace-grade aluminum frame and a glass back, available in an array of colors. The iPhone XR also supports wireless charging and is IP67 water and dust resistant, adding to its practicality. In Bangladesh, the Apple iPhone XR (3GB+128GB) is valued for its balance of performance and price, making it an attractive option for those seeking Apple’s ecosystem without the premium cost of the latest models. Pricing may vary, but it remains a competitive choice in the market. The Apple iPhone XR price in Bangladesh is BDT 85,000  for 3GB LPDDR4X RAM and 128GB NVMe ROM official variant. Also it has an unofficial variant; its price is BDT 61,000. This phone has another 2 different storage variants 64GB, and 256GB. It comes in 3 different colors:Space Black, Silver, Gold, and Deep Purple. You can buy this model from Apple official brand shops, Apple authorized shops, and online platforms.
