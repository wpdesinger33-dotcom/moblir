The Apple iPhone 11, with its 4GB RAM and 64GB storage variant, stands as a testament to Apple’s commitment to performance and design. Released in September 2019, the iPhone 11 comes in a variety of colors and three storage capacities: 64GB, 128GB, and 256GB. Its A13 Bionic chip ensures a seamless experience, whether you’re gaming or navigating through apps. The dual-camera system, featuring 12MP Ultra Wide and Wide lenses, invites users to capture moments in stunning clarity, further enhanced by Night mode and 4K video capability. Notable for its durability, the iPhone 11 boasts an IP68 rating, making it resistant to water and dust. Its 6.1-inch Liquid Retina HD display offers vivid visuals, while the TrueDepth front camera supports Face ID, adding a layer of security. With iOS 13, upgradable to the latest iOS version, users enjoy longevity in software support. The Apple iPhone 11 official price in Bangladesh is 87,999 for 4GB RAM and 64GB ROM. It has an unofficial variant priced at BDT 44,000. Also it has 2 different variants 4GB+128GB, and 4GB+256GB. The iPhone 11 is available in 6 different colors: Black, Green, Yellow, Purple, Red, and White. You can purchase this smartphone from the official Apple brand shops, Apple authorized shop and online marketplace.
