Apple has launched a new phone in their lineup, the iPhone SE 4, featuring an attractive design and specifications. It has a 6.1-inch OLEDdisplaywith a resolution of 1170 x 2532 pixels. Powered by the Apple A15 Bionic Octa chipset, which contains a hexa-core (2x3.22 GHz Avalanche + 4xX.X GHz Blizzard) and an Apple GPU. The camera system includes a 48-megapixel rearcamerawith an f/1.5 aperture, autofocus, LED flash, panoramic photos, and HDR capabilities. A 7-megapixel front camera with an f/2.2 aperture and LED flash enables selfies. A 3279 mAh non-removable battery provides power. The Apple iPhone SE 4 price in Bangladesh is BDT 60,000 (Expected) for 8GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. It comes in Black, Red, and White colors.
