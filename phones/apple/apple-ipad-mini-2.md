Apple introduced the Apple iPad mini 2 in Bangladesh in November 2013. It has a Retina display with a diagonal of 7.9 inches and a resolution of 2048 by 1536 pixels. The main operations of the tablet are under the operation of Apple A7 chip, 64-bit and M7 motion coprocessor. It features iOS 7 with support for upgrading to iPadOS 12.5.6. It features an impressive 5MP rearcamerathat delivers 1080p video capture and 1.2 MP front facing camera. It’s installed with a 6470mAhbatterycharge for up to 10 hours of web surfing, video watching, or music listening. It is slim and light with space gray and silver coloured aluminum finishes. The Apple iPad mini 2 price in Bangladesh is BDT 45,900 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
