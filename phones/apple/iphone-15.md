Apple iPhone 15 brings the Dynamic Island notch design from the Pro series, along with USB-C connectivity and a major camera upgrade to 48 MP.

## Specifications

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Display | 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED, 60Hz, 2556 × 1179 px |
| Processor | Apple A16 Bionic (4 nm) |
| RAM | 6 GB |
| Storage | 128 GB / 256 GB / 512 GB |
| Main Camera | 48 MP (main) + 12 MP (ultra-wide) |
| Front Camera | 12 MP TrueDepth, autofocus |
| Battery | ~3349 mAh, USB-C 20W, MagSafe 15W |
| OS | iOS 17 (upgradable to iOS 18) |
| Dimensions | 147.6 × 71.6 × 7.8 mm, 171 g |
| Colors | Black, Blue, Green, Yellow, Pink |
| Network | 5G / LTE |
| SIM | Nano-SIM + eSIM |
| IP Rating | IP68 |

## Key Upgrades over iPhone 14

1. Dynamic Island replaces the notch
2. USB-C replaces Lightning
3. 48 MP main camera (up from 12 MP)
4. A16 Bionic chip (up from A15)
