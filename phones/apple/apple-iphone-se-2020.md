Apple iPhone SE 2020comes with 4.7 inches 750 x 1334 pixels Retina IPS LCD screen. It has a classic Apple iPhone design. The back camera is of single 12 MP with PDAF, dual-LED flash, HDR, OIS etc. and Ultra HD 4K video recording. The front camera is of 7 MP. iPhone SE 2020 comes with 1821 mAh battery. It has 3 GB RAM and up to 2.65 GHz hexa-core CPU and Apple GPU (4-core graphics). It is powered by Apple A13 Bionic (7 nm+) chipset. The device comes with 64, 128 or 256 GB internal storage. There is a front-mounted fingerprint sensor in this device.
