The Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max represents a significant leap in smartphone technology. This flagship model boasts a larger 6.9-inch display with record-breaking thin bezels, providing an immersive viewing experience. The design incorporates a new titanium frame, enhancing durability while maintaining a sleek aesthetic. The iPhone 16 Pro Max is powered by the advanced A18 Pro chip, built on a 3nm process. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the upgraded camera system, featuring a 48-megapixel Ultra Wide lens and 5x optical zoom, ensuring stunning photo and video quality. For selfie camera is 12-megapixel. The device also supports Wi-Fi 7 and faster 40W USB Type-C charging, along with a 4,686mAh battery, the largest ever in an iPhone, ensuring extended usage without frequent recharging. Running on iOS 18, the iPhone 16 Pro Max introduces numerous AI-powered features. The Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max price in Bangladesh is BDT 205,000 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
