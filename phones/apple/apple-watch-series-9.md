Apple Watch Series 9 is a premium smartwatch that offers powerful features with premium build quality. It has a 1.9-inch Retina LTPO OLED touch screen, with a pixel density of 221 ppi, resolution of 484×396 pixels. It has the ability to go up to 2000 nits of peak brightness of the screen.The watch comes in 41mm and comes with weights of 42.3 grams in equal measure. Supported by the Apple S9 SiP, with 64-bit dual-core processor, and equipped with a four-core Neural Engine. LTE connectivity is also provided, as well as GPS, and the watch is IP6X compliant and can withstand 50 meters of water. It comes with a battery power of 308 mAh, and you can get as much as 18 hours of use. The Apple Watch Series 9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 41,000 (Unofficial) for 64GB of internal storage.
