Apple iPhone 16 Pro (512GB SG) is one of the best variants in this model. This phone has 3 different variants 128GB, 256GB, and 1TB. The main distinctions between these variants are the amount of storage space, so it is convenient for users to select it according to their preferences. It includes a gorgeous 6.3-inch Super Retina XDR display with, and supports 120Hz refresh rate. It is equipped with the A18 pro chip that comprises 6-core CPU and 6-core GPU. At the core of the phone is a powerful camera system that features a 48MP primary camera along with an additional 48MP ultra-wide-angle, and 12MP telephoto lens. It is available in a long-lasting titanium body with four refined color options; Black Titanium, White Titanium, Natural Titanium, and Desert Titanium.
