Apple has released the iPhone 17 Air, the thinnest iPhone ever at 5.6mm. This phone was launched globally on September 9, 2025, during an "Awe Dropping" event. This is a thin-as-air design with a 6.5-inch ProMotion OLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate and 3,000 nits peak brightness, which promises vivid images. The iPhone 17 Air is powered by the A19 Pro chip and has the new N1 wireless chip, which provides Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, and Thread that provide fast and secure connections. It is slim and still provides the iPhone 17 Air with an all-day battery life. The launch marks a new direction in Apple, which promises a combination of a sleek and a performance product in a small form.
