The Apple iPhone XR, with its 3GB RAM and 64GB storage variant, stands as a testament to Apple’s commitment to delivering quality and performance. Launched in October 2018, the iPhone XR brought a fresh palette to the iPhone lineup. Its 6.1-inch Liquid Retina IPS LCD screen, boasting a resolution of 828 x 1792 pixels, offers a vibrant display that is both pleasing to the eye and functional for daily use. Under the hood, the iPhone XR is powered by the Apple A12 Bionic chipset, ensuring smooth performance whether you’re gaming or multitasking. The device’s camera system, featuring a 12MP primary camera and a 7MP front camera, captures stunning photos and has the ability to record 4K videos. Notably, the iPhone XR supports Face ID, fast charging, and wireless charging, enhancing the user experience with convenience and security. The iPhone XR official price in Bangladesh is BDT 78,000 for 3GB RAM and 64GB ROM. There is also an unofficial variant of this model, priced at BDT 55,000. Available in colors including Black, Red, Yellow, Blue, Coral, and White. You can buy it from authorized Apple brandshop, retailers and online stores.
