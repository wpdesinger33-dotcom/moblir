The Apple iPad Mini (2019) that was launched on March 18, 2019 is a small and portable yet powerful tablet that is well-balanced in terms of performance and portability. It is a 7.9-inch Retina display with a resolution of 1536 x 2048. The device is powered by the Apple A12 Bionic chipset. The device is running iOS 12, which can be upgraded to iPadOS 18.2. For photography it has an 8MP rear camera and a 7MP frontcamera. The tablet is available in Space Gray, Silver, and Gold colors, covering all style preferences. It has a 5124mAhbattery. The Apple iPad Mini (2019) price in Bangladesh is BDT 43,000 (Unofficial) for 3GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage.
