The Apple iPhone XS, released in September 2018, epitomizes the marriage of sophistication and cutting-edge technology. With its sleek design, powerful internals, and impressive camera capabilities, the iPhone XS continues to be a sought-after device for tech enthusiasts and fashion-conscious users alike. Keep reading this article to know the whole details on Apple iPhone XS. The iPhone XS features a glass front and back, sandwiching a stainless steel frame. It’s IP68-rated for dust and water resistance. This makes it suitable for everyday use. The absence of a headphone jack is compensated by stereo speakers that deliver impressive audio quality. The device runs on iOS 12 (upgradable to iOS 17.3.1) and offers storage options of 64GB, 128GB, 256GB, or 512GB. The Apple iPhone XS is priced at BDT 100,000 in Bangladesh.
