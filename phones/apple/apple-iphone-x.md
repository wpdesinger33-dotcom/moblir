The Apple iPhone X, released in November 2017, marked a significant milestone in the evolution of smartphones. With its sleek design, cutting-edge features, and powerful performance, the iPhone X captured the imagination of tech enthusiasts worldwide. So, keep reading this article and know the full details on Apple iPhone X. The Apple iPhone X, released in 2017, boasts a 5.8-inch Super Retina HD display with an OLED panel and a resolution of 2436 x 1125 pixels. Its sleek design features a glass back and stainless steel frame. The phone is powered by the A11 Bionic chip and comes in 64GB and 256GB storage variants. The dual-camera system includes a 12MP wide-angle lens and a 12MP telephoto lens with optical zoom capabilities. Other notable features include Face ID, water and dust resistance, and Animoji.
