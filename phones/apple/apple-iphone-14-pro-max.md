The Apple iPhone 14 Pro Max is a flagship smartphone that combines cutting-edge features with stunning design. At its core, the iPhone 14 Pro Max boasts 6 GB of RAM and 128 GB internal storage. Its LTPO Super Retina XDR OLED display measures 6.7 inches and supports a 120Hz refresh rate, HDR10, and Dolby Vision. The ceramic glass front and stainless steel frame contribute to its elegant build. The 48 MP triple camera system captures jaw-dropping photos, while the TOF 3D LiDAR scanner enhances depth perception. Video capabilities include 4K at various frame rates and 10-bit HDR. The 12 MP selfie camera also supports cinematic mode. Notable features include Always-On Display, Ultra Wideband (UWB) Support, and Emergency SOS via Satellite. The super-fast Face ID ensures privacy and security. The price in Bangladesh starts at BDT 201,599 for the base variant 6GB+128GB. This phone has 3 more variants: 256GB, 512GB, and 1TB. It is available in Space Black, Silver, Gold, and Deep Purple, this device offers an incredible user experience. You can buy this phone at an iPhone brandshop, authorized shop, and online platform.
