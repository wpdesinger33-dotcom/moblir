The Apple iPhone 6s, announced in September 2015. Here we will share Apple iPhone 6s price in Bangladesh and specs. It features a 4.7-inch Retina HD display with 3D Touch. It’s powered by the A9 chip with 64-bit architecture and an embedded M9 motion coprocessor. The 12-megapixel camera supports Live Photos, autofocus with Focus Pixels. For selfie, 5MP front camera. Video recording includes 4K at 30 fps and 1080p at 60 fps. The device also boasts Touch ID for secure authentication and Apple Pay. Connectivity options include 802.11ac Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 4.2, and NFC. It is powered by a 1715 mAh battery. The Apple iPhone 6s price in Bangladesh is BDT 34,999 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage. Also this model has an unofficial variant, its price at 53,650. It comes in Space Gray, Silver, Gold, and Rose Gold colors.
