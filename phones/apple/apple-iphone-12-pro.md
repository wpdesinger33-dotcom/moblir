The Apple iPhone 12 Pro, with its 6GB RAM and 128GB storage variant, stands as a testament to Apple’s commitment to innovation and quality. Released in October 2020, this model quickly became a favorite among tech enthusiasts for its robust performance and sleek design. The iPhone 12 Pro doesn’t just come in a single configuration; it offers a choice of 256GB and 512GB storage options as well, catering to a range of needs from casual use to high-demand applications. Noteworthy features of the iPhone 12 Pro include the A14 Bionic chip, which delivers exceptional speed and efficiency. The device boasts a 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR display, providing users with an immersive visual experience. Its camera system is a significant draw, featuring a triple 12MP setup with a wide, ultra-wide, and telephoto lens, complemented by a LiDAR scanner for improved AR capabilities and low-light autofocus. The Ceramic Shield front cover and water and dust resistance add to its durability, making it a reliable companion for everyday use. Apple iPhone 12 Pro’s official price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,46,999 for 6GB RAM and 128GB ROM. Also this phone has an unofficial variant, its price BDT 55,000. This phone has 2 more storage variants: 256GB storage and 512GB storage. Available in elegant colors like Silver, Graphite, Gold, and Pacific Blue.You can find it at authorized Apple retailers and online stores.
