The Apple iPhone X (3GB+256GB) is a remarkable blend of design and technology. Launched in November 2017. This phone also has a different storage. 2 variants released on the same date. 2 have the same features, just price changes. It was introduced as a forward-thinking device with a 5.8-inch Super Retina OLED display, offering users an immersive viewing experience with high contrast and true-to-life colors. The iPhone X is powered by the A11 Bionic chip, a hexa-core processor that delivers seamless performance whether you’re gaming or using demanding applications. Its 3GB of RAM ensures smooth multitasking across iOS 11.1.1, which is upgradable to iOS 16.7.5. Photography enthusiasts appreciate the dual 12MP rear cameras that provide optical image stabilization and 2x optical zoom, capturing stunning images and 4K videos. The front houses a 7MP TrueDepth camera enabling Face ID for secure authentication. Despite not having a card slot for expandable storage, the 256GB internal memory offers ample space for apps, photos, and media. The iPhone X also boasts wireless charging capabilities, IP67 dust/water resistance, and lacks a traditional headphone jack, favoring a cleaner aesthetic. In terms of connectivity, it supports Wi-Fi 5, Bluetooth 5.0, and NFC, making it a versatile device for various wireless interactions.
