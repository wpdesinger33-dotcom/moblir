Apple introduced the iPad Air (2019) which combines premium power and portability in one device. The device includes an updated 10.5-inch Retina display at 2224 x 1668 pixels. The phone is powered by Apple A12 Bionic chipset and runs on iOS 12.1.3. The device has both an 8MP rearcameraand a 7MP front camera. This tab comes in an 8134 mAhbattery. Buyers can choose from three color choices including Space Gray, Silver and Gold to match their personal style. The Apple iPad Air (2019) price in Bangladesh is BDT 52,900 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage.
