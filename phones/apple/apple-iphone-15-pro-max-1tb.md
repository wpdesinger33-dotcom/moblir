The Apple iPhone 15 Pro Max (8GB+1TB) is a powerhouse of performance and capacity. Released on September 22, 2023. This model has a total of 3 different storage variants, all models released on the same date. And they all have the same features but prices vary based on storage. It runs on iOS 17 and is upgradable to iOS 17.4, ensuring users have the latest features and security updates. The device boasts a robust Apple A17 Pro chipset and a hexa-core CPU that promises smooth multitasking and efficient energy consumption. Its 6.7-inch LTPO Super Retina XDR OLED display delivers stunning visuals with a resolution of 1290x2796 pixels, supported by a 120Hz refresh rate for fluid motion. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the triple 48MP main camera system, which includes a periscope telephoto lens and an ultrawide lens, complemented by a TOF 3D LiDAR scanner for depth. The front camera is equally impressive, offering a 12MP sensor with depth/biometrics capabilities. The Apple iPhone 15 Pro Max doesn’t skimp on durability either, featuring a titanium frame and Ceramic Shield glass for both the front and back. It’s IP68 rated for dust and water resistance, and supports emergency SOS via satellite. For pricing, it’s positioned at the premium end of the market, reflecting its high-end specifications. The price in Bangladesh of the Apple iPhone 15 Pro Max is BDT 254,999 for 8GB LPDDR5 RAM and 1TB NVMe ROM official variant. Also it has an unofficial variant; its price is BDT 165,000. This phone has another 2 different storage variants 512GB, and 256GB. It comes in 4 different colors: Black Titanium, White Titanium, Blue Titanium, Natural Titanium. You can buy this model from Apple official brand shops, Apple authorized shops, and online platforms.
