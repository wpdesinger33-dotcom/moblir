The Apple iPhone 12, with its 6GB RAM and 64GB storage variant, stands as a testament to the brand’s commitment to innovation and quality. Launched in October 2020, this model quickly became a favorite among tech enthusiasts for its sleek design and powerful features. Notable for its Super Retina XDR OLED display, the iPhone 12 provides an immersive viewing experience with crisp, vibrant visuals. The dual-camera system, featuring 12MP wide and ultrawide lenses, captures stunning photos and 4K videos, making it a compelling choice for photography lovers. Its A14 Bionic chip ensures swift performance, whether you’re gaming or multitasking. Moreover, the Ceramic Shield front cover and aerospace-grade aluminum edges confer durability, while the compatibility with MagSafe accessories adds a layer of convenience. Apple iPhone 12 official price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,17,999 for 6GB RAM and 64GB ROM. There is also an unofficial variant of this model, priced at BDT 69,000. This phone has different storage: 128GB and 256GB. It comes in 5 attractive colors: black, white, red, green and blue, offering a variety of choices to suit personal style. You can buy it from authorized Apple retailers and online stores.
