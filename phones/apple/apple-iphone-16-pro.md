The Apple iPhone 16 Pro, announced in September 2024, represents a significant leap in smartphone technology. It features a 6.3-inch display with the thinnest borders ever seen on an Apple device, providing an immersive viewing experience. The device is powered by the new A18 Pro chip, built using 2nd-generation 3nm technology, which offers a 16-core Neural Engine and a 6-core CPU, delivering 15% faster performance and 20% more efficiency compared to its predecessor. The iPhone 16 Pro also supports USB-C speeds of up to 10GB/s, enhancing data transfer rates significantly. One of the standout features of the iPhone 16 Pro is its advanced camera system. It includes a new “Fusion” 48-megapixel camera with a 2nd-generation quad-pixel sensor. The ultra-wide camera now has autofocus, and the 5x tetraprism telephoto camera is available on both the iPhone 16 Pro and Pro Max models. Additionally, the device supports 4K120 fps video capture in full HDR, offering professional-grade video recording capabilities. It has a 3274mAh battery with 20W fast charging. The iPhone 16 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 189,999 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. It also boasts a titanium frame with a new blasted finish, available in several color options, including Black Titanium, White Titanium, Natural Titanium, and Desert Titanium.
