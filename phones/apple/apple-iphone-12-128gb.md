The Apple iPhone 12, released on 23 October 2020, marked a significant step in Apple’s mobile technology journey. With a sleek design featuring a 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display, the iPhone 12 offers a resolution of 1170x2532 pixels, protected by a Ceramic Shield glass. It runs on iOS 14.1, upgradeable to iOS 17.4, and is powered by the A14 Bionic chipset, ensuring smooth performance across applications. The 128GB storage variant comes with 6GB of RAM, providing ample space for apps and media without the need for an external card slot. Photography enthusiasts can enjoy the dual 12MP camera system, which includes a wide and ultra-wide lens, capable of capturing stunning images and 4K videos. The front-facing 12MP camera supports HDR and 4K video recording, making it ideal for selfies and video calls. The iPhone 12’s durability is enhanced with IP68 dust/water resistance, and it supports convenient features like Apple Pay and Face ID. In terms of battery life, the iPhone 12 houses a 2815mAh battery, supporting 15W wireless charging with MagSafe and 7.5W with Qi chargers. Available in a variety of colors, the iPhone 12 offers a blend of style, performance, and cutting-edge features, making it a compelling choice for users looking for a high-quality smartphone experience.
