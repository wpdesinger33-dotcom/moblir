The Apple iPhone 16e (8GB+256GB) smartphone delivers advanced functionality with a smooth operating experience to its users. It also exists in a variant that combines 8GB of RAM with 128GB of storage capacity. The main distinction between these two iPhone models exists in their memory capacity. The two different versions have the same set of outstanding features. This phone comes with a 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLEDdisplay. The A18 chip inside iPhone 16e delivers a smooth operation and efficient multitasking experience. It has a  48MP dual-camera system and it also has a 12MP front camera design. The device features both IP68 water and dust resistance standards. The Apple iPhone 16e price in Bangladesh is BDT 78,000 (Unofficial) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
