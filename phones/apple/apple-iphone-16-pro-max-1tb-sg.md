Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max (1TB SG) is the flagship model of this series, and this model has two versions, 256GB SG and 512 GB SG storage, the same OLED display, the same Apple Bionic chip and the same camera system. The main distinctions are seen in the memory amounts; therefore, the variants are clearly differentiated and it is convenient for the users to select the one they require. It comes with a beautiful 6.9 inch Super Retina XDR panel, with a refresh rate of 120Hz. It supports an A18 Pro chip combined with a 6-core CPU and graphics processing unit. The phone comes with a powerful camera set up of a 48 MP main camera, 48 MP ultra wide angle camera and 12 MP telephoto lens. And the body is made out of a high quality and virtually indestructible titanium that is available in four classy colors: Black Titanium and Desert Titanium, among others. The Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max price in Bangladesh is BDT 295,999 (Official) for 8GB+1TB SG. Alos this model has an unofficial variant, its price at BDT 198,000.
