The Apple iPhone SE (2025) is a small form factor smartphone that provides performance and features in one package. It comes with a 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display with a resolution of 1170 x 2532 pixels and 120 Hz refresh rate. Inside the iPhone SE (2025), there is the A18 chip. The phone runs on iOS 18. In the iPhone SE (2025) model, there are 48MP camera sensors. At the front there is a 12MP sensor for the front facing camera. The battery power is 3279 mAh and wireless charging is 7.5W. The Apple iPhone SE price in Bangladesh is BDT 40,000 (Expected) with 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. It comes in Black; other colors.
