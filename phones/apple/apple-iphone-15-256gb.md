The Apple iPhone 15 (6GB+256GB) is a very powerful and stylish mobile phone. This smartphone also has another model, 6GB RAM +128GB Internal Storage. Hardware and software features are almost the same, the only difference being amount of RAM, ROM and the price. This one boasts a 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED screen with a pixel resolution of 2556 x 1179, and a 60Hz refresh rate. It comes with the A16 Bionic chip. iPhone 15 comes with a quad-camera setup with a 1-inch main sensor of 48 MP and another 12 MP ultra-wide camera and 12 MP of telephoto camera with 4K Dolby Vision video recording support. A 3349mAh battery, 20W fast charging, and iOS 17 with updatability to iOS 181 are also available. The Apple iPhone 15 price in Bangladesh is BDT 171,999 (Official) for 6GB of RAM and 256GB of ROM. It has an unofficial variant, its price at BDT 90,000.
