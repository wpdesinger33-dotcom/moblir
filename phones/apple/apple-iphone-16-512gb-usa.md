Besides, the iPhone 16 has 8GB+128GB and 8GB+256GB variants, which offer the same high-performance capabilities but with different storage capacities. The 8GB+128GB variant is suitable for users who primarily use cloud storage or don't need extensive local storage. It offers all the powerful features of the iPhone 16 at a more affordable price point. The 8GB+256GB variant strikes a balance between storage and cost, for users who need more space than the base model but don't need the highest storage capacity.
