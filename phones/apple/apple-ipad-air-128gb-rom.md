The Apple iPad Air (1GB+128GB) variant was released in October 2020, is a powerful and portable tablet that offers a great balance of performance and design. This tablet was also released in 3 more variants 1GB+16GB, 1GB+32GB and 1GB+64GB. All the features of the 4 variants are the same, the main difference between them is only the ROM. It features a 9.7-inch Liquid Retinadisplaywith a resolution of 1536 x 2048 pixels. The tablet is powered by the Apple A7 Bionic chip, which includes a PowerVR G6430 GPU.  The tablet runs on iPadOS 7. It includes a 5MP rear camera and a 1.2MP front camera. The tablet is equipped with a 8600 mAhbattery, providing up to 10 hours of battery life. It features a sleek design available in space gray, and silver colors. The Apple iPad Air price in Bangladesh is BDT 81,900 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage.
