The Apple iPad mini (2024) is the latest smart tablet in the Apple series. It is equipped with a 8.3-inch Liquid Retina display and 2266 x 1488 pixels resolution. The iPad mini is built with Apple A17 Pro chip which is 30 percent faster in terms of CPU and a 25 percent improvement in GPU compared to the previous model. The iPad mini (2024) also offers 12MP rear camera, 12MP front camera. This tab is powered by a 5078 mAh powerful battery with support 19.3 Wh fast charger. The Apple iPad mini (2024) price in Bangladesh is BDT 80,000 (Expected) with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. This tab comes in Space Gray, Blue, Purple, and Starlight colors.
