The Honor Magic7 RSR Porsche Design is an elegant phone. The device features a 6.8-inch LTPO OLED screen with a pixel density of 2800 x 1280 pixels, and brightness level of up to 5,000 nits. At Its heart is theQualcomm Snapdragon 8 EliteExtreme Edition SoC. For the main rear setup you get a 50 megapixel lens, then a 200 megapixel telephoto lens with up to 100x digital zoom capability and finally a 50 megapixel ultra-wide lens. The front facingcamerapackage includes a 50MP. The Honor Magic7 RSR Porsche Design comes with a 5,850mAhbatterysupporting 100W fast wired charging and 80W wireless charging support. The Honor Magic7 RSR Porsche Design price in Bangladesh is BDT 165,000 (Unofficial) for 16GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage.
