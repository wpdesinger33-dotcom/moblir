The Honor X60 is a premium smartphone where technology and design are at the highest level. It has a weight of 189g with a 6.8 inches AMOLED display with a resolution of 1080 x 2412 pixels. The device is built around the Mediatek Dimensity 7025 Ultra chip. The Honor X60 comes with a dual camera setup that includes the 108MP main lens with an f/1.8 aperture and the front camera is 8MP with an f/2.0 aperture. The device comes with Android 14 . Further, it is dust and splash resistant and has an IP54 rating. The phone has support for 33W of fast charging. The Honor X60 price in Bangladesh is BDT 28,990 (Unofficial) for 8GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. This phone comes in Black, Green, and Silver colors.
