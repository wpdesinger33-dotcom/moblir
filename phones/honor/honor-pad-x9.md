The Honor Pad X9 (8GB+256GB) variant entered the Bangladesh market during July 2023 as a multi-functional tablet device. There is another variant of this tab 4GB+128GB. All the features of both variants are the same, only the RAM and ROM are different. The device contains an 11.5-inch TFT LCDdisplaythat delivers 1200 x 2000 pixel resolution at a pixel density level of 203 ppi. The device operates with a Qualcomm Snapdragon 685 chipset that has a combination of octa-core CPU and Adreno 610 GPU. The Honor Pad X9 operates with Android 13 and MagicOS 7.1 installed. Both the rear 5MP camera and front 5MPcameraon the device enable 1080p video recording. A 7250 mAhbatteryand supports rapid charging at 22.5W. The Honor Pad X9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 29,999 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
