The Honor X9c (12GB+512GB) variant is a high-performance smartphone released in November 2024. Another variant of the Honor X9c offers 12GB of RAM paired with 256GB of internal storage. The difference between the two variants is different only by the amount of ROM, in the case of the rest of the features, everything is the same. Color options, display, camera, processor, battery capacity, and fast charging are identical in both variants. It has a 6.78 inches AMOLEDdisplaywith a refresh rate of 120 Hz. It is backed with the Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 processing platform . The phone has a three-camera system, including a 108MP primary lens, 5MP ultra-wide lens, and a 2 MP macro lens. It also has a 16-MP front facing camera. The smartphone comes with a massive 6600mAhbatterysupport with 66W fast charging. It has an Ingress Protection or IP65 for dust and water resistance. The Honor X9c price in Bangladesh is BDT 34,000 (Unofficial) for 12GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage.
