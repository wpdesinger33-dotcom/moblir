The Honor Magic8 Pro entered the flagship market with AI innovation. It was officially released on 5 February 2026 in Bangladesh. The phone initially gained attention in the world with launches on 23 October 2025 in locations such as China and Singapore. It comes with Android 16 and MagicOS 10, which is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset. The smooth 6.71-inch LTPO OLED screen is glamorous with a 120Hz refresh rate and a maximum of 6000 nits of brightness. One of the headline features is a triple-lens system with a 50 MP maincamera, a 200 MP periscope telephoto, and a 50 MP ultra-wide. A large 50 MP front camera also deals with selfies. Under the hood, there is a 7100 mAhbatteryand 120 W wired and 80 W wireless charging. It has even IP68/IP69K water resistance, Wi-Fi 7 and 5G and on-screen fingerprint unlock. The Honor Magic8 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,59,999 (Official) for 12GB of RAM and 512GB of storage.
