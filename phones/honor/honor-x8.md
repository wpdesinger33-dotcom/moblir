The Honor X8 an Android smartphone released in March 2022. Here we will share Honor X8 price in Bangladesh and spec. The Honor X8 features a large 6.7-inch IPS LCD display with a 90Hz refresh rate, resolution of 1080 x 2388 pixels. Under the hood, it runs on the Qualcomm SM6225 Snapdragon 680 4G chipset. It runs on Android 11, with Magic UI 4.2. In terms of photography, the Honor X8 boasts a versatile quad-camera setup on the rear: 64MP primary lens, 5MP ultrawide lens, 2MP macro lens, and 2MP depth sensor. For selfies, 16MP front camera. It has a 4000mAh non-removable battery with 22.5W wired charging, and connectivity options like Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac, Bluetooth 5.0, and NFC (market-dependent). The Honor X8 price in Bangladesh is BDT 26,000 for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. It is an unofficial variant. This model comes in Titanium Silver, Midnight Black, and Ocean Blue colors.
