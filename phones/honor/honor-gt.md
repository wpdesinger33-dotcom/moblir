The Honor GT is a flagship smartphone that is targeted towards the use by tech-savvy clientele and gamers. It comes with a 6.7 inch AMOLED display with a refresh rate of 120Hz. TheSnapdragon 8 Gen 3processor, makes handling for gaming and multitasking a breeze while the 5300mAhbatterysupports 100W fast charging. The Honor GT has a dual-camera module with 50 MP, including OIS, on the rear side and 12 MP on the ultrawide camera. The frontcamerais 16MP. It comes in Black, White, Green colors and has an Ingress Protection rating of 65 which translates to, it is protected against limited amounts of dust and can withstand water spray from any direction. The Honor GT price in Bangladesh is BDT 41,500 (Unofficial) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
