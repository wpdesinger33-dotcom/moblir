The Honor Magic6 is a line of flagship smartphones that employ the latest technological breakthrough. The display is a 6.78-inch LTPO OLED screen, a resolution of 1264 by 2800 pixels. It boosts up to a 120Hz refresh rate and contains HDR10+. The Magic6 comes with the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset. The phone also has a 50MP triple rear camera system, apart from the 32MP telephoto lens, and the 50MP ultrawide lens. The battery capacity of the Magic6 is a 5450mAh battery, it supports 66W fast wired charging and 50W wireless charging. For privacy, the device comes with a fingerprint scanner and facial recognition as well, and supports Android 14 with MagicOS 8. The Honor Magic6 price in Bangladesh is BDT 74,999 (Unofficial) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of ROM. The device comes in Black, Blue, Green, Purple, and White colors.
