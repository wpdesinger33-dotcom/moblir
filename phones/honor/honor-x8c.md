The Honor X8c is an Honor smartphone, which is launched in the market in 27 April 2025. It has a display of 6.7-inch AMOLED panel which comes with a refresh rate of 120Hz.Displayresolution is 1080 x 2412 pixels. For the processing of the smartphone the Qualcomm Snapdragon 685 chipset has been used. The system is built on Android 15 with MagicOS 9. It is a dual-camera setup on the Honor X8c’s back panel, 108MP for the maincameraand 5MP for the ultra-wide camera. The front-facing camera is a 50MP sensor. It has a 5000mAhbatteryand supports fast charging of 35W. The Honor X8c price in Bangladesh is BDT 29,999 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage.
