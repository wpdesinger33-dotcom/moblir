The Honor X7c is an economy smartphone which was launched in November 2024 in Bangladesh. It brings a 6.77 inches TFT LCD size with 720 x 1610 pixels resolution and 120Hz refresh rate. The phone is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 685 chipset. On the side of the cameras, there is the 108 MP main camera, and a 2 MP depth sensor while the front camera is an 8 MP. The battery of the device is 6000 mAh with fast charging of 35W. The Honor X7c also boasts of dual SIM connectivity, Bluetooth, USB Type-C and FM radio. The Honor X7c price in Bangladesh is BDT 22,999 (Official) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage. This model comes in 3 different colors: Midnight Black, Forest Green, and Moonlight White.
