The Honor Watch GS 3 became available in Bangladesh during January 2022 as a modern smartwatch designed to meet the needs of active fitness practitioners and tech professionals. The device contains a 1.43-inch AMOLEDdisplaywhich delivers 466 x 466 pixels resolution. This smartwatch features multiple sensors which include an accelerometer together with gyro and heart rate monitor and barometer and compass and SpO2 sensor. The Honor Watch GS 3 integrates Bluetooth 5.0, GPS, along with NFC functionality which is limited to the China market for location tracking precision. This watch model features resistance against water at depths of 50 meters and therefore provides swimming and additional water-based activities support. The watch runs on a 451mAhbatterythat offers extended usage time and allows wireless charging. Available in black, steel, and gold colors. The Honor Watch GS 3 price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,999 (Official) for 32MB of RAM and 4GB storage.
