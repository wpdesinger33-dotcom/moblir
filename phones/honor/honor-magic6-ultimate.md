The Honor Magic6 Ultimate is a flagship smartphone that comes with lots of amazing features. This smartphone was released in the Bangladesh market on 22 March 2024. It has a sleek design with a glass front and an eco-leather back. It’s IP68 dust and water-resistant, making it durable. Keep reading this article to know about the Honor Magic6 Ultimate price in Bangladesh and its overall details. In terms of performance, the Magic6 Ultimate runs on Android 14 with MagicOS 8. It’s powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset, featuring an octa-core CPU. The camera setup is intriguing: a triple rear camera system includes a 50 MP wide lens, a 180 MP periscope telephoto lens with 2.5x optical zoom, and a 50 MP ultrawide lens. For selfies, there’s a 50 MP front camera. Video enthusiasts will appreciate the 10-bit video recording capability. The phone packs a hefty 5600 mAh battery, ensuring all-day usage. But what truly stands out is the charging speed: it supports 80W wired charging and 66W wireless charging. Additionally, it offers 5W reverse wireless charging. The Honor Magic6 Ultimate price in Bangladesh is BDT 145,000 (Unofficial) for its 16GB of RAM and 512GB of internal storage.
