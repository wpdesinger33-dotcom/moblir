The Honor Pad 8 functions as a cost-effective tablet because it provides multiple useful features to users. It sports a large 12-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 2000 x 1200 pixels. The Honor Pad 8 operates through the Qualcomm Snapdragon 680 chipset. Users of the Honor Pad 8 can take advantage of a 5MP rearcameraand a 5MP front camera that delivers satisfactory performance for photo and video communication purposes. The Honor Pad 8 operates under Android 12 by using the Magic UI 6.1 software interface. Thebatterysystem of the Honor Pad 8 comes with 7250mAh capacity which enables 22.5W fast charging. The tablet provides an 8-speaker sound system as one of its additional features. The Honor Pad 8 price in Bangladesh is BDT 23,999 (Official) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage.
