The Honor Magic6 RSR Porsche Design presents a smartphone solution that merges advanced technology with elegant design. The device displays content through a 6.8-inch LTPO OLEDdisplaywhich supports 1280 x 2800 pixels resolution and provides 120Hz refresh rate. This phone operates using the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 processor. The Magic6 RSR Porsche Design includes three cameras back for photography with its 50MP wide-angle main camera combined with a 180MP periscope telephoto lens having 2.5x optical zoom and a 50MP ultra-wide camera. The front camera includes a 50MP sensor. It delivers 80W wired charging capabilities with a 5,600mAhbattery. The Honor Magic6 RSR Porsche Design price in Bangladesh is BDT 210,000 (Unofficial) for 24GB of RAM and 1TB of phone storage.
