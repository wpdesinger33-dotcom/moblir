The Honor X6, released on 21 September 2022, is a budget-friendly smartphone that offers a blend of features. With a 6.5-inch HD+ display, it provides ample screen real estate for multimedia and browsing. The triple rear camera setup includes a 50MP main lens, complemented by 2MP macro and 2MP depth sensors. For selfies, there’s a 5MP front camera. Under the hood, the Honor X6 runs on a MediaTek Helio G25 chipset paired with 4GB of RAM. It operates on Android 12 with the Magic UI 6.1 interface. The device boasts a 5000mAh battery, ensuring extended usage without frequent recharging. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.1, and USB Type-C. Also, it features a side-mounted fingerprint sensor for secure unlocking. The Honor X6 price in Bangladesh is BDT 13,500 for the unofficial variant with 4GB RAM and 64GB internal storage. This phone comes in 3 different colors: Ocean Blue, Titanium Silver, and Midnight Black. You can buy this phone from retail shops, and online shops.
