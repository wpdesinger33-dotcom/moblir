The Honor Pad X8a is a high-quality product in the tablet market, arriving on the market on August 15, 2024. An 11-inch TFT LCD display along with 1200 x 1920 pixels of resolution and a 90Hzrefresh rate. The device is equipped with the Qualcomm Snapdragon 680 4G chipset. It is also equipped with a 5MP rear camera and a 5MP frontcamera. Android 14 with Honor's custom MagicOS 8 is running on the tablet. Another of its cool features is a big 8300mAh battery and the tablet supports 45W fast charging. It is being offered in the elegant black, and Space Gray colors. The Honor Pad X8a price in Bangladesh is BDT 17,500 (Unofficial) for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage.
