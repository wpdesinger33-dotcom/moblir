The Honor Pad 9 launched its Bangladesh market in December 2023 as a multi-purpose tablet providing excellence in productivity and entertainment. The device comes with a 12.1-inch IPS LCDdisplaythat produces 2560 x 1600 pixel resolution. The device runs on a Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 processor that comes with an octa-core CPU and Adreno 710 GPU. The Honor Pad 9 operates with Android 13 along with MagicOS 7.2. Users get access to a 13MP main camera and an 8MP frontcamerasystem. The tablet contains an 8300 mAhbatterythat enables fast charging at 35W to recharge the device quickly. It connects to Wi-Fi and Bluetooth 5.1 and acquires internet access through 4G LTE. The Honor Pad 9 price in Bangladesh is BDT 36,999 (Official) and 35,000 (Unofficial) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
