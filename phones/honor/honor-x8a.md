The Honor X8a is a smartphone that combines style, performance, and functionality. Its sleek form factor, measuring 162.9 x 74.5 x 7.5 mm, features a glass front, a plastic frame, and a plastic back. The 6.7-inch IPS LCD display with a 90Hz refresh rate provides an immersive viewing experience, covering nearly 90% of the front surface. Under the hood, the Mediatek Helio G88 chipset powers the X8a. It features an octa-core CPU (2x2.0 GHz Cortex-A75 and 6x1.8 GHz Cortex-A55) and a Mali-G52 MC2 GPU. Whether you’re gaming, multitasking, or streaming content, this phone delivers smooth performance.n The camera setup is intriguing: a triple rear camera system with a 100 MP wide lens, a 5 MP ultrawide lens, and a 2 MP macro lens. Capture stunning landscapes, detailed close-ups, and creative shots effortlessly. The 16 MP front camera ensures crisp selfies and video calls.
