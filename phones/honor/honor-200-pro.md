The Honor 200 Pro is a high-end smartphone that combines cutting-edge technology with sleek design. It was released in May 2024. It features a 6.78” OLED display. Its resolution is 1224 x 2700 pixels. Powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 chipset, the device boasts an octa-core CPU and Adreno 735 GPU, delivering exceptional performance for gaming and multitasking. It runs on Android 14 with MagicOS 8, providing a user-friendly interface and a host of customization options. It is equipped with a versatile triple-camera setup on the rear, including a 50MP wide lens, 50MP telephoto lens, and a 12MP ultrawide lens. The front dual camera setup includes a 50MP wide lens and a 2MP depth sensor. Battery life is robust with a 5200mAh capacity, supporting 100W wired and 66W wireless fast charging, ensuring quick power-ups.The Honor 200 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 76,999 (Official) for 12GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage. It comes in Black, Silver, Green, and Pink colors.
