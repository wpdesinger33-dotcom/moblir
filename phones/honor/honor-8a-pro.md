The Honor 8A Pro, announced in April 2019. It features a 6.09-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1560 pixels. It runs on Android 9.0 (Pie) with EMUI 9 and is powered by the MediaTek Helio P35 (MT6765) chipset, which includes an octa-core CPU (4x2.3 GHz Cortex-A53 and 4x1.8 GHz Cortex-A53) and PowerVR GE8320 GPU. Keep reading this article to know about the Honor 8A Pro price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of photography, the Honor 8A Pro sports a single 13 MP rear camera with an f/1.8 aperture, PDAF, LED flash, HDR, and panorama capabilities. For selfies, 8 MP front camera with an f/2.0 aperture. Both cameras can record videos at 1080p@30fps. Other notable features include a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor, accelerometer, proximity sensor, and a 3020 mAh non-removable battery. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac, Bluetooth 4.2, GPS, GLONASS, BDS, and FM radio. The Honor 8A Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 21,120 (Official) for its 3GB of RAM and 64GB of internal storage.
