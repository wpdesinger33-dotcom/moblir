The Honor 4X, released in October 2014, is a low-range Android smartphone developed by Huawei under its Honor brand. It features a 5.5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The device is powered by either a HiSilicon Kirin 620 octa-core processor or a Qualcomm Snapdragon 410 quad-core processor. In terms of photography, the Honor 4X is equipped with a 13 MP rear camera with autofocus and LED flash, capable of recording 1080p videos. The selfie camera is a 5 MP shooter, suitable for selfies. The device runs on Android 4.4.2 KitKat, upgradable to Android 6.0 Marshmallow, with Huawei’s custom EMUI skin on top. This interface, while functional, received mixed reviews due to its lack of an app drawer and somewhat cluttered home screens. It also boasts a 3000 mAh non-removable battery, which provides decent battery life, supporting up to 15 hours of talk time on 3G. The Honor 4X price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,990 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage. It comes in White, and Black colors.
