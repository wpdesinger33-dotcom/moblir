The Honor 6 Plus is a notable smartphone from Honor series. It features a 5.5-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. The device is powered by the HiSilicon Kirin 925 chipset, an octa-core processor with four Cortex-A15 cores clocked at 1.8 GHz and four Cortex-A7 cores at 1.3 GHz. One of the standout features of the Honor 6 Plus is its dual 8 MP rear camera setup. The front camera is also 8 MP, catering well to selfie enthusiasts. The device runs on Android 4.4.2 KitKat, upgradable to Android 6.0 Marshmallow, with EMUI 3.0 skin on top. The Honor 6 Plus is equipped with a non-removable 3600 mAh battery. The Honor 6 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 44,990 (official) for 3GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
