The Honor 100 Pro Price in Bangladesh reflects the impressive array of features and variants this smartphone offers. Launched in November 2023, the Honor 100 Pro comes in several variants, including options with 256GB, 512GB, and a whopping 1TB of internal storage, paired with 12GB or 16GB of RAM. Running on Android 13 with MagicOS 7.2, it’s powered by the robust Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset. Notable for its 6.78-inch OLED display, the Honor 100 Pro boasts a resolution of 1224x2700 pixels, HDR support, and a peak brightness of 2600 nits, making it a delight for media consumption. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the triple camera setup, featuring a 50MP main sensor, a 32MP telephoto lens with 2.5x optical zoom, and a 12MP ultrawide lens. The dual selfie cameras, including a 50MP primary sensor, ensure high-quality self-portraits and video calls. Reasons to consider the Honor 100 Pro include its 100W wired and 66W wireless charging capabilities, which promise rapid battery replenishment for the 5000mAh battery. Additionally, the absence of a memory card slot is compensated by the generous onboard storage options. Whether for gaming, photography, or general productivity, the Honor 100 Pro stands out as a versatile choice in the smartphone market.
