The Honor 100 Pro Price in Bangladesh reflects the impressive array of features and variants this smartphone offers. Launched in November 2023, the Honor 100 Pro comes in several variants with options up to 1TB of internal storage paired with 12GB or 16GB of RAM, powered by the robust Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset and running Android 13 with MagicOS 7.2. Notable for its 6.78-inch OLED display with 1224x2700 resolution, HDR support and 2600 nits brightness, making it excellent for media. The triple rear camera features a 50MP main sensor, 32MP telephoto lens with 2.5x optical zoom and 12MP ultrawide lens. Dual selfie cameras include a 50MP primary sensor for high-quality selfies and video calls. The Honor 100 Pro offers 100W wired and 66W wireless charging for its 5000mAh battery promising rapid replenishment. Generous onboard storage compensates for the absent memory card slot. Versatile for gaming, photography or productivity, the Honor 100 Pro stands out in the smartphone market.
