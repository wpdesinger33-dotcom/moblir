The Honor X7b is a budget-friendly smartphone and it strikes a balance between affordability and functionality. It comes with a large 6.8-inch IPS LCD display, offering an immersive viewing experience. Its slim profile and flowing colors—Flowing Silver, Emerald Green, and Midnight Black—make it visually appealing. Under the hood, the X7b houses the Qualcomm SM6225 Snapdragon 680 4G chipset, ensuring capable performance. The 6,000mAh battery provides ample juice for extended usage, and the 35W fast charging feature keeps you powered up. The 108 MP triple-camera system captures detailed shots, with a wide lens, ultrawide lens, and depth sensor. The Honor X7b Price in Bangladesh is BDT 22,999 for its 8/256GB variant.
