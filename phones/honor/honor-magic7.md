The Honor Magic7 is a flagship smartphone to provide the latest trends and a superfast processor. The device runs onQualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elitemobile platform. It sports a 6.78-inch LTPO OLED screen with pixel density of 1200 x 2800 pixels. The featured display has been designed for 120Hz refresh rate support, and has a peak brightness level of 5000 nits. In regards to camera modules, the Honor Magic7 has a row of three camera lenses on the rear side of the device including, a 50MP standard lens, a 50MP ultra wide-angle lens, and a 50MP telephoto lens with 3X optical zoom. This phone also comes with a 50MP frontcamerasensor as well. The device offers video recording up to 4K at 24/30/60fps, 1080P at 24/30/60/120/240 fps, whilst the gyro-EIS and OIS helps to ensure stable image stabilizations. The built-in battery of Honor Magic7 is 5,650mAhbattery, while wired charging can support up to 100W, wirelessly up to 80W. The phone comes with the Android 15-based MagicOS 9.0. Second, the Magic7 boasts Dust and Water Resistance of IP68 and IP69. The Honor Magic7 price in Bangladesh is BDT 100,000 (Unofficial) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
