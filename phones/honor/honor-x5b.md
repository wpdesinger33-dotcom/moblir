Honor X5b is an Android mid-tier smartphone that is now available in 09 January 2025, features a 6.56 inches TFT LCDdisplaywith 90Hz refresh rate. It has MediaTek Helio G36 chipset, which comprises an octa-core CPU and PowerVR GE8320 GPU. Those interested in photography will find the Honor X5b comes equipped with a 13MP rear camera with an f/1.8 aperture for those photo and 1080p video moments. The front-facing camera is a conventional 5MP. The device also has a 5200 mAhbattery, and has 10W charging. The phone is available in three colors: Ocean Blue, Starry Purple, and Midnight black where chosen as the names of these colors. The Honor X5b price in Bangladesh is BDT 9,999 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage.
