The Honor X6b offers a balanced combination of performance, camera capabilities, and battery life for users seeking a reliable smartphone experience. It features a 6.56-inch TFT LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1612 pixels. It runs on Android 14 with Magic OS 8. Under the hood, you’ll find the Mediatek Helio G85 chipset, an octa-core CPU. The device boasts a 50MP dual main camera setup (wide + depth) with LED flash and HDR capabilities, while the front camera is a 5MP wide-angle lens. The 5200mAh battery supports 35W wired charging, promising a 31% charge in just 20 minutes. Additionally, it features a side-mounted fingerprint sensor for added security. The Honor X6b price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,999 (Official) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. In terms of design, the Honor X6b comes in attractive colors like Forest Green, Starry Purple, Ocean Cyan, and Midnight Black.
