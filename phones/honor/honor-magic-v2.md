The Honor Magic V2 price in Bangladesh is set at a premium, reflecting its position as a high-end foldable smartphone. The Magic V2 combines luxury with cutting-edge technology. The Magic V2 runs on Android 13, enhanced by MagicOS 7.2, and is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset, ensuring smooth performance across applications.
