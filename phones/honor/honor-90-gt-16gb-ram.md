The device includes a dual-camera setup with a 50MP main sensor and a 12MP ultra-wide lens, providing versatile photography options. The 5000mAh battery supports 100W fast charging, allowing for quick recharges. Another variant of the Honor 90 GT is the top-tier model with 12GB of RAM and 256GB of internal storage.
