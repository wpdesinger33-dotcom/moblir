Honor Play 9T is an affordable device, which was planned to be released in September 2024. There’s a 6.77-inch TFT LCD panel with an HD+ resolution of 720 x 1610 pixels and 120Hz refresh rate. Pointing to the Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 platform, the device has an octa-core CPU and an Adreno 613 GPU. In terms of the camera it has a 50 MP dual camera set up on the back end and a 2 MP depth sensor. Also there is a 5.0MP front facing camera. Dual SIM connectivity is available with the phone, along with Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac, Bluetooth 5.0, USB Type-C, and GPS for regular connections. Some of them include the 6000 mAh battery and it has a 35W fast charging capability. The Honor Play 9T price in Bangladesh is BDT 20,000 (Expected) for 8GB of RAM and 128GB of ROM. The device comes in Black, White, and Green colors.
