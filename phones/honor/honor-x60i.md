Honor will be launched in their new device Honor X60i in the Bangladesh market. This model's expected release date is 11 August 2024. It features a 6.7" IPS LCD display, with resolution of 1080x2412 pixels and 391 ppi. For photography, it has a dual rear camera setup, 50 MP main camera, and 2 MP depth sensor. Also it has an 8 MP selfie camera. The Honor X60i is powered by Mediatek Dimensity 6080 with Mali-G57 MC2 GPU. Runs on Android 14 and Magic UI 7.1 OS. Battery life of Honor X60i is 5000 mAh, which supports 35W fast charging. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 6 (802.11 a/b/g/n/ac/ax) 5GHz, MIMO, and bluetooth 5.2. The Honor X60i price in Bangladesh is BDT 30,000 (Expected) with 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage. It comes in Black, Blue, Purple, and White colors.
