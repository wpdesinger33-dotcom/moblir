Honor300 has a striking balance of features for an affordable price and has a powerful performance. It comes with a 6.7 inches OLED screen with a density of 1200 x 2664 pixels. Also this phone has a  Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 chipset. It has a 50 MP maincameraand a 12 MP ultra-wide lens also for the front selfie it has a 50 MP. The device is fitted with a 5300 mAhbatteryand it supports 100W Fast charging. It is dual SIM supported along with Bluetooth, USB Type-C, and has an in-built FM radio. The Honor 300 price in Bangladesh is BDT 34,000 (Unofficial) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage. This model comes in Black, Silver, Blue, and Pink colors.
