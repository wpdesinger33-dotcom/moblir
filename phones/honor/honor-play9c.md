The Honor Play 9C is an affordable smartphone, to be released in September 2024. It includes a 6.56 inches TFT LCD that has resolution of 720 x 1612 pixels and a 90 Hz refresh rate. It is equipped with the MediaTek Dimensity 6100+ on Chip Technology and Mali-G57 MC2 GPU. The Play 9C comes with a 13MP rear camera and 5 MP front camera. The device also comes with the following connectivity options; dual SIM, Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac, Bluetooth 5.3, USB Type-C port, 5G connectivity. It has a super rechargeable battery of 5200 mAh with 10W fast charge. The Honor Play 9C price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,000 (Expected) for 6GB of RAM and 128GB of ROM. It comes in Black, and Blue colors.
