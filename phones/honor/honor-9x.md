The Honor 9X strikes a balance between affordability and functionality, making it a reliable option for budget-conscious consumers. Here we will share Honor 9X price in Bangladesh and specs. It boasts a 6.59-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 2340 pixels. The phone runs on Android 9.0 (Pie) with EMUI 9.1 and is powered by the Kirin 710F chipset. In terms of photography, the Honor 9X sports a triple rear camera setup: a 48MP primary lens, an 8MP ultrawide lens, and a 2MP depth sensor. The motorized pop-up selfie camera offers 16MP. The phone also features a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor, a 3.5mm headphone jack, and a USB Type-C port. Its 4000mAh non-removable battery supports 10W wired charging. The Honor 9X price in Bangladesh is BDT 24,990 (Official) for 4GB of RAm and 64G  of ROM. This phone has another unofficial variant, its price at BDT 19,000. It comes in Midnight Black, and Sapphire Blue colors.
