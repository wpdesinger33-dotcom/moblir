The Honor Magic V3 is one of the latest foldable smartphones. It comes with a 7.92-inch LTPO OLED with resolution of 2156 x 2344 pixels, and a 120Hz refresh rate. It is running onQualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3Chipset and an Adreno 750 GPU. It has a 50 MP wide lens, 50 MP telephoto, 3.5x optical zoom, 40 MP ultra-wide camera on the back. There is also a 20 MP front facing camera on the device. The device features a 5150 mAhbatteryand comes with 66W fast charging through the wired connection and 50W fast charging through the wireless connection. The Honor Magic V3 price in Bangladesh is BDT 179,999 (Official) for 12GB of RAM and 512GB of ROM.
