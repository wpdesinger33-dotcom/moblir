The Honor Magic7 Pro is a premium category smartphone and is aimed to present the best characteristics and options. All these have been made possible by theQualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elitechipset. Magic7 Pro, the largest AMOLED display of 6.8 inches has an LTPO variation with 1280 x 2800 pixels resolution. The display can keep up to 120Hz and goes as high as 5000 nits for brightness. Speaking of camera systems, there is a triple camera setup included at the back of Honor Magic7 Pro: 50 MP primary shooter, 50 MP ultra-wide-angle lens, and an impressive 200 MP periscope telephoto lens with 3X optical zoom. The frontcamerais also a 50MP sensor. Recording is expected to go up to 4K with the options of 24/30/60fps while 1080p can go up to 24/30/60/120/240 fps which are supported with gyro-EIS and OIS. It comes with a 5,850mAhbatteryand features both 100W wired and 80W wireless charging. The phone operates MagicOS 9.0 which is based on Android 15. Furthermore, the Magic7 Pro comes with dust and water resistance certifications of IP68 and IP69. The Honor Magic7 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 101,500 (Unofficial) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of ROM. It has a Black, White, Gray, and Blue colors.
