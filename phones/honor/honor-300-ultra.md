The Honor 300 Ultra is a flagship smartphone that sports a 6.78 inches AMOLED screen with a 120Hz and has a pixel resolution of 1224 x 2700. TheQualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3working as the processing chip for this phone. The Honor 300 Ultra bears Triple Camera System comprising, 50 MP wide-angle main camera, 50 MP periscope telephoto camera with 3.8x optical zoom and 12 MP ultra-wide-anglecamera. The front facing camera is 50 MP and 2 MP. The phone comes with a feature of 4K video recording. It comes with a 5300mAhbatteryand 100W rapid charging through the cable and up to 80W via a wireless cable. It comes with the Android 15 operating system with MagicOS 9.0 launch. The Honor 300 Ultra price in Bangladesh is BDT 80,000 (Unofficial) for 12GB of RAM and 512GB of phone storage.
