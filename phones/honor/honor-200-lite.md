The Honor 200 Lite is a flagship smartphone and has a good configuration and features in a reasonable price range. It features a 6.7-inch AMOLED display with a resolution of 1080 x 2412 pixels and a 20:9 aspect ratio. The device operates MediaTek Dimensity 6080 SoC and has an octa core CPU and a Mali-G57 GPU. On the phone’s rear part, we get a triple camera: the first one 108MP, the second one a 5MP ultra-wide lens and a third one a 2MP macro camera. The battery capacity is 4500mAh, and it guarantees one day of use, while fast charging is 35 watts. The Honor 200 Lite price in Bangladesh is BDT 22,000 (Unofficial) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
