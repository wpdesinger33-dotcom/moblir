The Honor Magic V Flip is the latest foldable smartphone that boasts of the best design and impressive performance. It has a size of 6.8 inches LTPO OLED having the pixel resolution of 1080 x 2520. The display has an intended refresh rate of 120Hz. The phone is portable and easy to carry as it has a thin and lightweight body when unfolded it measures 167.3 x 75.6 x 7.2 mm and weighs 193 grams. Supported by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 processor. It also comes with a 50MP main camera as well as a 12MP front camera. It is powered by a 4800mAh non-removable battery which supports 66W fast charging. The Honor Magic V Flip price in Bangladesh is BDT 85,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage. The phone comes in Black, White, Champagne, and Green colors.
