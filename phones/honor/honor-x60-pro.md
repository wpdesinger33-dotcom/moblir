The Honor X60 Pro is a powerful smartphone that comes with a mix of flagship and latest technology. It has a 6.78 inch curved AMOLED display with a density of 2448 x 1080 pixels, and a refresh rate of 120Hz. It can also support HDR10+ and the peak brightness of the screen is 3000 nits. The phone is built with the Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 platform. The phone is also dust and water resistant to the IP68 rating. The package of the camera is 108MP wide, 2MP depth, and 32MP for selfies. It has a battery of 6600 mAh with the feature of fast charging 66W. The Honor X60 Pro price in Bangladesh is BDT 33,990 (Unofficial) for 8GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage. It is available in Black, Green, Silver, and Orange.
