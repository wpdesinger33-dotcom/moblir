The Honor X9c Smart has been released in the market with a mix of style for the product and power for the user. This one has a 6.8 inches size TFT LCDdisplayand a resolution of 1080 x 2412 pixels with 120 Hz refresh rate. It has the qualitative MediaTek Dimensity 7025 Ultra chipset. As for the photography, on the back of the Honor X9c Smart there is 108MP main camera and 5 MP ultra wide camera on the back module while the front has a 16MPcamera. The phone has an option of shooting the video in 1080p at 30 frames per second. The Honor X9c Smart has been paired with a 5,800mAhbatteryand includes 35W quick charging by wire. It comes with Android 14 and MagicOS 8.0. The Honor X9c smartphone price in Bangladesh is BDT 25,500 (Unofficial) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
