The Honor 90 Lite Price in Bangladesh reflects the smartphone’s competitive edge in the market, offering a blend of high-end features and affordability. Launched in June 2023, the Honor 90 Lite boasts a sleek design with a 6.7-inch IPS LCD display, providing an immersive viewing experience with its 1080x2388 pixel resolution. Under the hood, the device is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 6020 chipset, coupled with 8GB of RAM, ensuring smooth performance across applications and multitasking scenarios. Photography enthusiasts will appreciate the Honor 90 Lite’s triple camera setup, featuring a 100MP main sensor that captures stunning images with clarity and detail. The phone also includes a 5MP ultrawide and a 2MP macro lens, catering to a variety of shooting conditions. For selfies and video calls, a 16MP front camera delivers high-quality results. The device runs on Android 13, enhanced by Magic OS 7.1, providing a user-friendly interface and access to the latest software features. In terms of battery life, the Honor 90 Lite is equipped with a 4500mAh battery, supported by 35W fast charging, which promises to keep users connected throughout the day. Despite not having a card slot for expandable storage, the generous 256GB internal storage should suffice for most users’ needs. The absence of a 3.5mm jack is compensated by the inclusion of high-resolution audio, ensuring a premium sound experience. Overall, the Honor 90 Lite stands as a compelling choice for consumers looking for a feature-packed smartphone at an accessible price point.
