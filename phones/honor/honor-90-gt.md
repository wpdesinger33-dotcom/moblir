The Honor 90 GT, released in December 2023, is a flagship smartphone that combines powerful performance with sleek design. It features a 6.7-inch AMOLED display with a resolution of 1200 x 2664 pixels, offering vibrant colors and a smooth 120Hz refresh rate. The device is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset, paired with up to 24GB of RAM and up to 1TB of internal storage, ensuring top-tier performance and ample space for all your needs. In terms of photography, the Honor 90 GT boasts a dual-camera setup on the back, including a 50MP main sensor and a 12MP ultrawide lens. The front houses a 16MP selfie camera. The camera system supports various features like HDR, panorama, and 4K video recording, making it a versatile option for capturing high-quality photos and videos. The phone runs on Android 13 with MagicOS 7.2, providing a smooth and user-friendly interface. It is equipped with a 5000mAh battery that supports 100W fast charging, allowing the device to charge from 3% to 100% in just 32 minutes. The Honor 90 GT price in Bangladesh is BDT 43,499 (Unofficial) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage. It comes in Black, Blue, and Gold colors.
