The Honor 8S is an Android smartphone that was announced in April 2019. It features a 5.71-inch IPS display with a resolution of 720 x 1520 pixels. The phone is powered by the Mediatek MT6761 Helio A22 chipset, which includes a quad-core 2.0 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and a PowerVR GE8320 GPU. It runs on Android 9.0 (Pie) with EMUI 9. Keep reading this article to know the Honor 8S price in Bangladesh and its overall details. In terms of design, the Honor 8S has a glass front, plastic back, and plastic frame. It weighs 146 grams and has dimensions of 147.1 x 70.8 x 8.5 mm. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 5.0, GPS, GLONASS, and FM radio. The phone is available in colors like Black, Gold, and Blue. It comes with either 32 GB of internal storage and 2 GB of RAM or 64 GB of storage and 3 GB of RAM. It has a single 13 MP rear camera with features like LED flash, HDR, and panorama. The front-facing camera is 5 MP. This phone is equipped with a 3020 mAh non-removable battery. The Honor 8S price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,499 (Official) for its 2GB of RAM and 32GB of internal storage.
