Honor Bangladesh has officially unveiled the Honor X5c, marking a major milestone as the brand's second locally manufactured smartphone in the country. Announced on February 2 and launched on February 3, the new device highlights Honor's growing focus on local production and long-term reliability. The Honor X5c comes with anti-aging durability technology designed to maintain smooth performance for up to four years, while lab tests show its battery retains over 87 percent capacity even after 1,000 charging cycles. According to Lang Guo, this launch reflects Honor Bangladesh's strong commitment to delivering high-quality smartphones tailored for Bangladeshi consumers.
