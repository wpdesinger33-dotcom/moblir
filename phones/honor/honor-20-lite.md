The Honor 20 Lite is an Android smartphone that was announced in April 2019. It features a 6.21-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 2340 pixels. The phone’s design includes a glass front and plastic back, along with a plastic frame. It weighs 164 grams and has dimensions of 154.8 x 73.6 x 8 mm. Keep reading this article to know about the Honor 20 Lite price in Bangladesh and its details. It is powered by the Huawei HiSilicon Kirin 710 chipset, which includes an octa-core CPU (4x2.2 GHz Cortex-A73 and 4x1.7 GHz Cortex-A53) and an ARM Mali-G51 MP4 GPU. This phone comes with a triple rear camera setup that includes 24MP primary camera, 8MP ultra-wide camera, and 2MP depth sensor. For selfies, there’s a 32 MP front camera with an f/2.0 aperture. The device supports 1080p video recording for both front and rear cameras. This phone is available in colors like Phantom Blue, Phantom Red, and Magic Night Black. Its 3400 mAh non-removable battery comes with 10W wired charging capability. The Honor 20 Lite price in Bangladesh is BDT 28,990 (Official) for its 4GB of RAM and 64GB of internal storage.
