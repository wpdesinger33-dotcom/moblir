The Honor Holly, released in October 2014, is a budget-friendly smartphone from Huawei’s sub-brand, Honor. It features a 5.0-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The device is powered by a quad-core 1.3 GHz Cortex-A7 processor. Running on Android 4.4.2 KitKat with EMUI 2.3, the Honor Holly offers a user-friendly interface, although it lacks support for 4G LTE connectivity. The device is equipped with an 8-megapixel rear camera with LED flash and a 2-megapixel front camera, which are adequate for basic photography needs. The 2000 mAh removable battery provides sufficient power for a day’s use under moderate usage. The Honor Holly price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,990 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
