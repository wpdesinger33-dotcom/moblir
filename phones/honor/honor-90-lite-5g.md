The Honor 90 Lite 5G is a remarkable smartphone that offers a blend of style, performance, and value. This model comes with an impressive 8GB of RAM and a generous 256GB of internal storage, catering to the needs of users who require ample space for their apps, photos, and multimedia content. Notable for its advanced features, the Honor 90 Lite 5G boasts a triple camera setup with a 100MP wide-angle main sensor, complemented by a 5MP ultrawide and a 2MP macro lens, ensuring vibrant and detailed photos. Also 16MP front camera is perfect for selfies and video calls. Its 6.7-inch IPS LCD display with a 90Hz refresh rate offers a smooth and immersive viewing experience. Powered by the Mediatek Dimensity 6020 chipset and running on Android 13 with Magic UI 7.1, the device promises a responsive and user-friendly interface. Honor 90 Lite 5G official price in Bangladesh is BDT 25,999 with 8GB RAM and 256GB ROM. This phone has a different unofficial variant 8GB RAM and 256GB ROM. It stands out in the market with its sleek design, available in three elegant colors: Titanium Silver, Cyan Lake, and Midnight Black. It is available for purchase through authorized Honor retailers, Honor brand shops, and online platforms.
