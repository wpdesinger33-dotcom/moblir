The Honor X5c Plus was launched on 18 February 2026 in Bangladesh, is a low-end smartphone that aims at balance over hype. The phone has a 6.74-inch HD+ TFT LCD screen with a 90Hz refresh rate, which provides smoother scrolling than the standard entry-level  phones. Driven by the MediaTek Helio G81 (12nm) processor that has an octa-core processor. The 50MP dual rearcamera(f/1.8) is the leader of photography and provides 1080p video recording, whereas the selfie camera is a 5MP camera that can be used in casual video calls and social posts.It has one of the most powerful features in the 5260mAhbatteryand 15W charge -designed to last the day. Android 15 with MagicOS 9.0, side-mounted fingerprint sensor, dual SIM 4G, and a slim body of 7.89mm, the X5c Plus is a practical, modern and well-thought-out product within the low-end market. The Honor X5c Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,999 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 128GB of storage. It comes in Ocean Cyan and Midnight Black colors.
