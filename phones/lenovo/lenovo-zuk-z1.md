TheLenovoZUK Z1 is a mid-range phone that was officially unveiled in September 2015. This one has a 5.5" IPS LCDdisplaywith resolution of 1080 x 1920 pixels. It uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon 801 chipset that incorporates the quad-core 2.5GHz Krait 400 CPU and Adreno 330 GPU. It has a 13 MP rearcamerawith OIS and dual-LED flash and an 8MP front camera. The device comes with a 4100mAhbattery, and 18W fast charge. It is running on the Android 5.1.1 Lollipop Operating system. The Lenovo ZUK Z1 price in Bangladesh is BDT 21,890 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage.
