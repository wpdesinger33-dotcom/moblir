The Lenovo K10 Plus is a best choice for low budget phones. It was released on 30 Sep 2019. It features a 6.22-inch display with a resolution of 720 x 1520 pixels. The phone is powered by the Qualcomm SDM632 Snapdragon 632 chipset, and Adreno 506 GPU. In terms of photography, the Lenovo K10 Plus boasts a triple rear camera setup: a 13 MP primary lens, an 8 MP ultrawide lens, and a 5 MP depth sensor. For selfies, 16 MP front camera with an ultrawide lens (13mm). The phone runs on Android 9.0 (Pie) with Lenovo’s ZUI 11 skin. The device is powered by a non-removable 4050 mAh Li-Po battery, which supports 10W wired charging via USB Type-C. The Lenovo K10 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,000 (unofficial) for 4GB of RAM and 64G of phone storage. It comes with Black, and Sprite colors.
