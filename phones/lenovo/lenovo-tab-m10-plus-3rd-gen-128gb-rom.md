The Lenovo Tab M10 Plus (3rd Gen) (4GB+128GB) variant entered the Bangladesh market in May 2022 as a flexible tablet device. This tablet has 2 more variants 4GB+64GB and 6GB+128GB. All the features of both variants are the same, only the RAM and ROM are different. This tablet device has a IPS 10.61-inchdisplaythat presents 2000 x 1200 pixels resolution. The device operates with a Qualcomm Snapdragon 680 4G chipset that integrates an octa-core CPU along with an Adreno 610 GPU. The Tab M10 Plus (3rd Gen) operates on Android 12 with MagicUI 7. This tablet features dual cameras with 8MP resolution for the rear and front sides. The device comes with a 20W fast charging capability that supports its 7700 mAh battery. The Lenovo Tab M10 Plus (3rd Gen) price in Bangladesh is BDT 34,800 (Unofficial) for 4GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage.
