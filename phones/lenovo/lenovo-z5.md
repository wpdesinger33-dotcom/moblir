The Lenovo Z5 was announced in June 2018, features a 6.2-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 1080 x 2246 pixels. It is a mid-budget smartphone that has various features. Its sleek design includes a glass front (protected by Corning Gorilla Glass 3), a glass back, and an aluminum frame. The phone runs on Android 8.1 (Oreo) with Lenovo’s ZUI 3.9 interface. keep reading this article to know about the Lenovo Z5 price in Bangladesh and its details. In terms of performance, you’ll find a Qualcomm Snapdragon 636 chipset paired with 6GB of RAM. The internal storage options include 64GB and 128GB, both of which are non-expandable due to the shared SIM slot. The device is powered by a 3300mAh non-removable Li-Ion battery, supporting 15W wired charging. It boasts a dual-camera setup on the back: a 16MP primary lens with f/2.0 aperture and phase detection autofocus (PDAF), along with an 8MP secondary lens. The front-facing camera is an 8MP shooter for selfies and video calls.
