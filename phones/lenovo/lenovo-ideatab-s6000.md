This tablet by Lenovo hit the market in September 2013 and is specially developed for business and as a home entertainment. It has an impressively large multi touch screen 10.1 inch IPS LCD resolution of 1280 x 800 pixels. The tablet comes equipped with MediaTek MT8125 processor featuring a quad-core 1.2 GHz Cortex-A7 processor and PowerVR SGX544MP2 GPU. It is a 5MP rear and VGA front cameras, which can allow standard image and video shooting. The battery is a built-in Li-polymer 6350mAh type and the multimedia usage can be between 8 hours. Regular internet and data transfer features consist of Wi-Fi 802.11 b//g/n, Bluetooth 4.0, and microUSB 2.0. The tablet also has stereo speakers and it also has a 3.5mm headphone jack for clear and improved sound. The Lenovo IdeaTab S6000 price in Bangladesh is BDT 25,000 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
