TheLenovoK5 Note is an affordable smartphone which was launched at the end of January 2016. This smartphone has a 5.5-inch IPS LCDdisplayalong with the pixel density of 1080 x 1920 pixels. The phone is running on the support of MediaTek Helio P10 chipset with Mali-T860 MP2 GPU. It offers a superior 13MP rear camera for the photographer's lovers and the frontal 8MPcamera. The device has been coupled with a 3500mAhbattery. Sponsored on Android 5.1 Lollipop. The Lenovo K5 Note price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,490 (Official) for 3GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage. This phone comes in Silver, Gold, and Gray colors.
