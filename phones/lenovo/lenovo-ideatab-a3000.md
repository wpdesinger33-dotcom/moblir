Lenovo IdeaTab A3000 was launched in Bangladeshi in May 2013, it is an attractive tablet that can be used regularly, especially for regular usage as it comes in a lower price range. It is equipped with 7.0 inches of IPS display of 1024 x 600 pixels. The tablet operates a 1.2GHz quad-core processor. It contains a 5MP rear camera and VGA front camera. A 3500mAh non-removable battery is used, and the tablet has up to 8 hours talk time, and 336 hours of standby time. Based on Android 4.2 Jelly Bean, the IdeaTab A3000 includes standard communications features like the wireless Fidelity Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth version 4.0, and Universal Serial Bus USB 2.0. The Lenovo IdeaTab A3000 price in Bangladesh is BDT 20,500 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 16GB of ROM.
