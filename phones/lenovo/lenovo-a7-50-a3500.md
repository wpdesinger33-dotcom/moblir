Lenovo A7-50 A3500 is an affordable tablet computer that was launched in June 2014 in Bangladesh. It is equipped with a 7.0 IPS LCD touchscreen display with a resolution of 800 × 1280 pixels. The tablet operates under the operation system of Mediatek MT8382 chipset and a Quad-core 1.3 GHz Cortex-A7 processor. The device comes with a 5 MP rear facingcameraand 2 MP front facing camera. The tablet is powered by abatteryof 3450 mAh. Other aspects of the phone include Bluetooth, FM radio, and the in-built torch that makes the phone very useful to users. The Lenovo A7-50 A3500 price in Bangladesh is BDT 18,500 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
