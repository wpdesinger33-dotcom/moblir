TheLenovoA1000 is an entry level smartphone that was unveiled on September 1, 2015, and came to the market soon after. It has a 4.0 inches of IPS, LCDdisplay, and 480 x 800 pixels resolution. The performance of the phone is due to the Spreadtrum SC7731 chipset that has a quad core cortex A7 processor of 1.3 GHz and Mali-400 MP2 GPU. It has a 5 Megapixel rear and 0.3 Megapixel front facingcamera. The device is powered by a 2000 mAh removablebattery. This device is running on Android 5.O (Lollipop). The Lenovo A1000 price in Bangladesh is BDT 5,599 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
