TheLenovoK5 is among the budget smartphones unveiled on February 21, 2016, and the company released it on June 13, 2016. It includes an IPS LCDdisplayof 5.0 in size, and a pixel resolution of 720 x 1280. The Qualcomm Snapdragon 415 is an octa-core cpu and Adreno 405 provides the graphics processing. This phone comes equipped with a 13 MP rearcameraand 5 MP front camera. The device is also endowed with a 2750mAh removablebattery. Operates on Android 5.1 Lollipop. The Lenovo K5 price in Bangladesh is BDT 8,499 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of ROM. This phone comes in Champagne Gold, and Platinum Silver colors.
