Lenovo K5 Play is a low budget phone, it provides best features in low budget. It was released Apr 2018 in Bangladesh. It features a 5.7-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1440 pixels and an 18:9 aspect ratio. Powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 430 chipset. The dual rear camera setup consists of a 13 MP main lens and a 2 MP depth sensor, while the front camera is an 8 MP. Other notable features include a rear-mounted fingerprint scanner, dual-SIM 4G LTE connectivity, and a 3000 mAh non-removable battery. The phone runs on Android 8.0 Oreo with Lenovo's ZUI 3.7 interface. The Lenovo K5 Play price in Bangladesh is BDT 14,490 (official) for 3GB of RAM and 32GB of phone storage. Also it has an unofficial variant, its price at BDT 11,990. It comes in Black, Gray, and Blue colors.
