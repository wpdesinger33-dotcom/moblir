It was announced in September 2015 and hit the market in October, 2015, and theLenovoVibe P1m is an affordable smartphone. The phone comes with a 5.0-inch IPS LCDdisplaysupporting a resolution of 720 x 1280 pixels. The phone is run by an operating system MediaTek MT6735P chipset with 1.0 Ghz quad-core Cortex-A53 processor and Mali-T720 MP2 GPU. It also has an 8MP rearcameraand a 5MP front camera. The device has 4000mAhbatterypower which also supports 15W fast charging. It operates on android OS, version 5.1 also known as Lollipop.  The Lenovo Vibe P1m price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,990 (Official) for 2GB of RAM and 16GB of phone storage.
