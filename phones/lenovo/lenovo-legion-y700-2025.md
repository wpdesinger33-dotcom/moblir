The Lenovo Legion Y700 (2025) is a gaming tablet that is packed with many features to ensure that every gamer is able to enjoy a good game. It consists of an 8.8 inch WQXGA (2560 x 1600) IPS LCD panel with 165Hz refresh rate. The tablet is built on the platform of Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3. It also has a very healthy battery of 6550mAh and can support fast charging of up to 68W. It has a dual rear camera with a 13 MP main lens, 2 MP macro lens. The front camera is 8MP. It is a portable device with a thin profile of only 7.8mm and weighs 350 grams. The Lenovo Legion Y700 (2025) price in Bangladesh is BDT 80,000 (Expected) for 12GB of RAM and 256GB of phone storage.
