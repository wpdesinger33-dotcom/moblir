The Lenovo A6 Note is a budget-friendly smartphone that was released in the Bangladesh market in September 2019. It features a 6.09-inch IPS LCD display with a resolution of 720 x 1560 pixels, resulting in a screen-to-body ratio of approximately 80.7%. Keep reading this article to know about the Lenovo A6 Note price in Bangladesh and its overall details. This phone is powered by the MediaTek Helio P22 (MT6762) chipset, which includes an octa-core CPU clocked at 2.0 GHz. It comes with 3 GB of RAM and 32 GB of internal storage (expandable via microSDXC). In terms of photography, the A6 Note sports a dual-camera setup on the rear: a 13 MP primary lens with an f/2.0 aperture and a 2 MP depth sensor. The front-facing camera is a 5 MP. The phone runs on Android 9.0 Pie and is equipped with a 4000 mAh non-removable battery that supports 10W wired charging. Other notable features include a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor, Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 5.0, GPS, FM radio, and a microUSB 2.0 port. The Lenovo A6 Note is available in Black and Blue color options. The Lenovo A6 Note price in Bangladesh is BDT 12,990 for its 3GB of RAM and 32GB of internal storage.
