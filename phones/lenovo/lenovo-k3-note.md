The Lenovo K3 Note is an android smartphone that is under the budget smartphones brand launched in march 2015. It comes with a 5.5 inch IPS LCD touchscreen with a pixel density of 401ppi. The phone is run by MediaTek MT6752 chip with an octa-core 1.7 GHz Cortex-A53 CPU and Mali-T760MP2 GPU. The K3 Note has a 2GB RAM, 16GB ROM with an additional support for a microSD card. This is backed with a 13MP rear camera with dual-LED flash, it has the ability to capture high quality images and videos at 1080p at 30fps. The front-facing camera is 5MP.  It uses Google’s Android 5.2 Lollipop operating system. With a 3000mAh battery. The connectivity in Wi-Fi, Bluetooth variant 4.1 and USB has a version of 2.0. The Lenovo K3 Note price in Bangladesh is BDT 15,000 (Official).
