TheLenovoA6000 is an affordable smartphone that the company launched to market on January 28, 2015. It has an IPS LCD touch screen of 5.0 inches having the pixel density of 720 x 1280. The Qualcomm Snapdragon 410 is the chipset and consists of a 1.2 GHz Cortex-A53 quad core processor and an Adreno 306 GPU. It possesses a rear shooters’ 8MP and front lens of 2 MP. The device comes with a 2300 mAh Li-ion removablebattery. It is functioning on the Android operating system version 4.4.4 KitKat. The Lenovo A6000 price in Bangladesh is BDT 11,999 (Official) for 1GB of RAM and 8GB of phone storage.
