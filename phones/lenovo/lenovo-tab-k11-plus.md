The Lenovo Tab K11 Plus gives users advanced performance features and powerful capabilities for both business applications and entertainment purposes. The tablet shows an 11.45-inch IPS LCDdisplaythat delivers 1200 x 2000 pixels resolution through its 90Hzrefresh rate. The tablet operates on a Qualcomm Snapdragon 680 4G chipset with its octa-core processor and Adreno 610 GPU inside. The photographic capabilities of this tablet consist of a 13MP rear camera and an 8MP front camera. The Tab K11 Plus stands out because it comes with an 8600mAhbatterythat enables 20W fast charging capabilities. It runs on Android 14. Available in Luna Grey. The Lenovo Tab K11 Plus price in Bangladesh is BDT 33,500 (Unofficial) with 6GB of RAM and 128GB of phone storage.
