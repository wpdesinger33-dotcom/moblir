The Bengal BG03 entered the Bangladesh market as a basic feature phone during January 2021. The phone includes a 2.8-inch QVGAdisplaythat shows 128 x 160 pixel resolution. The device contains an 0.8MP maincamera. Two phone numbers can be managed through the dual SIM feature of the device. It includes a 1500mAh removablebattery. This device supports 2G network connectivity along with Bluetooth 2.0 and microUSB 2.0. The device includes both an FM radio component as well as loudspeaker technology and a 3.5mm audio jack that lets users enjoy music and radio. The plastic construction of this device comes with two color options of black and pink. The Bengal BG03 price in Bangladesh is BDT 2,840 (Official).
