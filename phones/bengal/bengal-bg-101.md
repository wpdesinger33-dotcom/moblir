The Bengal BG 101 entered the Bangladesh market in February 2020 to provide basic communication features through its feature phone design. The phone displays information through its 1.8-inch TFTdisplaywith 240 x 320 pixel resolution. The phone features a 0.2MP maincameraas its camera system. Users accessing the phone through dual SIM functionality gain the ability to operate two different phone numbers at once. The phone functions with a 1000mAhbatterywhich users can remove for replacement. In terms of connectivity, the Bengal BG 101 supports 2G networks, Bluetooth 2.0, and microUSB 2.0. This phone includes an FM radio system and loudspeaker and a 3.5mm audio jack for listening to music and radio. The phone design consists of plastic material and features three color options which are black, blue and white. The Bengal BG 101 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,370 (Official) for 4MB storage.
