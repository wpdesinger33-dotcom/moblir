The feature phone Bengal BG01 made its Bangladesh launch in January 2021 to fulfill basic communication needs. This phone displays information on a QVGA resolution 1.44-inch screen. The primarycameraof this phone comes with 0.8MP resolution. The phone enables users to operate two phone numbers on its dual SIM functionality. It includes a 1000mAh removablebattery. Bengal BG01 supports 2G networks together with Bluetooth 2.0 and microUSB 2.0 as its connectivity options. The device offers an FM radio feature together with a loudspeaker and a 3.5mm audio jack which allow users to listen to music and radio. The device comes with a plastic build that exists in black and gold color options. The Bengal BG01 price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,799 (Official).
