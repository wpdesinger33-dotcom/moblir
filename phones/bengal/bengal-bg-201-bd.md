The Bengal BG 201 BD serves as a basic communication device after its September 2021 release in Bangladesh. The device comes with a 2.4-inch TFTdisplaywhich delivers 240 x 320 pixels resolution. The phone contains a 0.2MP primarycameraas one of its features. The phone operates with dual SIM capabilities which enables people to handle two different phone lines at the same time. It includes a 1400mAh removablebattery. In terms of connectivity, the Bengal BG 201 BD supports 2G networks, Bluetooth 2.1, and microUSB 2.0. It also features an FM radio. The phone design incorporates plastic back materials while offering black, blue, green and red color options for customers. The Bengal BG 201 BD price in Bangladesh is BDT 1,899 (Official).
