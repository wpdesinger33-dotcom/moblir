The Energizer Ultimate U6 is a rather powerful smartphone which targets customers in need of a reliable performing phone with an extremely long battery life. The smartphone is built with a 6.75-inch IPS LCD full screen with 720x1620 pixels resolution. It has the MediaTek Dimensity 6100+. The Ultimate U6G comes with a third lens at the back that consists of 50MP primary lens, 2MP macro lens and 2MP depth camera. At the front, there is a 8MP lens. The phone consisted of a 5000mAh battery with a fast charge of 33 watts. The Energizer Ultimate U6 price in Bangladesh is BDT 20,000 (Expected) for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage.
