The Energizer Hard Case P28K is a tough phone that focuses on hard wearing qualities and long battery life. It comes with a 6.78 inches IPS LCD size screen with a pixel density of 1080 x 2460 pixels. The phone comes equipped with MediaTek Helio G99 chipset, octa-core CPU and Mali-G57 MC2 GPU for a smooth functioning. The Hard Case P28K comes with a triple rear camera of 64MP main camera, 20MP ultra wide lens & 2MP macro lens. The front facing camera is a 16MP wide lens. The device comes with a 28000mAh battery pack that is compatible with 33W fast charging. Moreover, it also has an IP68/IP69K rating on dust and water resistance features. It is available in black. The Energizer Hard Case P28K price in Bangladesh is BDT 30,000 (Expected) for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of ROM.
