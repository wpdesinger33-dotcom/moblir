itel is preparing to launch its upcoming Super 26 Ultra smartphone, which has recently surfaced in FCC, TUV Rheinland, and Geekbench listings. It is also speculated that itel Super 26 Ultra may enter Bangladesh soon. The device is said to sport a 3D curved AMOLED display with 1.5K resolution, a 144Hz refresh rate, and Corning Gorilla Glass 7i protection. Under the hood, it's powered by a Unisoc T7300 SoC built on a 6nm process and houses a 6,000mAh battery in a slim 6.8mm body. It also supports AI Rainproof touch and is being marketed as an AI phone.
